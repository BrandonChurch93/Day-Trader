# TM09 draft: US stock desk with news

STATED: This draft asks whether adding a news scout helps the same US stock desk after assumed costs. The lead permits the limited Alpaca use described here on the lead's reading of published terms. This is not a registration, a decision on the pass mark, or permission to launch, send headlines to a model now, or read real-bar returns.

## Source rights

## Paired comparison

STATED: For each of those weeks, run each boss model in both arms with fresh, isolated sessions. Run one price scout for that week and give both arms its exact frozen notes, revealed at their original wakes. Run one news scout for that week; only the news arms see its corresponding notes. That is two scouts plus four independent boss sessions per week. Use identical model versions, prompts except the treatment field, price data, ticker basket, account start, position and risk limits, calendar, wake and fill rules, and cost model. Fix the scout models, note and input caps, rounding, and what to do with a failed arm before registration. Give both arms the same clock and action capacity. No world search, outside tools, cross-arm memory, old results or later data.

STATED: The news scout gets only the headline, source time and symbol. It does not get article body, filing body, summary, link or attachment. Feed content is never committed, logged to the repo or quoted in a report. The scout may name the event in its own words but may not paste a headline into a note. Keep the raw reader transient and prevent raw request or response logs and training reuse wherever the hosted setup permits; do not launch if those controls cannot be enforced. Both arms get the same frozen price notes, not a second independent price scout. In the news arm, notes must be sealed to their first eligible wake and not disclosed earlier; only then may a boss use them.

STATED: Start both arms flat each week. Use the repo's stock next-minute opening-price fill rule and same minute-volume cap, applying `tm_stock_half_spread_bps` on traded value on each side and no simulated stock commission as in TM04. Fix a common terminal valuation and closing-cost rule at registration, with no price lookahead in decisions. The registered assumption is not an execution guarantee. [Doc 136](../docs/research/136_measured_stock_trading_costs.md) has measured median half-spreads of 3.787 bp near the open, 1.591 bp midday, and 1.074 bp in the last half-hour for these stocks. The pooled opening 90th percentile was 10.055 bp across the study's 56 names; one late-window quote statistic does not price actual close-outs.

STATED: For each boss and week, subtract price-only net return from news-arm net return. Show each boss's paired differences and the equal-weight mean of the two bosses' differences per week. Treat the week, not the wake, ticker, or boss, as the independent unit for the planning calculation. Both arms get the same November 25 roster chosen after the fact. That equalizes access to the list inside a pair but does not undo selection bias or rule out an interaction between news and the selected names. Passing a published knowledge line and the recall probe does not rule out hindsight or prove recent news was absent from training. Results would be conditional on this tape and these names, not evidence of forward profitability.

## Power before the lock

MEASURED: The public [TM04 stock basket report](../docs/research/36_time_machine_tm04_stock_basket.md#run-1-against-run-2) and [aggregate result](results_tm04.json) give the same Opus model's run 1 minus run 2 whole-period net gain gap as -$29.93 on a $1,000 starting book and 5.56 of eight holdings in common per week on average. Both runs already had news, carried positions between weekly wakes, and made one pick per week. This shows run-to-run variation on the same slate. It is not the standard deviation of weekly paired net differences.

UNKNOWN: The candidate noise proxy is the TM04 Opus run 1 versus run 2 sample standard deviation of same-week net return differences, centered on their own mean. The public aggregate does not contain these weekly series. Dividing the whole-period gap by the number or square root of weeks does not recover it. A separately approved, locked price-only pilot could estimate run-to-run noise on unused full weeks after both maker lines and before July 6, if any existed. Under the chosen cutoff there are none, only the July 1 and July 2 sessions, which cannot furnish a weekly spread. The scored 20 weeks cannot be used for that pilot. TM09's actual paired spread and power are unknown; no real-bar returns were read or calculated for this draft.

STATED: With 20 independent weeks, a two-sided 5% test and 80% power, the optimistic normal approximation needs a true mean paired gain around 0.63 times the weekly paired standard deviation. At assumed weekly spreads of 50, 100 or 200 bp that is roughly 31, 63 or 125 bp per week, not estimates of achievable gains. Dependence between weeks and a small-sample adjustment would raise the required difference. The 20-week sample cannot be called adequate for a modest effect without a suitable measured proxy and a lead-set economically meaningful difference. If the design proceeds, treat it as diagnostic unless the lead can justify a powered decision before lock. The lead sets the mark; this draft sets none.

## Superseded crypto proposal

STATED: The coin desk with news is not launchable from the lawful free sources checked: all three tape sources are excluded for new runs, the old rows stay only as TM02's audit record, and the earlier collection must not be extended.

- **CryptoPotato:** Its [Use License](https://cryptopotato.com/terms/) permits a temporary copy “for personal, non-commercial transitory viewing only” and says the user may not “modify or copy the materials.”

- **CryptoSlate:** Its [terms](https://cryptoslate.com/terms-and-conditions/) bar bots and scripts used “to access, copy, extract, monitor, or interfere with CryptoSlate without prior written permission.”

MEASURED: No crypto tape rows or TM07 results were opened for this draft. The publisher check used terms or policy pages only, not crypto content endpoints.
