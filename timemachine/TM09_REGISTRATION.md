No real bars, returns, headlines or TM07 results were read for this registration.

## What is pinned

- **Config:** `tm09` in [thresholds.yaml](../config/thresholds.yaml) is the source of the registered values. Seal the resolved values of the stock config keys it references alongside it.
- **Weeks and roster:** Use every full New York Monday-to-Sunday week from `tm09.window.first_monday` through `tm09.window.last_monday`, inclusive, for `tm09.window.weeks` scheduled weeks. Use precisely the single-stock list at `tm09.roster_key`, with no ETFs and no substitutions. Do not drop quiet stocks or weeks. The list was chosen after the replay period. Using it in every arm makes the comparison fair on that list, not a point-in-time selection or a test of forward profitability.
- **Wakes:** Use the New York clock and the existing stock wake list referenced by `tm09.clock`. Admit only wakes on the recorded stock session calendar, at or after its open and strictly before its close.
- **News clock:** Use only `tm09.news_sources`. Start with the source creation time for Alpaca and the filing acceptance time for EDGAR. Exclude later edits identified by the tape and rows with missing or inconsistent clocks. Add `tm09.clock.known_at_lag_minutes` to the source clock and require the result to be strictly before a wake. Admit the item at its first eligible wake, including after a holiday or weekend, only for symbols on the fixed roster.
- **Coverage, MEASURED:** The [counts-only snapshot](tm09_stock_news_counts.json) records the existing calendar and news metadata check. Its equal creation and update times do not prove that an original headline version survived. UNKNOWN: The first historical availability of the stored wording. The lag fixes the replay clock, not the text's vintage.

## Arms, prompts and treatment

- **Scouts:** Run one price scout and one news scout per week, with the model IDs in `tm09.scouts`. Seal the price notes once by wake and give the exact same stream to every boss arm. Seal the news notes by wake and give that stream only to N. A boss may see a note only at its assigned wake, never earlier.
- **Bosses:** Run each model in `tm09.boss_models` once per arm per week, with fresh, isolated sessions. P1 and P2 are independent price-only repeats; N gets the same price notes plus news notes. Run the scouts first, then run every boss and arm combination for that week. The arms receive the sealed notes in wake order.
- **Equal treatment:** Keep the starting book, prices, rules, wake times, limits, tools, and boss prompt the same across arms except for the news-note field. No web, outside tools, cross-arm memory, other workers' outputs, or later data. Workers get only the input for their assigned role and the replay clock.
- **Source rights, STATED:** The lead reads Alpaca's [published terms](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/TermsAndConditions.pdf) as permitting the owner's private, noncommercial research as described in the draft. [EDGAR public filing content is free to reuse](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions). UNKNOWN: Express written Alpaca clearance for hosted-model reading. The owner has been told and can ask Alpaca for written confirmation.
- **Private delivery:** The news scout gets only headline, source time and symbol. No article or filing body, summary, URL or attachment. It may name the event in its own words, but may not paste a headline in a note. Feed content is never committed, logged to the repo, or quoted in a report. Prevent raw request or response logging and training reuse before launch. Send only paraphrased notes to N.
- **Rehearsal:** Before any scored week, use made-up headlines and made-up bars only. Show that the news scout writes a non-empty note, N receives it at the assigned wake and not before, and neither price-only arm receives it. Keep a pass record without any live headline or bar. Without this check, do not start the scored run.
- **Delivery mark:** For each scheduled week, count wakes with at least one admitted item and how many of those carry a non-empty news note. Report counts only. Divide the sum of non-empty-note wakes by the sum of item-bearing wakes across the full scheduled window, not an average of weekly fractions. A missing note at an eligible wake does not count as delivery. If the fraction is below `tm09.delivery.min_fraction_eligible_wakes_with_note`, label the result **treatment not delivered** and do not pass it.

## Fills, costs and valuation

- **Account:** Start every boss arm flat with the cash named by `tm09.execution.starting_cash_key`. Use the same order and risk rules in all arms. No real orders or money.
- **Fills:** An eligible order fills at the open of the next recorded minute, with the configured half-spread above the open for a buy and below it for a sell. Apply the configured minute-volume cap, cancel the remainder under the stock rule, and charge only the configured stock commission. Fractional shares are for the benchmark only.
- **Desk valuation:** On Friday, or the week's last recorded stock session, mark positions held after any fills from that session's last wake at that session's close less the configured half-spread. Do not expose that close to a worker or mistake the mark for an order fill. Net weekly return is the change from starting cash to ending cash plus marked holdings, divided by starting cash.
- **Benchmark:** Start each week in cash. Buy every roster stock in equal dollar weight at the next recorded minute open after the first configured wake of Monday's session, or of the week's first session if Monday is a holiday. Pay the configured half-spread on entry, allow fractional shares and do not apply a volume cap. Do not rebalance. Mark the holdings at the same last-session close less the half-spread. Use this benchmark only for the forward label.

## Weekly series and marks

- **Primary:** For each complete boss-week, subtract the mean of P1 and P2 net returns from N's net return. Average those differences equally across the included bosses for that week.
- **Placebo:** For each complete boss-week, subtract P2's net return from P1's. Average those differences equally across the same included bosses and weeks. Use the week, not the boss, trade, stock or wake, as the unit of scoring.
- **Test statistic:** For each weekly series, divide the mean weekly difference by its standard error, using the sample standard deviation. Use the signed primary t for the one-sided test and the absolute placebo t for the noise check. Use only the weeks with a primary value for t and both primary means. Do not round before applying the marks.
- **News helps:** All of these must hold: primary t at least `tm09.marks.primary_t_min`; at least `tm09.marks.positive_weeks_min` strictly positive weeks out of the full scheduled window; primary mean still strictly positive after removing the best week; primary mean at least `tm09.marks.mean_weekly_bps_min` basis points per week; and absolute placebo t strictly below `tm09.marks.placebo_abs_t_strict_max`, subject to the exact-zero exception below. A missing week does not count as positive. The delivery mark and failure rules also apply.
- **Undefined t:** If the primary sample variance is zero or the primary t is otherwise undefined, label the test **inconclusive**. If the placebo t is undefined with nonzero placebo mean, the placebo mark is not met. If every placebo difference is exactly zero, report that and treat the placebo mark as met. A failed placebo mark is **harness noise** and does not pass.
- **Worth following forward:** First pass **news helps**. Also require the mean weekly net return of N to be strictly above the mean weekly equal-weight benchmark over the same weeks. Compute weekly N as the equal-weight mean of the N returns for the included bosses, then compare the means over the same included weeks. A tie does not qualify. This label is not a claim about forward returns.
- **Power, UNKNOWN:** Power is unknown before the run. The placebo series is the measured noise yardstick at the end of the run. Report its weekly spread and t or why t is undefined. The counts-only census cannot estimate power.

## Failures and scoring custody

- **Recovery:** A lost worker has at most `tm09.failures.max_successors_per_lost_worker` logged successor. The successor continues the same session from its sealed handoff. Do not rerun a finished session.
- **Incomplete pair:** If any arm is unfinished, drop that boss from both series for that week and count a lost week for that boss. If the other boss has all its arms, use that boss alone for the week's primary and placebo differences and list the week and lost boss. If neither boss has all its arms, omit the week from both series and list it as missing.
- **Inconclusive:** More than `tm09.failures.max_lost_weeks_per_boss` lost weeks for either boss, or fewer than `tm09.failures.min_weeks_with_primary` weeks with a primary value, makes the test inconclusive. List the lost weeks per boss and the number of usable weekly values.
- **Scoring:** Keep weekly profit figures sealed until the end. Score once at the end. An independent checker who has not read the scorer recomputes from the sealed inputs and the registration. Resolve a disagreement against the sealed record, not by trying a different mark.
