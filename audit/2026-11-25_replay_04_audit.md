# Independent audit: Replay 04 coin basket

I reproduced K1 and K4 from the raw data without importing the trading rig. The ordinary monthly membership, signals and next-open fills passed tests that changed future prices, volume and trading activity. The delisting exit fails a separate, decisive look-ahead test: it uses the final bar identified from the full archive to cash out at a price no longer executable. It did not fire in this dataset. None of the changes I tested produced a pass against hold bitcoin. The closest case is Binance.US K4, where only deflated Sharpe fails: 0.93313 versus a required value above 0.95.

## Time-order checks

I altered opens, closes and dollar volume from 2023-01-01 and, separately, from 2026-04-01, and removed alternating future prints while keeping the original terminal timestamps for those two tests. For the selected month itself and all earlier months, all 56 and 95 membership records respectively were exactly the same, including K2 rankings and copy decisions. Prior targets were exactly equal for K1 through K5. Maximum pre-cut equity differences were 7.2e−15 and 1.7e−14, consistent with floating-point summing over slightly different column sets. In the separate terminal-date test, changing only future prints did change some prior K3 target records, but not real-sample equity (04-15). The synthetic terminal-price example did change equity (04-01).

In a 1,000-draw diagnostic that preserves K5's Wednesday signal phase, the Binance.US shuffle percentile moved from 0.914 to 0.919. Both readings fail mark 5.

- **Monthly rules:** Market age, 90-day trading activity, volatility, correlation, median traded dollars and K2 momentum all stop at day D−1 for membership effective D. K1/K2/K3 use that new membership in the target formed on D−1, then fill at the next open; intra-month P1 only reads closes through the signal day. K4 uses the same P1 on fixed BTC and ETH sleeves. Including any pair that later becomes a member in the allocated panel did not move any measured prefix.
- **K5:** Exponentials use exactly the trailing 180 closes for half-lives 1/5, 2.5/10, 5/20 and 10/40, with the right edge on the signal day. Targets update on the bar dated Wednesday (closing at next UTC midnight). The engine could rebalance an unchanged target outside Thursday if drift exceeds the band, but actual base fills were 208, all Thursdays, at both Binance.US and Kraken $10k tier. There is no ordinary same-close execution. Treat any live use of a daily close and immediately adjacent daily open as an unmeasured latency assumption.
- **Missing bars:** Inside a history gap, forward fills supply stale close for valuation and signals, zero volume for selection, and no open for execution. Actual P1 and K5 don't use subsequent raw close values to fill an internal gap. The engine retries against the newest available signal, as the amendment says. K3 had one waiting episode at each venue; all other base rules and both controls had none. A fabricated delist settlement differs from an ordinary missing-bar wait (04-01).

## Other checks that passed

- **Costs and controls:** Each buy pays the traded value plus the fee, each sell receives the traded value minus the fee, and both controls pay the same per-coin costs as strategies. At 2/25/38/80/90 bps venue fees, BTC and ETH pay 7/30/43/85/95 bps per side; other names pay 27/50/63/105/115. The 50 bps alt-slippage sensitivity leaves BTC and ETH rules unchanged. There is no free trading when the basket changes. Hold BTC pays once at the first traded open with all cash, carries through slices and correctly has no terminal sale. Hold basket is the more like-for-like comparison for K1's universe and a secondary benchmark, not a replacement for the stated alternative of BTC. Non-Kraken fees are comparison settings, not evidence that every coin is tradable on those venues.
- **Metrics and slices:** All-test includes first trading day, and development, held and post-cutoff use the prior closing equity before first return. The post ends on 2026-09-30. Trade sides use economic fills above the specified dust threshold, turnover uses prior closing wealth over elapsed days, and average weight and share held are distinct. Calendar years chain prior year-end holdings. Buy-and-hold carries rather than resetting within the slice.
- **Statistics:** DSR takes the five rules' sample standard deviation of daily Sharpes by venue, counts 49 and uses each rule's daily skew and raw kurtosis. CSCV evaluates all 12,870 complementary 8-of-16 allocations, with 3,040 rows retained (4 discarded), earliest-drop as verdict and latest-drop also reported. At Binance.US tie-inclusive/strict/half were 0.09596/0.08019/0.08807; no venue's reported variant approaches 0.25. The 30-day moving block bootstrap uses paired daily returns, 2,000 draws and seed 7. Break-even charges BTC and ETH the same trial cost as hold BTC and adds 20 bps to alts for the rule and basket comparison; cap status and crossing counts are explicit. The coarse grid cannot certify that no unsampled crossing exists, but it follows the specified method.
- **Checks and limits:** Both `fixtures2.py` and `fixtures_basket.py` printed ALL PASS when run read-only. The independent ledger and read-only probe scripts are alongside this report. None of my scripts writes to `research_rig/` or copies raw prices into the worktree. These results do not establish executable next-open fills, actual market impact, or out-of-sample profitability on a fresh market interval.
