# Fact check: `docs/playbook/03_role_of_ai.md`

Scope: 12 Key numbers rows and four Rules for the build sentences. Neither Bottom line sentence has a numeric claim or a `[MEASURED]` tag, so that section has no entries under the brief. Verdicts concern factual support; tag checks are reported separately, including when the figures are verified.

1. **Rule, line 11.** “Wrap Alpaca MCP tools in our allowlist; block destructive tools (`cancel_all_orders`, `close_all_positions`, `update_account_config`) from agents, and never point an agent at the hosted live URL before building human approval [MEASURED] (research §7).”
   - **Source URLs:** [Alpaca issue #127](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-mcp-server/issues/127), [cited tool inventory](https://tradingagentstack.com/data/tools.csv), [Alpaca tool reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/trading-api-mcp), [Alpaca hosted MCP guidance](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/agentic).
   - **Verdict: NOT IN SOURCE. Severity: Minor.** The capabilities are documented, but the project's precise allowlist and build-before-live policy are neither reported facts nor source requirements.
   - **Source quote:** Alpaca lists “Cancel all open orders,” “Liquidate entire portfolio,” and “Update account configuration.” Its hosted guidance says “Test agent workflows against paper trading or the Broker sandbox before switching to live” and “Review what your agent proposes before you confirm it” ([Alpaca tool reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/trading-api-mcp), [Alpaca hosted MCP guidance](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/agentic)).
   - **Tag check:** `[MEASURED]` is not right for the rule. The official tool list supports what the tools do, not a measured result for the house allowlist or a broker-enforced approval gate.
   - **Omitted context:** The open issue is one reporter's account of generated tools and clients that use safety annotations: “Only the three hand-written `place_*` tools set `destructiveHint`.” That is not a test of approval behavior on all clients or hosted deployments ([Alpaca issue #127](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-mcp-server/issues/127)).

2. **Rule, line 13.** “Do not route news-reaction trades through slow periodic LLM escalation; most news/earnings price discovery occurs within the first hour, leaving no significant edge by next open [MEASURED] (research §4).”
   - **Source URL:** [Wang, §VI-C/D and Table IX](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530).
   - **Verdict: WRONG. Severity: Major.** Neither pooled group records a majority of the gross move in the first hour under the study's own measure. The lack of significant return for next-open entry holds in this short holding-window test, not for all news or horizons ([Wang](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530)).
   - **Source quote:** The paper reports “+0.40% in the direction of earnings and guidance news (t=2.03) and by +0.09% in the direction of other news (t=3.0)” in the first hour, against whole moves of “+0.92% after earnings and guidance news (t=1.9) and +0.36% after other news (t=3.5).” Entering at the next open and exiting the next close “kept +0.31% and +0.04% (t=1.0 and 0.6)” ([Wang](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530)).
   - **Tag check:** `[MEASURED]` needs the single-preprint and event-study caveats. The routing instruction is a recommendation rather than the outcome of a tested routing comparison.
   - **Omitted context:** The analysis centered on ten large-cap stocks in 2026, used 5 bp per side, and had only one earnings season of minute bars. The same paper found five-day post-open drift after good earnings (+0.34% over SPY, t=2.2) and good sector news (+0.51%, t=2.1); the separately cited JFE paper found one-to-two-day drift particularly for smaller stocks and negative news ([Wang](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530), [Lopez-Lira and Tang](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X26001066)).

4. **Rule, line 17.** “Gate tiny live capital on at least 60 trading days AND 100 closed paper trades (models off, then shadow) using publication timestamps, realistic fees/slippage, and multiple seeds against buy-and-hold and trend baselines; require 6-12 months live evidence to scale and a written owner decision in `archive/decisions/` for any promotion (our recommendation; 0.68-year test gives true Sharpe 1.0 only 19% power) [STATED] (research §2, §3, §6).”
   - **Source URLs:** [Audit working paper source](https://github.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/blob/main/paper/source/paper.tex), [audit supplementary results](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/main/results/summary_statistics.txt), [audit calculation](https://github.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/blob/main/code/05_analysis.py), [Wang benchmark replay](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530).
   - **Verdict: WRONG. Severity: Minor.** The paper's prose repeats the claim, but its own output uses 145 observations with 252 per year to get 19%, not 0.68 years. The stated formula, applied to 0.68 annualized years at a true Sharpe of 1.0, gives about 20.6% ([audit working paper source](https://github.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/blob/main/paper/source/paper.tex), [audit supplementary results](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/main/results/summary_statistics.txt), [audit calculation](https://github.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/blob/main/code/05_analysis.py)).
   - **Source quote:** “At the median test length, a strategy with a true Sharpe ratio of 1 has a 19% chance of producing a significant result”; the output separately prints “test length yrs median 0.68 IQR 0.48-1.48” and “power true SR 1 at 145 days: 0.19,” and the code evaluates `N.cdf(s*math.sqrt(T0/252)-1.645)` ([audit working paper source](https://github.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/blob/main/paper/source/paper.tex), [audit supplementary results](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/main/results/summary_statistics.txt), [audit calculation](https://github.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/blob/main/code/05_analysis.py)).
   - **Tag check:** `[STATED]` is appropriate for the explicit house gates and as a label for an unreplicated working-paper estimate, but does not resolve the numerical error.
   - **Omitted context:** Power is an approximate normal calculation for a one-sided 5% test of positive annualized Sharpe. The source estimates powers using a literature sample with 35 usable papers for test lengths; it does not establish 60 days, 100 closed paper trades, or 6-12 live months as sufficient thresholds ([audit working paper source](https://github.com/Navid-A-08/LLM-Trading-Audit/blob/main/paper/source/paper.tex)).
