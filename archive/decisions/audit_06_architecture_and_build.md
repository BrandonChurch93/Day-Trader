# Audit: Architecture and Build Playbook

- BLOCKER | Lines 6, 11, 16 and 20–35: The diagram merges model output and baseline orders at the risk engine and then the OMS. The report uses `TradeIntent` as an advisory type and specifically requires shadow outputs not to execute in Phase 3; a veto does not make model-selected trades deterministic. The cross-repo platform report also flags vendor terms against unattended personalized decisions. Replace the diagram with: “Deterministic signals → risk checks → paper OMS → broker reconciliation. Separately: read-only evidence → cheap triage → frontier review → append-only shadow journal and ablation metrics. No model-to-OMS route.” Replace the promotion sentence with: “Keep model output research-only; promote it for research use only if post-cutoff shadow testing shows net improvement after API costs and execution delay.” ([Anthropic consumer terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms), [Anthropic usage policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup))
- BLOCKER | Lines 13, 15 and 58: “Query before retrying” leaves a false-negative path. Alpaca says an unsuccessful lookup does not prove that an order was never created, and its example CLI can retry some submissions automatically. Its paper skill requires startup and per-order verification, not just a live confirmation flag. Replace the two rules with: “For the deployed MVP, pin the paper endpoint and verify account and mode at startup and before every submission. Reject live flags or credentials; keep the stores separate. Any future live deployment requires separate out-of-band human approval (research §§4, 8) [STATED]” and “Persist one `client_order_id` per logical attempt. On ambiguous submission, look up and reconcile the original ID; if unresolved, halt and alert rather than resubmit. Disable automatic submission retries (research §5) [STATED].” ([Alpaca paper startup guard](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-skills/blob/39111abee6b60af7c11d40b5fc892dfc4fd791a4/skills/trading-api/paper-trading/SKILL.md), [Alpaca order audit and reconciliation](https://alpaca.markets/learn/ai-agent-audit-trail-alpaca-paper-order))
