- Every claim carries a label (MEASURED, STATED, UNKNOWN) and a "Last verified" date.
- Two layers of documents keep an agent's context small without losing the sources.
- Hard rules, a stop-and-ask list and a file that wins conflicts.
- Dated decision records, and audits whose fixes were applied.
- Paper by default, and live only on a written owner decision.
- A trial ledger and written cost assumptions before any result counts. That is a floor and a bar in the sense of `00_READ_FIRST.md` item 4.
