# Idea Round 38: Mining the Independent Replication Libraries

Date: 2026-11-26
Scope: Direct audit of seven public 2025 to 2026 trading-rule replication libraries that re-test published rules after publication, after transaction costs, against random entry or benchmarks, and with multiple-testing or locked-holdout controls.
Deliverables: (1) independence audit of each library, (2) every surviving rule in US brokerage-accessible assets ($500 cash account: stocks, ETFs, CEFs) and separately any needing a crypto venue, each with its Doc 117 screen (`N x (net_edge / noise)^2 >= 4`), and (3) a one-line cemetery of best-known killed rules so future rounds stop re-suggesting them.

## Summary Ruling

Across seven independent replication libraries covering more than 2,600 tested strategies (`1,778` Tickfloor tests, `1,027` Open-Alpha-Lab studies, `505` RIP Backtest variants, `108` KruegerAlgorithms locked holdouts, `10` eStockes tests, plus Dead Signals Lab and Empirica Lab), **zero new queue rows** clear our strict Round 37/38 bar (post-publication, after-cost, random-entry-beating, long-only $500 US brokerage account, Doc 117 `>= 4`, not already tested, queued, or skipped). Rows from 110 stay unused.

The sole active trading rule that survived across multiple independent libraries (`KruegerAlgorithms`, `RIP Backtest`, and `Empirica Lab`) is **Connors `RSI(2) < 10` on US equity index ETFs**, which we already queued in Round 37 as **Queue Row 109 (Plan 67, ledger 211)**. Every other survivor across all seven libraries is either (a) an overlapping same-family variant with thin excess (`Double 7s`, `+10 bps` over random uptrend dips, Doc 117 `0.28 < 4`), (b) short-only, futures-only, or leverage-dependent (`FTSE 100 closing-auction short`, `NQ Noise Area`, `HEWJ` vs short `EWJ` carry isolation), (c) a long-horizon core portfolio allocation rather than an active trading edge (`All-Weather` risk parity, `60/40`, `ANGL` permanent buy-and-hold credit ETF, `Storm-Shy` vol targeting), or (d) explicitly declared untradeable after real friction (`Dead Signals Lab` 10-day post-13D residue).

In crypto venues, **zero** rules passed global multiple-testing or earned `Signal = Real` plus `Tradability = Investable` across all seven libraries.
