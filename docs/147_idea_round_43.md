## Broker Approval Levels and Account Minimums ($500 US Account)

A $500 US retail account can buy single-leg calls and puts at both Alpaca and Fidelity, but debit spreads are barred in a Fidelity cash account (and require $10,000 minimum equity plus margin) and require Level 3 approval at Alpaca where sub-$2,000 live Level 3 eligibility is not stated in public documentation.

Alpaca (`STATED`, [Alpaca Cash Accounts Support](https://alpaca.markets/support/alpaca-cash-accounts), [Alpaca Options Trading Overview](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading-overview), [Alpaca Margin and Short Selling](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/margin-and-short-selling), [Alpaca Level 3 Options Guide](https://alpaca.markets/learn/level-3-options-trading), [Alpaca Options Level 3 Docs](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-level-3-trading), and [Alpaca Options Tutorial](https://alpaca.markets/learn/how-to-trade-options-with-alpaca)):
- Account structure (`STATED`): Alpaca does not offer pure cash accounts; it treats accounts below $2,000 as limited-margin accounts with 1x buying power. "No, we do not offer cash accounts. All accounts are set up as margin accounts. For those accounts with less than $2,000 in equity, we treat them as limited margin accounts, meaning you can trade on unsettled funds and are not limited to waiting for trades to settle before you can use the proceeds to purchase additional securities." ([Alpaca Cash Accounts Support](https://alpaca.markets/support/alpaca-cash-accounts)). Margin leverage and short selling require $2,000: "In order to trade on margin or sell short, you must have $2,000 or more account equity. Accounts with less than $2,000 will not have access to these features and will be restricted to 1x buying power." ([Alpaca Margin and Short Selling](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/margin-and-short-selling)).
- Long calls and long puts (`STATED`): Requires **Options Level 2** ("Level 1 Strategies - Buy a call - Buy a put"; "User must have sufficient options buying power"; "For both buying a call or buying a put sufficient buying power is necessary to pay for the premium of the contract otherwise an error will occur", [Alpaca Options Trading Overview](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading-overview)). No dollar account minimum above the contract premium is stated; commission is $0 for self-directed retail API accounts ("Commission-free trading means that there are no commission charges for Alpaca self-directed individual cash brokerage accounts that trade U.S.-listed securities and options through an API.", [Alpaca Options Tutorial](https://alpaca.markets/learn/how-to-trade-options-with-alpaca)).
- Debit spreads (`STATED` / `UNKNOWN`): Requires **Options Level 3** ("Level 1 and 2 Strategies - Buy a call spread - Buy a put spread", [Alpaca Options Trading Overview](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading-overview)) and a single covered multi-leg order ("Starting on day zero of Options Level 3 trading, an MLeg order is accepted only if all its legs are covered within the same MLeg order.", [Alpaca Options Level 3 Docs](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-level-3-trading)). Live Level 3 requires separate approval ("Before live trading Level 3 options with Alpaca, you will need to be approved.", [Alpaca Level 3 Options Guide](https://alpaca.markets/learn/level-3-options-trading)); whether Alpaca approves live Level 3 on an account under $2,000 equity is `UNKNOWN` from public documentation.

## Candidate 1: Pre-Earnings Straddles or Calls Sold Before Release

Verdict: **DROP** (midpoint-only pre-2014 paper; spreads consume the pre-release gross edge; post-2011 replication is negative net; Doc 117 `0.00 < 4`; no lawful free historical NBBO quotes).

Published baseline (`STATED`, [Gao, Xing, and Zhang, JFQA Dec 2018](https://www.pbcsf.tsinghua.edu.cn/__local/A/11/35/B0A1714D795BD9799087BCCC2AD_A01C4283_54641.pdf?e=.pdf)):
- Sample and count: "Our sample period is Jan. 1996 to Dec. 2013." (p. 2591). Table 1 Panel B (p. 2592) contains `76,848` straddle observations, dropping to `42,080` after liquidity filters (p. 2602), or roughly `2,338` to `4,269` events per year (clears 300 per year). No separate post-2010 numerical breakdown is reported, and footnote 14 (p. 2605) notes: "The time-series results show that the positive straddle returns have not become larger in the most recent years, which fails to support the earnings calendar popularity notion."
- Pre-release holding window `[-3, -1]` (bought 3 days before earnings and sold strictly before the release): Table 2 Panel B (pp. 2595 to 2596) reports `+1.90%` equal-weight (`t = 16.35`), `+2.18%` volume-weight (`t = 16.47`), and `+1.95%` dollar-open-interest-weight (`t = 16.74`). Under the stricter liquidity filter, Table 3 Panel A (p. 2604) reports `+2.62%` equal-weight (`t = 9.67`) and `+1.37%` dollar-open-interest-weight (`t = 3.81`). The headline `+3.34%` (`t = 6.71`) is for `[-3, 0]` equal-weight (Table 3 Panel A, p. 2604), which drops to `+1.10%` (`t = 2.00`) when dollar-open-interest-weighted.
- Assumed fill and spread exposure: Returns are strictly midpoint with no bid-ask spread deduction. "Therefore, we use bid-ask average prices to compute returns throughout this article, and we focus on holding-period returns in this section." (p. 2599). Table 4 Panel C (p. 2610) shows the effect lives in illiquid, wide-spread options: "For options with the lowest and highest bid-ask spreads, the straddle returns are 1.14% and 4.93%, respectively, and the difference is 3.79% with a t-statistic of 5.33." (p. 2612).

Typical option spreads and later post-2010 samples (`STATED`):
- Dubinsky, Johannes, Kaeck, and Seeger (2019, Review of Financial Studies, [VU Research Portal PDF](https://research.vu.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/108247883/Option_Pricing_of_Earnings_Announcement_Risks.pdf), Panel B): pooled average ATM single-stock option bid-ask spread is `6.17%` of midpoint on earnings days and `7.11%` to `7.71%` in 2011 to 2015 (`6.02%` to `7.11%` in pre-earnings days). "For instance, pooled average bid-ask spreads for ATM options are 6.17% on EADs versus 6.05% over the period from 11 to 20 trading days after the announcement."
- De Silva, Smith, and So (2024, Journal of Finance, [AFA Journal of Finance](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=122472), Jan 2010 to Feb 2021): "This panel demonstrates that the typical percentage half spreads that retail traders incur are on the order of 8%." (roughly `16%` full spread on retail earnings options).
- BSIC replication on S&P 500 stocks from Dec 2011 to Dec 2021 ([BSIC Part II, Apr 2023](https://bsic.it/straddling-outside-and-into-earnings-part-ii-2/)): "Over the analysed period, we find that without considering transaction costs, a straddle earns on average 1.17% in [T-3,T+1]." "Returns become negative when taking into account transaction costs, with an average return of -9.07%." (buying at ask, selling at bid).
- Khan and Khan ([SSRN 4832160](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4832160), 17-year S&P 500 backtest) confirm that longer pre-earnings holds fail: "Long at-the-money (ATM) straddles initiated 30 trading days prior to earnings announcements displayed a decline in value leading up to one day before the event, primarily due to theta decay outweighing potential gains from rising implied volatility."
- Even if a retail trader achieves Muravyev and Pearson (2020) effective spreads of 25% to 50% of the quoted spread (`1.5%` to `3.5%` round-trip on a `6%` to `7%` quoted ATM spread), that still erases the `+1.14%` (lowest-spread quartile) to `+1.37%` (dollar-OI-weighted `[-3, -1]`) midpoint return.
- Doc 117 screen: `events = 2,300/yr`, `published net edge <= 0 bp`, `noise ~ 2,200 to 3,100 bp` (ATM straddle return SD `31.44%`, robust SD `22.38%`, [Financial Review 2025](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/fire.70031)), `2300 * (0 / 2500)^2 = 0.00 < 4`.

## Candidate 2: Long Options Ahead of Other Dated Events (FDA, FOMC, Index)

Verdict: **DROP** (all three fail the `>= 300/yr` event count gate and fail net-of-spread long-option profitability; Doc 117 `0.00` to `0.36 < 4`).

FDA PDUFA and AdCom dates (`STATED`):
- Count and empirical outcome: Across `1,073` FDA decisions from 2020 to 2026 (`~179/yr < 300/yr`), [pdufa.bio](https://www.pdufa.bio/research/implied-vs-actual) reports: "Across 1073 FDA decisions from 2020 to 2026, at-the-money options roughly two weeks out priced a median move of ±6.7%. The median move at the first close after the decision was 1.4%, and within five trading days 3.1%. The actual move exceeded what was priced in 3.1% of cases at one day and 17.1% within five."
- No post-2010 peer-reviewed study documents positive net-of-spread returns for buying biotech options weeks before a PDUFA date and selling before the decision; small-cap biotech option spreads commonly run 10% to 30%+ of premium, and implied volatility is already elevated weeks ahead. Doc 117 screen: `0.00 < 4`.

FOMC announcements (`STATED`):
- Count: Only `8` scheduled FOMC meetings per year (`<< 300/yr`).
- Published returns: Bauer, Lakdawala, and Mueller (2019, [FRBSF WP 2019-12](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2019-12.pdf), Table C.3) report negative mean relative returns of `-1.6%` to `-9.1%` (`t = -6.8` to `-10.8`) for long Eurodollar straddles around FOMC meetings, with short straddles earning Sharpe ratios of `1.4` to `2.2`. For 0DTE SPX options, Song (Sept 2026, [AFA](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=260400), citing Johannes, Kaeck, and Seeger 2026) notes: "buying straddles loses significantly over the statement yet earns insignificant returns over the press conference." A 2025 Lund University study on Jan 2024 to Apr 2025 large-cap US equity straddles ([Lund University PDF](https://lup.lub.lu.se/luur/download?func=downloadFile&recordOId=9197108&fileOId=9197117)) finds: "The conclusions drawn from the results of this study are that delta-neutral straddles around macroeconomic announcements do not yield positive returns."
- Ai, Bansal, Gul, and Yaron (2023/2024, [NBER w31087](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31087.pdf), Table 4, 1996 to 2019 OptionMetrics) report a gross midpoint pre-FOMC ROIQ daily ATM SPX straddle coefficient of `+0.428%` (`t = 2.25`). Even if `+0.20%` survived spreads on 8 meetings per year (`80` in 10 years) against `~3.0%` daily straddle SD, Doc 117 is `80 * (0.20 / 3.0)^2 = 0.36 < 4`.

Index reconstitutions (`STATED`):
- Count and literature: Russell annual reconstitution occurs once per year (`1 date/yr`), and historical pro-forma add/delete lists are paid-only (Rounds 27 and 41). No post-2010 peer-reviewed study documents positive net-of-spread long-option returns ahead of Russell or S&P index reconstitutions. Doc 117 screen: `0.00 < 4`.

## Candidate 3: Cheap OTM Options on Our Two Passed Windows

Verdict: **DROP** (muni CEFs are non-optionable and occur once per year; `TLT` month-end occurs 12 times per year and cheap OTM option spreads plus 3-day theta destroy the `+0.2562%` equity edge; Doc 117 `0.00` to `0.18 < 4`).

Muni CEFs in January ([Replay 48](research/103_replay_48_muni_cef_january.md), `MEASURED` `+1.39%` mean net over 10 annual turns):
- Optionability (`STATED`): Major national municipal closed-end funds do not have listed OCC equity options. For example, Nasdaq's option chain page for `NAD` (Nuveen Quality Municipal Income Fund, one of the largest national muni CEFs) states: "Option Chain is currently not available." ([Nasdaq NAD Option Chain](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/nad/option-chain)).
- Count: Even if options existed, Replay 48 is `1` entry window per year (`10` turns in 10 years `<< 300/yr`).

`TLT` month-end ([Replay 25](research/69_replay_25_treasury_month_end.md), `MEASURED` `+0.2562%` mean net over `130` 3-trading-day windows, `t = 2.36`):
- Count: `12` windows per year (`<< 300/yr`).
- Published `TLT` option spread benchmarks (`STATED`): Across the entire `TLT` chain as of Nov 20, 2026 (`14.4M` open contracts, `1.8M` daily volume), "Average bid/ask spread across the chain is 2.34%" ([OptionsAnalysisSuite TLT Chain](https://www.optionsanalysissuite.com/etf/tlt/options-chain)). On the short-dated `TLT` Nov 23, 2026 chain with `TLT` at `$81.45` ([Yahoo Finance TLT Options](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TLT/options/)), cheap OTM calls have huge percentage spreads: `$82.00` call (`+0.68%` OTM) is `$0.07` bid / `$0.09` ask (`25.0%` full spread); `$82.50` call (`+1.29%` OTM) is `$0.03` / `$0.04` (`28.6%` full spread); `$83.00` call (`+1.90%` OTM) is `$0.01` / `$0.02` (`66.7%` full spread). On a 30-DTE OTM call (`$83.00` Dec 18, 2026 call), bid/ask is `$0.53` / `$0.55` (`3.7%` full spread, [OptionsAnalysisSuite TLT Chain](https://www.optionsanalysissuite.com/etf/tlt/options-chain)).
- Theta and spread arithmetic (`MEASURED` Replay 25 edge combined with `STATED` chain quotes): A `+0.2562%` expected move on `$81.45` `TLT` is `+$0.21` over 3 trading days. On a cheap 1-week OTM call (`$0.09` ask, ~0.25 delta), expected delta gain is `0.25 * $0.21 = +$0.0525`, while 3 trading days of theta on a 5-to-7-DTE OTM option erodes roughly 40% to 60% of extrinsic value (`-$0.03` to `-$0.05`) and crossing the `$0.02` spread (`25%` of premium) costs another `-$0.02`, yielding negative expected P&L (`-$0.0075` to `-$0.0175` per share). On a 30-DTE OTM call (`$0.54` mid, ~0.35 delta, ~$0.015/day theta), `+$0.073` delta gain minus `-$0.045` (3-day theta) minus `-$0.02` (full spread) leaves `+$0.008` (`+1.5%` on `$0.54`) against `~40%` 3-day option return SD, giving a 130-window Doc 117 score of `130 * (1.5 / 40)^2 = 0.18 < 4`.

## Candidate 4: Other High-Count Long-Only Options Rules (`>= 300/yr`)

Verdict: **DROP** (published post-2010 evidence shows long-only option buying loses money after real spreads because buyers pay both the variance risk premium and wide percentage spreads; Doc 117 `0.00 < 4`).

4a. 0DTE long options (`SPY` / `SPX` daily expirations, `>= 300` intraday windows/yr, `STATED`):

## How Options Backtests Could Fool Us

- Stale and modified quotes (`STATED`): Illiquid strikes carry stale or widened closing quotes; Alpaca's free `Indicative` feed alters real OPRA quotes ("the quotes are not actual OPRA quotes, they're just indicative derivatives", [Alpaca Historical Option Data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-option-data)), and Alpaca has no historical OPRA quote endpoint to verify actual closing NBBOs.
- Mid fills that never happen (`STATED`): Academic option studies such as Gao et al. (2018, p. 2599) compute returns at bid-ask midpoints (`0%` cost), whereas ATM single-stock spreads average `6.17%` to `7.71%` (Dubinsky et al. 2019) and retail half-spreads average `~8%` (De Silva et al. 2024); BSIC (2023) shows `+1.17%` midpoint flipping to `-9.07%` when buying the ask and selling the bid.
- Early exercise and expiration auto-liquidation (`STATED`): American-style equity options that finish `$0.01+` ITM auto-exercise if buying power suffices, but on a $500 account lacking funds to buy 100 shares, Alpaca force-liquidates before expiry ("If there is not enough buying power or shares, then Alpaca will liquidate while it's still ITM", [Alpaca Options Trading Overview](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading-overview); "within 1 hour before expiry", [Syntaqx Broker Table](https://www.syntaqx.com/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading)), forcing a taker exit into wide expiration-day spreads. Dividend-paying stocks also create early-exercise traps on short legs if spreads are ever used.
- Earnings date and timestamp errors (`STATED`): Gao et al. (2018, pp. 2601 to 2602) show that expected-date calendars match the actual release date only `28.45%` to `29.29%` of the time. Mixing before-market-open (`BMO`) and after-market-close (`AMC`) releases shifts Day `0` by a full session ([AEA 2024 Paper](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2024/program/paper/5GGEki7i)), turning a pre-release backtest into accidental overnight IV-crush exposure or look-ahead leakage.
