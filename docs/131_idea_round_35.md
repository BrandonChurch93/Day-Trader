### 2. Weekend gap: Monday open-to-close drift or reversal in `IBIT`, `MSTR`, `COIN`, and miners

- Source (opened): Ananda Banerjee (October 13, 2026, Charlie Quant Lab), [The Weekend Mirage](https://charliequantlab.com/Weekend_Mirage).
- Verbatim quotes (STATED): "After weekends of +3% or more, the gap faded intraday 33% of the time (average rest-of-Monday +0.7%); after weekends of −3% or worse, the gap bounced back 44% of the time (average rest-of-Monday +0.3%)." "This is a description of past gaps, not a strategy." "The fade test ignores borrow costs, slippage and the impossibility of trading the open at the open. A gap that fades on average can still run violently on any single Monday."
- Sample period and stated effects (STATED): 99 weekends from October 18, 2024 to October 9, 2026 (no t-statistics or p-values reported; post-publication survival UNKNOWN). On BTC weekends >= +3% (13 cases): rest-of-Monday averaged +0.3% for `IBIT` (38% faded), +0.5% for `COIN` (31% faded), and +1.3% for `MSTR` (31% faded). On BTC weekends <= -3% (9 cases): rest-of-Monday averaged +0.4% for `IBIT` (44% faded), -0.7% for `COIN` (33% faded), and +1.2% for `MSTR` (56% faded).
- Events per year, cost per side, data held, and power screen: About 11 extreme weekends (`|BTC| >= 3%`) per year (~31 events over the 2.8-year `IBIT` era; Alpaca SIP and Bitstamp/Kraken bars already held). Modeled cost: 5 bps per side at the volatile Monday open (10 to 20 bps RT for a 2-leg pair). For `IBIT` alone, rest-of-Monday is +0.3% to +0.4% regardless of weekend sign (no reversal or drift signal; screen 0). For `MSTR` hedged against `IBIT` (about +80 to +100 bps raw difference, ~+80 bps net after 20 bps open RT), `MSTR` carries ~93% to 102% annualized volatility (`R^2 = 0.44` vs BTC per Aufiero et al. 2025 -> ~350 bps intraday hedged SD): over 2.8 years (`N = 31`), `31 x (80 / 350)^2 = 1.62 < 4` (even over 5 years `N = 55`, `2.87 < 4`). Fails power screen and statistical significance.
