## Bottom Line

Costs are real, but costs alone did not kill most of our tests. Across all 44 scored tests in `docs/research/95_where_we_stand_after_32_tests.md`:

- Passed: 3 (Replay 20 offshore only, Replay 25 `TLT` month-end, Replay 48 muni CEFs January).
- Killed by costs (gross above at least one core return/Sharpe mark, net below): 3 (Replay 01 at Alpaca and Kraken, Replay 03 on absolute return, Replay 07 `QQQ` `N2` on Mark 1 and Mark 2).
- Lost before costs: 23.
- UNKNOWN, gross not printed: 15 (including Measurement 15, which was itself a fill-slippage gate).

Outside the repo, among US-legal spot crypto venues with a public API, **Binance.US** is the only venue with maker fees under 10 bp at the lowest volume tier (STATED 0.00% maker / 0.02% taker on most pairs, 0.01% on BNB/USD; Idaho eligible, 12 states excluded; thin altcoin books). Every other major US spot venue charges 15 bp to 60 bp maker and 18 bp to 120 bp taker at $500 to $2,000 size (and Interactive Brokers' $1.75 order minimum equals 35 bp per side on a $500 order). Moving from 35 to 95 bp/side spot fees down to Binance.US (0 to 2 bp fee plus spread) or US perp futures only plausibly helps a narrow set of crypto families (led by the un-scored Plan 70/71 overextension dip-buy and Replay 01/03), and only if forward measurement proves resting post-only orders fill without Measurement 121-style adverse selection.
