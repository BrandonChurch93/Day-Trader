## Purpose and Screening Criteria

Coin sweep Plan 70 left one family standing (buying the coin that fell further than bitcoin in a sharp hourly drop), but the dated 2017 to 2019 test could not run because honest power at half the published effect was 18% to 21% across 115 signal days with roughly 390 bps of day-to-day spread against a 42 bps half-effect. To reach real power on a dated test, a candidate must either fire on many separate days or carry a much smaller per-day spread by construction (paired or hedged).

Every candidate below is checked against four fixed rules:
- Fires on at least 200 separate days in history a $500 US account could have traded (US-listed stocks or funds on Alpaca data from 2016 onward, or US-tradable coins on spot or Coinbase CFM regulated futures).
- Has a published effect after costs (or explicit spread-adjusted evidence where noted) at least one quarter of its published per-day spread divided by the square root of the day count, meaning implied full-effect t is at least 0.25. Power is computed strictly from each paper's published spread, standard deviation, t-statistic, or Sharpe ratio using two-sided 5% normal power at both full published effect and half published effect.
- Uses a reachable entry price (exchange opening or closing auction via MOO or MOC orders, intraday bar execution, or US futures order book).
- Is not already queued in `docs/QUEUE.md` or closed in `docs/STATUS.md` or `docs/research`. Checked against `docs/QUEUE.md` (Running now, Next, Dropped, Done, and Rounds 17 to 43 skip lists) and `docs/STATUS.md` before writing.

Claim labels used throughout: MEASURED (calculated from fixed math on published inputs or verified in repo docs), STATED (reported by the cited source), UNKNOWN (not reported in the fetched source or untested in recent US data).

## Detailed Candidates

### 1. Pre-Earnings Liquidity-Provision Reversal (So and Wang 2014)

- One-sentence rule: Buy liquid US stocks whose market-adjusted return over days t-4 to t-2 before a scheduled earnings announcement sits in the bottom quintile (optionally hedged against SPY or inverse ETF SH, or paired against top-quintile shorts once margin-eligible), entering at t-1 and exiting at t+1.
- Source: Eric C. So and Sean Wang (2014), "News-Driven Return Reversals: Liquidity Provision Ahead of Earnings Announcements", Journal of Financial Economics 114(1), 20-35, [MIT Open Access PDF](https://dspace.mit.edu/bitstream/handle/1721.1/119665/So_News-driven%20return.pdf).
- Verbatim quote (STATED): "Specifically, we show that a long (short) position in firms whose returns strongly underperform (outperform) the market in the three days prior to earnings announcements yields an average return of 145 basis points (bps) during the announcement window. By comparison, the average return to a comparable portfolio during non-announcement periods is 22 bps, indicating that return reversals increase more than six-fold during earnings announcements."
- Published size and spread (STATED): Across 107,039 earnings announcements from 1996 to 2011 (64 calendar quarters; 1,202 calendar days with at least 25 announcements), the low-minus-high quintile return over t-1 to t+1 is +144.8 bps (positive in 58 of 64 quarters, Table 2 p = 0.00; Table 4 regression t = -6.35, or -6.91 controlling for earnings surprise). The long-only bottom-quintile leg (Q1) averages +81.3 bps over the 3-day window. Open-to-close execution earns +119.7 bps L/S and midpoint-to-midpoint earns +116.9 bps L/S. Transaction costs are UNKNOWN in the paper itself (no net-of-cost table is printed), so liquid-stock bid-ask spreads of roughly 5 to 15 bps round trip must be modeled explicitly in any replay.
- Power sum (MEASURED): Scaling the published 16-year regression t-statistic of 6.35 to a 10-year Alpaca window (2016 to 2025, roughly 2,000+ separate announcement days) gives full-effect implied t = 6.35 * sqrt(10 / 16) = 5.02 (two-sided 5% power 99.9%) and half-effect t = 2.51 (two-sided 5% power 70.9%). Even if the long-only Q1 leg retains only half the L/S t-statistic, half-effect power stays above 70% on a 10-year sample. Criterion 2 threshold (t >= 0.25) is cleared by 20x.
- Reachability and $500 US account mechanics: Entry at t-1 close or t-1 open via NYSE or Nasdaq MOC/MOO auction is reachable on Alpaca. A $500 US cash account can trade the long-only Q1 leg (or pair Q1 against SH) without shorting single stocks.
- Data needed and whether it is free: Historical US stock daily bars from 2016 onward (free via Alpaca) plus historical scheduled earnings announcement dates (free via SEC EDGAR 8-K timestamps or Yahoo Finance, though point-in-time expected vs actual announcement dates must be checked carefully because So and Wang Table 7 shows early surprise announcements do not reverse).
- Most likely way it fools us: Look-ahead bias from using actual announcement dates rather than pre-announced expected dates (So and Wang report pooled expected-date reversal drops from 145 bps to 65.2 bps, and early announcements return -14.7 bps), or post-2011 crowding by automated liquidity providers compressing the pre-earnings inventory premium.

### 2. Pre-Earnings 5-Day Run-Up of Past 12-Month Winners (Aboody, Lehavy, and Trueman 2010)

- One-sentence rule: Five trading days before a scheduled earnings announcement, buy liquid US stocks in the top 1 percent of prior 12-month returns (optionally hedged against SPY or SH) and exit at the close before or the opening auction after the announcement.
- Source: David Aboody, Reuven Lehavy, and Brett Trueman (2010), "Limited Attention and the Earnings Announcement Returns of Past Stock Market Winners", Review of Accounting Studies, [UCLA Anderson PDF](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/documents/areas/fac/accounting/earnings_paper08.pdf).
- Verbatim quote (STATED): "We document that stocks with the strongest prior 12-month returns experience a significant average market-adjusted return of 1.58 percent during the five trading days before their earnings announcements and a significant average market-adjusted return of -1.86 percent in the five trading days afterward. These returns remain significant even after accounting for transactions costs."
- Published size and spread (STATED): In the 1993 to 2005 TAQ bid-ask spread-adjusted sample, buying at the ask and selling at the bid over days -4 to 0 yields +0.94% (+94 bps, t = 2.60, n = 943 events), which implies a per-event spread of 94 * sqrt(943) / 2.60 = 1,110 bps (MEASURED from published mean, t, and n). Restricting to outside-trading-hours announcements (exiting at the Day +1 open) yields +1.66% (+166 bps, t = 4.09, n = 759 events), implying a per-event spread of 1,118 bps.
- Power sum (MEASURED): Projecting the 13-year TAQ event rate onto a 10-year 2016 to 2025 Alpaca window gives roughly 725 full-sample events or 584 outside-hours events. At the full-sample rate (+94 bps over 1,110 bps SD), full-effect t = 2.28 (power 62.6%) and half-effect t = 1.14 (power 20.7%). At the outside-hours rate (+166 bps over 1,118 bps SD), full-effect t = 3.59 (power 94.8%) and half-effect t = 1.79 (power 43.4%). Both clear criterion 2 (t >= 0.25).
- Reachability and $500 US account mechanics: 100% long-only (or paired with SH to hedge market beta), fully tradable on a $500 US cash account via MOC and MOO auctions on Alpaca.
- Data needed and whether it is free: Free Alpaca daily bars (2016 onward) for 12-month prior returns and market-adjusted returns, plus free historical earnings calendars (SEC EDGAR 8-K or Yahoo Finance) with pre-announcement timing flags (BMO vs AMC).
- Most likely way it fools us: Top-1-percent 12-month winners skew toward smaller, volatile, wider-spread names, and post-2010 retail attention and options gamma positioning around earnings may have shifted the run-up earlier or reversed it into a sell-the-news fade.

### 3. Intraday 1-Minute S&P 500 Same-Sector Pairs Trading (Stubinger and Endres 2018)

- One-sentence rule: Using 1-minute intraday bars of S&P 500 stocks within the same sector (such as energy and oil constituents), open a dollar-neutral pair when the spread crosses its calibrated entry threshold and close when the spread reverts to equilibrium or before the closing auction.
- Source: Johannes Stubinger and Sylvia Endres (2018), "Pairs trading with a mean-reverting jump-diffusion model on high-frequency data", Quantitative Finance 18(10), 1735-1751, [Econstor Working Paper PDF](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/157807/1/885569830.pdf).
- Verbatim quote (STATED): "The JDM achieves annualized returns of 98.55 percent before and 60.61 percent after transaction costs" and "Sharpe ratios after transaction costs of 5.30 for the JDM and 2.71 for the OUM."
- Published size and spread (STATED): Across March 1998 to December 2015 (~17.8 years, 391 one-minute bars per day) after charging 5 bps per share per half-turn (20 bps per full pair turn), the simplest baseline Classic Distance Model (CDM) earns +9.90% p.a. net with 3.76% annualized SD (23.7 bps per day) and net Sharpe 2.052. The Ornstein-Uhlenbeck model (OUM) earns +50.45% p.a. net (17.49% SD, Sharpe 2.713), and the Jump-Diffusion model (JDM) earns +60.61% p.a. net (10.84% SD, Sharpe 5.298).
- Power sum (MEASURED): Using ONLY the most conservative published baseline (CDM, net Sharpe 2.052, 23.7 bp/day spread) over a 10-year 2016 to 2025 Alpaca window (2,520 trading days) gives full-effect t = 2.052 * sqrt(10) = 6.49 (power 100.0%) and half-effect t = 3.24 (power 90.1%). Even at one quarter of CDM's published Sharpe (0.513), implied t is 1.62 (power 36.9%).
- Reachability and $500 US account mechanics: Intraday 1-minute bar entries on S&P 500 constituents are reachable via limit or marketable orders on Alpaca. Critical $500 constraint: shorting single stocks requires a US Reg T margin account (minimum $2,000 equity) and Pattern Day Trader rules cap day trades at 3 per 5 business days below $25,000 in margin accounts (cash accounts avoid PDT but must obey T+1 settled-cash limits). On $500 cash today, this rule can only be run as a settled-cash long-only switch (buy the cheap oil stock vs holding XLE as benchmark).
- Data needed and whether it is free: 1-minute US stock bars from 2016 onward are available via Alpaca historical market data (free tier includes IEX feed; SIP consolidated 1-minute feed requires paid Alpaca Algo Trader Plus at $99/month).
- Most likely way it fools us: Intraday 1-minute bar backtests using trade prints overstate fills on fast-moving ticks (buying at the ask and selling at the bid on 1-minute bars can easily exceed 5 bps/side during oil news spikes), and HFT market makers have heavily arbitraged S&P 500 intraday sector spreads since 2015.

### 4. Stock-Versus-Sector-ETF Volume-Time Residual Reversion (Avellaneda and Lee 2010)

- One-sentence rule: Regress each liquid US stock against its matching SPDR sector ETF in volume-weighted trading time, buy the stock against the sector ETF when the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck residual s-score drops below -1.25, and exit when the residual reverts toward zero.
- Source: Marco Avellaneda and Jeong-Hyun Lee (2010), "Statistical Arbitrage in the U.S. Equities Market", Quantitative Finance 10(7), 761-782, [NYU Courant PDF](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/AvellanedaLeeStatArb20090616.pdf).
- Verbatim quote (STATED): "ETF strategies which use volume information achieve a Sharpe ratio of 1.51 from 2003 to 2007." and "We observe, for the purpose of comparison, that the average Sharpe ratio from 2003 to 2007 was 0.6."
- Published size and spread (STATED): Backtested on actual US sector ETFs from 2003 to 2007 (~1,260 trading days) with transaction costs of 0.05% (5 bps) per trade (10 bps round trip), the calendar-time ETF-residual strategy achieves a net Sharpe ratio of 0.60, and the volume-time ("trading-time") ETF-residual strategy achieves a net Sharpe ratio of 1.51. Separate annualized mean and annualized volatility numbers for the ETF-only table are UNKNOWN from the fetched text, but the published net Sharpe ratio gives the exact signal-to-spread ratio needed for power.
- Power sum (MEASURED): On a 10-year 2016 to 2025 Alpaca window (2,520 trading days), the volume-time net Sharpe of 1.51 gives full-effect t = 1.51 * sqrt(10) = 4.78 (power 99.8%) and half-effect t = 2.39 (power 66.6%). Even the weaker calendar-time baseline (SR = 0.60) gives full-effect t = 1.90 (power 47.6%) and clears criterion 2 (t >= 0.25).
- Reachability and $500 US account mechanics: Entries on daily or intraday bars / closing auctions are reachable on Alpaca. Unlike single-stock pairs, the hedge leg is a liquid sector ETF, so a $500 US cash account can approximate this cleanly as a long-only switch (hold the cheap stock when s < -1.25, otherwise hold the sector ETF, measured on excess return over the sector ETF) without needing single-stock shorting.
- Data needed and whether it is free: Free Alpaca daily bars and volume (2016 onward) for liquid US stocks and SPDR sector ETFs (XLK, XLF, XLE, XLV, XLI, XLP, XLY, XLU, XLB).
- Most likely way it fools us: The published 2003 to 2007 sample precedes the August 2007 quant quake and modern ETF AP/HFT arbitrage; post-2010 daily stock-versus-ETF residual reversion may have decayed in the same way daily stock pairs decayed in Clegg and Krauss (2018).

### 5. Liquid Country-ETF Cointegration Spread (EWC and EZA; Pandini 2026)

- One-sentence rule: Compute the daily cointegration spread z-score between two liquid US-listed commodity-country ETFs (EWC Canada and EZA South Africa, or the three-ETF EWA-EWC-EZA basket), open long-cheap and short-rich when the z-score crosses plus or minus 2, and exit at plus or minus 1.
- Source: Davide Pandini (2026), "Stationary, But Not Profitable? A Critical Look at Pairs Trading", [NAAIM 2026 PDF](https://naaim.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Stationary-But-Not-Profitable-A-Critical-Look-at-Pairs-Trading-2026-Davide-Pandini-PhD.pdf).
- Verbatim quote (STATED): "Trading signals follow a symmetrical state-machine logic: long positions are entered when the z-score drops below -2 and exited at -1, while short positions are entered above +2 and exited at +1." and "The paper demonstrates that a modest 10 basis point cost can significantly reduce the Sharpe ratio of high-turnover strategies, such as in the AUS-CAN pair where the gross Sharpe of 0.51 dropped to a net 0.13."
- Published size and spread (STATED): Over the out-of-sample period January 1, 2018 to September 30, 2025 (1,947 trading days, 7.75 years) after a 10 bps transaction cost, EWC-EZA (CAN-RSA) delivers +5.6% annualized net return with 8.7% annualized volatility (54.8 bp/day spread) and net Sharpe 0.650 (gross 0.992). The three-ETF EWA-EWC-EZA spread delivers +4.3% annualized net return with 9.3% volatility (58.6 bp/day) and net Sharpe 0.460. In the same OOS table, EWA-EWC drops to +1.0% net (Sharpe 0.131), EWN-EWQ drops to -0.03% net (Sharpe -0.004), and GLD-SLV loses -60.3% p.a. net (Sharpe -0.601).
- Power sum (MEASURED): On a 10-year 2016 to 2025 Alpaca window (2,520 trading days), EWC-EZA's published OOS net Sharpe of 0.650 gives full-effect t = 0.650 * sqrt(10) = 2.06 (power 53.8%) and half-effect t = 1.03 (power 17.7%). On the paper's own 7.75-year OOS window (1,947 days), full-effect t = 1.81 (power 44.1%). Clears criterion 2 (t >= 0.25).
- Reachability and $500 US account mechanics: Daily MOC auction execution on US-listed iShares country ETFs (EWC, EZA, EWA) is reachable on Alpaca. On $500 cash, this must be framed as a long-only switch (hold whichever ETF is cheap relative to the spread, judged against a 50/50 buy-and-hold of EWC and EZA); full two-leg long-short requires $2,000 Reg T margin.
- Data needed and whether it is free: Free Alpaca daily bars (2016 onward) for EWC, EZA, and EWA.
- Most likely way it fools us: Selection bias across the 27 pairs screened in Pandini (2026): EWC-EZA was the top OOS survivor while EWA-EWC, EWN-EWQ, and GLD-SLV failed after 10 bps costs, so picking the winning pair ex post can turn a zero-edge family into a false positive.

### 6. Hourly Crypto Copula Pairs on US-Regulated Futures (Tadi and Witzany 2025)

- One-sentence rule: On hourly bars of US-tradable cryptocurrency perpetual-style futures (Coinbase CFM contracts such as BIP, ETP, SLP, LCP, XPP, ADP, DOP, and LKP), select the two altcoins most correlated with bitcoin, form a level-based or bitcoin-referenced copula mispricing spread, go long the cheap coin and short the rich coin when the mispricing crosses the 10% tail threshold, and close when it reverts.
- Source: Masood Tadi and Jiri Witzany (2025), "Copula-based trading of cointegrated cryptocurrency pairs", Financial Innovation 11, [Springer Open Access Article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40854-024-00702-7).
- Verbatim quote (STATED): "Using hourly data (with the EG test), the annualized return decreases with higher entry thresholds, ranging from 51.6% in alpha_1 = 0.10 to 35.1% at alpha_1 = 0.20." and "It is assumed that all trades are executed using market orders, which typically incur higher fees (known as taker fees) compared to the lower fees charged for limit orders (known as maker fees). For instance, on Binance, the maker fee of USDT-margined futures is set at 0.02%, while the taker fee is set at 0.04%."
- Published size and spread (STATED): Across January 22, 2021 to January 19, 2023 (~728 days) on 20 liquid crypto futures net of 4 bps per side taker fees (8 bps RT per leg, 16 bps full pair turn), the conventional level-based copula benchmark is positive in all scenarios with annualized net return up to +9.3% and net Sharpe ratio close to or above 0.95 (implied annualized SD <= 9.8%, daily SD <= 51.3 bp/day). The authors' BTC-referenced copula earns +35.1% to +51.6% annualized net on hourly bars with max drawdowns of -36.6% to -41.6% (exact hourly Sharpe is UNKNOWN from the fetched text; 5-minute net Sharpe is 3.77). Critically, plain linear Engle-Granger and KSS hourly cointegration pairs in the same paper had negative net returns after taker fees.
- Power sum (MEASURED): Using the conservative published level-based copula net Sharpe of 0.95 over a 5-year US-tradable coin history (1,825 days, 2021 to 2025) gives full-effect t = 0.95 * sqrt(5) = 2.12 (power 56.5%) and half-effect t = 1.06 (power 18.6%). Over a shorter 3-year window (1,095 days), full-effect t = 1.65 (power 37.9%) and half-effect t = 0.82 (power 12.9%). Clears criterion 2 (t >= 0.25).
- Reachability and $500 US account mechanics: This is the ONLY candidate in Round 45 where a $500 US account can hold BOTH the long leg and the short leg simultaneously today, using Coinbase CFM nano perpetual-style futures (BIP, ETP, SLP, LCP, XPP, ADP, DOP, LKP) at roughly 5.76 to 10 bps round-trip fees on 24/7 books.
- Data needed and whether it is free: Free hourly OHLCV bars from Coinbase public API (spot proxy for pre-2025 history) and Coinbase CFM funding/futures history (2025 onward; pre-2025 CFM history is limited, so early years must be tested on Coinbase USD spot bars with CFM fee and funding schedules charged).
- Most likely way it fools us: Coinbase CFM lists fewer altcoins than Binance's 20-coin universe and carries slightly higher taker fees plus funding-rate drag; plain linear hourly crypto cointegration already lost money after 8 bps RT fees in both Tadi and Witzany (2025) and AIMS Quantitative Finance and Economics (2026), so the edge depends heavily on the copula specification not being overfit to 2021 to 2022.

### 7. Daily US Equity Distance and Cointegration Pairs Trading (Rad, Low, and Faff 2016)

- One-sentence rule: Over each rolling 12-month formation window on liquid US stocks, select the 20 closest-distance (or Engle-Granger cointegrated) pairs, open a dollar-neutral pair on the next trading day when the normalized spread exceeds 2 historical standard deviations, and close when the spread crosses zero or the 6-month trading window ends.
- Source: Hossein Rad, Rand Kwong Yew Low, and Robert Faff (2016), "The profitability of pairs trading strategies: distance, cointegration and copula methods", Quantitative Finance 16(10), 1541-1558, [Bond University Pure PDF](https://pure.bond.edu.au/ws/portalfiles/portal/36339487/AM_The_profitability_of_pairs_trading_strategies.pdf).
- Verbatim quote (STATED): "In terms of economic outcomes, the distance, cointegration, and copula methods show a mean monthly excess return of 91, 85, and 43 bps (38, 33, and 5 bps) before transaction costs (after transaction costs), respectively."
- Published size and spread (STATED): Over July 1, 1962 to December 31, 2014 (13,216 trading days, 630 months) after time-varying institutional commissions (declining to 9 bps) plus 20 bps market impact doubled for two legs, Distance pairs earn +38 bps/month net with 1.10% monthly SD (annualized SD 3.81%, daily SD ~24.0 bp/day), monthly Sharpe 0.3498 (annualized Sharpe 1.212), and t = 5.39. Cointegration pairs earn +33 bps/month net with 0.99% monthly SD (annualized Sharpe 1.211) and t = 5.14.
- Power sum (MEASURED): Projecting the published Distance net Sharpe (monthly 0.3498, annualized 1.212) onto a 10-year 2016 to 2025 Alpaca window (120 months, 2,520 trading days) gives full-effect t = 0.3498 * sqrt(120) = 3.83 (power 96.9%) and half-effect t = 1.92 (power 48.2%). Clears criterion 2 (t >= 0.25).
- Reachability and $500 US account mechanics: Wait-one-day execution at the next closing auction (MOC) is reachable on Alpaca. Two-leg shorting of single stocks requires a $2,000 Reg T margin account; a $500 cash account can only test a long-only leg against SPY or wait until margin-eligible.
- Data needed and whether it is free: Free Alpaca daily bars (2016 onward) on liquid US equities (S&P 500 or Russell 1000 constituents; point-in-time constituent lists are needed to avoid survivorship bias).
- Most likely way it fools us: Severe structural decay after 2009. Rad, Low, and Faff (2016) report a 35% to 40% drop in trade count from 2007-2011 to 2012-2014, and Clegg and Krauss (2018), [Econstor PDF](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/140632/1/858609614.pdf), explicitly find that on S&P 500 constituents from January 2010 to October 2015, distance, cointegration, and partial-cointegration daily pairs all turned negative after costs (-0.18%, -1.67%, and -2.76% p.a. net).

### 8. VIX-Conditioned Sector or Industry Short-Term Reversal (Nagel 2012)

- One-sentence rule: On days when the VIX index sits in an elevated regime (such as VIX >= 25), buy the liquid US sector ETFs (or large liquid stocks) that lagged the cross-section over the prior 1 to 5 trading days (optionally hedged against SPY or paired against recent winners) and hold for 1 to 5 days as liquidity pressure clears.
- Source: Stefan Nagel (2012), "Evaporating Liquidity", Review of Financial Studies 25(7), 2005-2039, [NBER Working Paper 17653 PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w17653/w17653.pdf).
- Verbatim quote (STATED): "Even reversal strategies formed from industry portfolios (which do not yield high returns unconditionally) produce high rates of return and high Sharpe Ratios during times of high VIX." and "Expected returns and conditional Sharpe Ratios increase enormously along with the VIX during times of financial market turmoil, such as the financial crisis 2007-09."
- Published size and spread (STATED): From January 1998 to December 2010 (~3,270 trading days), market-hedged individual-stock quote-midpoint reversal earns +0.17% per day with 0.54% daily SD (annualized Sharpe 4.91, gross before explicit trading costs). Market-hedged industry-portfolio reversal earns +0.01% per day unconditionally with 0.47% daily SD (annualized Sharpe 0.44, gross), but Table 3 reports a VIX slope of +0.08 annualized Sharpe per VIX point (intercept -0.12), implying a conditional annualized Sharpe of 1.88 at VIX = 25 and roughly +0.20% per day at VIX = 60. Explicit net-of-cost returns are UNKNOWN in the paper, though liquid SPDR sector ETFs carry 1 to 2 bps half-spreads.
- Power sum (MEASURED): On a 10-year 2016 to 2025 window, unconditional industry reversal (SR = 0.44 over 2,520 days) gives full-effect t = 1.39 (power 28.5%) and half-effect t = 0.70 (power 10.7%). Conditioning on high-VIX days (roughly top 20% of days, ~500 days = 2.0 active years at conditional SR = 1.88) gives full-effect t = 1.88 * sqrt(2.0) = 2.66 (power 75.8%) and half-effect t = 1.33 (power 26.4%). Clears criterion 2 (t >= 0.25).
- Reachability and $500 US account mechanics: MOC auction execution on SPDR sector ETFs (XLK, XLF, XLE, XLV, XLI, XLP, XLY, XLU, XLB) is reachable on Alpaca, and a long-only bottom-tercile sector-ETF basket judged against SPY fits a $500 US cash account with zero shorting.
- Data needed and whether it is free: Free Alpaca daily bars (2016 onward) for SPDR sector ETFs and SPY, plus free daily CBOE VIX close series (FRED `VIXCLS` or Yahoo Finance `^VIX`).
- Most likely way it fools us: Published returns are before explicit crossing spreads and bid-ask spreads widen sharply on high-VIX days; also, high-VIX episodes cluster into a few regimes (2020 COVID, 2022 bear market, April 2025 tariff shock), so effective independent day count is lower than raw trading-day count.

## Checked and Rejected in This Round

- Natural Gas EIA weekly storage puzzle (Prokopczuk, Wese Simen, and Wichmann 2021, [Reading PDF](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/90003/1/NG_paper_final_round.pdf)): Collapsed post-2011 to -0.97% p.a. net, Sharpe -0.17 (p = 0.843).
- Commodity ETF overnight-versus-daytime session anomaly on GLD, SLV, DBA, DBP (MDPI 2026, [MDPI PDF](https://www.mdpi.com/2813-2432/5/4/26/pdf)): Over 2007 to 2025 (4,776 days), fails at 2 bps trading cost (GLD overnight-only net Sharpe 0.026 vs buy-and-hold 0.654; "no statistically robust evidence of net outperformance").
- Commodity ETF intraday half-hour momentum on USO, GLD, SLV (Wen, Gong, and Cai 2020, [PMC PDF](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7480318/pdf/main.pdf); Indriawan et al. 2023, [Adelaide PDF](https://digital.library.adelaide.edu.au/server/api/core/bitstreams/aee73b81-111e-4bef-9e0e-7b1164f368a1/content)): Gross annualized means of 0.81% to 2.35% p.a. across 252 daily trades/year (~0.3 to 0.9 bp/trade gross) cannot survive 2 to 4 bps round-trip ETF spreads; reported Sharpe ratios divide annualized mean by daily SD.
- Bitcoin turn-of-the-candle 15-minute effect (PMC 2023, [PMC PDF](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10015199/pdf/main.pdf)): Gross gain is 0.58 bps per minute and requires hitting Bitfinex's $7.5M 30-day rolling volume zero-fee tier, unreachable on a $500 US account paying 5.76 to 10 bps RT on Coinbase CFM.
- Intraday cross-sectional half-hour periodicity (Heston, Korajczyk, and Sadka 2010, [UH PDF](https://www.bauer.uh.edu/departments/finance/documents/Heston_Korajczyk_Sadka_paper_UH.pdf)): Authors explicitly report that "strategies that attempt to take advantage of the daily periodicity lose money, after paying the bid-ask spread."
- Gold ETF pair GLD and IAU student thesis (Aalborg 2024, [AAU PDF](https://vbn.aau.dk/ws/files/679802741/Master_Thesis.pdf)): Deducted "5% from the signal value" rather than return, indicating a modeling bug rather than a verified after-cost result.
- Perpetual-versus-spot crypto basis arbitrage (He, Manela, Ross, and von Wachter 2022/2024, [arXiv 2212.06888](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2212.06888)): Already skipped in `docs/QUEUE.md` Round 30 (perpetual basis extremes).

## Top Three Recommendations

1. Candidate 1 (Pre-earnings liquidity-provision reversal, So and Wang 2014): Best blend of high independent day count (~2,000+ days over 10 years), strong half-effect power (70.9%, full t = 5.02), and immediate $500 US cash feasibility (long-only bottom-quintile Q1 leg or Q1 vs SH, no single-stock shorting needed). Must enforce strict pre-announced expected earnings dates to avoid look-ahead bias.
2. Candidate 4 (Stock-versus-sector-ETF volume-time residual reversion, Avellaneda and Lee 2010): Explicitly paired low-spread design with 66.6% half-effect power (full t = 4.78) at 10 bps RT over 2,520 daily observations, 100% free Alpaca stock and sector-ETF data, and clean adaptation to $500 US cash as a long-only stock-versus-sector-ETF switch.
3. Candidate 3 (Intraday 1-minute S&P 500 sector pairs, Stubinger and Endres 2018) on power, paired with Candidate 6 (Hourly crypto copula pairs on Coinbase CFM, Tadi and Witzany 2025) on true $500 two-leg reachability: Candidate 3 has the highest statistical power of all eight (90.1% half-effect power even on the conservative CDM baseline at 23.7 bp/day spread), though $500 cash must run it as a long-only switch vs XLE until $2,000 Reg T margin; Candidate 6 is the only candidate where a $500 US account can hold both long and short legs live today on regulated US futures, though its conservative level-copula power is much lower (56.5% full, 18.6% half).
