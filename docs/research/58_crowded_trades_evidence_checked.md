## What this means for our tests

- Shorting generic altcoin run-ups or extreme positive funding has a strong negative prior across every survivorship-free, after-cost test we checked. In [FundingArb](https://fundingarb.net/learn/funding-rate-extremes-directional-backtest) (895 Binance perps including delisted, 2022 to 2026), shorting extreme positive funding lost 10.7% a year (Sharpe -0.33) because 1 in 10 weeks squeezed +36% or more; in [dimaquant](https://dimaquant.substack.com/p/a-15-sharpe-crypto-short-strategy), generic 25%+ pumps with no Upbit listing news lost -0.2% per trade after 15 bps/side and funding.
- Adding Binance `metrics` (open interest and top-trader long/short ratios) to a peak-short rule is unlikely to create an edge by itself: [OctopusTakopi](https://github.com/OctopusTakopi/funding-rate-alpha) found 24h OI change is mostly trailing return reversal in disguise and top-trader L/S predicts continuation (+0.013 IC), while [Janorium](https://janorium.com/articles/long-short-ratio-open-interest-backtest) found 0 of 180 OI/L-S tests passed multiple-testing correction.
- Buying the bounce after a liquidation flush is rejected across every after-cost test ([ByStock](https://bystock.fun/guide/liquidation-levels-backtest), [chaudharidhruv](https://github.com/chaudharidhruv/crypto-stat-arb), [Kaizen Trader](https://www.prateekjain.io/kaizen-trader/strategies/liquidation_cascade/), [The Print Room](https://theprintroom.io/learn/liquidation-cascade/)), and `data.binance.vision` has NO historical USD-M `liquidationSnapshot` folder.
- Any test using Binance USD-M `metrics` or `markPriceKlines` must sort `metrics` rows by `create_time` (raw daily CSVs are unsorted) and gate tradability on live `klines` volume (dead delisted coins still receive flat `markPriceKlines` and stray zero-OI `metrics` files).
- For US live execution, the combined US-shortable roster is roughly 35 mostly large-cap coins (including `RENDER` via Kraken Pro US spot margin `:BTNL`, not Coinbase or Kraken perpetuals), so any strategy requiring Binance rank 51-100 altcoins cannot be traded on a US-regulated account today.

## What the scout missed (after-cost studies on shorting altcoin perpetuals after a run-up)

- **STATED | Pre-registered survivorship-free test of shorting extreme positive funding after run-ups (`FundingArb`, Oct 20, 2026; 895 Binance USD-M perps including delisted, `2022-01-15` to `2026-08-31`, trading costs + daily funding):** Lost **-10.7% APR, 61.0% max drawdown, Sharpe -0.33** on the point-in-time universe (vs +2.3% APR / Sharpe +0.26 on a hindsight list of 50 surviving coins). Exact passage from [FundingArb](https://fundingarb.net/learn/funding-rate-extremes-directional-backtest): "Extreme positive funding marks a pump in progress. The median short candidate had already risen 44% over the previous 30 days." and "In most holding weeks it drifted lower (median -5.3%), which looks like the short is working. But in one week out of ten it rose another 36% or more, and those squeezes outweighed all the small wins: on average, the coin rose 1.75% a week against the short."
- **STATED | Shorting Binance perpetuals after sharp 25%+ pumps vs Upbit KRW listing pumps (`dimaquant`, Oct 26, 2026; Jan 2020 to Oct 17, 2026, 15 bps/side + actual funding, 3-day hold, hard stop at pump high capped at 25%):** Generic 25%+ pumps without an Upbit listing notice lost money, whereas Upbit KRW listing pumps averaged +2.8% net (+2.5% at 30 bps/side; shorts paid ~1.1% in funding per trade, 4x fees). Exact passage from [dimaquant](https://dimaquant.substack.com/p/a-15-sharpe-crypto-short-strategy): "Upbit listings made +4.0% per trade. The same coins on other dates made -0.1%, other pumps made -0.2%." and "The average trade was +3.1% before fees, +2.8% at 15 bps per side and +2.5% at 30 bps."
- **STATED | Survivorship-free decomposition of shorting high funding across 863 Binance USD-M perps (tommybook crypto-momentum, Oct 10, 2026; `2020-01-01` to `2026-08-31`, public GitHub, bucket inventory without live-list bias, fees + slippage):** Cross-sectional funding carry collected +348.0 pp in funding, lost -142.1 pp (41%) on the price leg and -78.9 pp (23%) to fees and slippage (+126.9 pp net, failing on a competed-away premium). Exact passage from [tommybook/crypto-momentum](https://github.com/tommybook/crypto-momentum/blob/master/docs/findings.md): "A high funding rate marks a crowded long, which is to say a coin that is going up. Shorting the rate is therefore shorting momentum, and the two studies in this repository are structurally opposed."
