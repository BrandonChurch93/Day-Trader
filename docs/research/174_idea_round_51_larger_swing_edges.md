# Idea Round 51: Larger Swing Edges (2% Per Trade or Event)

Written: 2026-11-26 20:12 UTC.
Scope: swing ideas (5 to 40 sessions for US stocks, long only on a $500 account; 3 to 30 days for coins) where a directly opened source reports a per-trade or per-event effect of about 2% or more after costs (or with subtractable costs), on a sample that includes years after 2015, and where free dated events number at least in the hundreds per year.
No price data was pulled and no return calculation was run in this round.

## Why the bar moved to 2%

Plan 74 (`research_rig/PREREG_74_swing_cards.md`, section "What each card's test can and cannot find") reports INFERENCE (synthetic power) counts on 60 calendar-month clusters:
- A true +1% per-signal effect cannot be reliably found at any count (at most about 60% power for Card A and about 73% for Card B), because common month shock sets a floor on the standard error.
- For Card A (40-session limit, middle SD), finding a true +2% per signal requires about 1,000 signals (600 to 1,250 across SD rows); at 150 signals it finds +2% only about 38% of the time.
- For Card B (10-session limit, middle SD), finding a true +4% requires about 400 signals (300 to 500) and finding +2% requires about 2,000 signals (1,000 to 2,500).

### Rank 1: Momentum-filtered Form 4 insider purchases (liquid stocks already up at least 10% over prior 20 sessions, 20-session hold)

Nearest ledger relatives and how this differs: `Ledger 171` (`Plan 36`, `Queue 60`) and `Ledger 193` (`Plan 56`, `Queue 15`) tested plain multi-insider Form 4 cluster buys with no prior-momentum filter and no $1 million daily dollar-turnover floor. Both closed without a live edge (`Ledger 171` on 5.71% missing coverage; `Ledger 193` on 9.20% synthetic power at +53 bps net 20d). The new evidence below agrees that plain cluster buys and dip-buying insiders fail, and isolates a distinct rule: any single or clustered Form 4 open-market purchase in a liquid stock that has already risen at least 10% over the previous 20 trading days.

Exact rule as stated:
- STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "The rule: an open-market purchase + a liquid stock (>= $1m daily turnover) + the stock has already risen >= +10 % over the previous 20 trading days." (Exact page text uses `≥ $1m` and `≥ +10 %`.)
- STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "Entry at the open on the day after the filing, hold for 20 trading days."
- STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "Holding period 20 trading days, measured as excess return against the S&P 500 (SPY) over the same window."

Stated effect, sample years, and post-2015 behavior:
- STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "So we measured it: 301,904 open-market purchases from the official Form 4 filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission, 2016 to 2026, 8,319 stocks."
- STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "An insider purchase is a signal when the stock is liquid and already rising: at least +10 % over the 20 trading days before the filing. Such cases returned +2.11 % against the S&P 500 over 20 trading days after costs. The same days, the same filter, but without an insider purchase: −0.62 %."
- STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "0.20 % trading costs per trade, round trip – realistic for liquid US stocks."
- STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "The difference is +2.74 percentage points, with a confidence band from +2.10 to +3.41 – entirely above zero. And it is positive in all eleven years, including 2019, 2022 and 2024, when both groups were negative in absolute terms."
- STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "Search and test period separated. Rules were searched in 2016–2022 and checked in 2023–2026. Never the other way around." The table reports 5,879 qualifying cases over 11 years (about 534 events per year), with median -0.63% and hit rate 47.5%, versus 29,105 control cases (median -1.70%, hit rate 43.3%).
- Partial directional parallel on microcaps (different signal and horizon), STATED ([Zhao 2026, arXiv:2602.06198](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06198), unreviewed preprint, code not public): "The analysis covers 17,237 open-market purchases across 1,343 issuers from 2018 through 2024, restricted to market capitalizations between $30M and $500M." (With a $200,000 trailing 30-day ADDV floor, not $1 million.)
- STATED ([Zhao 2026, arXiv:2602.06198](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06198)): "At the opposite extreme, transactions disclosed after price increases exceeding 10% yield mean CAR of 6.3% and outperformance probability of 36.7%." Note that Zhao defines price deviation as the move from transaction price to disclosure price (not a 20-day prior run-up), measures a 30-trading-day Fama-French 3-factor CAR (not 20-day vs SPY), and defines outperformance probability as `CAR_[1,30] > 10%` (median CAR in that bucket is +1.93%, winsorized mean 5.44%).
- STATED ([Zhao 2026, arXiv:2602.06198](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06198)): "Even assuming a conservative effective spread of 2% and price impact of 1%, the mean CAR of 6.3% in the highest momentum bucket would be reduced to approximately 3.3%, though still positive."

Free data needed and $500 long-only tradability:
- Free data: SEC EDGAR Form 4 XML filings (code P open-market purchases and acceptance timestamps) plus daily US stock OHLCV bars with delisted names kept (the same bar table Plan 74 already requires). No analyst estimates, paid news, or vendor feeds are needed.
- Event count: STATED 5,879 cases over 2016 to 2026 (about 534 per year), more than 5x Plan 74's 1,000-signal power target for +2%.
- $500 long-only tradability: Long only; the $1 million daily turnover floor ensures a $500 whole-share order has negligible market impact. However, because the median trade is -0.63% and the hit rate is 47.5%, a $500 account holding 1 to 2 positions at a time cannot hold the 40 concurrent positions used in the study, so realized account variance will be high (per-account live Sharpe on $500 is UNKNOWN).

Strongest published reasons it should fail:
- Survivorship bias in the primary blog study, STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "Survivors distort the picture. Of 5,463 stocks checked, 2,089 no longer had price data – acquired, delisted, bankrupt. Those cases are missing. Since the event and control groups are drawn from the same pool this affects both, but it is not perfectly clean." Missing delisted names can still inflate the absolute +2.11% excess return even if event minus control survives.
- Negative median and concentration in winners, STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): "The hit rate is below 50 % and the median negative. Most individual trades lose slightly; the average comes from the winners. This only works across many positions at once – around 40 on average – never across a single one."
- Peer-reviewed Form 4 liquidity refutation, STATED ([Finance Research Letters 72, 2025](https://www.hb.fh-muenster.de/opus4/files/20594/Insider_filings_as_trading_signals.pdf)): "Based on those filters, we obtained a total data set of 58,732 filings between November 2018 and November 2023." And: "We find positive but lower abnormal percentage returns than in previous studies for short holding periods, but they vanish and even become negative when limiting the tradable dollar amount for each trading signal to a reasonable size." While $500 is far smaller than institutional size and the $1 million turnover floor excludes penny shells, survivorship-free SIP bid-ask testing is essential before trusting the +2.11% figure.

### Rank 2: Form 4 multi-insider buy clusters conditioned on concurrent 8-K or DEF 14A positive-outlook tone and 3-to-5-day disclosure lag (5-session hold)

Nearest ledger relatives and how this differs: `Ledger 171` (`Plan 36`, `Queue 60`) and `Ledger 193` (`Plan 56`, `Queue 15`) tested raw Form 4 clusters over 20 sessions with no concurrent 8-K or DEF 14A filing text and no disclosure-delay filter. This candidate shortens the hold to 5 trading days (inside the 5-to-40-session window) and conditions on concurrent SEC narrative disclosures filed within 5 trading days of the cluster terminal trade, specifically prioritizing clusters whose explanatory 8-K or DEF 14A arrives 3 to 5 days after the final insider trade.

Exact rule as stated:
- STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "A multi-insider open-market buy cluster is a firm-date at which at least two distinct reporting-owner central index keys and at least one flagged officer (from the Form 4 isOfficer field, corresponding to titles including Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Chief Operating Officer, and President) have each executed at least one open-market purchase transaction within a rolling thirty-calendar-day window ending at that date."
- STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "fetch the concurrent 8-K and DEF 14A text within a ±5-trading-day window of the cluster terminal date, and classify the resulting 14,141 disclosure documents with a zero-shot ensemble of three large language models — OpenAI’s GPT-4o-mini, Meta’s Llama 3.3 70B, and DeepSeek’s Chat v3.1 — into two categories: positive-outlook, assigned only when the text contains at least one of seven pre-specified triggers (raised guidance, a new or upsized share repurchase, a material contract win or regulatory approval, a product or clinical milestone, a positive earnings surprise, explicit near-term operational confidence, or buyer-side accretive M&A; the full prompt is in Appendix A), and neutral otherwise."
- STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "Filling the same thirty slots with positive-outlook clusters first, then neutral clusters, then clusters without a concurrent disclosure — a priority-fill construction that uses the sparse positive label opportunistically at the top of the portfolio queue rather than restricting the strategy to the 4.5% positive slice, which would leave the portfolio starved on most trading days — lifts the net Sharpe to 2.28, a +29% gain."

Stated effect, sample years, and post-2015 behavior:
- STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "I assemble a survivorship-free 2015–2025 panel of 1,932,506 Form 4 accessions, detect 38,261 multi-insider open-market buy clusters" (about 3,478 clusters per year).
- STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "The aggregate five-trading-day cumulative abnormal return on the 32,098-event pool is +1.68% residualized against Fama-French five factors plus momentum (|t| = 32.94; the market-adjusted analogue against the SPDR S&P 500 exchange-traded fund is +1.44%, |t| = 32.11; both use winsorization at the first and ninety-ninth percentile computed on a past-events-only basis to prevent foresight in the winsorization cut)." (The 32,098-event CAR pool is a subset of the 35,161 portfolio-eligible pool and 38,261 detected clusters that also has a 252-day factor pre-window.) Subtracting the paper's 25 bps round-trip cost leaves +1.43% (Fama-French) or +1.19% (vs SPY) on the pooled baseline; the exact standalone per-event percent for the positive-outlook slice or the 3-to-5-day gap slice is UNKNOWN (the paper reports portfolio Sharpe ratios rather than separate slice CARs).
- STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "Baseline equal-weighted fill of a thirty-position long-only portfolio on all cluster events yields a five-trading-day annualized net Sharpe ratio of 1.77 at 25 basis-point round-trip transaction cost."
- STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "Disclosure timing matters: clusters whose disclosure arrives on the same day as the final insider trade earn a net Sharpe of 0.64, versus 1.86 when the disclosure arrives three to five days later, consistent with same-day filings being reactive news that is already priced."

Free data needed and $500 long-only tradability:
- Free data: SEC EDGAR Form 4, Form 8-K, and DEF 14A filings (all public and free), plus survivorship-free daily US stock bars. If the 3-LLM ensemble is replaced by deterministic keyword rules for the seven triggers, inference cost drops to zero, though whether deterministic rules match the LLM ensemble is UNKNOWN.
- $500 long-only tradability: STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "On a single-day participation limit of 50 basis points of the twenty-day trailing average dollar volume (a conservative single-trade share of a name’s own liquidity), the small tier tolerates roughly $5,000–$25,000 per position on the median event and the micro tier below $5,000" and "The strategy is tractable at retail scale; an institutional deployment would need to concentrate on the mid- and large-cap tiers, where the reported net Sharpe is materially lower."

Strongest published reasons it should fail:
- Heavy microcap concentration and understated spread cost: STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "Micro-cap events (< $1 million average daily dollar volume) account for 48% of the tier-partitioned pool, small-cap events ($1–$5 million) another 22%" and "No live thirty-day paper-trading pilot on a SEC EDGAR real-time feed against the same priority-fill construction is reported here." Real retail round-trip spreads on sub-$1 million ADV microcaps routinely exceed 25 bps (often 100 to 200+ bps), which would erase a +1.44% pooled 5-day edge.
- Sparse trigger and LLM execution fragility: only 4.5% of clusters are positive-outlook, and STATED ([MarketWizards, Reading the Insider Cluster](https://www.marketwizards.net/files/research/insider-trading/reading-the-insider-cluster.pdf)): "The re-classification run halted at 7,656 successfully parsed events (61% of the 12,527 labeled pool) when the language-model budget was exhausted."
- Independent 2023 to 2026 refutations of plain Form 4 cluster buys: STATED ([Form4API](https://www.form4api.com/guides/cluster-buy-underperformance-study)): "At three months, 2,820 cluster events had a median of +2.68% and a mean of +6.72%, against +2.38% and +6.74% for 25,787 other events: a mean difference of −0.03pp (95% CI −1.69 to +1.67)." STATED ([WolfeWaveSignals](https://www.wolfewavesignals.com/en/insider-study/)): plain cluster buys earned "+0.26 %" ("worthless"). If the pooled 5-day +1.68% CAR is mostly microcap spread bounce rather than real post-spread drift, the rule fails immediately on SIP quotes.

- Sharp analyst or guidance events and small-cap PEAD: dropped because self-referenced 8-K guidance raises are already queued as `Queue 130`, analyst post-recommendation drift vanished post-2003 (Round 50), and small-cap PEAD fails both the 2% post-2015 bar and the free-data long-only bar ("since 2006, I show, analyst earnings surprises fail to positively predict post-announcement returns over 60 days for all-but-microcap stocks, and since 2016 for microcap stocks" in [Martineau 2021](https://cfr.ivo-welch.org/forthcoming/papers/martineau2021rest.pdf); small-cap Q5-minus-Q1 60-day spread fell from "+2.2" in 2023-24 to "−0.2" in 2025-26 with no standalone long-only Q5 figure reported in [Gupta 2026](https://guptagaurav.info/articles/earnings-drift); plain earnings drift already screened in `Ledger 213 / Queue 17`).
