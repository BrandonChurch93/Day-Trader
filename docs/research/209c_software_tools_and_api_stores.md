## Apify Store: metered data or document utility

1. OWNER ONCE: Accept terms and configure a verified payee; Apify may require “government-issued identification, proof of address, tax documentation, and ultimate beneficial ownership information” and will not pay until verification is complete ([publishing terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions)). INFERENCE: The owner keeps KYC, bank changes and tax acknowledgments out of the agent's reach.
2. AGENT ALONE + SCAFFOLDING: Build one deterministic converter from explicitly reusable government data or customer-owned files, with input/output schemas, bounded run time and tests for broken inputs. Apify explicitly says one can monetize “your automation, AI agent, and web scraping projects” as paid Actors ([monetization docs](https://docs.apify.com/actors/monetize)). INFERENCE: This invites automation in the product, not use of a barred source, running past a block, or unattended acceptance of legal terms. MEASURED: Apify's official [build-and-monetize workshop](https://github.com/apify/build-deploy-monetize-ai-agents) lists “Claude Code, Cursor, or similar,” and shows deployment with its CLI; development is expressly encouraged, autonomous billing/publishing authority is not. The [Treasury Fiscal Data terms on its API documentation](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/api-documentation/) offer data “available to copy, adapt, redistribute, or otherwise use for non-commercial or commercial purposes”; INFERENCE: The proposed test uses synthetic fixtures and no API call.
3. SCAFFOLDING + OWNER EVERY TIME for approval gaps: Build, QA, stage listing, pricing, documentation and support expectations. Apify says “We perform regular automated testing of the functionality and performance of all Actors published in Apify Store” ([publishing terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions)). UNKNOWN: Unrestricted agent authority to submit or update every marketplace listing field. INFERENCE: Route operations that lack documented delegated access to the owner; no browser impersonation. MEASURED: The [publication checklist](https://docs.apify.com/actors/publishing/publish) requires a successful build/run, README, and input/output schemas; the [Acceptable Use Policy](https://docs.apify.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy) excludes conduct infringing third-party rights or burdening infrastructure.
4. SCAFFOLDING: Store handles usage and collection; Actor executes with rate ceilings and returns a file without a human per job. Apify calculates developer pay as “80% of the fees paid by Users for your Actor, minus Platform usage costs for the corresponding Actor runs” and makes third-party charges the developer's responsibility ([publishing terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions)). INFERENCE: Start without a paid upstream or model call in the delivery path.
5. AGENT ALONE for routine replies + OWNER EVERY TIME for rights disputes, sensitive data or unresolved faults: Use a reply library and a failure queue, never send raw customer input to a third-party model. Apify requires responses to Apify within three business days and user issues addressed as soon as possible and within 14 days; refunds/uncollectible charges can reduce payout ([publishing terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions), [payout help](https://help.apify.com/en/articles/10057167-how-developer-payouts-work)). UNKNOWN: Blanket permission for unattended customer replies on the store.
6. SCAFFOLDING + OWNER EVERY TIME for disputes or tax filings: Reconcile the monthly invoice, which auto-approves absent action, to payment released on days 21–25 of the following month under the published schedule ([payout help](https://help.apify.com/en/articles/10057167-how-developer-payouts-work)). Wire threshold is $100; terms explicitly say $20 for PayPal and $100 for other options, while the help page also lists $20 for Wise; use the wire threshold or confirm Wise in writing before relying on it ([publishing terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions), [payout help](https://help.apify.com/en/articles/10057167-how-developer-payouts-work)).

- STATED, evidence: Apify's [creator page](https://apify.com/partners/actor-developers) says “$2M Paid out monthly” and 7,500 community developers, without a dated month, active-payee denominator or ledger. UNKNOWN: Fraction paid, median net income, and the date a new tool would first reach $100. Do not divide those two marketing numbers or transfer the usage estimates in Doc 205h to dollars.
- INFERENCE, bank-cash cross-check: At the published 80% share, $125 in paid runs yields at most $100 before usage, adjustments and transfer charges, so gross charges must be higher for wire eligibility if there are costs ([terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions)). Plan $0 bank cash until the modeled run and refund economics work and real demand is separately shown; do not mistake the platform's usage counts for paying users.
- INFERENCE, test and cost: WORTH A TEST. No cash needed to run a private prototype. Lock 40 synthetic clean/dirty files, event ledger and usage assumptions; require every output or error to match the reference, no personal details in logs, one charge per simulated success despite retries, and positive contribution after modeled compute, failed-event refunds and a reserve. Run 20 fictional support tickets plus a source-withdrawal drill; any unsafely answered ticket or unhandled kill switch fails. If this passes it shows safe operations, not discoverability. First cash depends on genuine paid usage, threshold and pay calendar, not simply a publish date. Agent advantage is cheap ongoing schema/test upkeep; a similar automated listing can offer the same thing, so don't build a catalog on speculation.

## Shopify App Store: merchant utility subscription

1. OWNER ONCE: Open the Partner route, verify payee and tax details, and pay the App Store registration fee only after approval of a live trial. Access to the revenue-share plan costs a one-time $19 per Partner account; qualifying smaller developers pay 0% on the first $1M of lifetime gross app revenue counted since January 2025, 15% above it, and 2.9% processing even below the cap ([revenue share](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share)). Some locales add a regulatory operating fee ([revenue share](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share)).
2. AGENT ALONE + SCAFFOLDING: Build one narrow task, such as a bulk import preview for a merchant's own catalog, with least privilege, installation/uninstall, clear prices and isolated stores. Shopify requires using “Shopify App Pricing or the Shopify Billing API for any app charges” and says “Apps that use off-platform billing cannot be distributed through the Shopify App store” ([App Store requirements](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/app-store-requirements)). UNKNOWN: General permission for independently AI-written app submissions, autonomous listing or customer replies; the billing requirement only permits a specified interface. MEASURED: The [Partner Agreement](https://www.shopify.com/partners/terms) restricts even anonymous, aggregate or derived merchant/customer data from use to “create, develop, train, fine tune, or improve” AI systems without specified written consent.
3. SCAFFOLDING + OWNER EVERY TIME for required review or attestations: Prepare listing, screenshots, test instructions, privacy and scopes. The review team “can reject an app at their discretion if it doesn't meet the set standards” and the App Excellence Team conducts regular checks ([App Store requirements](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/app-store-requirements)). INFERENCE: Permit tested releases through a supported interface only; no workaround for review or self-reviewed changes in data access. MEASURED: The official [deployment docs](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/deployment/deploy-app-versions) do allow a CI/CD pipeline “to programmatically deploy your app components,” but some versions still require review and the separate web app must be hosted by the developer.
4. SCAFFOLDING: Shopify bills and remits with permitted billing; merchants must be able to upgrade and downgrade “without having to contact your support team” ([App Store requirements](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/app-store-requirements)). AGENT ALONE for deterministic fulfillment; a failed webhook must retry without granting or revoking a customer's access twice.
5. AGENT ALONE for routine triage + OWNER EVERY TIME for novel incidents, bad data or contested refunds: Staff a defined exception queue and keep raw catalog, order and customer data out of generic models. Merchant credits/refunds appear in earnings and “Refunds aren't taken into account” in the revenue-share calculation ([revenue share](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share)). INFERENCE: Don't confuse the refund rules that apply to a merchant's customer orders with refund authority for the app subscription itself.
6. SCAFFOLDING + OWNER ONCE for verified payout setup + OWNER EVERY TIME for tax filing or reconciliation failures: Reconcile Partner account disbursements to bank and track fees, refunds and hosting; the revenue-share page says “Payouts are still made separately at the Partner account level” even for associated developer accounts ([revenue share](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share)). [Shopify Partner payout guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/getting-paid) gives a $25 minimum and twice-monthly Hyperwallet schedule for qualifying balances; funds typically reach the payout schedule five business days after midmonth or month-end, with late-month earnings pushed to the next midmonth cycle. The [payout guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/getting-paid) describes a first-time-referral hold, not a hold for new apps, and warns that partners may need to remit taxes.

- STATED, platform-reported evidence: Shopify's [April 2026 report](https://www.shopify.com/news/billion-dollar-ecosystem) says it paid developers more than $1.3B “last year” and had more than 21,000 live apps by publication. UNKNOWN: New paid-app fraction, median payout, median discovery time, and how many developers can run support unattended. INFERENCE: One date's catalog is not the period's denominator.
- INFERENCE, test and cost: WORTH A TEST only if a specific merchant job can be stated without inventing demand. Avoid fees and use a local mock store, model checkout/renewal/failure/uninstall and verify “no mutation without consent” on 50 synthetic imports; compare the work saved versus a spreadsheet baseline. Isolate tax, review and staff-dependent cases. No hosting commitment, ad spend or review submission now. The agent may automate regression checks and prefill useful support answers; merchant trust, external services, review, billing privacy and integrations that need custom onboarding are the blockers. First sale and first bank receipt are UNKNOWN.
