| A1 | VERIFIED | [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07355), [full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07355) | Submitted March 28, 2026. Abstract: “For Cohort 1, final Kalshi returns range from -16.0% to -30.8%.” Full paper Table 1 gives the six individual returns. | Six agents, $10,000 each, January 12 to March 9, 2026. These are account returns on funded trades; open positions were marked to the bid, and exchange fees were accounted for in solvency checks. Weather dominated settled Kalshi positions. A separate four-model cohort was paper-only. The funded cohort averaged a smaller loss on Polymarket, so this is not a universal loss range for prediction markets. |
| A2 | VERIFIED | [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34510), [full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34510) | Submitted September 28, 2026. Four selected methods, not four interchangeable frontier LLMs and not the full 32 tested in backtests. Median live return was -0.56%, versus +11.63% for bitcoin buy-and-hold. Live taker fees were 0.10% per trade for spot longs and 0.05% for perpetual-futures shorts. |
| A3 | VERIFIED | [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19530), [full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530) | Submitted November 14, 2026. Not proof that every agent lacks an edge. |

| P3 | VERIFIED | [NBER Working Paper 14804](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w14804/w14804.pdf) | March 2009.
