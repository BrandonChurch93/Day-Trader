Reports for locating sources: /home/user/workspace/data_run/ai_trader_test_design.md, /home/user/workspace/moonshot_run/penny_and_microcap_stocks.md

A1. arXiv 2604.07355: in a live funded test of AI models trading on Kalshi, every model in the cohort lost money, between -16.0% and -30.8%.
A2. arXiv 2609.34510: in a live funded test on OKX, 0 of 4 AI models beat simply holding bitcoin.
A3. arXiv 2611.19530, "LLM Trading Agents Earn Beta, Not Alpha": 91 runs, and no statistically significant alpha after accounting for market exposure.
A4. Stated knowledge or training cutoffs: Claude Fable 5.2 June 2026; Claude Opus 5.5 June 2026; GPT-6.2 Sol knowledge cutoff June 1, 2026; Gemini 4 Carbon March 2026. Check each on the maker's own model page.
A5. The "Rallies" arena, where AI models showed gains, uses simulated money, not funded accounts.
P1. Ang, Shtauber and Tetlock (NBER w19309): the OTC stock market as a whole returned about -0.8% a month over 1977 to 2008. State exactly what the -0.8% is (which portfolio, which weighting).
P2. SEC DERA paper by White (December 2016), "Outcomes of Investing in OTC Stocks": the median retail OTC position return was -13.4% over a median holding period of 16 days, after costs. Check each of the three parts.
P3. Bali, Cakici and Whitelaw (NBER w14804): stocks with the highest maximum daily return last month earn about 1.03% a month less than those with the lowest. State weighting and sample years.
P4. Cohen, Malloy and Pomorski (NBER w16454): a portfolio following opportunistic insider trades earns value-weighted abnormal returns of 82 basis points a month, 1986 to 2007; routine trades earn about zero. State whether the 82 bps is long-only buys or long-short.
P5. Brav, Jiang, Partnoy and Thomas: activist hedge fund 13D filings show abnormal returns of about 7% to 8% in the window from 20 days before to 20 days after the filing, 2001 to 2006, with no reversal in the following year. State how much of that comes before the filing date.
P6. Greenwood and Sammon (NBER w30748): the S&P 500 inclusion effect fell from 7.6% in the 1990s to 0.8% in 2010 to 2020.
P7. Alpaca: most OTC stocks cannot be bought through Alpaca's Trading API. Alpaca charges margin of $2.50 a share (or 100%) on short positions in stocks priced under $5.
P8. Nasdaq changed its rules in 2025 so that companies failing the $1 minimum bid price are delisted faster (for example, no second compliance period after a recent reverse split, or immediate delisting below $0.10). State the actual rule and effective date.
P9. Day trader loss rates: in Brazil about 97% of persistent day traders lost money (Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti); in Taiwan under 1% were reliably profitable (Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean). Check both.
