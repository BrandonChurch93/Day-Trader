# A fair forward test of an AI trader

> Checked 2026-11-25 by fresh checkers on different models. Their tables are in `check/`. Lines changed after the check are marked "Corrected 2026-11-25". The corrected summary is the numbered file one folder up.
> Check notes: in the OKX funded test, three of the four live methods were older machine-learning models and one was a language model (Qwen), which made 3.72% while holding bitcoin made 11.63%. In the 91-run study the six models were mid-size and small models, not the largest ones, and the runs were simulated.


Research date: November 24, 2026. Scope: simulated orders only in the proposed experiment. MEASURED means I opened the cited primary page and checked what it reports, not that I independently audited the underlying trades. STATED means a secondary source only. UNKNOWN means I could not establish the item, not that it is absent.

## Unfinished or not verified

- **UNKNOWN:** This is a public-source inventory, not proof that every benchmark or private contest in 2024–November 2026 was found. Unless a study supplies timestamps or exchange evidence, an author's description of a test as live is not an independent audit. Exact logs, missing model runs, fills, costs and statistical tests are labeled below where not found.
- **UNKNOWN:** Separate pretraining end dates for OpenAI and Google's top general models, as distinct from their published knowledge cutoffs. Also unknown: whether the nominal API IDs can be pinned to immutable model builds, whether the practice setup is expressly approved for this precise account and interface, and simulated fills versus real executable fills.
- **UNKNOWN:** A 2024 genuinely prospective LLM trading trial; announced Ephod U.S./Taiwan contest execution and whether contestants use LLMs; complete primary Nof1 Season 1 final results, Season 1.5 capital/fees and a Season 2 launch. Later release notes or paper revisions may change the inventory.

## What actually traded on live or forward data

Exact percentages below are from the cited pages. A positive return is not the same as beating a comparable passive portfolio. If a study proposes to be live but does not expose enough run timing, it is marked accordingly.

## What to learn from the failures

- **MEASURED: A leaderboard mixes skill, exposure and luck.** In the 91-run look-ahead-controlled study, no run had significant four-factor alpha (largest absolute t-statistic “1.79”), mean yearly alpha was “−2.3%” with a 95% interval “−4.7% to +0.1%”, and 9,101 decisions netted “−0.10%” vs SPY over five days (95% interval “−0.30% to +0.09%”). Yet many paths made money in the rising market. It reports 5.2 percentage points average separation across 54 seed pairs and 5.4 points for 24 repeat pairs. Its own caveats include today's large-cap universe, incomplete anonymization and some Claude comparisons with only one seed. [LLM Trading Agents Earn Beta, Not Alpha, November 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530)
- **MEASURED: Windows, stocks and seasons can select the winner.** FINSABER finds reported LLM advantages weaken when tested over more symbols and conditions. It does not remove all training-memory concerns. AMA compares vote and agent architecture as well as model, while Alpha Arena openly says early wins might be luck. Don't report only the best season, ticker, prompt, or run, and don't treat several portfolios driven by one market week as independent. [FINSABER](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07078), [AMA](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.11695), [Alpha Arena research](https://alphaarena.ai/blog)
- **MEASURED: Historical training leakage can survive well-meaning time filters.** The point-in-time LLM paper selected two windows with similar buy-and-hold, “+25.32%” and “+24.75%”, and saw a drop in excess return of “−17.23” points for Llama 3.1 8B and “−21.77” points for DeepSeek 3.2. The authors acknowledge five large tech stocks and two market periods; regime differences can still explain some of the contrast. In a separate trial-ledger study a deliberately leaked oracle scored DSR “1.00”; statistical correction did not clean bad inputs. [Point-in-time comparison, January 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.13770v1), [What survives honest evaluation?, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27734v1)
- **MEASURED: Time means availability, not the calendar date on a record.** The beta/alpha study's date-joined earnings/guidance rule showed “+32.0” basis points per trade; publication-time plus next-open execution was “−6.7”. Changing which test cut landed on actual decision days exposed planted leakage the first implementation missed. Catalyst Bench shows how later SEC acceptance and amended or cumulative feeds can leak even with date-filtered search. [Beta/alpha timing audit](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530), [Catalyst Bench](https://fundamentalresearchlabs.com/research/catalyst-bench)
- **MEASURED: Trading and AI costs both matter.** A published primary reproduction started from USD 100,000; buy and hold ended at “\$104.8K” while TradingAgents went from “\$106.4K” gross to “\$102.3K” net. That study is a historical reproduction, not a live verdict. ## How much evidence is enough

**MEASURED: Start with the paired, net return series, not the raw trade count.** Use the same scheduled marking intervals across all accounts and define \(x_t\) as model net return minus its preregistered comparator net return. The simple zero-mean statistic is \(t=\bar{x}\sqrt n/s_x=\sqrt n\widehat{SR}\), using the nonannualized mean divided by sample standard deviation. This ratio is called an information ratio when returns are benchmark-relative. The algebra is exact, but the classic t-test needs assumptions such as independent, normally distributed returns. Overlapping multiday holds and correlated assets can make it overconfident. Use a paired time-block bootstrap or a preregistered autocorrelation-robust standard error for primary inference; block the same dates across portfolios and seeds. [Lo, The Statistics of Sharpe Ratios](https://alo.mit.edu/research-page/the-statistics-of-sharpe-ratios/), [Lo's response on nonnormality and dependence](https://quantresearch.org/Wolf_Lo_2002.pdf), [time-clustered example](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530)

**MEASURED: Bailey and López de Prado's minimum track record is confidence conditional on an observed edge, not power.** With periodic (not annualized) observed Sharpe \(\widehat{SR}\), target \(SR_0\), skewness \(\gamma_3\), raw kurtosis \(\gamma_4\) and one-sided confidence \(p\), for \(\widehat{SR}>SR_0\):

\[
n_{\min}=1+\left[1-\gamma_3\widehat{SR}+
\frac{\gamma_4-1}{4}\widehat{SR}^{\,2}\right]
\left(\frac{\Phi^{-1}(p)}{\widehat{SR}-SR_0}\right)^2.
\]

**MEASURED / arithmetic illustration:** With IID normal periodic returns and zero hurdle, at 95% one-sided confidence this gives approximately 273 independent observations if the observed periodic Sharpe is 0.10, or 1,085 at 0.05. For planning with 80% power and 5% one-sided Type I error, the simple normal approximation \(n\simeq(1.645+0.842)^2/d^2\) gives about 619 and 2,475 independent observations for predeclared standardized effects \(d=0.10\) and \(0.05\). These are hypothetical observation counts, not project durations or assumed returns; autocorrelation and heavy tails can change them, and no number of repeated API calls creates more market periods. Use pre-test data from deterministic comparators to choose a dependence-aware power calculation, hurdle, block method and data-quality gates before the model sees the live test. [Sharpe Ratio Efficient Frontier, July 2012, pp. 8–10 and 20](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/sharpe-frontier.pdf), [Lo](https://alo.mit.edu/research-page/the-statistics-of-sharpe-ratios/)

**MEASURED: Account for how many strategies got a chance to win.** The Deflated Sharpe Ratio raises the comparison threshold toward the maximum expected from the set of tried ideas, then applies a skew/kurtosis adjustment. Track every prompt variant, model, universe, fee choice, date filter and discarded pilot that could influence picking a winner. Do not treat correlated trials as independent or assume DSR cures data leaks. Prespecify one main comparison; label extra baselines, snapshots and ablations clearly, and use a predefined multiplicity adjustment or a fresh forward confirmation for any selected alternative. Repeated calls help quantify model randomness, but the time series must be clustered jointly because the runs all share the same market. [Deflated Sharpe Ratio, July 31, 2014](https://davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf), [trial ledger and leakage counterexample](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27734v1), [same-market seed variance](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530)

## Proposed one-page test

**Question and pass mark.** Can one frozen Claude Fable 5.2 setup add reliable, cost-adjusted value over a simple trend rule, given the same information, capital and risk budget? Timestamp a written protocol before the first scored call. Name one primary comparator and return series. Set a positive economic hurdle in return units, one-sided 95% confidence rule, minimum effective sample and market coverage, and risk and operational pass marks. A pass requires the lower bound for paired net outperformance to clear the hurdle and risk/data gates to pass. Report buy-and-hold and random benchmarks regardless. If precision or coverage is insufficient, call it inconclusive. Do not stop because results look good or bad. [Bailey and López de Prado](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/sharpe-frontier.pdf), [PLOS Biology preregistration study, December 9, 2020](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3000937&type=printable)

**Money and permissions.** Isolate paper credentials and disable live routing. Use equal fictional balances; start with fixed liquid U.S. ETFs and BTC/ETH spot, scored separately. No leverage, derivatives or transfers. The validator, not the model, sets volatility-based size, caps exposure and rejects stale/invalid orders. Save broker fills; score all arms with the same next-eligible quotes, fees, slippage, lot rules and dividends. Mark open positions at the end with predeclared liquidation costs. [Paper/live execution difference](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34510), [Anthropic policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup)

**Baselines and repeated runs.** Match clocks, risk budgets and execution. Compare fixed-weight buy and hold (plus a risk-matched passive reference), a preset moving-average trend rule, and random entries with the same holding horizon and volatility sizing. Fix random seeds and entry rate in advance. If model exits are discretionary, separately compare randomized entries matched on realized holding length and size. Run isolated Claude replicas with identical prompts and budgets; score the aggregate, report every path, and cluster uncertainty by market time, not calls. [Matched random and repeat tests](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530), [StockBench repeated seeds](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.02209)

**Inputs, timing and logs.** Start with prices, volume, preset indicators, fictional positions and prior decisions. Ask for a structured action and short justification, not private chain-of-thought. No direct orders, web or external memory. Test timestamped news or a read-only calculator only in separate registered arms. Log each decision deadline, source/publication/receipt times, inputs and hashes, model version, prompt, settings, response, tool results, validation/retries, quotes, order and fill IDs, costs and state. Log holds and failures, with a fixed no-response rule. Commit the record before execution and replay saved answers to reproduce fills. [Catalyst Bench timestamp controls](https://fundamentalresearchlabs.com/research/catalyst-bench), [replay and deliberate leakage tests](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.19530)

**Stop rule and unknowns.** Predeclare when version changes, stale data and routing defects stop or split a cohort; retain failures. Report net return and uncertainty, exposure, drawdown, turnover, fills, costs and abstentions. **UNKNOWN:** Provider approval for this exact practice setup, immutable model identity, effective sample, economic hurdle, regime coverage, fill bias, crypto clock alignment and added value of news or tools. No mid-test changes to chase a win. No transition to live money is authorized. [Lo](https://alo.mit.edu/research-page/the-statistics-of-sharpe-ratios/), [LiveMACE operational exclusions](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09872v1)
