# What a US account pays to go long or short crypto

Status: CURRENT. Checked against official venue sources on 2026-11-25 UTC. For a $300 to $500 Idaho account, eligible US customers now have low published fees to go long or short larger coins. Account approval, minimum order size, and the actual cost still matter.

- STATED: [Robinhood US perpetual futures](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/perpetual-futures-fees/) charge about 1 bp per side through 2026-12-31 on [8 coins](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/about-perpetual-futures/); the commission minimum raises the effective rate on small orders.
- STATED: [Kalshi](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf) charges 5 maker or 12 taker bps per side at the exchange on [19 crypto contracts](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/markets); retail broker markup is UNKNOWN.
- STATED: [OG.com / Crypto.com](https://help.crypto.com/en/articles/17734025-perpetuals-launch-offers-faq) waives trading fees through 2026-11-26 on [9 coins](https://help.crypto.com/en/articles/17741688-perpetual-trading-in-the-us); the fee after that is UNKNOWN.
- STATED: [Coinbase](https://api.coinbase.com/api/v3/brokerage/market/products?product_type=FUTURE&limit=250) lists 24 perpetual-style contracts and [Kraken](https://blog.kraken.com/product/kraken-derivatives/us-fractional-crypto-perps) lists 17. Normal live spreads across the perpetual venues are UNKNOWN; one Coinbase quote is shown below, not a spread assumption.
- ESTIMATE from STATED fees and a MEASURED quote: Among the venues checked, Render can be shorted only through [Kraken US spot margin](https://api.kraken.com/0/public/AssetPairs?execution_venue=bitnomial_exchange), at about 180 bps for a taker round trip under the example below. The [exact Render margin rate](https://support.kraken.com/articles/us-margin-fees) is UNKNOWN.

One basis point (bp, plural bps) is 0.01 percentage points. STATED means the venue publishes it; MEASURED means the checker logged a public market observation, not that we placed an order. UNKNOWN means this check did not establish it. A maker order adds to the order book if it rests; a taker order crosses the spread. A round trip opens and closes the position. Costs below apply to the position size, not the cash posted as collateral.

## Corrections to the scout's write-up

- STATED: Kraken's non-US app fee and funding schedule do not apply to US fractional perpetuals. Use [the US futures fee page](https://support.kraken.com/articles/us-futures-fees), and keep the actual fractional-order charge UNKNOWN; the US roster includes [TRX](https://blog.kraken.com/product/kraken-derivatives/us-fractional-crypto-perps).
- STATED: Kalshi's entry maker fee is [5 bps, not the lower figure in the scout's notes](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf); the taker fee is 12 bps at the exchange. The scout missed [Crypto.com's temporary US fee waiver](https://help.crypto.com/en/articles/17734025-perpetuals-launch-offers-faq).
- UNKNOWN: The public [Coinbase Advanced spot page](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/trading-and-funding/advanced-trade/advanced-trade-fees) does not verify the scout's numeric entry tier. The [Coinbase Exchange fee table](https://help.coinbase.com/en/exchange/trading-and-funding/exchange-fees) and [Coinbase futures beta rate](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/guides/futures) are not current Advanced retail quotes.
