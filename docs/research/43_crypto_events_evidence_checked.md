# Round 6 checked: Dated crypto events for a small US account

Checked November 25, 2026. Counts: **14 confirmed, 6 wrong, 1 not found**. Each bullet is one verdict; repeated or closely linked numbers count together. **MEASURED (by its authors)** means a source reports results from data, not that we ran the test. **MEASURED** means a live API or file download was tested directly on November 25, 2026. **STATED** means a description or summary without primary tables opened. **UNKNOWN** means the claimed result or free data feed was not established. A basis point is 0.01 percentage point.

## 1. New listings (Coinbase, Kraken, Robinhood, Binance)

- **MEASURED (by its authors) · CONFIRMED: 2017 to 2021 announcement jump and pre-listing run-up.** Li et al. (George Mason working paper, 2017 to 2021) measure 112 Coinbase and 300 Binance spot listing announcements and report raw Day 0 mean returns of +32.8% (median +18.4%) on Coinbase and +22.7% (median +10.1%) on Binance, with pre-announcement CR[-5,-1] run-ups of +11.9% on Coinbase and +19.5% on Binance ([Li et al., George Mason working paper](https://www.loyola.edu/_media/sellinger-business/documents/academics/departments/finance/wealth-management-conference/speakers/J_Li.pdf)). No transaction costs or spreads are deducted, and the sample ends in 2021 ([Li et al., George Mason working paper](https://www.loyola.edu/_media/sellinger-business/documents/academics/departments/finance/wealth-management-conference/speakers/J_Li.pdf)).
- **MEASURED (by its authors) · CONFIRMED: 2023 to 2026 front-loading before live trading opens.** The Tie (July 13, 2026) analyzes 1,844 spot listings from January 2023 through June 2026 across 7 exchanges (Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Bybit, Kraken, Upbit and Bithumb; its footer text says 5 major exchanges) using daily VWAP indexed to the first trade date ([The Tie, Jul 2026](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026)). It reports gains are front-loaded before Day 0, BTC-adjusted returns turn flat or negative shortly after live trading opens and mean-revert within one to two weeks, Coinbase roadmap additions typically precede live trading by 48 to 72 hours, and Robinhood is excluded; exact return percentages and trading costs are not printed ([The Tie, Jul 2026](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026)).
- **MEASURED (by its authors) · CONFIRMED: Staggered perpetual introductions widen spot spreads.** Across 95 staggered perpetual introductions covering 75 token pairs on Binance, OKEx and Huobi from December 2019 to September 2022, adding a perpetual raised daily spot dollar volume by $98,248 (+146%) and widened relative quoted spreads by 0.85 percentage points (+200%) ([AFA Perpetual Futures Market Quality](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=169764)). Directional spot abnormal returns after 2025 to 2026 perpetual announcements remain UNKNOWN ([AFA Perpetual Futures Market Quality](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=169764)).

## 2. Delistings and trading-halt notices

- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Binance monitoring-tag announcement and next-day drops.** AI Quant Forge's November 6, 2026 summary of Zhang, Kang and Sun reports 167 Binance monitoring tags (155 first-time, 142 matched) across 26 announcements from July 2023 to October 2026, with raw announcement-minute losses of -3.16% (-3.14% matched; -3.15% on Coinbase, -4.40% on Bybit), another -8.61% relative loss over the next day, and 53% to 58% post-day-0 losses for eventually delisted tokens, with zero spreads or trading costs deducted ([AI Quant Forge summary](https://aiquantforge.com/articles/exchange-warnings-and-crypto-prices-evidence-from-monitoring-tags)). The underlying paper itself was not directly accessible outside OpenAlex, and the summary notes that tokens without perpetual futures drove 5.7 percentage points more day-1 loss than perpetual-listed tokens ([AI Quant Forge summary](https://aiquantforge.com/articles/exchange-warnings-and-crypto-prices-evidence-from-monitoring-tags)).
- **MEASURED (by its authors) · WRONG: Blockonomi delisting figures use untradeable publication-minute prices.** The draft repeats Blockonomi's summary of CoinSight (-26.3% median 24-hour drop, -36.9% at 7 days, and 38% losing over 30% across 101 Binance delistings) ([Blockonomi summary](https://blockonomi.com/what-happens-when-a-cryptocurrency-gets-delisted-from-an-exchange/)). Opening CoinSight's own primary study (August 2022 to November 2026, n = 95 to 96 Binance delistings) shows that measured from 3 minutes after publication (the first tradeable price), the median drop is -16.8% at 24 hours (-16.6% vs BTC) and -32.7% at 7 days, with 20% falling over 30% in 24 hours, whereas -27.5% at 24 hours and -41.1% at 7 days come from the publication minute explicitly labeled not tradeable; no fees or spreads are deducted ([CoinSight Exchange Announcement Study](https://getcoinsight.com/en/research/exchange-announcement-study), [CoinSight Binance Delisting Research](https://getcoinsight.com/en/research/binance-delisting-price)).
- **MEASURED (by its authors) · CONFIRMED: Historical delisting wipeouts and exchange service halts.** Ammann et al. (2022) track 1,222 delisted coins from 2014 to 2021 and measure an average delisting return of -77.8% with 76.3% (932 coins) falling to -100% ([Ammann et al., 2022](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/2bc8397d-47dd-4f66-8467-9004b2c9d212/content)). Lee and Wie (Heliyon 2023) study Upbit's 20-hour service halt on October 15 to 16, 2022 across 13 coins and find negative abnormal returns during the outage (for example, -0.396 over the first 8 hours across all 13 coins, p < 0.10), with no transaction costs deducted ([Lee and Wie, Heliyon 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10395473/)).

## 6. Hacks, exploits and depegs

Crucially, Table 2 weights events by stolen value over market cap on small protocols (median market cap $65.63 million; 25th to 75th percentile $20.99 million to $148.27 million), almost none of which have US perpetual or margin markets ([NY Fed SR 1153](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1153.pdf)). arXiv 2510.00669 (22 DeFi crimes, 2020 to 2022) confirms 55% caused significant drops averaging -13.5% +/- 4.6% (range -59.3% to -1.5%, pre-cost) ([arXiv 2510.00669](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.00669)).

## Five proposed tests: verdict on the evidence

- **Test 1 (Contested and economic DAO governance proposals): WEAK SUPPORT, key citation misapplied.** Live queries confirmed Snapshot GraphQL provides free 2025 to 2026 proposals plus vote-level timestamps (`created`, `vp`) so pre-close running tallies can be built without look-ahead ([Snapshot API docs](https://docs.snapshot.box/tools/api)). However, Lo Monaco et al.'s +4.7% RDD return was measured on Aragon and DAOHaus (not Snapshot) as a same-day vote-outcome return that includes pre-close movement, with zero post-close returns or trading costs reported ([Lo Monaco et al., Economics Letters 2025](https://aisberg.unibg.it/retrieve/10a7b3a5-9af2-4539-80e5-97088c0961d5/Lo%20Monaco%20Momtaz%20Vismara%20ECONLET25%20Distributed%20governance%20and%20value%20creation%20in%20decentralized%20autonomous%20organizations%20Evidence%20from%20a%20regression%20discontinuity%20design.pdf)). The -7.55% to -11.69% whale-snipe shock (2020 to 2022, pre-cost) applies only to a DAO's first-ever occurrence, and only 2 of its 75 DAOs (AAVE and CRV) trade on US perpetuals or margin for shorting ([Institut Louis Bachelier paper](https://callforpapers.institutlouisbachelier.org/Papers/69242d5f-f879-4260-8916-70595794e81a.pdf)). Worth an exploratory replay only because clean free Snapshot data exists, not because the cited papers prove a post-close net edge.
- **Test 2 (Post-public-announcement DeFi hack and exploit reaction): WEAK SUPPORT, blocked by timestamps and US roster.** NY Fed SR 1153 confirms -9.9% (+3h) to -20.6% (+12h) post-disclosure drift before costs, but weighted by stolen value over market cap on small DeFi protocols (median $65.63 million market cap) that rarely have US perpetual or margin markets ([NY Fed SR 1153](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1153.pdf)). Worse, DefiLlama's free hacks archive timestamps all 517 hacks in 2025 to 2026 at midnight UTC (day-level only), so it cannot supply minute-level public disclosure times, and very few 2025 to 2026 hacks directly hit US-perp coins ([DefiLlama API review](https://rya-sge.github.io/access-denied/2026/10/22/defillama-architecture-api-adapters/)).
- **Test 3 (Token unlock crowded-short fade vs pre-unlock team-cliff short): WEAK SUPPORT, blocked by missing free unlock calendar.** Tokenomist (November 2026) confirms 2024 to 2026 pre-unlock short crowding (41% to 44% negative funding on >=10% cliffs) and Keyrock (2024) confirms -25% pre-unlock team drawdowns versus +1.18% ecosystem unlocks, all pre-cost ([Tokenomist, Nov 17, 2026](https://tokenomist.ai/research/what-the-market-does-around-a-token-unlock), [Keyrock, Dec 2024](https://keyrock.com/from-locked-to-liquidity-what-16000-token-unlocks-teach-us/)). However, no free machine-readable historical unlock calendar back to 2025 was found (DefiLlama emissions returned HTTP 402, CryptoRank returned HTTP 403, and Kim's GitHub repo lacks the 52-event CSVs), so testing requires manually curating the ~10 US-perp cliff schedules or paying for an API ([Tokenomist unlocks](https://tokenomist.ai/unlocks)).
- **Test 4 (Catalyst-gated sector leader-to-follower catch-up, 2 to 24h hold): NOT SUPPORTED by cited evidence.** Cited sector papers show spillovers are overwhelmingly contemporaneous (lagged TCI below 10% vs 64% to 66% contemporaneous) or occur at 4-minute lags over a 3-day OOS sample before costs, while static sector narratives align weakly with return factors (Tucker congruence 0.341) ([arXiv 2503.01148](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.01148), [Tasci, PeerJ CS 2026](https://peerj.com/articles/cs-3810/), [arXiv 2601.20336v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20336v1)). No cited source measures a 2-to-24-hour catalyst-gated catch-up return after 50 to 75 basis points of US spot costs, though free 2025 Binance Vision 1-minute data makes a falsification run cheap.
- **Test 5 (Attention spike with source-type filter, 1 to 7 day hold): NOT SUPPORTED.** Maître et al.'s next-day Twitter effect (+11 basis points per SD, 2018 to 2022, pre-cost) is well below US round-trip fees before slippage ([CFR WP 25-02](https://cfr-cologne.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/cfr-25-02.pdf)), Haase et al.'s hourly buy signal reverses at hour +1 (-4.7 bps) and its 0.3%-fee backtest entered at the signal hour's open ([Electronic Markets, 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12525-025-00815-6)), weekly Wikipedia and Google Trends attention-leads correlations are weak (0.05 to 0.14) with a 24-hour Wikipedia lag ([arXiv 2611.10674](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.10674)), and Wikimedia's REST API returned HTTP 429 rate limits from our sandbox IP ([Wikimedia API docs](https://doc.wikimedia.org/generated-data-platform/aqs/analytics-api/documentation/getting-started.html)).

## What this changes for our queue

Written by the builder, 2026-11-25 18:16 UTC.

- Governance votes (test 1): kept as a cheap look, not a counted test yet. The paper the idea leaned on measured a different thing (the vote day's own return on small DAOs, before costs). What is real is the data: Snapshot gives every vote with its time, free, so the running tally before a vote closes can be rebuilt without seeing the future. Queue row 9j: for the DAOs whose coins trade on Coinbase, describe what the coin did in the hours after a close vote ended, against the same coin at random hours. If nothing shows before costs, drop it.
- Hacks (test 2): parked. The free hack list only has the day, and the fall happens in the first hours. Without the minute of first public word there is no honest test. Most hacked tokens also cannot be shorted from a US account.
- Token unlocks (test 3): parked. No free dated unlock calendar back to 2025 was found; the nearest feed is paid. About ten coins that a US account can short have had cliff unlocks, which is too few to judge a rule even with the data.
- Sector catch-up (test 4) and attention spikes (test 5): dropped. The cited papers do not measure the thing proposed, and the attention finding matches our own count: Wikipedia and Reddit attention had no link to the next day's or week's return on our coins (`docs/research/45_attention_feed_measured.md`).
- New listings: dropped as a trade. The gain comes before live trading opens, where a small US account cannot be.
- Useful for the fast lane whatever happens here: Binance's public archive answers from a US address without a key (minute bars for spot and futures, funding every 8 hours, open interest every 5 minutes). That fills the funding and open-interest history the futures book needs.
