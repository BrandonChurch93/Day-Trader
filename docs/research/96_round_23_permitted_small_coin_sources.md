## Round-trip cost baseline for a $500 US account

MEASURED from `docs/research/40_leverage_venues_checked.md` and `docs/research/93_round_21_small_coin_events.md`:

- Coinbase Advanced retail spot taker: 1.20% round-trip fee (0.60% per side) plus spread and slippage, totaling roughly 1.4% to 2.5% round trip on small coins.
- Kraken Pro retail spot taker: 0.80% round-trip fee (0.40% per side) plus spread and slippage, totaling roughly 1.0% to 2.0% round trip.
- Binance.US retail spot (where the coin is listed): 0.02% to 0.20% round-trip fee plus spread, totaling roughly 0.15% to 1.0% round trip on liquid pairs.
- Defensive exit (avoiding a held coin's drop): one-way spot exit fee (0.40% to 0.60%) plus half-spread.

STATED from [Philadelphia Fed Working Paper 26-42 (Sep 2026)](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/-/media/FRBP/Assets/working-papers/2026/wp26-42.pdf): whale alerts alter 24h volatility (raising BTC volatility and compressing ETH volatility) rather than producing a tradable directional return after US spot costs.
