# 205g. Crypto income that is not directional trading

2026-11-27, Nampa, Idaho. INFERENCE: First look for a fixed-price code, design, film or data job with a named payer and clear acceptance criteria. A milestone grant for work already underway is second; a paid API only makes sense if a real buyer wants its output. UNKNOWN: No published evidence here establishes reliable first-month net income for a new solo Idaho operator. Do not buy GPUs, stake or market-making inventory for this test. Automated quest farming, fake volume and using many identities to collect rewards are OUT. This does not reopen airdrops, funding carry or Ethena-style yields.

## Legal and measurement gates

- US and Idaho tax, MEASURED: The [IRS digital asset guide](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/digital-assets) treats digital assets as property and payments for business goods or services as ordinary income; a later disposition can create a gain or loss. MEASURED: [IRS Revenue Ruling 2023-14](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rr-23-14.pdf) says cash-method taxpayers include staking rewards at fair market value when they gain dominion and control. MEASURED: [Idaho's tax guide](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/income-tax/individual-income/online-guide/) says Idaho taxes residents' income from all sources. INFERENCE: For each lane log date, USD value, basis, expenses and dispositions; confirm self-employment, conditional grant or lockup questions with a preparer.
- US and Idaho access, MEASURED: Idaho [defines money transmission](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title26/T26CH29/SECT26-2902/) and [requires licensing](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title26/T26CH29/SECT26-2903/) unless exempt; [FinCEN's federal guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf) addresses accepting and transmitting virtual currency for others. MEASURED: [FinCEN's own-use mining ruling](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/administrative-rulings/application-fincens-regulations-virtual-0) says mining Bitcoin solely for oneself does not make the miner an MSB; it is not a ruling on all protocols. INFERENCE: The legal screen below is for an owner doing and selling lawful work, not holding or forwarding customer funds. “Permissionless” and no state list are not Idaho clearance; confirm sponsor, facilitator, role and venue eligibility before any later launch.
- Test discipline, INFERENCE: Read allowed public pages only. No accounts, posts, submissions, deposits, live tests, signing or movement of money. The agent can read, draft, run local tests and reconcile costs. Under separate approval the owner alone signs, handles keys and identity checks, and moves money. Count failures and time spent; an award is not a payment, an address is not a person, and a transfer is not necessarily a sale. All test budgets below are estimates. Start with zero receipts budgeted for the first two weeks.
