Running Sybil multi-wallet bots violates protocol eligibility rules and risks 100% disqualification (STATED via [Luo et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14316)).

- Strongest reason it is false or already gone: Per-wallet rewards for most groups are under $200 before taxes and bridge/exploit risk, many groups lose money after fees, and Sybil filtering disqualifies automated farming clusters to $0 (STATED via [Luo et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14316)).
