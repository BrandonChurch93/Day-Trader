### Airdrop and points farming: DROP

Most studied groups' average modeled per-address reward did not exceed $200, and their highest expected amount was below $350 in the cited comparison. NOT FOUND / UNKNOWN: exact taxes, current eligible count, rule publication clocks, one-wallet expected profit or net gain after costs. INFERENCE: Criteria and title to rewards must already be knowable at entry for a fair replay; no multi-wallet evasion and no new trade work. Round 15's parent-coin veto remains separate.

### CVRs, Delaware appraisal and liquidating trusts: DROP the proposed trades, not the legal categories

- **Public life-sciences CVRs.** CONFIRMED / STATED: The [Harvard governance forum practitioner survey](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2026/09/21/a-breakout-year-for-cvrs-2025-and-first-half-2026-trends-in-life-sciences-public-ma) reports 28 of 59 announced public life-sciences deals in 2025 and six of 19 H1 2026 biopharma deals. It finds no transferable public life-sciences CVR since the 2019 Celgene deal, and identifies a transferable 2024 non-life-sciences exception. This is evidence about the sample, not a law or all industries. [Cleary's 65-deal practitioner review](https://www.clearygottlieb.com/-/media/files/clearys-pharma-bites/clearys-pharma-bites-contingent-value-rights-in-pharma-deals.pdf) reports four full and six partial payouts among 30 paid-or-expired deals with known payout data, not 30 out of 65 randomly observed or reliable net profits. DROP the targeted secondary buy rule absent a dated, reachable transferable universe.
- **Delaware appraisal.** CONFIRMED / STATED: [DGCL §262(g)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc009/index.html) sets listed-share proceeding-wide exceptions to dismissal: aggregate shares entitled exceed 1%, aggregate merger consideration for those shares exceeds $1 million, or a merger was approved under §253 or §267. WRONG: Neither number is an individual stake minimum. [Jiang, Li and Thomas, Boston University Law Review (2020)](https://www.bu.edu/bulawreview/files/2021/01/JIANG-LI-THOMAS.pdf) report an annualized market-adjusted average of −3.2% gross in 35 observed return cases from their 2015–June 2019 analysis, and hedge-fund filings at roughly one-quarter of the 2015–2017 level after the referenced court decisions. Their $1–$3 million figure cites an earlier typical plaintiff legal-expense estimate, not each holder's cost or a minimum budget. Settlements in their data took a median 378 days from filing to resolution; court-decided cases took 880. NOT FOUND / UNKNOWN: owner-specific net after fees, ability to meet the procedural conditions, or short holding-period evidence. DROP for horizon and execution, not automatic legal ineligibility.
- **Liquidating trusts.** CONFIRMED / STATED: The [2014 FSP incoming no-action request](https://www.sec.gov/divisions/corpfin/cf-noaction/2014/fsp-liquidating-trust-081314-12g-incoming.pdf) describes that trust's unlisted units as not transferable except by will, intestate succession or operation of law. WRONG: An incoming request is not a general SEC requirement that no trust can trade. NOT FOUND / UNKNOWN: a transferable, properly dated universe with modeled net payouts for this account. DROP the claimed secondary trade; existing E07 ETF measurement is not a test of trust units.
