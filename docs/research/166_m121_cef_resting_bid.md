### Order and fill

STATED: An eligible snapshot is the current-session NBBO in the configured tape with a quote-condition array exactly in `continuous_quote_condition_arrays`, age no greater than `quote_max_age_seconds`, positive sizes, both displayed venues on the eligible lit list and strictly positive bid and spread; at entry also require the reference bid to be at or above `min_bid_usd` and on the `tick_usd` grid ([SEC minimum pricing increment rule and dated transition](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/exorders/2025/34-104172.pdf)). STATED: Any exact-boundary quote makes the snapshot unknown; if the latest preceding timestamp is tied, collapse identical records but mark conflicting quotes unknown rather than resolve them from paging. STATED: Quote size is in round lots for these sessions; convert the reported size using `round_lot_shares` before comparing with our modeled size, and pin the sizing-day vendor codebooks by `m121_sizing.tape_dictionaries_sha256` ([Alpaca size-unit change](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/changelog/marketdata-bid-and-ask-size-display-change)).

STATED: Set limit \(L\) at the placement bid and size as the floor of `notional_usd` divided by \(L\), in whole shares; an order with no whole shares is not placed. STATED: Route the hypothetical order only to the placement bid venue `bx`; it becomes live after `entry_delay_seconds` only if a fresh eligible quote strictly before activation shows the same bid and venue and an ask above the limit, and it expires `rest_seconds` after activation with no price change, reroute or replacement. STATED: Adjacent live intervals share only the excluded expiry/activation instant, not any live time; exit marks may overlap later placements because orders are independent measurements, not a shared cash book. STATED: A quote failure at either clock makes the fill state unknown; a valid activation snapshot with a moved bid or venue means known no placement, not an unfilled placed order. ### Exit marks and measurement

STATED: At each `m121_order.mark_seconds_after_fill` horizon and at `close_bid_clock`, use the same strictly-prior eligible quote rule as above, then mark a sell at that bid \(B_h\) only when displayed bid size in shares covers the full order. STATED: The close mark is the last specified clock just before the regular close, not the exchange closing auction, closing print, or a claim of a sale. STATED: No later quote, earlier eligible substitute, midpoint substitution or forward fill beyond the age cap is allowed when a mark is missing. STATED: The sell mark may be on another venue and is a gross displayed bid for the stated size, not an actual execution.

STATED: Let \(M_0\) be the placement midpoint and \(M_h\) the midpoint of the same quote used for the displayed bid mark. STATED: Report gross bid markout \(B_h-L\), the adverse move \(M_0-M_h\) with positive meaning against the buyer, and the entry half-spread \(M_0-L\), each per share and in basis points of \(L\) using `m121_reporting.bps_scale`. STATED: Also report the exit bid concession \(M_h-B_h\) and the fraction of the entry half-spread kept as the sum of \(B_h-L\) divided by the sum of \(M_0-L\) over the same marked fills, with no clipping. STATED: This separates entry spread, market move and cost of crossing the exit quote without treating midpoint as a sale or the gross bid mark as profit.

STATED: Report by fund type and pooled: scheduled slots, no placements, unknown states by reason, known placed orders, fills, eligibility failure counts, odd-lot splits, per-mark availability, means and medians, along with distributions of opening spread and order size. STATED: This is only a description of entry price versus waiting, not an assumption that a full-size ask purchase was reachable, a paired trade, or a decision criterion.

STATED: A fully paged, coherent stream with observed trades and no qualifying print is an unfilled placed order; a terminal empty stream, unresolved identity, known halt in the live order window, or unusable critical fields is unknown and is never evidence for no fill. STATED: Report missing exits per horizon using all fills as the denominator and include only marked fills in that horizon's markout; quote absence, halt at the mark, stale snapshot or insufficient displayed size is missing, never zero. STATED: A type with no fills fails the primary-mark coverage floor, not a coverage rate of zero or one by convention.
