## 1. Post-2023 Evidence on Multi-Day Sector and Narrative Persistence

### A. Industry and Sector Momentum Studies

- Pre-2023 baseline only (not post-2023, not long-only, pre-cost): MEASURED (by authors) in [Empirical Crypto Asset Pricing (arXiv 2405.15716v1, updated Aug 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15716v1) on 210 US-exchange-listed coins from January 1, 2018 to December 31, 2022 using Messari industry classifications: "The time-series average weekly excess return spread (and annualized Sharpe ratios) for the zero-investment long-short strategies are 1.5% (0.78) for two week momentum, 1.2% (0.75) for one month industry momentum, 1.4% (0.87) for two month industry momentum." Four constraints prevent carrying this over to a 2026 $500 US spot account: the sample ends in December 2022, it reports zero-investment long-short spreads rather than long-only returns, it deducts zero costs ("These univariate factor results do not account for feasibility of short selling and transaction costs, such as spreads, trading fees, margin fees, price impact, and slippage"), and it does not state whether Messari industry labels were point-in-time or static.
- Post-2023 cross-sectional momentum collapse: MEASURED (by authors) in [Han, Kang, and Ryu (Oxford Review of Asset Pricing Studies, Oct 13, 2026)](https://academic.oup.com/raps/advance-article-abstract/doi/10.1093/rapstu/raag011/8914819): "When portfolios are marked to market daily, many cross-sectional momentum portfolios are liquidated, and those with statistically significant mean returns often earn negative profits." and "Evidence of time-series momentum is strong, whereas evidence of cross-sectional momentum is almost nonexistent."
- Post-2023 out-of-sample decay in liquid altcoins: MEASURED (by author) in [prams2104 crypto-momentum-backtest (Oct 2026)](https://github.com/prams2104/crypto-momentum-backtest) across 25 liquid cryptocurrencies from January 2020 to February 2026 using 20-day cross-sectional momentum: training (2020 to 2022) produced +36.2% annual alpha (t = 2.02, p < 0.05, Net Sharpe 0.42), validation (2023 to 2024) fell to +13.5% alpha (t = 1.08, p = 0.28, Net Sharpe -0.48), and out-of-sample (2025 to Feb 2026) failed at -12.5% alpha (t = -0.81, Net Sharpe -2.18).
- Narrative rally duration shrank by two-thirds in 2025: MEASURED (by Wintermute on OTC altcoin flows) in [Wintermute Digital Asset OTC Markets 2025 (Jan 2026)](https://www.wintermute.com/insights/market-color/reports/digital-asset-otc-markets-2025) and [PR Newswire summary](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wintermutes-otc-markets-2025-report-shows-cryptos-upper-tier-becoming-an-established-asset-class-as-liquidity-concentrates-302659976.html): "The average altcoin rally lasted approximately 19 days in 2025, down from 61 days in 2024, reflecting reduced follow-through and insufficient liquidity to carry narratives beyond their initial phase." [TradingView / Cointelegraph](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph:b67698620094b:0-rattled-retail-retreats-to-bitcoin-ether-after-october-crash/) adds: "From 2022 to 2024, altcoin rallies typically lasted from 45 to 60 days with sustained narratives including memecoins and AI. However, the median length of altcoin rallies in 2025 was just 20 days."
- Relative leader persistence vs contrarian laggard rotation (2024 to 2026): MEASURED (by authors) in [Rotator Tool (Sep 25, 2026)](https://rotatortool.com/crypto-capital-rotation.html) across 771 days of Binance coins (June 27, 2024 to August 6, 2026, ~3,800 pairs per horizon): rotating out of leaders into laggards lost at every horizon ("48.8%" win rate at 7 days, "48.3%" at 14 days, "46.9%" at 30 days vs 50.0% random). This confirms weak relative continuation of leaders over laggards, but does not measure long-only absolute returns net of US spot fees.
- Narrow on-chain AI-subnet exception (not US-spot reachable): MEASURED (by author) in [Common Risk Factors in Decentralized AI Subnets (arXiv 2603.29751, Mar 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29751) across 128 Bittensor subnets (February 14, 2025 to March 26, 2026): 30-day momentum (`WML_30`) earned "+0.94% (t=3.09) in the first half and 0.47% (t=3.65) in the second" per day, while short-term reversal was "-0.86% per day, NW t=-3.62" (attenuating from -1.40% to -0.31% in the second half). However, these are pre-cost long-short sorts on on-chain Bittensor AMM subnet pools, not US-exchange spot coins, and persistence halved within one year.

### B. Kaito Mindshare and Narrative Attention Measures (2025 to 2026)

- Manufactured mindshare fails to produce positive net returns: MEASURED (by Chris Davis) in [Blockworks Intel (Jul 31, 2025)](https://app.blockworks.com/report/an-analysis-of-post-tge-kaito-campaigns) on post-TGE Kaito leaderboard campaigns: "Post-TGE Kaito Leaderboards increased average and median monthly mindshare in 88% of projects analyzed, with gains ranging from single-digit to over 8,000%." yet "Net price action remained largely flat or negative, suggesting that investors tend to discount manufactured attention when it isn't supported by improving fundamentals."
- Narrative popularity diverged sharply from 2025 returns: STATED by [CoinGecko 2025 Narrative ROI Report via Yahoo Finance (Dec 28, 2025)](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/coingecko-ranks-crypto-narratives-roi-100216984.html) across the top 10 representative tokens per narrative: "While RWA delivered the strongest returns in 2025, popularity did not always translate into profits. Memecoins remained the most talked-about narrative globally, capturing 25.02% of investor interest, yet still finished the year in negative territory." Specifically, "Despite high popularity, memecoins and AI narratives recorded losses of 31.61% and 50.18%, respectively" (reversing 2024's +2,940% AI and +2,180% memecoin averages). Moreover, [CoinGecko's 2026 Narrative Guide (Nov 2026)](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/crypto-narratives) explicitly warns: "Week-to-week price action in crypto is still frequently driven by idiosyncratic, narrative-independent events (a single protocol's news, a token unlock, a celebrity-linked launch), so a hot narrative doesn't guarantee any individual asset within it will move in lockstep."
- Kaito platform discontinuity: STATED by [CoinGecko's Kaito Guide (Nov 2026)](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/what-is-kaito-earn-yap-points): Kaito sunset its `Yaps / Yapper Leaderboard` on January 15, 2026, replacing it with Kaito Studio and Polymarket Attention Markets. No free historical Kaito mindshare archive or reproducible multi-day trading backtest exists.

### C. Memecoins by Chain (Solana, Base, Ethereum, BSC)

- Severe negative median returns and rapid extinction across chains: MEASURED (by authors) in [A Midsummer Meme's Dream (arXiv 2507.01963)](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01963) and [MemeChain (arXiv 2601.22185)](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22185) across 34,988 memecoins on BSC (16,405), Solana (11,829), Ethereum (3,814), and Base (828) tracked from October 2024 to January 2025: "Ethereum shows the largest median price decrease at 42.13%, followed by BSC and Base with median decreases of 38.19% and 36.95%, respectively, while Solana tokens demonstrated greater resilience with a median decrease of only 26%." Over two months, "15,099 meme coins (47.46% of our sample) had no transaction activity after December 15, 2024" (BSC 80.96%, Ethereum 37.32%, Base 16.20%, Solana 9.09%), and only 0.30% (BSC) to 1.14% (Base) met the >100% success threshold.
- Structural mismatch with US retail spot venues: STATED by [Galaxy Research (Oct 1, 2025)](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/memecoins-pump-fun-solana-kols): "More than 57 million tokens have been created across these chains combined, with Solana dominating (56%)." and "At the base sit millions of disposable tokens. These are PvP gambling chips with lifespans measured in minutes or hours, dominated by bots and scalpers." By November 2026, [Cryptopolitan / CryptoNews (Nov 24, 2026)](https://cryptonews.net/news/market/33801135/) reported memecoins cooled to 2% of altcoin volume as Solana's share dropped from up to 90% to ~51%. Almost none of the chain-level memecoin launches trade on Coinbase, Kraken, Robinhood, or Gemini during their multi-day breakout window.

Three distinct biases break naive crypto narrative backtests:

- Delisted-coin survivorship: MEASURED (by authors) in [Ammann et al. (2022, cited in doc 46)](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/2bc8397d-47dd-4f66-8467-9004b2c9d212/content), excluding 1,222 delisted coins (out of 3,904 from 2014 to 2021) inflates equal-weighted returns by +62.19% per year. More recently, [Mobility, Entry, and Survival over Cryptocurrency's Full History (arXiv 2610.23283, Nov 2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23283) tracked 8,970 assets across 690 weekly cross-sections (2013 to 2026) and found: "The bottom decile earns 27.5% per week on paper and nothing among assets that trade, on capacity of $293,000."
- Retrospective category creation: CoinGecko lists 782 categories as of November 2026 ([CoinGecko Categories](https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories)), including themes created after their initial rallies: `AI Agents` (647 coins), `Made in USA` (270 coins), `x402 Ecosystem` (127 coins), `Robinhood Chain Meme` (438 coins), and `Prediction Markets` ("Before 2024, prediction markets did not even exist as a distinct category", [CoinGecko Jul 2026](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/crypto-market-shift-from-narrative-to-pmf)). Pulling today's category list and backtesting 2023 to 2025 guarantees lookahead bias.
- Retrospective coin re-tagging: Existing tokens are added to hot categories mid-cycle (for example, legacy compute or L1 tokens re-tagged into `Artificial Intelligence (AI)` in 2023 to 2024, or US-founded coins re-tagged into `Made in USA` in 2025). Neither CoinGecko's [Categories API](https://docs.coingecko.com/reference/coins-categories) nor CoinMarketCap's [info endpoint](https://concretumgroup.com/building-a-survivorship-bias-free-crypto-dataset-with-coinmarketcap-api/) returns the date a tag was attached.

## 3. What a Count-First Test Would Need (and $500 US Spot Cost Line)

If the owner or lead ever wants to falsify multi-day sector/narrative breakout persistence directly without repeating Replays 13, 16, or 17, a count-first feasibility check must come before any ledger row or replay:

- Point-in-time sector membership (mandatory): Either log CoinGecko `/coins/categories` and `/coins/markets?category=` daily going forward under an active paid subscription (Queue row 81, currently BLOCKED and not worth asking the owner for now) or reconstruct monthly category membership across 2023 to 2026 strictly from Wayback Machine CDX snapshots of 6 to 8 long-standing CoinGecko/CMC categories (`Artificial Intelligence`, `Gaming`, `Meme`, `Layer 2`, `DeFi`, `DePIN`, `RWA`, `Privacy`), intersected with coins already listed on Coinbase or Kraken on that snapshot date (retaining coins later delisted from those venues). Do not use today's CoinGecko category lists.
- Exact non-hindsight event trigger: An equal-weighted US-spot sector basket (minimum 5 US-listed coins in the sector on that date) outperforms BTC by at least +15% (or +20%) over trailing 5 or 7 days AND sector dollar volume is at least 1.5x its trailing 30-day median, with a 14-day cooldown per sector so adjacent days of one run are not double-counted.
- Count-first gate (before any return calculation): Measure (1) how many point-in-time snapshots exist per category, (2) how many US-spot-listed coins fall inside each category on each date (likely only 5 to 15 per sector), and (3) how many independent sector-breakout events occur (no profit test until at least 30 independent events exist across sectors).
- $500 US retail spot cost floor (`docs/research/60_us_venue_costs_checked.md`): Rotating a 3-to-5-coin US spot basket costs ~1.0% to 2.0% all-in RT on Kraken Pro (0.40% maker / 0.80% taker per side plus spread), ~1.2% to 2.5% on Coinbase Advanced, ~1.9% to 2.5% on Robinhood Crypto, and ~0.15% to 1.0% on Binance.US (narrower coin list, up to 94 bps USD spread on smaller coins like RENDER-USD). Holding for 3 to 14 days requires a net excess edge well above +2.0% per event.
- Mandatory benchmarks if ever promoted to a replay: Must beat (1) BTC buy-and-hold, (2) equal-weighted US-spot top-50 altcoin basket, (3) coin-level 7-day hot-five momentum (Replay 13 Rule A, to prove sector grouping adds value beyond single-coin momentum), and (4) random-date entries in the same sector baskets, net of 160 to 200 bps RT.
