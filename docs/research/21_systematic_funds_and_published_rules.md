# 21. Rule-based funds and published rules with live records

Status: research summary. Proposed by research. No owner decision is recorded here.

Date: 2026-11-25. Written by a research agent, then the claims that matter most for decisions were checked against their sources by a second model. Claims not listed in the check log were not rechecked. The check log is in `docs/research/17_21_checklogs/`.

## Bottom line

- MEASURED: The live SG Trend Index earned 8.2% per year in 2000-2009 and 1.8% in 2010-2019, and fell 19.7% from its April 13, 2015 high to December 31, 2018 ([AQR 2022](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/White-Papers/Trend-Following-Why-Now-A-Macro-Perspective.pdf?sc_lang=en), [AQR 2020](https://images.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Journal-Articles/JPM-You-Cant-Always-Trend-When-You-Want.pdf)). A long record did not prevent multi-year losses.
- MEASURED: The clearest live, codeable stock-timing ETF lagged buy-and-hold on raw return. Pacer Trendpilot (PTLC) returned 9.35% per year versus 13.35% for the S&P 500 from June 11, 2015 to December 31, 2024 ([PTLC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616668/000089418925006695/pacertrendpilotuslargecape.htm)).
- MEASURED: An independent replay of Mebane Faber's five-asset monthly rule returned 6.05% per year with an 11.7% maximum drawdown from 2006 to March 2025 before taxes, commissions, and slippage ([Concretum replication](https://concretumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GlobalTacticalAssetAllocation.pdf)). It is a replay, not a live fund.
- MEASURED: Live managed-futures ETFs diverge sharply. Through December 31, 2025, DBMF returned 8.28% per year since May 2019 (+13.85% in 2025), while KMLM returned 5.77% per year since December 2020 (-3.21% in 2025) ([DBMF prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1020425/000119312526197034/d62276d497k.htm), [KMLM prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1547576/000182912626008212/kraneshares-kmlm_497k.htm)).
- MEASURED: Global X's Bitcoin trend ETF (BTRN) beat spot Bitcoin in 2025 (+4.81% vs -7.09% on Coinbase spot), but trailed spot from launch on March 20, 2024 to December 31, 2025 (5.41% vs 15.46% annualized) ([BTRN prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1432353/000143235326000251/a497kbitcointrendstrategy.htm), [FRED CBBTCUSD](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=CBBTCUSD&cosd=2023-03-19&coed=2026-01-02)).
- STATED: Test simple monthly drawdown-control rules against a risk-matched passive portfolio first. Do not use hedge-fund records to justify chasing short-horizon stat-arb or raising starting risk.

| D. E. | Two Sigma | MEASURED: A 2025 SEC order states Two Sigma used hundreds of forecast models. ### Sizing, volatility targeting, and risk parity

| Topic | Primary evidence | What to copy or reject |
|---|---|---|
| Volatility scaling | MEASURED: Moreira and Muir (2017) scale next-month exposure by the inverse of prior-month daily realized variance, normalized to unconditional sample volatility. Spanning regression alphas were 4.86%/yr for U.S. market (1,065 months) and 12.51%/yr for momentum (1,060 months) ([Moreira and Muir](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/sites/default/files/assets/documents/Volatility%20Managed%20Portfolios.pdf)). Cederburg et al. tested 103 equity strategies and found volatility-managed portfolios did not systematically beat unmanaged ones directly and generally earned lower out-of-sample Sharpe ratios and utility ([Cederburg et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3357038)). | STATED: Do not assume volatility scaling improves returns. Only test a capped, no-leverage version as a clean on/off ablation against a fixed-exposure baseline. |
| Risk parity | MEASURED: Leverage-aversion theory argues that when investors avoid leverage, high-beta assets offer lower risk-adjusted returns, so overweighting safer assets and levering the mix can help ([AQR risk parity](https://www.aqr.com/insights/research/journal-article/leverage-aversion-and-risk-parity?aqrPDF=1)). Anderson, Bianchi, and Goldberg (1926-2010, 85 years) found levered risk parity beat 60/40 before realistic frictions (6.87% vs 4.77% excess return), but trailed after LIBOR borrowing and trading costs (4.15% vs 4.66%). In 1946-1982, 60/40 beat risk parity ([Anderson et al. 2012](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~anderson/Risk%20Parity%20March%202012.pdf)). | STATED: Classic risk parity relies on borrowing to lever bonds, which is outside a cash-only account. |
| Real-fund risk controls | MEASURED: DBMF discloses a volatility target (8-10%) and limits gross notional on volatile contracts ([DBMF prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1020425/000119312526197034/d62276d497k.htm)). STATED: Dunn discloses a variable 99% monthly VaR budget (8-22%), not a loss ceiling ([Dunn factsheet](https://res.cloudinary.com/dx34nupzw/image/upload/v1/post/mym7mxw2lidd6udo0ruv.pdf)). | UNKNOWN: Opened fund filings do not show fixed Kelly fractions or public calendar drawdown stops. STATED: Treat project guardrails like max_kelly_fraction and drawdown_derisk_band in config/thresholds.yaml as house rules to test, not proven industry formulas. |

## What it means for a small automated long-only account

- Copy the engineering discipline, not the headline return. STATED: Run closed-bar signals, long-or-cash exposure, version-checked parameters, and daily position reconciliation. Separate operational errors from market drawdowns ([Two Sigma SEC order](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/admin/2025/34-102207.pdf)).
- Do not confuse cash-funded ETFs with cash-only strategies. MEASURED: Buying DBMF or KMLM in a cash account avoids account-level margin, but still embeds futures and short exposures ([DBMF prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1020425/000119312526197034/d62276d497k.htm), [KMLM prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1547576/000182912626008212/kraneshares-kmlm_497k.htm)). STATED: Keep them as reference benchmarks unless the owner explicitly approves embedded derivatives.
- Compare against a risk-matched passive baseline. STATED: A lower drawdown is not an edge if holding less stock and more cash achieves the same drawdown for lower cost. Every test must report net CAGR, excess return over actual cash yield, max drawdown, turnover, and pass project replay gates in config/thresholds.yaml (replay_max_drop_ratio_vs_hold, replay_min_deflated_sharpe, replay_min_shuffle_percentile, and backtest_min_breakeven_cost_multiple).

## Appendix: Verified rule definitions for replication

### Faber 10-month GTAA rule
- MEASURED: Five equal-weight sleeves (20% each): S&P 500, MSCI EAFE, U.S. 10-year government bonds, broad commodities (GSCI), and U.S. REITs (NAREIT) ([Faber paper](https://www.cambriainvestments.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/A-Quantitative-Approach-to-Tactical-Asset-Allocation.pdf)).
- MEASURED: Evaluated once per month on the last day of the month; intra-month moves are ignored. Hold a sleeve if its dividend-adjusted month-end price is above its 10-month simple moving average; otherwise hold 90-day T-bills for that sleeve ([Faber paper](https://www.cambriainvestments.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/A-Quantitative-Approach-to-Tactical-Asset-Allocation.pdf)).
- STATED: For local testing, keep prior position on exact ties, execute at the next eligible close, and treat a four-sleeve variant without commodities as a separate test.

### Global Equities Momentum (GEM)
- MEASURED: Evaluated at month end using trailing 12-month total returns for U.S. equities, non-U.S. equities, and T-bills. If U.S. equities beat T-bills, hold whichever of U.S. or non-U.S. equities had the higher 12-month return; otherwise hold U.S. aggregate bonds for the next month ([Petit 2026 paper](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Petitmarius/Backtest_Strategies/main/research_paper/Petit_2026_Global_Equities_Momentum.tex)).
- STATED: Lock ties to U.S. equities or keep the current holding before testing, and test Antonacci's 2013 absolute-only variant (U.S. equities vs T-bills, moving to T-bills when negative) separately ([Antonacci 2013](https://www.naaim.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/00D_Absolute-Momentum_gary_antonacci.pdf)).

### Pacer Trendpilot US Large Cap (PTLC)
- MEASURED: Uses the S&P 500 Total Return daily close and its 200-day simple moving average. Changes take effect at the close of the first business day after the indicator date ([PTLC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616668/000089418925006695/pacertrendpilotuslargecape.htm)).
- MEASURED: Equity Indicator (5 consecutive closes above 200-day SMA) sets 100% S&P 500. 50/50 Indicator (5 consecutive closes below 200-day SMA) sets 50% S&P 500 / 50% 3-month T-bills, with weights allowed to drift. T-Bill Indicator (after 50/50 is triggered, 200-day SMA closes lower than its value 5 business days earlier) sets 100% T-bills; can skip 50/50 if both trigger simultaneously. From 100% T-bills, only the Equity Indicator returns to stocks ([PTLC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616668/000089418925006695/pacertrendpilotuslargecape.htm)).
- MEASURED: Special Indicator (added Apr 16, 2025): if S&P 500 closes 20% below its 200-day SMA, reset to 50/50 at the next business-day close (unless already 50/50) until the Equity Indicator triggers ([PTLC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616668/000089418925006695/pacertrendpilotuslargecape.htm), [Pacer presentation](https://www.paceretfs.com/media/trendpilot_institutional_presentation.pdf)).

### CoinDesk Bitcoin Trend Indicator (BTI / BTRN)
- MEASURED: Daily input is the last calculated CoinDesk Bitcoin Price Index (XBX) value before 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time, rounded to 2 decimal places, using the 180 most recent daily observations including today ([CoinDesk BTI methodology](https://downloads.coindesk.com/cd3/CDI/Trend-Indicator-Methodology.pdf)).
- MEASURED: Compute exponentially weighted moving averages for four short/long half-life pairs: (1, 5), (2.5, 10), (5, 20), and (10, 40) days, with decay factors \(\lambda_h\) of 0.5, 0.757858283, 0.870550563, 0.933032992, 0.965936329, and 0.982820599, normalized over the 180-day window so weights sum to 1 ([CoinDesk BTI methodology](https://downloads.coindesk.com/cd3/CDI/Trend-Indicator-Methodology.pdf)).
- MEASURED: Score each pair +1 if short >= long (ties are +1) and -1 otherwise. BTI is the mean of the four signs (-1, -0.5, 0, +0.5, +1), published around 4:10 p.m. ET ([CoinDesk BTI methodology](https://downloads.coindesk.com/cd3/CDI/Trend-Indicator-Methodology.pdf)).
- MEASURED: Index exposure to Bitcoin futures is (1 + BTI) / 2 (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100%), with the remainder in 1-3 month T-bill ETF, adjusted weekly each Wednesday (or prior calculation day if a holiday) ([BTRN methodology summary](https://assets.globalxetfs.com/dev/documents/btrn/Index-Methodology-Summary.pdf), [CoinDesk BTIUSF factsheet](https://downloads.coindesk.com/cd3/CDI/BTIUSF+Factsheet.pdf)).
