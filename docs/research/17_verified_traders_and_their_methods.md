# 17. Traders with a verified record and what they did

Status: research summary. Proposed by research. No owner decision is recorded here.

Date: 2026-11-25. Written by a research agent, then the claims that matter most for decisions were checked against their sources by a second model. Claims not listed in the check log were not rechecked. The check log is in `docs/research/17_21_checklogs/`.

## Bottom line

- MEASURED: The US Investing Championship checks designated real-money accounts against brokerage statements. That is evidence for reported contest returns, not a public audit of every account or trade ([organizer rules](https://financial-competitions.com/rules), [2020 final results](https://apnews.com/press-release/business-wire/technology-latin-america-and-caribbean-north-america-south-america-illinois-9e1be0f0539a42d69d7754dd625689de)).
- MEASURED: In 2026, 115 of 688 entrants reported profits at six months and 85 of 712 at nine months. Non-reporters are not confirmed losers ([organizer rules](https://financial-competitions.com/rules), [six-month release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260728048545/en/United-States-Investing-Championship-First-Half-Results-17-Report-Profits), [nine-month release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261029918093/en/United-States-Investing-Championship-Nine-Month-Results)).
- MEASURED: Some traders posted strong results in several years. The organizer shows Sean Ryan reported gains in all seven contests from 2019 to 2025; Martin Luk had two strong full years and was still leading at ten months in 2026 ([2025 final release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260202090143/en/Multiple-World-Records-Set-in-International-Investing-Competition), [nine-month release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261029918093/en/United-States-Investing-Championship-Nine-Month-Results), [October standings](https://financial-competitions.com/)).
- STATED: Kell and Luk describe buying strong stocks, using close stops and increasing exposure in favorable conditions. Both describe using margin; Luk also describes shorting. These are not cash-only playbooks, and the contest does not verify which rules produced the returns ([Kell interview](https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/oliver-kell-interview-the-mind-and), [Luk interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKNEJA5r8zw), [organizer rules](https://financial-competitions.com/rules)).
- MEASURED: The IBD 50 ETF and its predecessor returned 3.72% a year versus 13.71% for the S&P 500 Total Return Index from April 2015 through December 2025. That is evidence against this fund as an implementation of the idea, not a test of a trader's full CAN SLIM method ([SEC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002104659/000121390026047001/ea0286847-05_497k.htm)).
- Proposal: Keep the current large-ETF replay first. If suitable stock data become available, compare a simple long-only new-high breakout with a monthly high-ranking control before testing extra chart patterns. No source here establishes a return target for this account.

## What it means for a small automated long-only account

- Treat the contest as evidence that some people earned large real-money gains, not as a sample of ordinary investor outcomes or a source of expected bot returns. The rules allow tools this account will not use, and the standings do not identify the contribution of each tool ([organizer rules](https://financial-competitions.com/rules), [October standings](https://financial-competitions.com/), [Luk interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKNEJA5r8zw)).
- Separate signals that are easy to define from choices the interviews leave to the trader. A previously observed high, moving average or range is measurable. Choosing a chart level by eye or increasing exposure because trades feel strong is not one fixed rule ([Kell interview](https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/oliver-kell-interview-the-mind-and), [Luk interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKNEJA5r8zw)).
- Model a stop as a signal or an order, not a guaranteed loss cap. For daily signals, use the next tradable price and count gaps, spreads, fees and missed fills. For intraday entries, only use volume that had already traded when the order would have been entered ([Wilcox and Crittenden execution example](https://enjoytheride.world/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/WilcoxCrittenden2005.pdf), [TickerGuard daily-bar assumptions](https://tickerguard.com/articles/qullamaggie-backtest-study)).
- Use histories that include stocks no longer listed, dated universe membership and corporate actions. Today's surviving stock list is suitable for a plumbing check only. One preprint reports a material difference when today's index membership is reused in older years ([Wilcox and Crittenden](https://enjoytheride.world/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/WilcoxCrittenden2005.pdf), [Roskill abstract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm?abstractid=7429238)).
- Record every tried variant, including stops and exposure filters, before scoring a held-back period. Keep the project gates by key: `backtest_max_pbo`, `backtest_min_breakeven_cost_multiple`, `replay_max_drop_ratio_vs_hold`, `replay_min_deflated_sharpe` and `replay_min_shuffle_percentile`. These are project thresholds, not takeaways from trader results.
