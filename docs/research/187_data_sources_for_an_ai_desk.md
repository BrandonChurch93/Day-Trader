# Data sources for an AI trading desk

- STATED: Start with original SEC filings for a narrow stock-risk veto, not as a general buy signal ([EDGAR APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces)).
- MEASURED: This project already tried Treasury auction trades, Congress following and crypto listing trades; a new API does not make the underlying ideas new.
- MEASURED: Most other keyless feeds returned fields that date the event or the record, not when the desk could first see it.
- STATED: Keep new captures small and forward-only where permitted; do not buy data or treat a public endpoint as a licence ([FINRA terms](https://www.finra.org/terms-of-use), [Coinbase market data terms](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data)).
- UNKNOWN: No newly identified ranked source cleared the strict first-public check; Federal Register inspection is a new date-only replay candidate, not a historical text feed.

MEASURED means a status/schema check or an existing project result, not that a collector ran. STATED means a source says it. UNKNOWN means the evidence was not enough. The first-public test asks when a specific version could be read, not just whether an event can be dated. Items are grouped, not ranked.

## What a feed is good for

- STATED: The old 13D, Form 4, IPO-lockup and Treasury studies motivate return hypotheses, not a demonstrated net edge for this desk. Here, their overlaps were rejected, closed for feasibility, or require a truly different universe ([13D study](https://www.ecgi.global/sites/default/files/2026-10/law-098-2008_the_returns_to_hedge_fund_activism.pdf), [IPO lockup study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/0022-1082.00334), [Treasury auction study](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/46329/)).
- Proposed vetoes: original adverse 8-Ks, scheduled macro releases, FDA calendar, venue incidents and depth. Check whether a fixed veto reduces losses or false fills relative to the existing rule, after extra costs. Deribit, CFTC TFF, chain activity and stablecoin supply are possible risk context; a quote, funding rate, transfer or mint is not itself a trade signal.
- Proposed research only: Snapshot event coverage and Base factory-to-executable-market mapping. Neither is proof of an accessible return trade. Wikipedia's prior null means it is a control at most, not a reason to reopen the strategy.

## The legal line

- STATED: For securities, do not trade on material nonpublic information in breach of a duty; materiality asks whether a fact changes the reasonable investor's total mix ([SEC investor glossary](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/insider-trading), [SEC SAB 99](https://www.sec.gov/interps/account/sab99.htm)).
- STATED: A duty can run to the information source; confidentiality agreements and some relationships can create duties, and tipping analysis considers breach, what the recipient knew, and personal benefit ([O'Hagan](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/521/642/), [Rule 10b5-2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240/subject-group-ECFR4bb6d4a0ab76c75/section-240.10b5-2), [Dirks](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/463/646/)).
- STATED: Tender offers have a separate rule that can apply without a fiduciary breach; use only properly disseminated disclosures ([O'Hagan](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/521/642/)).
- STATED: Confidential crypto listing tips led to a wire-fraud conspiracy plea and a separate SEC settlement; the OpenSea judgment was vacated and remanded, not declared lawful ([DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/former-coinbase-insider-sentenced-first-ever-cryptocurrency-insider-trading-case), [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-98), [Second Circuit](https://ww3.ca2.uscourts.gov/decisions/OPN/23-7038_complete_opn.pdf)).
- STATED: CFTC Rule 180.1 prohibits intentional or reckless fraud or manipulation in covered commodity transactions; it is not a general duty to disclose all nonpublic information ([17 CFR 180.1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-I/part-180/section-180.1)).
- Desk rule: no hacked, leaked or confidential tips, no bypass, and no source the desk cannot retain or use as proposed; public access alone does not authorize it ([SEC EDGAR hacking charges](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2019-1), [Senate eFD notice](https://efdsearch.senate.gov/search/home/), [FINRA terms](https://www.finra.org/terms-of-use)).

## What the second check changed

- Downgraded current snapshots, scheduled dates, chain clocks, and observation buckets from replay-ready to forward-only or permission-gated. EDGAR original filings remain useful with a conservative next-session gate; the index and current frames are not event clocks.
- Reclassified Treasury auctions, lockups, earnings drift, Form 4 clusters, CEF 13Ds, Congress and Wikipedia against prior project work. Kept price and outcome testing separate from counting, rights and availability.
- Replaced public-access assumptions with the actual use restrictions and removed unsupported prices and claimed edges. See the four category checks for publisher quotes, status results and remaining gaps.
