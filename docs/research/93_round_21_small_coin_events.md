## QUEUE Candidates (2, Both Count-First)

### 1. Bonus (k): Exchange Monitoring Tags and Caution Warnings (Queue Row 79) - QUEUE

- Distinct from existing repo work: Queue row 72 covers only final delisting notices. Monitoring Tags (Binance) and Investment Caution designations (Upbit and Bithumb under South Korea's DAXA framework) are published weeks to months before a delisting decision and frequently hit US-listed coins (such as `DOT`, `KAVA`, `SKY`, `ZIL`, `CVC`, `BICO`, and `ONE`).
- Published post-2023 evidence (three independent 2026 datasets):
- STATED by [Zhang, Kang, and Sun (2026) via AI Quant Forge](https://aiquantforge.com/articles/exchange-warnings-and-crypto-prices-evidence-from-monitoring-tags), studying 167 Binance Monitoring Tags across 26 announcements from July 2023 to October 2026 (155 first-time tags, 142 matched to 3 untagged peers): "A holder who reads a Binance monitoring announcement still faces most of the token's expected loss. Zhang, Kang and Sun find that tagged tokens lose 3.16% during the announcement minute. Over the next day, they lose another 8.61% relative to comparable untagged tokens." Pre-release leakage is minimal: "The pre-release return is -0.073% across 79 tags, significant only at the 10% level."
- MEASURED by [CoinSight (May 2023 to November 2026)](https://getcoinsight.com/en/research/upbit-caution-warning-price) from +3 minutes after publication: "Caution / monitoring tag · Upbit, from 3 minutes after the announcement: median -1.2% after 1 hour, -5.4% after 24 hours and -13.6% after 7 days; 26% of coins were higher 24 hours later (n = 46)." Across all tracked exchanges combined (n = 63, [CoinSight Exchange Announcement Study](https://getcoinsight.com/en/research/exchange-announcement-study)), median moves from +3 minutes are -5.4% at 24h (-4.9% vs BTC) and -13.4% at 7d, with only 30% up at 24h.
- MEASURED by [CryptoListing.ws (April to November 2026)](https://cryptolisting.ws/listing-price-reaction/): Across 31 Binance Monitoring Tag additions in 2026, median 24h trough was -15.2% (middle half -17.7% to -12.6%), 97% moved at least 10%, and median time to trough was 1.5 hours. [KoinBulteni (November 2026)](https://koinbulteni.com/en/binance-delisting-list) reports 67% of tagged coins were later delisted: "The tag does not guarantee a delisting, but of the 67 coins that got it recently, 45 were delisted, most within a few months."
- Two uses stated separately:
- Use 1 (Desk exit and avoid rule for a held coin, reachable on US spot): Companion to Queue row 72. Exiting any held US spot coin and blocking new buys on a Binance Monitoring Tag or Upbit/Bithumb Caution notice costs one-way spot exit fees (0.40% to 0.95% plus half-spread) to avoid an -8.6% to -13.6% post-notice drift.
- Use 2 (Short trade on a US-reachable short venue): Requires a checked US-reachable short venue (`docs/research/40_leverage_venues_checked.md` and `docs/research/60_us_venue_costs_checked.md`): Kraken US spot margin (`:BTNL`, 29 USD pairs, ~180 bps taker RT plus 4h rollover), Coinbase Financial Markets US perpetual-style futures (24 contracts), Kalshi perpetual futures (19 contracts), Kraken Derivatives US fractional perpetuals (17 coins), OG.com / Crypto.com US perpetuals (9 coins), or Robinhood Derivatives US perpetuals (8 coins). Because the union of these checked US rosters covers only roughly 35 larger coins (and offshore perps like Hyperliquid, dYdX, and Gemini perpetuals exclude US persons), most small-cap tagged coins cannot be shorted by a US retail account. Count-first step: count how many 2023 to 2026 Monitoring Tag and Caution notices hit those ~35 US-shortable coins before opening any replay.
- Point-in-time data needed: Official Binance Support Monitoring Tag notices and Upbit/Bithumb caution notices (Queue row 77 Wayback-verified feed) + Coinbase/Kraken public 1m/1h candles.
