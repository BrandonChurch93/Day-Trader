# What successful small-account traders look for

2026-11-26, Boise. Reading and rule writing only. No market bars, quotes, price files, or new replay were opened or run. Market comparisons are published annual index returns. MEASURED means a counted result, a checked statement or a reported figure with an identified third-party check; STATED means a trader's description, a source's claim or a proposed rule; UNKNOWN means the evidence here does not establish it. CODEABLE means a stated condition has a fixed definition; JUDGMENT means some choice is still left to the trader.

The recurring idea is not to buy what's cheap. It is to find a stock that is already showing unusual demand, wait for an entry close to a price that would prove the idea wrong, and risk more only once trades are working. The major exception is that Fous's daily Revival starts with an old downtrend, and Kullamägi's news pivot can start in an overlooked stock. A checked result for one account does not show that these rules explain its return or that a new account can repeat it ([Kell](https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/oliver-kell-interview-the-mind-and), [Luk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKNEJA5r8zw), [Kullamägi](https://qullamaggie.com/my-3-timeless-setups-that-have-made-me-tens-of-millions/), [Fous Revival](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr4crUKJ0tg), [contest rules](https://financial-competitions.com/rules)).

The scope is swings held from a day to about eight weeks, with a few weeks the goal, not a requirement to trade intraday or hold for six months ([owner's decision](../DECISIONS.md)). Fous's original swings, Kell's shorter holds and Luk's active swing management are the closest examples; Fous's F1–F3 and Ross Cameron's day trades are context, and David Ryan's six-to-twelve-month winners show why his payoff does not transfer to this horizon ([Fous interview](https://www.secretentourage.com/success-stories/cameron-fous-alerts/), [Kell interview](https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/oliver-kell-interview-the-mind-and), [Luk interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKNEJA5r8zw), [Ross Cameron's worksheet](https://cdn.warriortrading.com/warriortrading.com/assets/2025-Small-Account-Tool-Kit.pdf), [Ryan interview](https://www.valueplays.net/wp-content/uploads/41775536-Market-Wizards.pdf)).

## Cameron Fous: what he teaches for free

### Stocks

Fous says the original Fous4 uses four daily lead-in patterns and was for swings lasting days to weeks; Part 2 added intraday patterns after 2008. A free lesson names Revival, Survival, Force and Gold. These are conditions before the breakout, not four clock times; F1, F2 and F3 are the intraday set. His newer retelling mentions an Armageddon signal at the bull-market top, but does not give a different full original list; do not swap that signal for Gold or import the crypto definitions as historical stock rules ([Fous interview](https://www.secretentourage.com/success-stories/cameron-fous-alerts/), [daily lesson, 8:13–10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KZgw1F4iYQM), [newer retelling](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HuPNSOK9spc)).

MEASURED: the FTC brought a case over earnings claims about users of the service, distinct from checking Cameron's personal trading schedules ([free worksheet](https://cdn.warriortrading.com/warriortrading.com/assets/2025-Small-Account-Tool-Kit.pdf), [FTC case](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/2023198-warrior-trading-inc-ftc-v)).

The strong common elements are leadership, an entry near invalidation, cutting bad trades and adding exposure only with favorable feedback. ## Two criteria cards

STATED: These are fixed proxy rules, not trading recommendations, known edges or reproductions of anyone's track record. The numbers below that are not credited to a trader are choices to make one possible test reproducible. No ledger entry or pass mark is proposed here.

### Shared data and fill rules

- Warm up from first available 2016 session; evaluate January 2017 through December 2025, by NYSE session, and keep 2026 out. Use point-in-time primary listings (NYSE, Nasdaq and NYSE American), US ordinary common shares only, including former listings. Exclude OTC, funds, warrants, units and preferreds. Link by permanent security ID, and use the alias and venue known that day. Alpaca regular-session SIP daily bars since 2016 supply stock bars; one-minute bars are useful for a separate stop/fill audit. At the stated 200 requests/minute batch and checkpoint when collection is separately authorized. Coinbase and Kraken bars are not inputs to either stock card.
- Use as-traded signal-day price and dollar thresholds, and split-normalize earlier OHLC and share volume to the current share basis with only splits effective by signal close; do not use future-adjusted data for the $1 gate. Convert holdings and stop prices on effective dates; credit cash distributions when paid. Historical listings, split/delisting terms, security IDs, and in Card A dated shares/filings are separate inputs, not guaranteed by bars. Require all named signal windows to be present. A missing scheduled entry open refuses that order; a missing mark on an open holding blocks a score unless a documented halt permits last-mark carry and next eligible sale, or a delist has independently dated settlement terms. No dropping losing holdings from the book.
- Run one long-only cash book per card, $500 initial equity, whole shares, no borrowing or interest, one position per security; sell orders before buys at each open. Limit total committed share value to settled cash available at the open; apply historical settlement (T+3 before 2017-09-05, T+2 from that date to 2024-05-27, then T+1; exchange business days). The sale decision cannot reuse same-session unsettled proceeds. Mark remaining positions at the last regular close, subtract assumed selling friction. For pending entries and exits use only the next regular open, never the close that created the order. For stops already in force, sell at the opening print if at/below the stop; otherwise if the day's low reaches it, use the stop level as an optimistic daily-bar proxy before friction. Count gap exits separately; the stop is a trigger, not a guarantee. Apply fixed adverse 10 bp entry/exit in A and 50 bp in B (assumptions, not measured fill costs). Base position risk uses the adverse entry and stop sale prices. Whole-share quantity is rounded down. Count raw symbol-day signals, distinct signal dates, funded initial trades, and adds separately.
- Limit initial orders to one queued per date via each card's ranking. Predetermine the maximum acceptable raw open using the signal close, then submit only shares affordable and risk-sized at that worst permitted price. For daily simulation fill a buy only if the next regular open lies inside the stated range and above the stop; do not add to the preauthorized share count at the open. A real opening order may not support all cancellation conditions: report daily-price fills as a proxy, and separately verify opening timestamps or trade at the next eligible minute after the observed open with the same cap. Do not silently assume the same open can be observed and traded. Count orders blocked by settlement, open limits or zero shares.

### Card A: leader with earnings news, stay out in a bad tape

- Find leading small/mid stocks at close t. Require raw close $5–$75, average daily raw dollar turnover (close times volume) over the 20 sessions ending t−1 at least $5 million, and market cap $300 million–$10 billion at t−1 using the latest publicly available share total across common classes known at t−1, mapped to the traded share basis. Use single common-share-class issuers only where that cap is not unambiguous. Require the 63-session split-adjusted close return ending t−1 to be in the top 10% of eligible names (ceiling of 10% of count, ID tie-break), and t−1 close at least 95% of the highest high in the 252 sessions ending t−1. Require the range of the most recent five sessions through t−1 divided by their mean close to be at most 75% of that ratio for the preceding five sessions.
- Require the issuer's first public earnings release for its quarter, delivered after the close five sessions before t and no later than the close of t. Use SEC 8-K Item 2.02 acceptance and exhibit, or an earlier explicitly timestamped issuer release if pinned before use. Do not infer time from a report date. Use the originally published quarter's consolidated revenue, not updated data; require at least 20% year-over-year growth with positive comparable prior revenue. No parse or timestamp means no signal. Then require t close strictly above the highest prior 10-session high, no more than 3% above it, and t volume at least 1.5 times its preceding 20-session mean.
- Market switch: use both QQQ closing above its 20-session EMA and IWM closing above its 50-session SMA at t; otherwise queue exit from every name next open and cancel fresh buys/adds. Seed EMA with the first full 20-session simple average. If both are on, rank by 63-session stock return, then signal-day relative volume, then ID. Set initial stop to the low of t. Use raw entry window strictly above the greater of the old pivot and t low, through 1.005 times t close; refuse if the worst allowed raw entry is more than 3% above the stop as a share of entry. Size to the smaller of 0.5% of t equity divided by adverse entry-to-stop dollars and 20% of t equity divided by adverse entry price, with a cash cap. At most four open names.
- Hold until the fixed stop, market exit, first completed close below the stock's 10-session EMA (simple-average seed), or the 40th trade-session close including entry, whichever comes first. Exit after a close signal at next regular open. From the first through fifth session after entry, allow one add after a completed close at least one initial risk unit above the actual initial entry, only if no exit was signaled. One risk unit is initial adverse entry less estimated adverse initial stop. For that add set the new stop at the greater of the old stop, initial adverse entry and add-signal day's low. Freeze that stop at the next open if the add fills; the add does not restart the hold clock. Rank simultaneous adds by signal close divided by initial adverse entry minus one, then security ID; place one add before any new entry and no new entry that date if an add was queued. Use raw open strictly above the new stop through 1.005 times the add-signal close; refuse if maximum open implies a stop wider than 3%. Add no more than half the original shares and cap add shares so the total planned open-to-stop risk on both lots (valuing old shares at maximum permitted add open, adverse exit and new adverse entry) is at most 0.75% of signal-close equity and total position at most 30%. If the next open skips the add, no retry. Exit all shares together. Gap loss can exceed all planned budgets.

### Card B: Fous daily Gold and Revival only

- Scan raw signal close $0.50–$10, average split-basis share volume over the 20 sessions ending t−1 at least 200,000, average daily as-traded dollar turnover at least $500,000. No catalyst, market gate, or short trade. Block any candidate with a split effective in the prior 126 sessions including t, or one announced as of t for the next open. The volume scale follows his public discussion; the price band and dollar floor are our choices, not his scan.
- An ascending base uses the 20 sessions through t−1. The low of the last ten must be at least 3% above the low of the first ten. Each half must have a high at least 97% of the entire base high. Define the pivot as that 20-session high. Revival: additionally require t−21 close at least 30% below t−81 close, and require t close strictly above pivot. Gold: instead require every one of the prior 126 raw closes below $1 and t close strictly above both $1 and the pivot; set the operative line to the greater of $1 and the pivot. For Revival the operative line is the pivot. In both cases require t close at most 5% above the line and t volume at least twice the prior 20-session mean. If both trigger, tag Gold only; rank Gold first, then relative volume descending, then permanent ID.
- Buy next session in the raw open window strictly above the operative line through 1.02 times t close. Initial stop is 0.98 times the line; refuse if the maximum acceptable raw open is more than 8% above the stop as a share of entry. Size to 0.5% of signal equity at the adverse planned entry-to-stop distance, capped at 20% of equity and available cash, at most four open names. Do not add. No retry for ten sessions after any exit; require a new full signal after the wait.
- Exit entire book position on a protective stop, or after the first close below either the operative line or daily EMA13 (simple-average seed), or after ten trade-session closes including entry, at next regular open. If no full exit is queued, when a first completed close is at/above $2 for Gold or at/above actual entry plus twice initial dollar risk for Revival, sell half the shares rounded down at next open (no partial if position has one share). If that open breaches the stop, stop all shares at the adverse open. A stop hit later in the session exits the rest. This makes the Gold goal testable without claiming a guarantee or a faithful Fous target.
