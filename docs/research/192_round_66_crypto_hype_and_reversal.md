### 1. Exchange Top-Gainers Lists and Large 1-Hour or 1-Day Jumps: Shorting the Pop

- Evidence: Three opened academic sources plus repo replays. Third, [EconStor Intraday Bitcoin Shocks (working paper, 2016 to 2021 Kraken hourly BTC)](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/314207/1/1841191159.pdf) found that after +0.5% to +5% positive hourly BTC shocks, 24-hour cumulative returns were positive (+0.286% to +2.001%, pre-cost), not negative. ### 5. Retail Hours and Weekend Crowd Buying (US Evenings and Weekends)

- Independent trades a year: 52 weekends or ~250 evenings a year, but zero post-2015 return edge (STATED).
- Strongest reason it is false or already gone: Weekend crypto volume has shrunk to less than half of weekday volume as the market institutionalized onto Wall Street hours, and a 500-coin 2018 to 2024 replication found no robust return seasonality (STATED via [MDPI Wall Street Time](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/19/9/707/pdf) and [Finance Research Letters 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612324004598)).
