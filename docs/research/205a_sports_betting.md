## What bettors kept and what books can stop

- MEASURED: New Jersey's completed 2025 events show $12.246 billion staked, $1.163 billion kept, 9.5% hold; football 5.2%, basketball 3.8%, baseball 4.3%, parlays 19.2%, other 6.2% ([NJ Casino Control Commission annual report](https://www.nj.gov/casinos/about/reports/pdf/2025_ccc_annual_report.pdf)). [DraftKings' own Massachusetts December 2024 report](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/DraftKings-Cat.-3-Sports-Wagering-Revenue-Report-December-2024.pdf) shows an 8.36% hold on $364.58 million of settled handle and $30.49 million of win. These are turnover-weighted realized shares, not the percentage of people who lost. MEASURED, arithmetic: equal -110 prices imply a 4.76% overround, 52.38% break-even hit rate and a 4.55% expected loss of stake on a true 50/50 side. An offer margin, a book's actual hold and a bettor's realized loss are different quantities.
- MEASURED: The [British Gambling Commission](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/blog/post/commercial-restrictions-by-betting-operators/) observed 14,923,840 active betting accounts in 2024, 4.31% with commercial restrictions and 2.23% closed for commercial reasons. Across reported accounts, 25.42% were in net profit and 72.54% in net loss on lifetime settled bets; 46.78% of restricted accounts were in profit. Those are lifetime bet histories for accounts active during a year, not a one-year winner rate or unique US people; some broke even and two respondents had reporting issues ([British Gambling Commission](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/blog/post/commercial-restrictions-by-betting-operators/)). STATED, actual operator records: a 32,262-person February 2015 European cohort lost a median €25 over eight months, with a median 15 sports bets ([Journal of Behavioral Addictions study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8997203/)). UNKNOWN: net winner share and median net dollars over one complete year for US sports-bettor individuals, or by method. The [two-year follow-up on the European cohort](https://www.greo.ca/Modules/EvidenceCentre/files/Nelson%20et%20al%20%282024%29_Long-term%20betting%20trajectories%20among%20online%20sports%20bettors_final.pdf) does not give the overall yearly winner share either. STATED, dated example only: a research team's 2016 five-month real-money soccer experiment reported 265 bets, $957.50 net and restrictions after weeks or months, depending on the passage; among examples, maximums of $1.25 and $11.11 and manual review or refusal. MEASURED: The paper's own $50 per bet and 265 bets would make $957.50 equal to 7.23% of stake, not its headline 8.5%; an exact turnover denominator was not given ([Kaunitz et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.02824)).

## Candidate methods

### Better prices than a sharp close

- INFERENCE, how: Compare like-for-like pregame odds with a liquid, low-margin reference book and strip the reference margin. Bet only if the offered payout implies better value under that probability; the losing party in expectation is a book whose offered price is stale or deliberately generous. The actual later close is a diagnostic, not information you have at entry.
- STATED, evidence: [Simon's Management Science study](https://aura.american.edu/articles/journal_contribution/Inefficient_Forecasts_at_the_Sportsbook_An_Analysis_of_Real-Time_Betting_Line_Movement/30546293) followed 3,681 MLB games at four books; lines could overreact and weekend day-game forecasts at start were worse than 90 minutes before. The abstract reports historical profit examples, not an attainable quote record or guaranteed future calibration. [Pinnacle's own article](https://www.pinnacle.com/betting-resources/en/betting-strategy/using-the-closing-line-to-test-your-skill-in-betting/7e6jwjm5ykejuwkq) gives one selected bettor's 1,214 bets and +2.19% fair-closing-price advantage versus +5.73% realized turnover return over eleven weeks, not independent evidence or a base rate. [Massachusetts operator data](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/Meeting-Materials-9.30.25-OPEN.pdf) link consistent better-than-close prices to smaller limits, not to a measured proportion winning.
- UNKNOWN: Annual winner share, median dollars, available limits and time to first payout for this method. INFERENCE: $0 at risk and no returns for a two-week paper check; no real-money income in Idaho via conventional books, and no travel just for the check. Use approved data or archived research results; record clock, price availability, final close and all misses on one agreed sport, with no claim after two weeks. An agent standardizes markets and keeps time; owner alone handles any later authorized location, account and manual wager. The traps are stale screens, benchmark errors, sample selection, limits, taxes and manual lag.

### Arbitrage across books

- INFERENCE, how: Opposite sides of the exact same settlement pay more than the full stake across books if both prices hold at the accepted size. The books fund the gap from inconsistent pricing, not from a better forecast; suspended, rejected or repriced second legs turn it into a naked bet.
- STATED, evidence: [Franck, Verbeek and Nüesch, Economica](https://www.unifr.ch/tim/en/assets/public/uploads/Publication%20list/2013/Franck_Verbeek_N%C3%BCesch_2013.pdf) found book-only opportunities on 102 of 12,782 matches averaging 0.9% return; in five European soccer leagues in 2004/05 to 2010/11, book/exchange matching found short-side opportunities on 2,287 of 11,933 matches averaging 1.4%, allowing up to 5% exchange commission. Match-clock alignment used assumptions and potentially available historical quotes, not two independently confirmed fills. [Massachusetts limiting materials](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/Meeting-Materials-9.30.25-OPEN.pdf) flag arbitrage as a possible limit trigger, not a method-specific timeline.
- UNKNOWN: Current US pairwise fill rate, median profit, usable first-dollar clock and legal account count on one trip. INFERENCE: Even one perfect $300 matched turnover at 0.9% makes only $2.70 before data, taxes or travel, with cash split across both books. One licensed Oregon online book is not enough; the agent can flag mismatches with authorized data, never place bets or bridge locations ([Oregon rules](https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/displayDivisionRules.action?selectedDivision=6940), [FanDuel DC manual wager terms](https://account.dc.sportsbook.fanduel.com/terms)). Cheapest test: paper-check both legs against realistic sequential delays, common rules for overtime, pushes, nonstarters and voids, and default misses to not filled. $0 and one to two weeks on permission-compatible old data only.

### Bonuses and matched betting

- INFERENCE, how: A book pays a marketing subsidy to acquire a customer; an eligible user can sometimes keep part of it, with independent opposing exposure if allowed by both sets of terms. A bonus bet or credit is not cash, and exclusions, qualifying losses, deadlines, nonreturn of bonus stake or a failed hedge can erase the gain.
- MEASURED: Oregon's [Lottery rules](https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/displayDivisionRules.action?selectedDivision=6940) say bonuses have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn and may expire or be all-or-nothing; [DraftKings Oregon](https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/legal/or-terms-of-use) prohibits “any type of bonus abuse.” The [Massachusetts Gaming Commission's $25,000 order](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/Cant-Lose-Parlay-Final-Decision.pdf) against Penn's “Can't Lose Parlay” establishes marketing enforcement, not that matched bettors profit. UNKNOWN: independent realized conversion, annual win share, median monthly dollars or speed of restriction for legitimate single-account bonus use.
- INFERENCE: Possibly a modest one-time gain only if the owner is already in an eligible place, has a genuine single account, and the written offer allows the exact treatment. No repeat monthly income established. Paper-audit one current eligible offer free: model worst-case loss, stake not returned, two sets of settlement rules, deadlines, withdrawal and tax. Record $0 until written terms clear the assumption; no travel or qualifying deposit just to try. An agent can audit the fine print, not use accounts, claim multiple offers or conceal a hedge. Time to cash after qualifying and settlement UNKNOWN.

### Middles

- INFERENCE, how: Take opposing spreads or totals at different cutoffs so both can win if the result lands between them. Outside the gap you normally lose the price difference, so it is a probabilistic bet, not arbitrage or guaranteed income.
- STATED, evidence: [Dmochowski, PLOS ONE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10306238/) studied 5,412 NFL games across 2002 to 2022; spreads explained 86% of median-margin variation across spread groups and frequently sat inside the authors' near-even quantile bounds. That supplies a model of thresholds, not a verified middling edge or acceptance data. MEASURED, hypothetical equal -110 legs: risk $11 twice, win $20 inside, lose $1 outside; ignoring pushes, the gap must hit more than 1/21 of the time, or 4.76%, before taxes or travel.
- UNKNOWN: Actual attainable gaps, win rate, net median and days to first payout. INFERENCE: Same multi-book location, terms and manual fill limits as arbitrage; an agent can measure frequencies from old outcomes, not reserve both prices. Cheapest two-week desk test is to fix one spread interval before reading scores, count exact outcome masses and pushes, and fail if the lower confidence edge after rules and execution is not positive. Spend $0; DROP as a small-bankroll plan.

### Player props, niche models and general ML

- INFERENCE, how: Model a player's or team's event distribution separately from the bookmaker; if the book understates an outcome often enough at accepted odds, the book pays. Model advantage is better probability and rules matching, not more predictions or a high win rate at expensive odds.
- STATED, published holdout: [Walsh and Joshi](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S266682702400015X) fit through NBA 2017/18 and simulated 2018/19 at Westgate closing moneylines. Calibrated selection returned +32.45% fixed-size or +36.93% with eighth Kelly on $10,000 initial bankroll; accuracy selection was +5.56% or -75.9%, respectively. These are final bankroll changes with the offered margin embedded, one season, not returns on turnover, live accepted prices or prop returns. [Wilkens' tennis study](https://content.iospress.com/download/journal-of-sports-analytics/jsa200463?id=journal-of-sports-analytics/jsa200463) used 38,959 ATP/WTA matches and seven 2013 to 2019 rolling holdouts; best ML accuracy 69.1% versus bookmaker 69.0%, but bookmaker probabilities won on log-loss and AUC. Forecast accuracy does not establish after-margin profit. [BetBetter's props write-up](https://zenodo.org/records/19077987/files/betbetter_nba_props_research.pdf?download=1) is from the vendor behind the service and supplies neither dated test folds, underlying fills nor an independent audit; STATED marketing research, not proof of profit.
- STATED, LLM out-of-sample preprint: [2026 World Cup benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.17765) scored 335 simulated bets on the same 104 games. Returns on staked dollars at quoted odds with mean overround 5%: Claude -18.1% (73 bets), ChatGPT +8.0% (55), Gemini +3.7% (103), Grok +10.3% (104). No model beat the market Brier score, a simple flat favorite comparator profited in this tournament, and mostly one book's opening prices were not verified fills. One favorite-friendly tournament with correlated bets, flexible sizing and model differences is not a stand-alone edge.
- UNKNOWN: Real after-margin prop median, annual winners, time to limit and first dollar. INFERENCE: Idaho paid Player Picks are not offered by [PrizePicks](https://www.prizepicks.com/help-center/eligibility); the agent can normalize stats and use time-stamped public injury announcements, but cannot interact with retail accounts. A two-week $0 paper check can freeze one market and compare quotes with a de-vigged model baseline on untouched dates, logging the news clock, unavailable markets and cancellation rules. If lawful dated data is not already available, do not buy it. No defensible monthly profit forecast.

### Live betting

- INFERENCE, how: Take a price after a state change before a book or opposing bettor reprices it. The opposite side pays only if the price is still valid and you reach it first.
- STATED, evidence: [Croxson and Reade, Economic Journal](https://academic.oup.com/ej/article-abstract/124/575/62/5076978) found prices updated swiftly and fully around soccer goals at half-time in high-frequency exchange data. A real operator cohort of 24,794 live bettors was reported at 18% loss as a proportion wagered, not a guaranteed individual margin or modern US figure ([LaBrie et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10899-007-9067-3)). [DraftKings MA house rules](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/DraftKings-House-Rules-8.18.25.pdf) allow arbitrary acceptance delays and repricing before acceptance, and [The Odds API](https://the-odds-api.com/sports-odds-data/update-intervals.html) does not refresh ordinary books at event speed.
- UNKNOWN: Placeable live edge, median dollars and time to first gain or cap. INFERENCE: $0 spent; one-week desk-only lag audit from published evidence is enough to reject the basic fast-alert premise. No “courtsiding,” account automation, prohibited data or travel for this. A round-the-clock agent may see more games, but delayed feeds and required manual bets cannot outpace an exchange or sportsbook reacting directly.

### Betting exchanges and federal sports contracts

- INFERENCE, how: Match with another trader, not a fixed-odds house; an exchange earns commissions or transaction fees. To keep money, the bettor needs better forecasts at the after-fee price or enough spread to pay for being picked off on stale quotes.
- STATED, evidence: [Economica's historical Betfair study](https://www.unifr.ch/tim/en/assets/public/uploads/Publication%20list/2013/Franck_Verbeek_N%C3%BCesch_2013.pdf) found theoretical book/exchange gaps, not a verified Idaho maker income. [Kalshi's published fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf) lists a standard taker coefficient of 0.07 times contracts times price times one minus price, with rounding and series-specific fees. MEASURED, hypothetical arithmetic: 100 contracts costing $0.50 face $1.75 standard taker fee on $50 risked, or 3.5%, before spread; paying to get out can add another fee, and combined contracts can differ. [Kalshi developer terms](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf) are own-trading only. [Betfair](https://www.betfair.com/en/aboutUs/Terms.and.Conditions/) is not a US path.
