# Claims: high-risk crypto
Report for locating sources: /home/user/workspace/moonshot_run/high_risk_crypto.md

K1. Ammann, Burdorf, Liebi and Stoeckl (November 2022): in a sample of 3,904 coins, 2014 to 2021, 1,222 were delisted; survivorship and delisting bias in equal-weighted returns was 62.19% a year, against 0.93% value-weighted.
K2. Le and Ruthbah (Monash, August 2023), "Trend-following Strategies for Crypto Investors": on an index of large coins excluding bitcoin and ether, January 2019 to January 2023, a 20-day long-or-cash trend rule returned 67.16% a year before costs and 48.76% a year at a 0.50% cost, against 18.48% for buy and hold. State whether 20 days was the best of several lookbacks tried.
K3. Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu (NBER w25882): 1,707 coins, 2014 to 2018; small minus large size premium 3.4% a week (t = 2.56); momentum winner-minus-loser spreads of about 2.5% to 4.1% a week. State gross or net and long-short or long-only.
K4. Review of Asset Pricing Studies, October 2026 (raag011): under more realistic assumptions, cross-sectional momentum evidence in crypto is "almost nonexistent", and time-series momentum holds up better.
K5. Li et al. (listing study, Coinbase 112 listings and Binance 300 listings, 2017 to 2021): average run-up of +11.9% (Coinbase) and +19.5% (Binance) over days -5 to -1 before the announcement, and announcement-day moves of +32.8% and +22.7%.
K6. Ante and Meyer (250 cross-listings): abnormal return +6.51% on the listing day and -1.51% over days +1 to +3.
K7. "Meme Coin Factories" preprint (arXiv 2609.10246): about 1.02% of Pump.fun launches "graduate", out of about 15.25 million launches from January 2024 to January 2026.
K8. CoinGecko wallet study (May 7, 2026): 69.92% of 5.22 million Pump.fun wallets were losing in June 2025, against 26.72% of 3.14 million in April 2026.
K9. Li, Shin and Wang (pump and dump schemes, 500 events): an insider who buys before the alert makes up to +18.44%; an outsider buying in the first 10 seconds makes at best +6.53%; buying after 20 seconds generally loses.
K10. Hamrick et al.: across 1,034 Discord and 3,767 Telegram pump signals in 2018, the median price spike within five minutes was 3.5% and 5.1%.
K11. FBI IC3 2025 report: 181,565 complaints involving cryptocurrency with $11.366 billion in reported losses; Idaho had 1,023 such complaints and $48.30 million.
K12. Chainalysis (February 2025): 74,037 of 2,063,519 tokens launched in 2024 (3.59%) were suspected pump and dump tokens.
K13. US Senate: a cloture vote on the CLARITY Act (digital asset market structure bill) failed 49 to 50 on September 15, 2026, and the bill is not law.
K14. Small-coin spreads: the report gives median quoted spreads of about 37 to 41 basis points for small coins on US venues. Find the source it cites and check the figure, the venue, the date and the coin set.
K15. Riksbank Working Paper 413: daily short-term reversal in the top 100 coins returned 2.187% a day gross equal-weighted and 0.558% value-weighted; net of costs the value-weighted figure is 0.058% and not significant.
K16. Cong et al. (NBER w30783): on 26 unregulated exchanges, estimated fake (wash) volume averaged 70.85% equal-weighted and 77.50% volume-weighted.
