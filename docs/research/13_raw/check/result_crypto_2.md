# Second fact check: high-risk crypto claims (K1 to K16)

Checked 2026-11-25 UTC (about 07:00 to 07:30 UTC). Every claim was opened at a primary source today.

Unchecked or partly checked:
- K2: the Monash PDF blocks direct download (HTTP 403, a "Just a moment" bot check). The text came from a fetch tool's cached copy of that same Monash URL, and SSRN 4551518 was read live for the dates. The numbers match, but I could not get a live, uncached copy today.
- K6: d-nb.info returned 502 and the fetch failed. I used the open-access copy of the same published article on EconStor (also a cached read).

Totals: VERIFIED 16, OVERSTATED 0, WRONG 0, NOT IN SOURCE 0, UNREACHABLE 0. K2 and K6 were confirmed only from cached or mirror copies.

Most serious problem for someone investing money: the large return figures in K2 (67% a year), K3, K9 and K15 are all in-sample and gross of real costs or depend on short legs. K2's best figure comes from choosing the best of 4 lookbacks over one 4-year window, on an index rather than coins you can trade, and it was not replicated. Also, the K14 spread figure is a single 10-coin snapshot whose median hides spreads near 400 bps.
