# High-risk crypto for a small Idaho spot account

> Checked 2026-11-25 by fresh checkers on different models. Their tables are in `check/`. Lines changed after the check are marked "Corrected 2026-11-25". The corrected summary is the numbered file one folder up.


Research date: November 24, 2026, America/Boise. Scope: own money, long-only spot, no options, leverage, shorts, or perpetual futures. The strongest candidate for paper replay is a slow trend filter on a liquid basket. It is not a proven edge for this account. The evidence does not justify funding a microcap or launchpad sleeve now. That is an assessment, not a return forecast.

### Self custody, launchpads and U.S. law

- STATED: Holding and swapping in a noncustodial wallet for one's own account is distinct from accepting/transmitting value for other people. FinCEN does not generally treat an own-use virtual-currency user as a money transmitter. That does not decide whether a particular offering is a security, restricted asset, or sanctioned transaction ([FinCEN FIN-2019-G001](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf), May 9, 2019; [OFAC virtual-currency guidance](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/913571/download?inline), October 2021).
- STATED: Uniswap Labs separates the front end from the protocol. Its own terms bar users who are “the subject of economic or trade sanctions” and users of a jurisdiction “subject to comprehensive country-wide, territory-wide, or regional economic sanctions by the United States”; there is no blanket U.S. spot ban. They prohibit “circumvent[ing] access restrictions on a token,” including geographic or issuer restrictions ([Uniswap Labs Terms](https://support.uniswap.org/api/v2/help_center/en-us/articles/30935100859661.json), July 28, 2026). Jupiter says “Jupiter does not interact with digital wallets located in, established in, or a resident of the United States” and bars VPN circumvention ([Jupiter Terms](https://docs.jup.ag/user-docs/legal/terms-of-use), undated). Raydium says the protocol is “not available to residents of” the USA; its representation also names the interface and related smart contracts. Do not assume its LaunchLab interface is permitted from Idaho ([Raydium disclaimer](https://raydium.io/docs/disclaimer/), undated; [LaunchLab](https://raydium.io/launchpad/), undated). Contract accessibility and an interface's permission are different.
- STATED: Pump.fun's spot launchpad lets a creator initiate a token on a bonding curve; a token that fills the curve moves to its canonical PumpSwap pool. Its general “Prohibited Jurisdiction” list does not include the United States as such. Its feature terms do expressly bar a “U.S. person” from Prediction Markets and bar residents/people located in the U.S. from the Perpetuals Feature; the Pump Bridge also imposes U.S. restrictions on particular assets. There is no promise that a specific coin or feature is available or lawful ([Pump Terms](https://pump.fun/docs/terms-and-conditions), November 16, 2026; [Pump fees and pools](https://pump.fun/docs/fees), November 16, 2026). Graduation describes the venue transition; it does not establish an exit price or investment return.
- STATED: A presale platform is different from a permissionless curve. CoinList says all participants must pass KYC/AML screening and that eligibility is detailed “on each deal page.” Completing U.S. verification does not make every sale available to a U.S. non-accredited investor. Do not classify a token offering by its marketing label; read its own sale and transfer restrictions ([CoinList FAQs](https://coinlist.co/faq?locale=en), undated; [SEC/CFTC interpretation](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/23/2026-05635/application-of-the-federal-securities-laws-to-certain-types-of-crypto-assets-and-certain), March 23, 2026).
- STATED: SEC Corporation Finance staff said transactions in the described ordinary meme coins “do not involve the offer and sale of securities under the federal securities laws.” Purchasers do not get federal securities-law protections on those facts; economic reality controls, the statement has no legal force, and fraud can still be prosecuted under other laws ([SEC meme coin staff statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/staff-statement-meme-coins), February 27, 2025). SEC and CFTC staff separately said existing law did not prohibit specified registered exchanges from facilitating certain spot crypto products; that was not a blanket registration or safe harbor for all spot venues ([Joint SEC/CFTC staff statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/sec-cftc-project-crypto-090225), September 2, 2025).
- STATED: The 2026 SEC interpretation and CFTC guidance distinguish digital commodities, collectibles, tools, stablecoins and digital securities. Digital commodities, collectibles and tools as defined are not in themselves securities; stablecoins depend on their characteristics, and a non-security token can still be offered as part of an investment contract. The CFTC says it will administer the Commodity Exchange Act consistently with the interpretation; it is not blanket approval of token sales, DEXs or manipulation ([Federal Register, 91 FR 13714](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/23/2026-05635/application-of-the-federal-securities-laws-to-certain-types-of-crypto-assets-and-certain), effective March 23, 2026).
- MEASURED: The House passed H.R. 3633 CLARITY 294–134 July 17, 2025; the amended bill was placed on Senate calendar June 1, 2026. Cloture on the motion to proceed failed 49–50 September 15, 2026. Congress's status did not show enactment; do not treat it as operative law ([official H.R. 3633 action data](https://www.govinfo.gov/bulkdata/BILLSTATUS/119/hr/BILLSTATUS-119hr3633.xml), September 15, 2026). By contrast, GENIUS became Public Law 119-27 July 18, 2025 and establishes rules for payment stablecoins; the statute's effective date is the earlier of 18 months from enactment or 120 days after final implementing regulations, not automatically its date of passage ([GENIUS enrolled law](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ27/html/PLAW-119publ27.htm), July 18, 2025).
- STATED: The OCC says its October 23, 2026 implementing final rule was the first federal final rule under GENIUS. The 18-month statutory clock wins: its effective date is January 18, 2027, not yet in force on November 24. The rule requires identifiable segregated reserves at least equal to issuance and bars issuer-paid yield solely for holding, using or retaining the stablecoin. This is not a guarantee of sub-second redemption or an absence of secondary-market discounts ([OCC final rule, 91 FR 67000](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/23/2026-21706/implementing-the-guiding-and-establishing-national-innovation-for-us-stablecoins-act-for-the), October 23, 2026).
- STATED: U.S.-regulated futures exist, including Coinbase's perpetual-style product and Kraken's domestic perpetual futures, distinct from offshore derivatives. CME lists options on Bitcoin and Micro Bitcoin futures. None is needed for the proposed experiment. The strongest loss from the house rules is inability to trade the published short legs of factor and reversal tests, not the loss of a demonstrated after-cost retail opportunity ([Coinbase U.S. futures](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/coming-july-21-us-perpetual-style-futures), July 2025; [Kraken U.S. perps](https://blog.kraken.com/product/kraken-derivatives/announcing-cftc-regulated-us-perps), June 2026; [CME Bitcoin options](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/cryptocurrencies/bitcoin/bitcoin/overview), undated; [CFTC Uniswap derivatives order](https://cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8961-24), September 4, 2024).

## What the return studies actually measure

### Factors and timing

| Effect | Primary result and counterevidence |
|---|---|
| Size and cross-sectional momentum | MEASURED: Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu's May 2019 working paper covers 1,707 coins in 2014–2018 with market cap >= $1 million and price/volume coverage. Weekly gross, in-sample, value-weighted excess returns for the smallest and largest cap quintiles were 4.7% and 1.3%; small minus large was 3.4% (t=2.56). Ranking on past 1/2/3/4 weeks produced gross weekly winner-minus-loser spreads of 2.7%/3.3%/4.1%/2.5%, respectively. These are cross-sectional portfolio averages, not a net, executable U.S. long-only strategy. The later Journal of Finance paper reports the market, size and momentum factor model; see next row for conflicting replications ([NBER working paper tables](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w25882/w25882.pdf), May 2019; [Journal of Finance](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jofi.13119), April 2022). |
| Cross-sectional robustness | MEASURED: The authors of a replication thesis using CoinGecko reproduced short-horizon momentum spreads in overlapping historical tests (about 3% gross weekly in the 2014–July 2020 replication period), with joint regression support much weaker. Ammann et al., using 3,904 coins 2014–2021 and modeling actual delistings, found size persisted but the surviving-sample size premium was overstated by 50%; no positive one-week momentum relationship survived their correction. A 2026 peer-reviewed paper reports “almost nonexistent” cross-sectional evidence under more realistic assumptions; many marked-to-market long-short portfolios liquidate and some significant mean returns coexist with negative compounded profits. That is not a direct test of long-only spot ([replication thesis](https://arc.hhs.se/download.aspx?MediumId=5522), sample ends May 2022; [Ammann et al.](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/2bc8397d-47dd-4f66-8467-9004b2c9d212/content), November 28, 2022; [Review of Asset Pricing Studies](https://academic.oup.com/raps/advance-article-abstract/doi/10.1093/rapstu/raag011/8914819), October 13, 2026). |
| Trend / time-series momentum | MEASURED: Liu and Tsyvinski found strong BTC own-past-return predictability: 2011–May 2018 gross next-week mean 11.22% after highest-quintile BTC weeks vs 2.60% after lowest-quintile weeks. XRP (August 2013–May 2018) had positive short daily coefficients, weaker with bootstrapped errors; ETH (August 2015–May 2018) had a positive one-day and negative five-day coefficient, neither compelling by bootstrap. These are historical predictor tests, not reliable altcoin trade returns. The independent 2026 paper favors time-series over cross-sectional momentum. Le and Ruthbah tested an ex-BTC/ETH large-cap index, January 2019–January 2023: its 20-day long/cash trend returned 67.16% annualized before costs and 48.76% at a modeled 0.50% cost per traded volume, vs 18.48% gross buy-and-hold, with modeled T-bill return on the trend rule's cash. Best of several lookbacks, in-sample index simulation, not a filled U.S. spot basket or independently replicated result ([Liu and Tsyvinski](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24877/w24877.pdf), August 2018; [2026 momentum study](https://academic.oup.com/raps/advance-article-abstract/doi/10.1093/rapstu/raag011/8914819), October 13, 2026; [Le and Ruthbah, Exhibits 3–4](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/pdf_file/0011/3744821/Trend-following-Strategies-for-Crypto-Investors.pdf), August 2023). |
| Short-term reversal | MEASURED: The Riksbank working paper uses USD pairs across over 80 CEXs, March 2017–March 2022, selecting the top 100 by lagged liquidity. Daily loser-minus-winner in-sample simulations returned 2.187% gross EW and 0.558% gross VW. Its net Table 5 reports 1.687% EW and 0.058% VW; 0.726% EW net for low-volume reversal, 0.125% VW net, insignificant. Table 5 specifies “20 basis points on the long side and 30 basis points on the short side”; the main-text cost discussion says 30/40 bps. Do not silently adopt either as this account's fills. Stronger equal-weight numbers, daily turnover and the short leg make translation particularly doubtful ([Riksbank WP 413, tables 2–5](https://www.riksbank.com/globalassets/media/rapporter/working-papers/2022/no.-413-trading-volume-and-liquidity-provision-in-cryptocurreny-markets.pdf), 2022). |
| MAX / low volatility | MEASURED: Grobys and Junttila's 20 initially large ex-BTC coins in 2016–2019 had −1.54%/week gross high-minus-low MAX and −1.82% with a one-day delay (lagged maximum daily return, long-short and retrospective). The other paper, using a wider sample with a $5 million filter and six-month seasoning, January 2014–September 2020, found the opposite: controlling for total volatility, VW high-minus-low MAX was +1.58% weekly gross, and volatility/IVOL sorts became insignificant after controlling for MAX. Different frequency, universe and MAX lookback mean this is not a clean replication. Neither establishes a robust low-vol premium or spot retail jackpot edge ([Grobys and Junttila](https://jyx.jyu.fi/bitstreams/323af8ba-f93c-453e-9ae1-a97f53de8178/download), 2021; [Lottery-like preferences and the MAX effect](https://d-nb.info/1246248476/34), sample ends September 2020). |
| Weekend, overnight and narrative | MEASURED: In October 2020–May 2021 intermarket data, with a longer CEX check, BTC/ETH volume and volatility dipped on weekends; Coinbase Pro volume was particularly low during 05:00–12:00 UTC. A liquidity pattern is not a signed return edge in a market that does not close overnight. The 2018–2022 U.S. exchange factor panel has 210 assets and tracks classifications, but does not validate a profitable real-time sector/narrative rotation rule. UNKNOWN: any independently replicated, after-cost directional edge from these labels or times for an Idaho altcoin basket ([Periodicity in Cryptocurrency Volatility and Liquidity](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.12142), November 3, 2021 revision; [Empirical Crypto Asset Pricing](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15716v1), May 2024). |

### Listing news, unlocks, airdrops and issuance

- STATED: Coinbase's roadmap notice named AXIS, PEARL and STABLE as “New additions to the Coinbase asset listing roadmap,” not as markets open for trading. In a separate case, DOJ says a former Coinbase employee tipped traders about forthcoming announcements between June 2021 and April 2022. They bought before disclosure and sold afterward. This is evidence of a timing advantage, not a post-news strategy or an estimate of how often it occurs ([Coinbase Markets roadmap](https://x.com/CoinbaseMarkets/status/2112293243568501177), October 19, 2026; [DOJ Wahi sentencing](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/former-coinbase-insider-sentenced-first-ever-cryptocurrency-insider-trading-case), May 9, 2023).
- STATED: For an airdrop after a hard fork, the IRS recognizes ordinary income when the recipient can exercise dominion and control; not necessarily when the chain forks. This is not a ruling on every promotional airdrop. Preserve the time and fair market value at receipt separately from the later sale record ([IRS Rev. Rul. 2019-24](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2019-44_IRB#REV-RUL-2019-24), October 28, 2019 bulletin).
- MEASURED: Jiasun Li et al.'s 112 Coinbase and 300 global Binance listings in 2017–2021 showed gross average run-ups of +11.9% and +19.5% on days −5 through −1, announcement-day moves of +32.8% and +22.7%, and day 0 through +4 cumulative moves of +42.2% and +17.6%. These are retrospective daily opens/closes across venues and include the jump; they do not give a fill after a public timestamp. Coinbase usually started trading the next day in this sample. Ante and Meyer studied a different event, actual cross-listing, using 250 ICO-token listings through 2019. Their gross BTC-model abnormal price moves were +4.97% over days −3 through −1, +6.51% on day 0, and −1.51% across +1 through +3; on Binance, +16.3% on day 0 and −6.8% over +2 to +3. Ersoy's 60 Coinbase announcements through June 26, 2021 showed abnormal +16.8% over −1 through +1, +22.52% through +4, and −0.84% by +18, with little pre-event abnormal drift by his model. This does not contradict the gross raw run-up mechanically, but it does show baseline and window sensitivity. These are event studies, not real money or executable post-alert replication; no proof that buying on the listed venue after the news clears costs ([Li et al. manuscript](https://www.loyola.edu/_media/sellinger-business/documents/academics/departments/finance/wealth-management-conference/speakers/J_Li.pdf) and [published extended abstract](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.AFT.2025.11), October 6, 2025; [Ante and Meyer](https://d-nb.info/1232794554/34), accepted February 16, 2021; [Ersoy thesis](https://tspace.library.utoronto.ca/bitstream/1807/123489/1/Ersoy_Sedat_202206_PhD_thesis.pdf), 2022).
- MEASURED: Industry researcher Keyrock reports 16,000+ unlock events in 40 tokens, uses ±30 days and ETH normalization, and describes 90% as exerting downward pressure; team unlocks showed −25% in its headline, ecosystem unlocks +1.18%. This is retrospective price association, not new-information surprise, causal impact or an executable return; exact sample dates and independent replication were not verified. Anticipation and overlapping vesting are reasons not to buy or short a schedule blindly. As a spot rule, treat large scheduled cliff risk as a possible exclusion filter, not alpha ([Keyrock's original unlock study](https://keyrock.com/from-locked-to-liquidity-what-16000-token-unlocks-teach-us/), no printed date found).
- MEASURED: Liu et al. modeled Hop's June 2022 airdrop by subtracting observed fees from theoretical rewards: 104 of 150 flagged groups (69.3%) would have positive profits under that counterfactual, but their qualifying bridge addresses were actually excluded from the airdrop. LayerZero's May 2024 snapshot is a separate sample with no equivalent modeled payoff reported. This is a retrospective case study, not a broad eligible-user or after-tax edge. Do not treat undisclosed, uncertain rewards as imminent yield; ordinary-income recognition can also differ from the investment sale clock ([Toward Resilient Airdrop Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.14316), March 18, 2025; [IRS digital assets](https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets), undated).
- MEASURED: Benedetti and Kostovetsky's published ICO dataset had 2,390 completed sales, nearly all capital raised since January 2017. The reported average ICO-to-first-market-open return was +179% over an average 16 days; the first 30 trading days showed gross +48% average buy-and-hold abnormal return. These are historical offer and trading prices, not proof of being allocated, not documented net of costs, and not a long-run forward result. The ECGI study following 523 successful ICO/STO issuers through 2022 reports 52 failed companies (10%) in the detailed sample versus 12% in its abstract; company survival does not tell token investor returns ([Digital Tulips?](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3182169), revised January 4, 2021; [The Long-Run Performance of Initial Coin Offerings](https://www.ecgi.global/sites/default/files/Paper:%20The%20long-run%20performance%20of%20Initial%20Coin%20Offerings.pdf), August 30, 2023).
- MEASURED: Momtaz's historical CoinMarketCap sample covers 2013–April 2018 and has 1,403 time series. Among the 495 ICOs with one-year data, gross mean buy-and-hold return was +207.3% and median −36.7%. At two years, just 302 observations remained; gross mean was +829.4% and median −30.5%. These are retrospective first-market tracking returns, not real-money allocations. No independent exact replication was verified. Benedetti and Kostovetsky's offer-to-open returns use a different sample and horizon. The shrinking denominator, unspecified delisting recovery, and mean/median gap make the means poor sizing inputs ([Momtaz, table III](https://w3rl.com/documents/202103The%20Pricing%20and%20Performance%20of%20Cryptocurrency.pdf), W3RL 2021–3; [journal version](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1351847X.2019.1647259), July 28, 2019).

### Memecoins, wallet copies, snipers and manipulation

- MEASURED: The September 2026 “Meme Coin Factories” preprint reports an approximately 1.02% overall Pump.fun graduation baseline across the first two years (January 14, 2024–January 14, 2026; 15.25 million recorded launches). Marino et al. observed 4,338 graduations of 655,770 September 1–October 1, 2025 launches, writes “approximately 0.63%,” and the printed counts divide to 0.66%. Another preprint found 1,651 fast graduations among 832,941 resolved records May 8–June 10, 2026 (0.198%), but its audited collection missed graduations after about six minutes. It explicitly calls this a lower bound. These are real on-chain/census or collection results, not investor PnL probabilities. Failure to graduate is not equivalent to a literal zero liquidation value ([Meme Coin Factories](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10246), September 9, 2026; [Marino et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1), February 2026; [Graduation Regime Windows](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02823v3), 2026 revision).
- MEASURED: CoinGecko's original wallet-level Pump.fun/PumpSwap analysis of realized PnL marked 69.92% of 5.22 million wallets as losing in June 2025 versus 26.72% of 3.14 million in April 2026. In April, 65.14% of all observed wallets made $1–$500, 2.77% $500–$1,000, 5.37% over $1,000; 25.23% lost $1–$500, 0.71% $500–$1,000, 0.78% over $1,000. Real on-chain data, not people or long-run account equity; gas, tax, off-platform trades, transfers and unrealized positions are not fully established in these tallies. The improvement prevents a claim that most exiting wallets always lose. It does not show positive expected return at a random entrant's matched dates or fills. Marino et al. also found that several top SOL net-sell rankings were sell-only wallets, warning against copying rankings that omit incoming inventory basis ([CoinGecko wallet analysis](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback), May 7, 2026; [underlying Dune dashboard](https://dune.com/coingecko_content/pump-fun-annual-trader-profitability), April 2024–April 2026; [Marino et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1), February 2026).
- MEASURED: A January 2026 copy-trading preprint reports one chronological test with +3% average modeled net copier return vs +14% on selected “smart money” wallets; simpler ranking baselines lost. This is a model simulation over part of 6,000 projects, with calendar dates/cost decomposition not published on the page and no independent/live replication. A November 2026 separate study simulated 10 windows from April 2024–September 2026; its retrospective rankings and target-exit threshold used whole-window information, and even its pseudo-online variant uses future wallet links. Marked inventory was often a major part of the paper PnL. This is not permission to buy an address's next trade at its price ([Resisting Manipulative Bots](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08641), January 2026; [Retrospective Team-Following Analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.11436v1), November 2026).
- MEASURED: Gafarov recorded September 26–30, 2026 Pump.fun data; on 51,912 tokens with at least two known early-group members, mean group per-token cash flow was +0.21 SOL vs +0.04 for timing/size-matched early buyers and −2.34 SOL for late buyers. Open holdings matter: even optimistic net-event-fee liquidation estimates still marked late buyers −2.16 SOL. These are observational group cashflows/marks, not individual net realized returns. On Ethereum, Daian et al. documented ordering competition and trading fees paid away; a July 2015–August 2023 Ethereum dataset replay found 53.1% of successful private front-run opportunities in one comparison were no longer profitable if charged expected public failed-gas costs. That counterfactual is not a measurement of Solana sniper returns. The observed advantage accrues to located early parties, creators, fast searchers and block-order intermediaries, not demonstrably to an ordinary follower ([Gafarov preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.39458), October 29, 2026; [Flash Boys 2.0](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.05234v1), April 2019; [Remeasuring MEV](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.17944v1), May 2024).
- MEASURED: In a January 2019 draft Li, Shin and Wang's 500 events (May 2017–August 2018) show a pre-alert insider's best reported sale result +18.44% vs +6.53% for a modeled outsider buying in the first 10 seconds and using the best sale delay; buying after 20 seconds generally lost. Assumes commission-free fills at VWAP as if the trader was the only seller, not actual fills. Xu and Livshits estimate +18% organizer returns, 199.52 BTC across 348 usable events from June 2018–February 2019. Their paper calls this “net profit” relative to acquisition, but no commission or participant-level matching is verified; follower losses are inferred as the transfer counterpart, not individually measured. Hamrick et al. matched 1,034 Discord and 3,767 Telegram signals January–July 2018. Median largest five-minute jumps were 3.5%/5.1%, measured anywhere in a window from 48 hours before to 48 hours after the signal because the authors could not rely on the signal time (corrected 2026-11-25: this is a peak, not a move a follower could capture after the alert), while median later minima were 41%/38% below the pre-pump price; neither is an attainable follower return. Qualitative wealth transfer is supported across studies, not a matched replication of the same rate. Avoid organized pumps, including nominally “early” entries ([Li, Shin and Wang full draft](https://abfer.org/media/abfer-events-2019/annual-conference/investment-finance/AC19P3078_Cryptocurrency_Pump-and-Dump_Schemes.pdf), January 2019; [published paper](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/abs/cryptocurrency-pumpanddump-schemes/97149DCD519BAF838F269F98DC76D682), April 4, 2025; [Xu and Livshits](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.10109v2), August 17, 2019; [Hamrick et al.](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10098691), 2018 sample).
- MEASURED: Cong et al. applied statistical tests to 448 million BTC/ETH/LTC/XRP trades on 29 exchanges July 9–November 3, 2019. For 26 classified unregulated exchanges, estimated fake volume averaged 70.85% equal-weighted and 77.50% volume-weighted without controls, versus under 5% in the regulated-exchange cross-validation. These estimates are historical, model-based, and do not designate current U.S. affiliates. The 2026 Pump.fun factory study separately found at least 4 million wash-trading proxy transactions in its two historically sampled transaction datasets. Prefer observable dollar depth and fills to reported volume or “trending” rank ([Cong et al.](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w30783/w30783.pdf), December 2022; [Meme Coin Factories](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10246), September 9, 2026).

### Size a lottery only if there is an edge

STATED: A simple repeated two-outcome Kelly model assumes a fraction \(f\) of wealth at stake, probability \(p\) of a net profit of \(b\) per dollar, and probability \(1-p\) of total loss. With no shorting the maximizing fraction is \(\max(0,[bp-(1-p)]/b)\), so there is no bet if the conservative after-cost edge is not positive. All-in with any positive probability of total loss has expected log growth of minus infinity. Fractional Kelly dampens estimation and drawdown risk but cannot turn negative expectancy positive. In real tokens the jackpot probability and realizable exit multiple are unknown, payouts are fat-tailed, and failure events can cluster across the same chain/venue. Do not plug in the historical top wallet or the graduation rate as \(p\) ([Thorp, The Kelly Criterion in Blackjack, Sports Betting, and the Stock Market](https://web.williams.edu/Mathematics/sjmiller/public_html/341/handouts/Thorpe_KellyCriterion2007.pdf), 2007).

STATED assessment: A practical barbell is a real separation of the core from a capped, disposable speculative budget, with hard limits at the sleeve and common-driver level, and no automatic refill after losses. It is a way to keep a losing sleeve from damaging the rest, not evidence for filling it. Leaving the cap unused is a valid outcome even if the user can afford to lose it ([Thorp's discussion of fractional sizing and error](https://web.williams.edu/Mathematics/sjmiller/public_html/341/handouts/Thorpe_KellyCriterion2007.pdf), 2007).

## Replay first, data needed, and what to rule out

1. **First replay: slow liquid-basket trend.** Fix one U.S. venue, one precommitted slow lookback and rebalance clock, a lagged dollar-depth/trading-history eligibility rule, and an explicit cash choice. Compare to the identical eligible basket held without the trend, BTC spot, and a top-ranked cross-sectional momentum basket under the same timing and costs. Set the signal from past data only, insert a full one-day separation as the default before evaluation fills, and use the first actual eligible quote thereafter, not the signal close. Test on untouched rolling periods with both stress and calm. Report cash outflows, compounded equity, benchmark-relative performance, worst exit, turnover, capacity, tax lots and fraction of gains from the top few trades; reject if the outcome depends on a chosen lookback, missing coins, ideal fee tiers or one winner.
2. **Next, listing events as a falsification replay only.** Record first public roadmap, formal announcement, deposits enabled, tradability and first firm two-sided quote separately with UTC precision. Ask what an Idaho account could buy before the listing, if anything. Enter at the first quote after a predeclared delivery/order-delay window on that eligible venue; use order-book-size-aware exits, market controls and all adverse launches. Do not buy in the simulation before the announcement or at an aggregated day's opening price merely because that is where a paper's return starts.
3. **The data that matters most: dead coins.** Maintain immutable snapshots of point-in-time product eligibility, all listings/delistings/halt/withdrawal notices, contract and chain IDs, symbol changes and migration ratios, timestamped actual trades/books and depth, fee/minimum-order history, benchmark and cash prices, and exit availability. Treat transfers or recoveries only when documented; bracket no-exit assets with zero liquidation and a documented realistic recovery sensitivity. Remove neither closed positions nor stale/no-bid assets. For on-chain hypotheses also require full creation/mint cohorts, failed transactions, actual fee schedules, reserves, transaction ordering, and wallets joined only with information public before decision time. A current top-coin list is not a historical universe.
4. **Rule out for real money now.** Naked launchpad baskets, pump calls, buy-the-alert market orders, leaderboard copies, sniper/MEV competition, high-turnover daily reversal, yield in place of cash, and apparent edges requiring untradeable shorts or idealized stop fills. Test a sizing/time-stop regime only as a risk overlay after proving an entry rule; no independent edge was found for the overlay. One credible net result in held-out periods would warrant more paper observation, not a forecast.
