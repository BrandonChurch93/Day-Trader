## What the power numbers mean

- MEASURED: for comparison with doc 155, the arithmetic below uses the two-sided 5% normal test, power = Phi(d - 1.959964) + Phi(-d - 1.959964), with d the assumed mean divided by its standard error. We reran it with Python's standard normal CDF. At zero edge it is 5%; the positive tail alone is 2.5%. A two-sided rejection in the negative direction is not a useful trading result.
- STATED assumption, not verified: d = annual Sharpe times sqrt(years) assumes the same strategy, costs, capital normalization, mean excess return and long-run variance in the target period, with independent daily portfolio returns or equivalent long-run variance. A one-day or one-hour sample count is not an independent trade count. Half effect holds noise fixed. Halving an old **net** mean is not the same as halving its gross mean then charging the same costs. If long-run variance doubles, divide d by sqrt(2).
- UNKNOWN: actual modern net effect, clustered noise, tradeable counts and joint pass chance for all four cash-account adaptations. The checks below are sensitivities, not the doc 117 joint pass screen. In particular, d = 0.25 is nowhere near a meaningful pass forecast: the two-sided power there is about 5.7%. Do not use it to clear a price gate.

## What $500 can actually place

- STATED, shared equities limits: [Alpaca account support](https://alpaca.markets/support/alpaca-cash-accounts) says it does not offer cash accounts; below $2,000 it uses limited-margin accounts, 1× buying power, and allows use of unsettled proceeds. [Alpaca short-sale rules](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/margin-and-short-selling) confirm the $2,000 broker minimum plus borrow availability. A literal cash account cannot borrow the short legs. In a true cash account, fully paid same-day long round trips are possible, but the same cash cannot be recycled into another completed round trip before settlement without independent money to cover it. [SEC cash-account bulletin](https://www.sec.gov/investor/alerts/cashaccounts.pdf) explains payment and freeriding; [SEC T+1 bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/new-t1-settlement-cycle-what-investors-need-know-investor-bulletin) gives the May 28, 2024 change for stocks and ETFs.
- STATED, WRONG blanket PDT claim: the old four-in-five designation was conditional on over 6% of margin-account trades, not a law giving everybody exactly three; $25,000 applied if designated. [Alpaca's change notice](https://alpaca.markets/blog/finra-retires-the-pdt-rule-introducing-alpacas-new-intraday-margin-framework/) says its new framework began June 4, 2026.
