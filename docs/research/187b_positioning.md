- STATED: [FINRA Website Terms](https://www.finra.org/terms-of-use) bar use of “any portion of the FINRA Website” “in conjunction with any machine learning, neural network, deep learning, predictive analytics or other artificial intelligence computer or software program.” They also prohibit creating a database absent express other terms. Distinct [FINRA API Terms](https://developer.finra.org/finra-api-terms-service) say not to “use any process other than the Licensed APIs to monitor or copy the Licensed Materials in bulk”; a licensed API still requires applicable Specific Terms for intended use, users and retention. Do not treat no-key files as an API exception.
- STATED: [Deribit FZE clause 2.10](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944532191645-Deribit-Exchange-Membership-Terms-Deribit-FZE) says market data and derived data are “for personal use only”; clause 27.1(i) says members will not “conduct any systematic or automated data collection activities (including without limitation scraping, data mining, data extraction and data harvesting) on our systems.” The [Panama client terms](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944471089437-Terms-of-Service-DRB-Panama-Inc) likewise include API data in their market-data definition and bar systematic or automated collection. These are member/client terms. The public endpoint does not establish the applicable grant for an Idaho nonmember; no logger without written clearance.
- STATED: Paid vendors do not fix the clock automatically. [Velo](https://docs.velo.xyz/api/) advertises shorter monthly than annual history. [Laevitas](https://www.laevitas.ch/) only clearly advertises historical API data in its enterprise plan. [CoinGlass](https://docs.coinglass.com/reference/plans-and-permissions) lists different monthly versus annual rates, and the short-window plans cap intraday history even when daily history is offered. No paid feed was sampled through authentication. Dated positions or venue clocks are not a first-release/vintage trail.
- STATED: [Brav et al.](https://www.ecgi.global/sites/default/files/2026-10/law-098-2008_the_returns_to_hedge_fund_activism.pdf) and [extended tables](https://people.duke.edu/~brav/HFactivism_March_2019.pdf) discuss event-window reactions, not a current after-fee 13D trade. [Sanders et al.](https://jareonline.org/articles/smart-money-the-forecasting-ability-of-cftc-large-traders-in-agricultural-futures-markets/) find little forecasting evidence in ten agricultural futures markets, not a test of financial TFF; years not in opened abstract. [Hoang and Baur](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/fut.22144) find option implied volatility helps forecast 7–15 day BTC volatility, not return direction or an interpolated-skew trade. [Pan and Poteshman](https://haas.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/volume.pdf) find one-day to one-week predictive evidence from Cboe buyer-initiated opening options volume in 1990–2001, not raw open interest; no trading costs deducted.
