# Day Trading US Stocks: What the Evidence Says Works (and Does Not)

Prepared November 24, 2026. Scope: retail-scale, automated day trading and short-horizon trading of US equities and equity ETFs.

Evidence tags used throughout:

- MEASURED: independent empirical data (peer-reviewed or regulator/audited data) or official rule text.
- STATED: claimed by an author, vendor, or promoter (including authors' own unreplicated backtests, blogs, and non-peer-reviewed independent replications) without independent verification.
- UNKNOWN: could not be verified.

Bottom line up front: the base rate for retail day traders is dismal and well measured. A handful of intraday effects are real in peer-reviewed data, but the most-cited "profitable day trading" papers are authors' own backtests, and the independent replications we found show large decay after publication and fragility to realistic costs. The most defensible strategy families for a small automated account are lower-turnover, cost-aware, rules-based approaches (trend/time-series momentum, volatility-managed exposure, and narrowly scoped, conditional intraday trend on the most liquid ETFs), not discretionary-style stock picking by an LLM.

0DTE options (2023-2026 literature):

- [Beckmeyer, Branger, Gayda](https://wp.lancs.ac.uk/fofi2024/files/2024/04/FoFI-2024-146-Leander-Gayda.pdf) find over 75% of retail S&P 500 option trades are 0DTE and retail lost an average of $241,000 per day from Feb 2021 to Sep 2023 (over $125 million total), rising to $350,000 per day after Cboe introduced daily expirations on May 16, 2022; the estimates exclude broker commissions and regulatory and clearing fees, so they are conservative. Tag: STATED (conference working paper, not peer-reviewed).
- [Bogousslavsky and Muravyev (2024)](https://www.lsu.edu/business/files/event-files/2025-finance-mardi-gras/retail_option_trading_v2.pdf) find 0DTE trades lose about 4.6% per trade while other option trades earn roughly zero, so retail option losses are concentrated in 0DTE. Tag: MEASURED.
- [de Silva, So, Smith (MIT)](https://ide.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Retail_Options.pdf) estimate retail option buyers lose 5-9% around earnings announcements on average (10-14% for high expected-volatility events), about $3 billion in their sample. Tag: MEASURED.
- Counterpoint: [Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/0-dt-es-decoded-positioning-trends-and-market-impact/) says over 95% of 0DTE trades are limited-risk structures and characterizes retail as disciplined. Tag: STATED (exchange that profits from volume).

US regulators publish no percentage. The [SEC's day trading alert](https://www.sec.gov/files/investor/pubs/daytips.htm) states day traders "typically suffer severe financial losses in their first months of trading, and many never graduate to profit-making status." Tag: MEASURED (official statement, no number).

Important 2026 rule change: on April 14, 2026 the SEC approved FINRA's replacement of the pattern day trader rules, eliminating the 4-trades-in-5-days designation and the $25,000 minimum equity requirement in favor of intraday margin standards ([Federal Register notice, Release 34-105226](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/17/2026-07485/self-regulatory-organizations-financial-industry-regulatory-authority-inc-notice-of-filing-of)). The effective date is June 4, 2026 with a phase-in ending October 20, 2027 ([FINRA Firm Element Focus Q3 2026](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-09/Q3-2026-FEF.pdf); [FINRA Regulatory Notice 26-10](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10)). Tag: MEASURED. Practical implication: a small account can now day trade more freely, but whether a given broker has already switched off PDT logic during the phase-in is UNKNOWN and must be checked per broker. Removing the gate removes a guardrail, not the base rate.

Systematic independent test of common intraday signals: [Mesfin (2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.04004) evaluated 14 OHLCV intraday momentum families (including ORB) on micro futures (primarily MNQ) 2021-2025 with instrument-specific friction and walk-forward out-of-sample tests; none passed, and 11 of 14 had gross edge below a 2-point round-trip friction floor. Tag: STATED (independent preprint, not peer-reviewed), but it is the most methodologically careful recent test we found.

Publication decay in general: across 97 published predictors, returns were 26% lower out-of-sample and 58% lower post-publication ([McLean and Pontiff 2016, Journal of Finance](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12365)). Tag: MEASURED. All of the Zarattini/Aziz papers were published 2023-2024 and are widely circulated among retail traders, which is exactly the condition under which McLean-Pontiff predicts decay.

Conflict-of-interest note: Andrew Aziz is affiliated with Bear Bull Traders, a day-trading education community whose site hosts the authors' promotional slides ([Bear Bull Traders presentation](https://bearbulltraders.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Money_Show_Final.pdf)); the same slides show losing years for the SPY strategy (2016 about -12.8%, 2017 about -6.9%). This does not make the results wrong, but it is why they are tagged STATED.

## 3. Why most day traders fail

- Costs scale with turnover. Basic high-turnover anomaly strategies, before cost mitigation, cost more than 1% per month and their net spreads mostly vanish, while anomalies with under 50% monthly turnover largely survive ([Novy-Marx and Velikov](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w20721/w20721.pdf)). Tag: MEASURED.
- Fees convert gross skill into net losses. Heavy Taiwan day traders were profitable before costs but lost money after commissions and transaction taxes ([Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean](http://www.econ.yale.edu/~shiller/behfin/2004-04-10/barber-lee-liu-odean.pdf)). Tag: MEASURED.
- Adverse selection against faster traders. In E-mini S&P futures, the median HFT firm earned an annualized Sharpe of 4.3 with revenue concentrated in a few fastest firms ([Baron, Brogaard, Kirilenko, CFTC working paper](https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/idc/groups/public/@economicanalysis/documents/file/oce_riskandreturn0414.pdf)), and relative latency explains large differences in HFT performance ([JFQA 2019](https://hagstromer.org/publications/)). An agent polling the market periodically and calling an LLM is many orders of magnitude slower. Tag: MEASURED.
- Attention-driven trading loses. Top stocks bought by Robinhood users had average 20-day abnormal returns of -4.7% ([Barber, Huang, Odean, Schwarz 2022](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jofi.13183)). A "stocks in the news" screener fed to an LLM risks reproducing this. Tag: MEASURED.
- Overconfidence and persistence. Overconfidence explains excessive trading ([Barber and Odean 2000](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/returns/individual_investor_performance_final.pdf)); in Taiwan, unprofitable day traders persist and account for about 72% of day-trading volume ([Barber et al., Learning Fast or Slow](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2019/preliminary/paper/ZKnGb4Zh)). Tag: MEASURED.
- Leverage. The SEC notes day trading strategies "demand using the leverage of borrowed money," which is "why many day traders lose all their money and may end up in debt" ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/investor/pubs/daytips.htm)). Most headline Zarattini results rely on leverage or 3x ETFs ([ORB paper](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Can-Day-Trading-Really-Be-Profitable-Evidence-of-in-Zarattini-Aziz/4d55f526cc56f08662cb8976796cd3b719ef6d2b); [VWAP paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=4631351)). Tag: MEASURED / STATED respectively.

## 4. Backtest pitfalls

- Multiple testing. A new factor needs t greater than 3.0, and "most claimed research findings in financial economics are likely false" ([Harvey, Liu, Zhu 2016, RFS](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20592)). Tag: MEASURED.
- Overfitting budget. With five years of daily data, no more than about 45 strategy variations should be tried, or a Sharpe of 1.0 or more is likely by chance even if the true Sharpe is zero ([Bailey et al.](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/overfit-tools-at.pdf)). Tag: MEASURED (mathematical result).
- Deflated Sharpe and PBO. The Deflated Sharpe Ratio corrects for selection under multiple testing and non-normal returns ([Bailey and Lopez de Prado](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf)); the Probability of Backtest Overfitting measures how often the in-sample winner underperforms the median out of sample ([Bailey et al.](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf)). An LLM that iterates on strategy ideas is a multiple-testing engine; every trial must be logged. Tag: MEASURED.
- Unrealistic fills. The SPY momentum paper assumes $0.001/share slippage ([paper](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/content)); the VWAP reproduction breaks even below about 1 bp round trip ([QuantConnect](https://www.quantconnect.com/terminal/cache/embedded_backtest_b115d0894231d55994b1068202f6c0ae.html)). Stop-market entries on breakouts fill at the worst moment. Tag: STATED.
- Look-ahead and survivorship. "Stocks in play" relative volume must be computed only from bars already closed; stock universes must include delisted names; earnings/news timestamps must be point-in-time. The dot-com collapse reported by QuantConnect commenters shows regime dependence ([QuantConnect](https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/p1)). Tag: STATED.
- Structural change. Intraday momentum is linked to dealer and leveraged-ETF gamma hedging and is absent when gamma is not short ([Baltussen et al.](https://pure.eur.nl/en/publications/hedging-demand-and-market-intraday-momentum/)); 0DTE growth plausibly changed that hedging, but the direction is unproven ([literature review](https://github.com/Mbaka11/OpeningRangeTradingStrategy/blob/master/research/phase2-improvement/step11-literature-review.md)). Tag: MEASURED mechanism, UNKNOWN current sign.

## 5. Swing trading, longer horizons, and realistic edge

- Time-series momentum (trend) is the best-documented systematic effect at multi-week to 12-month horizons: significant for each of 58 liquid futures, with returns persisting 1-12 months and partially reversing later ([Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen 2012, JFE](https://research.cbs.dk/da/publications/time-series-momentum/)). Its strength in individual stocks is debated ([Huang et al., "Time-series momentum: Is it there?"](https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?params=/context/lkcsb_research/article/7520/&path_info=Time_series_momentum_JFE_sv.pdf)), but a stock-level study finds strong TSMOM in US stocks 1927-2017 ([Lancaster](https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/id/eprint/128366/1/Time_Series_Momentum_in_Nearly_100_Years_of_Stock_Returns.pdf)). Tag: MEASURED.
- Lower turnover survives costs. Anomalies with under 50% monthly turnover mostly survive costs, while high-turnover ones mostly do not ([Novy-Marx and Velikov](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w20721/w20721.pdf)). Tag: MEASURED.
- Holding overnight captures the premium. Momentum and reversal premia accrue overnight ([Lou, Polk, Skouras](https://personal.lse.ac.uk/polk/research/TugOfWar.pdf)), so swing horizons are structurally favored over flat-at-close for those signals. Tag: MEASURED.

Realistic expected edge for a small retail account: after accounting for McLean-Pontiff decay of roughly half for published signals and Novy-Marx-Velikov costs, a credible net target for a diversified systematic approach is a modest improvement in risk-adjusted return over buy-and-hold (Sharpe in the 0.5-1.0 range out of sample), not the 2-3 Sharpe and 20-40%/yr shown in author backtests. That range is our synthesis (STATED, our inference), consistent with the VWAP reproduction's out-of-sample Sharpe decaying toward about 0.7 ([QuantConnect](https://www.quantconnect.com/terminal/cache/embedded_backtest_b115d0894231d55994b1068202f6c0ae.html)). Any backtest showing Sharpe above 2 after costs from an LLM-designed rule should be presumed overfit until it passes a deflated-Sharpe test.

## 6. Risk management evidence

- Fractional Kelly. Full Kelly maximizes long-run growth but its bets "may be very large" and it is "very risky in the short term"; betting exactly twice Kelly reduces growth to the risk-free rate; long-term investors should bet Kelly or less ([MacLean, Thorp, Ziemba](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/157/Papers/Good_Bad_Kelly.pdf)). Because edge estimates are noisy and decay, overestimating the edge pushes you toward over-Kelly. Use a small fraction (quarter-Kelly or less) of an edge estimate that is itself haircut for decay (our recommendation). Tag: MEASURED theory.
- Stop losses. Under a random walk, simple stop-loss rules always reduce expected return; they add value only when returns exhibit momentum ([Kaminski and Lo](https://econpapers.repec.org/paper/hhssifrwp/0063.htm)). In 1950-2004 data, certain stop-loss rules added 50-100 bps/month during stop-out periods ([Kaminski and Lo, MIT](https://dspace.mit.edu/bitstream/handle/1721.1/114876/Lo_When%20Do%20Stop-Loss.pdf?sequence=1)). So stops fit trend strategies and hurt mean-reversion strategies. Tag: MEASURED.
- Drawdown control. If you want to never lose more than a fixed fraction of peak wealth, the optimal policy invests in proportion to the cushion above the floor ([Grossman and Zhou 1993](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-9965.1993.tb00044.x)); adjusting risk aversion with realized drawdown controls drawdowns with little efficiency loss ([Boyd et al., Stanford](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/pdf/multiperiod_portfolio_drawdown.pdf)). Tag: MEASURED theory.
- Volatility targeting. The SPY intraday paper's base version earned about 6.2%/yr with Sharpe 0.61 per a secondary review ([literature review summary](https://github.com/Mbaka11/OpeningRangeTradingStrategy/blob/master/research/phase2-improvement/step11-literature-review.md)), versus Sharpe 1.33 for the full version with VWAP trailing stops and volatility-scaled sizing capped at 4x ([CXO Advisory](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/momentum-investing/complex-intraday-time-series-momentum-strategy-applied-to-spy/)). Tag: STATED.
- Daily loss limits: we found no peer-reviewed study showing hard daily loss limits improve expected returns for retail. They are justified as operational circuit breakers (bugs, runaway agents), not as alpha. Tag: UNKNOWN for return impact.

| 5 (speculative) | ORB on "stocks in play" | STATED only ([Zarattini et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4729284)); no cost-inclusive independent multi-year replication found | Realistic slippage on stop-market entries in volatile names; point-in-time relative-volume calc; survives 2000-2002 and 2008 out of sample; paper trade at least 6 months before capital |

Explicitly not recommended: buying 0DTE options (MEASURED losses), chasing attention/news momentum (MEASURED losses), any sub-minute or spread-dependent scalping (HFT adverse selection), and leveraged/3x ETF versions of the above.

## Implications for our build

1. Start with the base rate. Treat "the agent loses money" as the default; the system's job is to prove otherwise with logged out-of-sample evidence before scaling capital.
2. Let deterministic code generate signals, not the LLM. Use the cheap monitoring agents for data hygiene, anomaly and regime detection, and risk checks; reserve the expensive model for regime/allocation decisions and post-mortems. Trade-level discretion by an LLM is untested and multiplies the multiple-testing problem.
3. Prefer swing/daily horizons. Families 1-2 above have the strongest evidence and tolerate the latency of periodic polling; true intraday edges depend on cost and latency the architecture cannot match.
4. Make costs first-class. Model spread plus slippage of at least 2-5 bps per side for liquid ETFs and more for single stocks; reject any strategy whose break-even cost is under 2x the modeled cost.
5. Keep a trial ledger. Log every strategy variant the agents try, compute Deflated Sharpe and PBO on the full ledger, require t above 3 ([Harvey, Liu, Zhu](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20592)), and stay inside a variations budget (about 45 for five years of daily data).
6. Size conservatively. Volatility-target each position; cap at quarter-Kelly of a decay-haircut edge; use a Grossman-Zhou style drawdown cushion (for example, cut exposure linearly as drawdown approaches 15-20%); add hard daily loss and order-count circuit breakers as operational safety.
7. Test strategy stop losses for trend strategies; do not assume they help mean reversion. Keep separate system-level loss and exposure limits regardless.
8. Check the new FINRA intraday margin regime with the chosen broker; the $25,000 PDT gate is gone as of June 4, 2026 but phase-in runs to October 20, 2027.
9. Paper trade for 6-12 months, then go live at small size with pre-written kill criteria (for example, live Sharpe below 0 after 12 months or drawdown beyond the backtest's worst by 50%).
