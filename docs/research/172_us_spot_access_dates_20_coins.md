# Plan 75: US dollar spot access for 20 coins

This price-free review covers January 1, 2020 through October 31, 2026 UTC, with the 2020–2023 repeat reviewed first and the later part preliminary. Scope is US retail outside New York, in a US state where Coinbase offered standard spot trading throughout the period. Hawaii is also excluded because of its [2017 to August 2024 service gap](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2024/08/13/coinbase-returns-to-hawaii-seven-years-after-exiting-market). The off-repo interval ledger preserves quotes, opened links, evidence status and UTC-day decision flags. This is not an approved eligibility receipt.

The flags in the ledger are `eligible` and `sell_allowed` at the UTC decision open. Unproved states are ineligible; unresolved sale permission is not a liquidation instruction. Limit-only permits matching but forbids market orders, so it is different from cancel-only, post-only, a halt or a sell-only window. The latter was not found in this review. A dated full-mode notice is not proof of nonstop operation; no exhaustive venue-wide outage history was established. On Oct 25, 2025, Coinbase confirmed [cancel-only then downtime on X](https://x.com/CoinbaseMarkets/status/1982103530505715830), followed by [recovery reporting](https://www.rootdata.com/news/403801). A [supplemental Coinbase Reddit notice](https://www.reddit.com/r/Coinbase/comments/1oe9arv/coinbase_system_maintenance_1025/) puts recovery at 23:15 UTC, before the next open; the recovery report alone does not fix the UTC clock.
