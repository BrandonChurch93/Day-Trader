# Plan 75: US dollar spot access for 20 coins

This price-free review covers January 1, 2020 through October 31, 2026 UTC, with the 2020–2023 repeat reviewed first and the later part preliminary. Scope is US retail outside New York, in a US state where Coinbase offered standard spot trading throughout the period. Hawaii is also excluded because of its [2017 to August 2024 service gap](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2024/08/13/coinbase-returns-to-hawaii-seven-years-after-exiting-market). The off-repo interval ledger preserves quotes, opened links, evidence status and UTC-day decision flags. This is not an approved eligibility receipt.

PROVED requires a dated full-trading notice. INFERRED uses the dated USD-book opening or a scheduled opening followed by evidence of trading there, then waits seven UTC calendar days. UNKNOWN admits nothing and includes known non-admitting intervals, distinguished by the ledger's access state. No inferred restoration after removal is allowed.

The 2020–2023 repeat contains 29,220 coin-days: 1,112 PROVED, 17,100 INFERRED, and 11,008 UNKNOWN or otherwise not admitted. Mean admitted names per UTC day: 12.4654, before any price coverage or warm-up checks. The outstanding product-specific gaps that could change a holding are BNB's USD-book opening and ZEC's full-mode restoration.

The flags in the ledger are `eligible` and `sell_allowed` at the UTC decision open. Repeat flags are binary; non-admitted days are 0/0 unless the ledger establishes a separate sell permission. Limit-only permits matching but forbids market orders, so it is different from cancel-only, post-only, a halt or a sell-only window. The latter was not found in this review. A dated full-mode notice is not proof of nonstop operation; no exhaustive venue-wide outage history was established. On Oct 25, 2025, Coinbase confirmed [cancel-only then downtime on X](https://x.com/CoinbaseMarkets/status/1982103530505715830), followed by [recovery reporting](https://www.rootdata.com/news/403801). A [supplemental Coinbase Reddit notice](https://www.reddit.com/r/Coinbase/comments/1oe9arv/coinbase_system_maintenance_1025/) puts recovery at 23:15 UTC, before the next open; the recovery report alone does not fix the UTC clock.

For the later stretch only, the Sep 21–22, 2026 [Advanced Trade incident](https://alphawire.xyz/markets/crypto-ecosystem/coinbase-outage-trading-balance-access-restored/) overlapped the Sep 22 UTC open, but affected some customers and was not proved venue-wide. No modeled removal and no effect on the repeat hash ([Coinbase Support incident notice](https://x.com/CoinbaseSupport/status/2102182094512197883), [resolution](https://x.com/CoinbaseSupport/status/2102193567447896455)).
