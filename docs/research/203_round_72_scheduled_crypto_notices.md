# Round 72: Scheduled Crypto Notices and Mechanical Flows on US Retail Spot Venues

**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Scope:** Idea generation and venue-first screening for a $300 to $500 US retail spot account (Binance.US spot, Kraken spot, Gemini spot; no leverage, long-only).
**Result:** Nothing registered. None of the eight scheduled-notice categories clears our test bar for a standalone long-only strategy over days to weeks.

## Short Answer

An always-on agent that watches every US exchange notice feed across all coins and all hours has a clear advantage for defensive risk control, which this repo already uses through its live Kraken pair-delisting monitor and AlphaGrowth unlock logger (`docs/QUEUE.md` rows 36, 72, and 84, STATED). Turning that same breadth into a standalone long-only profit strategy over days to weeks on a $300 to $500 US spot account fails on all eight candidate categories (INFERENCE).

Three structural barriers block the entire set:

- Entry-tier US spot fees on Kraken Pro (40 bp maker, 80 bp taker per side; 160 bp round-trip taker) and Gemini ActiveTrader (60 bp maker, 120 bp taker per side; 240 bp round-trip taker) absorb most scheduled-notice dislocations (`docs/research/60_us_venue_costs_checked.md`, STATED).
- Binance.US has low stated fees (0 bp maker, 2 bp taker per side; `docs/research/60_us_venue_costs_checked.md`, STATED), but its public announcement archive shows very thin annual event counts: 21 delisting notices across June 2022 to August 2026 (~4.5 notices/year), 17 token migration/swap/rebranding notices total (14 distinct events), and 5 network migration notices total ([Binance.US Announcements](https://support.binance.us/en/collections/10384537-announcements), MEASURED).
- Higher-frequency operational notices (wallet pauses and maintenance windows) revert in minutes to hours rather than days to weeks ([Morin and Moore 2023](https://tylermoore.utulsa.edu/wacco23arbitrage.pdf), STATED), while higher-frequency multi-day schedules (cliff unlocks) show near-zero 3-day abnormal return across 913 Binance events ([Albiez 2026](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7567460), STATED) and post-unlock momentum fade rather than rebound across 846 unlocks ([Tokenomist 2026](https://tokenomist.ai/research/what-the-market-does-around-a-token-unlock), STATED).

No plan is registered from this round. If the owner wants one zero-price, count-only check before closing this angle completely, the cheapest check is counting how many coins delisted by Kraken US/Canada or Binance.US in 2024 to 2026 stayed listed on the other permitted US spot venue at the deadline (Candidate 1).

## Candidate 1: Single-Venue US Delisting Forced-Sell Overshoot on a Surviving Venue

**Verdict:** Rank 1, WATCH (count-only overlap check only; not ready for a plan). Note that buying on the delisting venue was already skipped/killed in Round 69 Candidate 5 and Round 70 Candidate 3, and protective exit monitoring already runs in Queue rows 72 and 84 (`docs/QUEUE.md`, STATED). The only untested long-only sub-angle is buying on a surviving US venue after a regional or single-venue delisting deadline.

- **Mechanism and forced trader:** When one US venue announces a regional or compliance delisting with a fixed halt timestamp, spot holders who cannot or will not self-custody and margin holders facing automatic liquidation must sell before the deadline. On Kraken, the November 23, 2026 US and Canada delisting notice sets a hard deadline of December 9, 2026 at 15:00 UTC when deposits, trading, and margin disable and all remaining open margin positions are forcibly closed ([Kraken US and Canada Delistings](https://support.kraken.com/articles/us-ca-delisting), STATED). A long-only account on a surviving US venue (for example Binance.US when Kraken US/Canada delists a coin, or Kraken when Binance.US delists a coin) would buy after forced liquidation finishes and hold for days to two weeks for global-price reversion (INFERENCE).
- **Primary sources opened:** [Kraken US and Canada Delistings](https://support.kraken.com/articles/us-ca-delisting) (16 assets delisted Dec 9, 2026 with forced margin close), [Kraken Status](https://status.kraken.com/) (August 2026 delisting of 21 assets with trading disabled Sept 11, 2026 and withdrawals Dec 10, 2026), and [Binance.US Announcements](https://support.binance.us/en/collections/10384537-announcements) (21 delisting notices covering 33 named assets from June 19, 2022 to August 19, 2026).
- **Events per year on permitted US venues:** Binance.US averaged ~4.5 delisting notices (~8 coins) per year across 2022 to 2026 (MEASURED). Kraken delisted 37 assets across its August and November 2026 notices alone (MEASURED). However, the cross-listed overlap where a coin delisted on one permitted US venue remains actively traded on another permitted US venue is UNKNOWN and likely much smaller (~3 to 15 coins/year, INFERENCE).
- **Realistic cost per side at $250:** On Binance.US as the surviving venue, 2 bp taker fee plus UNKNOWN thin-altcoin spread (likely 15 to 60+ bp per side, INFERENCE). On Kraken Pro as the surviving venue, 80 bp taker fee (40 bp maker) plus spread (~85 to 120+ bp per side, INFERENCE). On Gemini, ~122+ bp per side taker (STATED fee).
- **Historical data and terms:** Kraken downloadable OHLCVT (`assets.kraken.com`) allows private research without redistribution, with a section-9 automated-access caveat (`docs/research/194_data_source_terms_audit.md`, STATED). Binance.US public REST endpoints (`docs.binance.us`) require no key, though `data.binance.us` bars commercial use and full Terms of Use remain UNKNOWN (`docs/research/194_data_source_terms_audit.md`, STATED). Gemini Market Data Agreement permits personal internal analysis (`docs/research/194_data_source_terms_audit.md`, STATED). Historical delisting notice timestamps must be logged from [Binance.US Announcements](https://support.binance.us/en/collections/10384537-announcements) and Kraken support notices; full automated-collection terms on those Zendesk/Intercom help centers are UNKNOWN.
- **Why it does not clear the bar yet:** Adverse selection is severe. Coins delisted by one US venue often suffer permanent liquidity loss or subsequent delisting on the surviving US venue, and single-venue US forced selling is often small relative to global offshore volume (INFERENCE).
- **Single cheapest count-only first step (no returns):** Export the symbol list from the 2024 to 2026 Binance.US and Kraken US/Canada delisting notices and intersect it with current/historical symbol metadata on the other venue (Kraken `AssetPairs` and Binance.US `exchangeInfo`). Stop immediately if fewer than 30 cross-listed delisting events exist over 2024 to 2026.

## Candidate 2: Token Migrations, Contract Swaps, and Redenominations

**Verdict:** Rank 2, WATCH (too few events per year on permitted US venues to meet our sample-size bar).

- **Mechanism and forced trader:** During a token migration or ticker rebrand, exchanges freeze margin markets ahead of the swap and forcibly close open margin positions before halting old-ticker spot trading. On Kraken's RNDR to RENDER migration, RNDR/USD margin went Reduce Only on July 16, 2024 (12:00 UTC), all remaining open margin positions were forcibly closed on July 23, 2024 (12:00 UTC), and RNDR delisted/converted 1:1 to RENDER on July 29, 2024 (12:00 UTC) ([Kraken RNDR Migration](https://support.kraken.com/articles/kraken-to-support-rndr-migration-to-render), STATED). Non-1:1 redenominations or multi-day trading halts can also cause temporary retail confusion or post-relist dislocation (INFERENCE). However, many contract migrations do not even interrupt spot trading: Kraken's Telcoin (TEL) 1:1 Polygon migration pauses funding from November 30 to December 1 to 4, 2026 with zero spot trading interruption ([Kraken TEL Migration](https://support.kraken.com/articles/telcoin-tel-migration), STATED).
- **Primary sources opened:** [Binance.US Announcements](https://support.binance.us/en/collections/10384537-announcements) (17 migration/swap/rebranding notices covering 14 distinct events, including MATIC to POL, LOKA to A2Z, DAR to D, FTM to S, MKR to SKY, EOS to A, RNDR to RENDER, LSK, STG, REP, KNC, UST, IOTA, and POLY to POLYX), [Kraken RNDR Migration](https://support.kraken.com/articles/kraken-to-support-rndr-migration-to-render), [Kraken TEL Migration](https://support.kraken.com/articles/telcoin-tel-migration), and [Kraken Status](https://status.kraken.com/) (TEL and XION to VERONA migrations scheduled Nov 30, 2026).
- **Events per year on permitted US venues:** ~3 to 4 coins/year on Binance.US (17 notices / 14 distinct events across ~4 to 5 years, MEASURED) and an estimated ~3 to 6 coins/year across Kraken US and Gemini combined (INFERENCE; exact multi-year Kraken/Gemini count UNKNOWN). This is far below the 30+ independent-event floor in `docs/research/95_where_we_stand_after_32_tests.md` (STATED).
- **Realistic cost per side at $250:** Binance.US 2 bp taker plus ~12 to 47+ bp half-spread (RENDER/USDT single-snapshot spread was 23.4 bp, RENDER/USD 94.0 bp, MEASURED); Kraken Pro ~84 bp per side taker (40 bp maker plus half-spread); Gemini ~122+ bp per side taker (STATED fees).
- **Historical data and terms:** Same venue OHLCV terms as Candidate 1 (`docs/research/194_data_source_terms_audit.md`, STATED), plus ticker-mapping discontinuity risk because old and new symbols split across separate OHLCV files (INFERENCE).
- **Single cheapest count-only first step (no returns):** Count every 2023 to 2026 US-supported token migration across Binance.US Announcements and Kraken Support that included either a forced margin close or a spot trading halt of 24+ hours. Do not pull prices unless the count reaches at least 25 to 30 distinct US events.

## Candidate 3: Scheduled Deposit and Withdrawal Suspensions (Local Venue Discount)

**Verdict:** Rank 3, SKIP for a days-to-weeks US spot strategy (wrong holding horizon and prohibitive fees on Kraken/Gemini).

- **Mechanism and forced trader:** When an exchange suspends deposits and withdrawals for a coin while spot trading stays open, cross-venue arbitrageurs cannot send coins onto or off the venue. If local holders sell urgently into a locked wallet, the venue can trade at a temporary discount to global spot until wallets reopen. Conversely, if local buyers panic-buy during a fiat withdrawal freeze (as Bitfinex experienced in 2017 to 2019), the venue trades at a premium ([Crépellière, Pelster, and Zeisberger 2022](https://en.wiwi.uni-paderborn.de/fileadmin-wiwi/cetar/TAF_Working_Paper_Series/TAF_WP_067_CrepellierePelsterZeisberger2022_rev.pdf), STATED).
- **Primary sources opened:** [Morin and Moore 2023](https://tylermoore.utulsa.edu/wacco23arbitrage.pdf) (40 Bitfinex interruption events from 2015 to 2022: 68.5% of event periods coincided with arbitrage deviations versus 46.8% non-event, p = 1.58e-7, but median arbitrage duration was only 20 to 40 minutes and mean duration was 81 to 109 minutes, STATED), [Crépellière, Pelster, and Zeisberger 2022](https://en.wiwi.uni-paderborn.de/fileadmin-wiwi/cetar/TAF_Working_Paper_Series/TAF_WP_067_CrepellierePelsterZeisberger2022_rev.pdf) (18 fiat-to-crypto and 10 crypto-to-crypto exchanges, 2017 to 2021: average maximum cross-exchange price gap fell from 2.8% in 2017 to 2018 to 1.1% after 2019, and net cross-platform arbitrage returns turned negative after trading costs from April 2018 onward, STATED), [Kraken Status](https://status.kraken.com/) (5 current/announced deposit or withdrawal pause notices covering 6 assets plus 3 completed funding maintenance windows Nov 18 to 24, 2026, MEASURED), and [Gemini Status](https://status.gemini.com/) (2 deposit/withdrawal pauses Nov 18 to 23, 2026: ZEC scheduled maintenance and TRON temporary pause, MEASURED).
- **Events per year on permitted US venues:** High on Kraken and Gemini (dozens per year across all listed coins, extrapolating from 3 to 6 pauses per two weeks on [Kraken Status](https://status.kraken.com/) and 2 per two weeks on [Gemini Status](https://status.gemini.com/), INFERENCE). Binance.US annual wallet-pause count is UNKNOWN because Binance.US does not expose a historical status-incident archive on its announcements page.
- **Realistic cost per side at $250:** Kraken Pro 80 bp taker (40 bp maker) plus spread (~165+ bp round-trip taker) and Gemini 120 bp taker (60 bp maker) plus spread (~243+ bp round-trip taker) exceed the post-2019 1.1% average maximum cross-exchange price gap reported by [Crépellière, Pelster, and Zeisberger 2022](https://en.wiwi.uni-paderborn.de/fileadmin-wiwi/cetar/TAF_Working_Paper_Series/TAF_WP_067_CrepellierePelsterZeisberger2022_rev.pdf) (STATED/INFERENCE). Only Binance.US (2 bp taker) could clear small discounts, and its wallet-pause duration is typically hours rather than days (UNKNOWN exact).
- **Historical data and terms:** Statuspage incident logs on [Kraken Status](https://status.kraken.com/) and [Gemini Status](https://status.gemini.com/) show only recent ~14-day windows on the front page; neither page advertises a public RSS/JSON archive (MEASURED), and automated-collection terms are UNKNOWN. Testing requires synchronized 1-minute or 5-minute spot bars across two venues (`assets.kraken.com` OHLCVT + Binance.US REST, subject to `docs/research/194_data_source_terms_audit.md` limits).
- **Why it fails:** The reversion horizon is minutes to hours (median 20 to 40 minutes in [Morin and Moore 2023](https://tylermoore.utulsa.edu/wacco23arbitrage.pdf)), which breaks our days-to-weeks holding-period rule and requires fast execution rather than broad attention.
- **Single cheapest count-only first step (no returns):** Log [Kraken Status](https://status.kraken.com/) and [Gemini Status](https://status.gemini.com/) daily for 30 days and count only pauses that last more than 24 hours while spot trading remains open. Skip permanently if fewer than 1 to 2 multi-day pauses per month occur.

## Candidate 4: Large Scheduled Token Unlocks (What Is New Beyond Our Running Logger)

**Verdict:** Rank 4, WATCH ONLY via our existing forward logger (`docs/QUEUE.md` row 36 on AlphaGrowth); do not register a new plan.

- **Mechanism and forced trader:** Venture, team, or ecosystem vesting cliffs release locked tokens on a known calendar date. A long-only US retail account cannot short pre-unlock supply; the only long-only angle is buying a post-unlock rebound after forced or early-recipient selling finishes (INFERENCE).
- **What is new since Round 69 (primary sources opened):** Two large-sample October and November 2026 studies weaken the post-unlock rebound thesis sharply. [Albiez (October 2026, SSRN 7567460)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7567460) tested 913 scheduled cliff unlocks on Binance and measured a 3-day market-model abnormal return of -0.14% (95% CI: -0.81% to +0.53%), finding no statistically detectable price drop and no scaling with unlock size or illiquidity (STATED). [Tokenomist (November 17, 2026)](https://tokenomist.ai/research/what-the-market-does-around-a-token-unlock) analyzed 846 unlocks across 239 coins (January 2022 to October 2026) against 1,426 ordinary dates and found that volatility rises 7 to 8 percentage points before the unlock and fades over two weeks, while momentum fades in the week after the unlock (MACD turning down on 34% of coins versus 27% on ordinary days; RSI falling 2 to 4 points and staying lower for the rest of the month), with no price rebound reported (STATED). By contrast, [Kim (April 2026, SSRN 6632838)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6632838) reported a -16.97% mean 0 to 72-hour return across 52 hand-collected Binance unlocks (2023 to 2025), but explicitly labeled the sample preliminary and correlational and did not report a post-72-hour rebound (STATED).
- **Events per year on permitted US venues:** High globally (846 unlocks over ~4.8 years in [Tokenomist 2026](https://tokenomist.ai/research/what-the-market-does-around-a-token-unlock), STATED), but exact count of large (>5% float) cliff unlocks listed on Binance.US or Kraken US is UNKNOWN until our existing AlphaGrowth logger accumulates a full year.
- **Realistic cost per side at $250:** Binance.US 2 bp taker plus ~10 to 50 bp half-spread; Kraken Pro ~82 to 100+ bp taker per side (STATED fees, INFERENCE spreads).
- **Historical data and terms:** AlphaGrowth unlock feed (terms at alphagrowth.io/terms, allowed with credit per `docs/research/194_data_source_terms_audit.md`, STATED) is already logging forward events in `docs/QUEUE.md` row 36. Historical unlock calendars from Tokenomist or other paid providers are not audited here (terms UNKNOWN).
- **Single cheapest count-only first step (no returns):** Keep the existing AlphaGrowth forward logger running and cross-tag each logged unlock with whether the token is tradable on Binance.US or Kraken US (zero price pulls). Do not open a study until the logger counts at least 30 large US-tradable cliff unlocks.
