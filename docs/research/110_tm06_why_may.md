# TM06: why May happened

MEASURED. The six picker models (TM06-P, [page 102](102_time_machine_tm06_six_models.md)) bought coins that were already at the top of the screen they were shown. May 2026 was the month in which that paid by far the most: the plain top_7d_rise yardstick, which holds the five coins with the biggest 7-day rise at each daily wake, made +55.2% in May on the same coins the models held, against +10.8% for holding all 20 coins and -6.8% for bitcoin. The yardstick's next-day lead over the 20-coin average was +1.29 percentage points a day in May and between -0.52 and +0.41 in the other seven months. The yardstick also made money in Apr, Aug and Sep, but it beat holding all 20 coins only in Apr, May and Aug: by 44.4 percentage points in May against at most 10.0 in the others.

MEASURED. Of 1,126 numbers the models wrote in their May reasons, 1,094 match that coin's screen at that wake and 23 match another coin on the same screen. The other 9 are ratios worked out from screen counts, one portfolio weight and one daily-bar look. The screens for April to June carried 0 headlines, and no May reason names an outside event (0 event words found).

Verdict: screen-based for all six. Memory is not ruled out. A model that remembered May could still pick the right coins and justify them with screen numbers, and each model's knowledge line for these months is UNKNOWN. One pick is thin: GPT-6.2 Sol bought XLM on 2026-05-27, when it was the number 1 24-hour riser on a red day, "on 1.29 times turnover while most coins fell". It rose 31% the next day. That single pick cannot be told apart from foresight.

## July to November

MEASURED. The models kept the same habit: most adds were coins already near the top of the 24-hour list (median 24-hour rank by model and month between 1 and 5). What changed was the market: the yardstick's next-day lead fell to +0.03 (Jul), +0.41 (Aug), +0.12 (Sep), -0.04 (Oct), -0.52 (Nov), so buying what had just risen stopped paying.

## Verdict and limits

- Screen-based for all six models. The reasons cite screen values, the buys follow visible moves, and the mechanical screen rule made most of the same money in May.
- Memory not ruled out. UNKNOWN: each model's knowledge line for April to November 2026. A strong spring and a weak summer to autumn is also what a spring knowledge line would produce. TM07 runs on weeks after the stated knowledge lines and is the test that can separate the two.
- One thin pick named: GPT-6.2 Sol's XLM buy on 2026-05-27.
- Next-day leads are gross and one wake ahead; they say which way the picks leaned, not what a trader would net.
- The number check is a tolerance match. A number that is on the screen could still have been chosen for another reason.
