## The rule

STATED, from the [frozen plan](../../research_rig/PREREG_53_taxable_cef_tax_loss.md), [cash amendment](../../research_rig/AMENDMENT_53_CASH.md) and [identity amendment](../../research_rig/AMENDMENT_53_JUMPS.md). Use the latest eligible public PCEF holdings before the December cutoff, excluding municipal fund-years. At the second-to-last December close, rank complete, affordable, unsplit-through-entry funds by raw price return from the first January close. Buy the worst 5, each in a $100 whole-share sleeve at the last December close, and sell at the last January close. Cash is credited on eligible ex-dates, not reinvested; idle cash earns nothing. PCEF is one $500 whole-share sleeve. The equal-weight roster averages all eligible $100 sleeves.

## Costs as charged

STATED. Each side used the measured closing half-spread plus 5 bp, with SIP quotes from 15:54:00 through 15:59:00 New York time, a 60-second quote life and a 180-second minimum cover. Short cover uses a 50 bp half-spread proxy plus the same side fee. A missing exit carries the last usable close, charging the larger of the entry half-spread and the proxy, plus the side fee. There is no separate commission or tax charge.

MEASURED. 9 held sides had short quote cover. Compared with zero cost at the same whole-share sizes, the charged costs reduced mean portfolio return by 0.36 percentage points, PCEF by 0.24 percentage points and the roster by 0.38 percentage points.
