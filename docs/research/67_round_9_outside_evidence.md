## Leveraged ETF close flows

- DROP as pitched; same-side U.S. late-session continuation and auction-to-open reversal are different claims.
- MEASURED: Princeton researcher Yinhong Zhao's preliminary preprint reports +122.43 bp gross and +117.51 bp after only the entry half-spread for 46 post-launch Korean name-days, from May 27 through the native data ending June 30, 2026; it forms a signal at 15:10 local, enters with the flow from 15:11 and exits at the next printed open, not a fade entered at the closing auction ([Zhao, Table A18](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03703v1)).
- MEASURED: Closing-auction exit for the with-flow rule lost 16.84 bp after that cost; the +117.51 bp result had a reported t-statistic of 1.78, and the U.S. Nvidia/AMD differential was +0.04 with a t-statistic of 0.30; exact U.S. strategy profits cannot be inferred ([Zhao preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03703v1)).
- STATED: The $50 billion peak is an estimate without a disclosed peak-day reconstruction in the preprint; paid-research publisher Concretum maps 804 funds from March 2017 to November 2026 but the free preview omits strategy performance ([Zhao preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03703v1), [Concretum preview](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/do-leveraged-etfs-fuel-intraday-trends)).
- MEASURED: Separately, Barbon et al. find a U.S. association between estimated LETF pressure and last-half-hour returns in 2012 to 2019; the recent U.S. null is not evidence that all same-session effects were zero, and an association is not the scout's executable return ([Barbon et al.](https://www.abarbon.com/assets/Liquidity_Provision_to_Rebalancing_Flows_from_Leveraged_ETFs_and_Equity_Options.pdf), [Zhao preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03703v1)).
- STATED: $300 to $500 can buy eligible fractions in the regular session but cannot assume exact fractional auction execution or a short-side fade; intraday use of cash requires settled funds and actual broker rules, not a universal PDT cap ([Alpaca orders](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca), [FINRA Rule 4210](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/4210), [SEC margin bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/margin)).
- STATED: Date-stamped issuer share/NAV files and minute bars allow a rough lagged-flow proxy; SEC N-PORT describes month-end observations, not daily AUM, and neither ETF math nor IEX bars reveal net swaps, offsetting creation/redemption or the official auction's size ([SEC N-PORT datasets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-n-port-data-sets), [Alpaca data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-stock-data-1), [Zhao flow analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03703v1)).
- STATED: The likely failure is that forecastable demand is small relative to U.S. auction depth, partly offsets, and is already priced before the chosen fill ([Zhao preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03703v1), [Barbon et al.](https://www.abarbon.com/assets/Liquidity_Provision_to_Rebalancing_Flows_from_Leveraged_ETFs_and_Equity_Options.pdf)).

- KEEP WITH CHANGES, replay calibration only, not a live edge.
- MEASURED: Avi Arora and Ritesh Malpani's preprint reports $66.68, or 1.67% over four separate $1,000 January 2026 replays, after simulated per-fill fees, with $64.10 from the single 37.4-hour BTC episode; aggregate 98.6% fills are simulated, and the paper does not define that denominator ([original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00133)).
- STATED: Oddpool is acknowledged, Arora uses an Oddpool email, and Oddpool sells history and orderbook access; independent financial positions are UNKNOWN ([original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00133), [Oddpool](https://oddpool.com/)).
- STATED: Despite the paper's and scout's “daily high” label, the repository describes the BTC event as Jan. 20 price thresholds at 5 p.m. EST, consistent with Kalshi's same-series settlement rule; the findings do not transfer to daily maxima ([benchmark repository](https://github.com/oddpool/PredictionMarketBench), [Kalshi threshold rules](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxbtcd/bitcoin-price-abovebelow/kxbtcd-26nov2717)).
- STATED: The benchmark does model resting orders behind displayed volume and fills from historical trades, but omits live latency, venue-specific priority and strategic response; it uses primarily GTC maker orders, not proof that all fills were POST_ONLY ([original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00133), [project README](https://github.com/oddpool/PredictionMarketBench)).
