### 5. Spot BTC and ETH ETF creation and redemption cut-off effects around US holidays and 4:00 p.m. ET fixing (DROP)

MEASURED: US spot Bitcoin ETFs launched January 11, 2024 ([WisdomTree](https://www.wisdomtree.com/us/insights/blog/january-2024-month-in-review-us-spot-bitcoin-etfs-approved)) and US spot Ether ETFs launched July 23, 2024. From January 11, 2024 through November 26, 2026 there have been only 28 full-day NYSE holidays (9 remaining in 2024, 10 in 2025, and 9 in 2026 through Thanksgiving) for BTC ETFs and 22 for ETH ETFs, below the 30-event minimum.

STATED: Published 2025 to 2026 empirical studies show a volatility and volume shift on US holidays and at the 3:00 to 4:00 p.m. ET fixing, not a tradeable post-public directional return:

- Holiday volatility lull, not directional return: [Bitcoin on Wall Street Time (MDPI JRFM, 2026)](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/19/9/707/pdf) states: "On these days, in the institutionalized 2022-2025 sub-period, the U.S.-hours variance share collapses by 13.9 percentage points relative to matched adjacent weekdays (0.419 versus 0.557; t = 3.81), reverting most of the way to the uniform benchmark of 0.375."
- Post-public ETF flow chasing shows zero positive edge: [Fabus, Vartiak, Stalmasekova, and Kicova (SSRN 7545019, September 2026)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7545019) analyzed 386 trading sessions from January 11, 2024 to July 28, 2025 plus the post-in-kind subsample after July 29, 2025 ([SEC Press Release 2025-101](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025-101-sec-permits-kind-creations-redemptions-crypto-etps)) and found: "Aligning the return window to the 24 hours after the session close reduces the coefficient to 1.67 (HAC p = 0.007, adjusted R2 = 0.036), and a window that starts after the flow is public shows no positive association."
- Daily 3:00 to 4:00 p.m. ET BRRNY fixing has negligible price pressure: [Kaiko](https://www.kaiko.com/resources/btc-etfs-impact-on-spot-market-structure) notes fixing-window volume rose from 4.5% to 6.7% of daily BTC volume, and [CF Benchmarks / Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/suitability-analysis-cme-cf-bitcoin-reference-rate-new-york-variant-basis-regulated) states: "BRRNY can be replicated with a high degree of confidence and usually with slippage of no more than 1 basis point. On only 6.97% of days would slippage have been greater than 5 bps."
- Holiday return studies are gross, pre-ETF dominated, or conditional: [CoinGecko Research (2026)](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/best-days-to-buy-bitcoin) reports a gross `+0.77%` average next-day BTC return on US holidays over 2013 to 2026 (no costs, no t-statistic, pre-ETF dominated); [QuantPedia (2026)](https://quantpedia.com/surprisingly-profitable-pre-holiday-drift-signal-for-bitcoin/) notes pre-holiday drift works "only when coupled with a short-term momentum trigger"; and [SimianX (2026)](https://www.simianx.ai/stories/bitcoin-thanksgiving-every-year-since-2011) concludes for Thanksgiving: "The honest reading is 'no detectable holiday effect', not 'Thanksgiving is bad for Bitcoin'."
