# Measurement 134: Binance.US order-book costs

Ledger 234, queue 139. Measurement 133 stopped unread on its second wake because a busy shared gate crashed its loop. This is a fresh run and carries over no observations. It measures order-book costs, not profits, and places no orders.

- **What is measured:** Binance.US public USD books only, from `api.binance.us`. For each of the same 20 coins, record top of book and the cost to buy and sell pretend $25 and $250, walking the book against its midpoint and adding the stated taker fee. The roster, duration, sizes, fees, Bitcoin signal and decision line are inherited in `measure_134`. Keep all 20 coins in the count. Take the listing inventory at the first wake only; if it is unavailable, do not replace it.
- **When:** Start at the first scheduled wake after approval and cover 720 hourly wakes. The wake is at HH:08:00 UTC; books received from HH:08:00 through HH:10:00 inclusive count, and dispatch is barred at or after HH:10:00 on a stable clock. There is no separate shared-gate check at a wake. Retry only an unsent busy request, for at most 20 seconds of monotonic elapsed time. A request sent in time retains the ordinary HTTP timeout and the HH:10:00 receipt cutoff. Count lost or thin books and void missed or interrupted wakes; no catch-up. The nominal schedule avoids the other collectors' usual windows, but delayed E12 follow-ups or E13 work can still contend for the gate. The shared cooldown and terminal stop remain in force.
- **Clock limit:** Use a synchronized host UTC clock. The recorder checks fresh time at claim and gate admission. A rollback between the final gate check and the shared transport's own clock read can still send a request early; the reply is discarded and never used. This narrow race is accepted under ledger 234's implementation ruling without changing the shared gate.
- **How to read it:** Read once after the last window. Show median and 90th-percentile costs per side by coin and size, for all hours, midnight UTC and Bitcoin signal hours. The inherited decision line is a median at or below 25 bp on both sides at the larger size for at least 15 coins, each with at least 25 paired midnight books. Otherwise keep the inherited fallback.
- **Limit:** These books measure costs eight minutes after the hour, not at the moment an hourly rule would trade. E12 and E14 use their own books for that.

Both `alive` and the public summary show counts and status, not cost figures. A captured wake with a missing candle or book is partial. Captured wakes with no valid books and void wakes have separate counts. The shared venue gate is unchanged.
