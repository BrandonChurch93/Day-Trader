# Measurement 59. Bitcoin evening window costs

STATED. November 26, 2026 Mountain Time. Plan 58, queue 98, ledger 196, closes on costs before any historical replay price was read. Measurement 59, ledger 197, used quotes only to measure spread and coverage. It kept no trade prices and calculated no returns. There was no freeze, lock, score or replay. This is not a finding about Bitcoin returns.

## Fixed gate and result

- **STATED. Fixed before collection.** [Measurement 59](../../research_rig/MEASURE_59_crypto_clock_costs.md) required fresh eligible IBIT quotes in the first minute at both 21:00 and 23:00 UTC on at least 95% of full Nasdaq sessions overall and 90% in each New York daylight and standard time group. On each paired date, add half the relative spread at entry to half the relative spread at exit. Take the mean across paired dates, then add the fixed 0.30 bp sell fee allowance. The result must be strictly below 3.50 bp because the published +7 bp reference must exceed twice cost. Holidays and early closes do not count as full sessions.
- **MEASURED. IBIT coverage passed.** Eligible quotes at both edges on 690 of 716 sessions (96.37%), including 474 of 493 daylight sessions (96.15%) and 216 of 223 standard-time sessions (96.86%). These are quotes, not fills.
- **MEASURED. IBIT cost failed.** Mean paired half-spread sum was 7.827517 bp, and median was 6.874348 bp. Adding 0.30 bp makes the modeled round-trip cost **8.127517 bp** against the strict limit of 3.50 bp. Even the mean spread component alone fails. The pooled duration-weighted full spreads were 6.279385 bp at 21:00 UTC and 9.857892 bp at 23:00 UTC. Those per-edge means are shown for context, not used in the gate.
- **STATED figures and MEASURED arithmetic. Futures shown only.** At the documented November 26 reference of $60,735 per BTC in [Round 31](120_round_31_cfm_futures_cost_rescreen.md), a [BIP contract](https://help.coinbase.com/en/derivatives/perpetual-style-futures/contract-specifications) covers 0.01 BTC, or $607.35, and a tick is $0.05. The [Coinbase fixed charges](https://www.coinbase.com/derivatives-trading/us-derivatives) of $0.30 per round trip are 4.94 bp, already above the limit. Fixed charges plus one tick are 5.76 bp, before variable commission and funding. That is an illustrative floor, not a measured futures spread. The futures route cannot replace IBIT in this plan.
- **MEASURED. ETHA shown only.** Both-edge coverage was 517 of 585 full sessions (88.38%), including 348 of 402 daylight and 169 of 183 standard-time sessions. Mean paired half-spread sum was 20.952296 bp, before any fee. ETHA is not a substitute for IBIT.
- **UNKNOWN. Actual execution.** Eligibility and quote spreads do not establish fill prices, broker routing at either edge, market impact, or the all-in futures cost. Plan 58 does not test these or the underlying return.
