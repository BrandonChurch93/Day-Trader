## Ranked Candidates

### Rank 1: Non-Crypto Single-Stock Leveraged and Inverse ETF End-of-Day Rebalancing (`NVDL`, `TSLL`, and Late-2025/2026 S&P 500 Launches)

- Mechanism (who is forced, why, when): Daily single-stock leveraged and inverse ETFs (arrived in the US in July 2022 and expanded to 275 individual stocks by November 2026) and their swap counterparties must trade the underlying stock near the 4:00 p.m. ET close in the same direction as the day's move to restore the daily leverage multiple `L`, with notional trade `L * (L - 1) * A_{t-1} * r_t`, which is positive for every `L > 1` or `L < 0` (STATED, [arXiv:2611.09286](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09286) and [Concretum Group, Nov 2026](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/do-leveraged-etfs-fuel-intraday-trends)).
- Sources opened and verbatim quotes (STATED): From [arXiv:2611.09286 (Nov 2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09286): "A leveraged or inverse ETF with daily leverage multiple L rebalances in proportion to L(L − 1) times the day's return, which is positive for every such fund, so it buys after a rise and sells after a fall (Cheng and Madhavan, 2009), although Ivanov and Lenkey (2018) find that fund flows offset much of this rebalancing. Single-stock leveraged ETFs arrived in the United States in July 2022." "The reversal falls by 3.5 bps (t = −1.3), the predicted direction. Under the pre-registered rule this estimate is exploratory, because fewer than 20 of the 34 lie in the primary sample." "For those 12, the reversal falls by 13.1 bps, and it falls for every one of them; for the 22 treated stocks outside our sample, it rises by 1.7 bps." "Because this split was chosen after the confirmatory run, we treat it as a hypothesis for future testing rather than as evidence." From [Concretum Group (Nov 2026)](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/do-leveraged-etfs-fuel-intraday-trends): "one way or another, the rebalancing flow still 'reaches' the underlying."
- Measured numbers: STATED from [arXiv:2611.09286](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09286), across 34 S&P 500 stocks with a first single-stock LETF launch from July 2022 to December 2025 (250 sessions before vs 250 sessions after launch, Alpaca SIP bars, 3:30 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. ET return on prior-close-to-3:00 p.m. rank `ROD3`), the pooled 3:30 to 4:00 p.m. reversal fell by 3.5 bps (`t = -1.3`, exploratory, not passed under Holm correction). In the post-hoc split, the 12 primary-sample (non-top-80 volume) stocks fell by 13.1 bps (12 of 12), while the 22 mega-cap names outside the primary sample rose by +1.7 bps (consistent with closing auctions absorbing flow in `NVDA` and `TSLA`). A follow-up test on roughly 35 post-December 2025 S&P 500 launches was pre-registered November 5, 2026 and completes around end-2027. [Concretum Group (Nov 2026)](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/do-leveraged-etfs-fuel-intraday-trends) maps 804 leveraged funds across 86 ETFs and 275 single stocks but publishes zero strategy returns, Sharpe ratios, or costs.
- Exact rule (for a forward-only pretend-money log): Freeze the daily roster of non-top-80 S&P 500 stocks with active US 2x or 3x single-stock leveraged ETFs whose estimated closing rebalance `L * (L - 1) * AUM * |r_15:00|` exceeds 1.0% of trailing 20-day closing-auction dollar volume. On days where prior-close-to-15:00 ET return `r_15:00 >= +3.0%`, log a long-only entry at the 15:30 ET SIP ask and exit at the 15:59 ET SIP bid (continuation leg); on days where `r_15:00 <= -3.0%`, log a long-only entry at the 15:59 ET SIP ask and exit at the next session's 09:35 ET SIP bid (next-morning reversal leg).
- Free point-in-time data and event counts: Alpaca SIP minute bars and NBBO quotes are already held off-repo; issuer daily AUM and share counts are free on ETF issuer pages (GraniteShares, Direxion, Defiance, T-Rex) if logged forward daily. Historical point-in-time daily ETF AUM is UNKNOWN as a free download. Event count so far: 34 pre-2026 S&P 500 launches plus roughly 35 post-December 2025 launches (~69 stocks), generating an estimated 100 to 250 big-move stock-days per year once AUM filters are applied.
- What a $500 US account can do: Long-only single shares on a US equity broker at 1 to 3 bps spread (zero commission). Shorting on big down days is unavailable on a $500 cash account, so only the positive-day continuation leg and negative-day next-morning reversal leg are reachable.
- How it could fool us: Creation and redemption flows offset much of mechanical rebalancing (Ivanov and Lenkey 2018); US closing auctions are deep (roughly 10% of daily volume), which already killed the effect in the 22 largest stocks (+1.7 bps) and in `MSTU`/`MSTX`/`CONL` (Round 34); and a 3.5 to 13 bps pre-cost shift can easily vanish under 4 to 6 bps round-trip spreads plus single-stock intraday volatility.
- Verdict: `forward log` (pretend-money only, no historical replay yet, because US post-launch history on non-mega-cap stocks is short, the 13.1 bps split was post-hoc, and free historical daily ETF AUM is missing).

Bitcoin trades continuously across the 48-hour weekend gap, so `IBIT` (91.4% Blue Ocean share), `MSTR` (76.2%), `COIN` (74.0%), and `MSTU` (86.2%) reprice at 8:00 p.m. ET Sunday. However, no participant is forced to trade at stale Friday prices: overnight market makers adjust quotes to live spot BTC immediately and halt volatile names.
- Sources opened and verbatim quotes (STATED): From [BlackRock (2025)](https://www.blackrock.com/gls-download/literature/whitepaper/blackrock-market-spotlight-24-hour-trading.pdf): "For the most actively traded iShares ETFs, bid-ask spreads were, on average, nearly 10 times wider in overnight trading hours than regular market hours, reflecting the lower levels of liquidity present in the overnight session." "For example, for less frequently traded iShares ETFs, we have experienced spreads 30 times wider than during core trading hours." From [Sapinover (Nov 2026)](https://sapinover.com/research/overnight-venue-patterns): "When a name has thin expected overnight liquidity, or the underlying exposure moves materially while the US session is closed (leveraged Korean ETF during Seoul hours, leveraged crypto during Asia hours), a passive quote becomes a pricing-time arbitrage target." From [Alpaca 24/5 docs (Nov 2026)](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/245-trading): "Overnight trade executions and market data are facilitated by the Blue Ocean Alternative Trading System (BOATS)." - Verdict: `drop`.

### Rank 4: Options on Spot Bitcoin and Ether ETFs (`IBIT`, `ETHA`) Friday Expiry Pinning and Close Effects

- Mechanism (who is forced, why, when): Listed options on `IBIT` (launched November 2024) and `ETHA` (2025) create US-regulated dealer gamma hedging in ETF shares into weekly and monthly third-Friday expirations. When dealers are net long gamma (e.g., from covered-call overwriting), their delta hedges dampen moves toward heavy strikes; when net short gamma, hedges amplify moves.
- Sources opened and verbatim quotes (STATED): From [Weiss et al. (Finance Research Letters, Sep 2026)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612326008688): "We source high-frequency transaction data on Bitcoin options from the Deribit exchange for the period from 1 January 2021 to 31 December 2023." "As reported in Table 2, the price move from 7:00 am to 8:00 am fully reverses by 10:00 am under these conditions." (Note: this paper does not cover `IBIT` or `ETHA`.) From [Quant Data (Oct 2026)](https://quantdata.uk/research/does-max-pain-pin): "Across 1146 graded observations (43 tickers, 29 expiration dates, snapshots 2026-07-10 to 2026-10-02), 465 of 1146 (40.6%) finished expiration day closer to the recorded max pain level than they stood on the snapshot day." "The median distance to max pain went from 2.3% at the snapshot to 2.8% at the expiration close — it widened." "The honest answer today is: not established either way."
- Measured numbers: No measured evidence exists yet for `IBIT` or `ETHA` Friday expiry pinning or directional close returns, only a mechanism. The closest 2026 Bitcoin options expiry study ([Weiss et al. 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612326008688)) studied pre-ETF **Deribit** options in 2021 to 2023 (1,058 days, 8:00 a.m. UTC settlement, pre-expiry `High OI x NGamma` -0.17% and post-expiry reversal `-1.05 * ReturnPre`, `t = -2.83`), not US ETF options. Meanwhile, live 2026 US option-expiration testing across 43 tickers including `MSTR` and `COIN` ([Quant Data 2026](https://quantdata.uk/research/does-max-pain-pin)) found prices moved away from max pain more often than toward it (40.6% closer).
- Exact rule: None substantiated. Any GEX-conditioned Friday rule requires full strike-by-strike dealer positioning across `IBIT`, CME, and Deribit simultaneously.
- Free point-in-time data and event counts: Roughly 24 monthly third-Friday expiries for `IBIT` since November 2024 (~100 weekly Fridays). Free historical point-in-time US option chains with strike open interest and signed dealer volume do not exist (requires paid OPRA or OptionMetrics IvyDB).
- What a $500 US account can do: Buy or hold `IBIT` or `ETHA` shares long-only; cannot trade multi-leg volatility or pin-capture option structures cleanly on $500 and cannot verify dealer gamma sign without paid chain data.
- How it could fool us: Conflating Deribit 8:00 a.m. UTC settlement reversals with US 4:00 p.m. ET `IBIT` expirations; assuming all open interest is customer-long or dealer-long without signed trade classification; and testing only 24 monthly dates.
- Verdict: `drop`.

### Rank 5: Defined-Outcome / Buffer ETF Mechanical Option Rollovers (Added Post-2024 Forced-Flow Product)

- Mechanism (who is forced, why, when): Defined-outcome ("buffer") ETFs (such as Innovator `PJAN` through `PDEC`, First Trust, and peers; 266 buffer ETFs tracked) mechanically roll predefined 1-year FLEX and listed option packages at the start of each calendar outcome period regardless of market conditions (STATED, [AFA 2026 paper](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=195748)).
- Sources opened and verbatim quotes (STATED): From [AFA (June 2026)](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=195748): "a one-standard-deviation increase in buying pressure from rollovers results in a 0.008 standard deviation increase in implied volatility on average." "The estimated effect is economically small and statistically insignificant." "The limited intersection of option contracts reduces the direct influence that the trading activities of option-trading ETFs have on the VIX." "I do not record the exact time frame of trading but rather the date of major purchase."
- Measured numbers: STATED from [AFA (June 2026)](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=195748) (Jan 2018 to Jun 2023, 23,591 to 23,781 option observations across 266 buffer ETFs): rollover buying pressure raises affected option implied volatility by 0.008 standard deviations (`t = 4.60 to 5.11`), has zero statistically significant effect on VIX (`t = 0.10 to 1.18`), and has no measured effect on underlying equity or ETF returns.
- Exact rule: None for an equity/ETF account; harvesting a 0.008 SD option-IV bump would require trading specific FLEX/listed strikes.
- Free point-in-time data and event counts: 12 monthly series resets per year across major buffer families; option-chain IV data requires paid OptionMetrics/OPRA.
- What a $500 US account can do: Nothing; a $500 long-only stock/ETF account cannot sell or buy multi-leg index FLEX options to capture a sub-1% SD implied-volatility blip.
- How it could fool us: Mistaking a statistically significant implied-volatility coefficient (`t = 5.11`) for an economically tradable equity return, or ignoring that rollovers are spread across multiple days with no public exact execution clock.
- Verdict: `drop`.
