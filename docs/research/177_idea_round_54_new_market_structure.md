## Ranked candidates

### Rank 1: Non-crypto single-stock leveraged ETF closing rebalancing

- STATED, [Vidovich and Zerjal's arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09286): single-stock funds began in the US in July 2022. For a daily leveraged or inverse fund, the simplified exposure adjustment is proportional to `L*(L-1)*A*r`. The multiplier is positive for leveraged-long and inverse funds, so the adjustment follows the sign of the stock move. Fund flows can offset it. This is not proof that each fund or swap dealer must place a stock order at 4:00 p.m. The public excerpt of the [Concretum Research Substack](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/do-leveraged-etfs-fuel-intraday-trends) says counterparties are likely to hedge in spot or futures and maps 804 funds across 86 equity ETFs and 275 stocks, including 160 delisted funds, March 2017 to November 2026. No strategy return or cost is visible in the excerpt; the rest is paywalled.
- STATED, [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09286): 34 S&P 500 stocks with a first leveraged-ETF launch and complete windows, July 2022 through October 2025 in this group, under a December 2025 launch cutoff. The comparison uses 250 sessions before and after each launch and subtracts the same change in three untreated stocks of similar dollar volume. The outcome is the 3:30 p.m. to last trade before 4:00 p.m. return, excluding the auction, regressed against the daily cross-sectional rank of the prior-close-to-3:00 p.m. return. A smaller reversal is a shift in a relative slope, not a signed long-only trade result. The pooled reversal fell 3.5 bp, t = −1.3. The registered mechanism test required 20 treated names in the primary sample; only 12 qualified, so it was not judged and counted as not passed. The pooled number is exploratory, not a Holm-adjusted rejection or pass.
- STATED, [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09286): reversal fell 13.1 bp across 12 primary names and fell in all 12; it rose 1.7 bp for 22 names outside the primary sample. The primary list itself was fixed before the study, but using the 12-versus-22 split as a result was expressly post-hoc. No split t-statistic is reported. The paper warns that shared controls or common shocks may explain the unusually similar estimates. The primary screen excludes a private fixed list of about 80 heavily traded companies previously examined. It is not the public proxy that drops the 80 largest by prior-month dollar volume, and the other 22 should not be relabeled 22 proven mega-caps. The +1.7 bp does not establish that closing auctions absorbed the effect.
- STATED, [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09286): the authors registered the follow-up on November 5, 2026 and deposited it November 6. It targets 35 later-launch names, 32 provisionally in the primary sample by membership, with issuer launch dates still to be confirmed. The criterion is t at least 3 for the predicted reduction with at least 15 stocks. It is to run once around end-2027, after the final 250-session window. Five November and December 2025 launches lacked full windows and sit outside both groups. No result on the later names was reported.
- MEASURED in [doc 136](136_measured_stock_trading_costs.md), not this round: median half-spread across the saved medians for 38 single stocks was 1.074 bp in the last half hour and 3.787 bp in the first. The pooled last-half-hour p90 for all 56 instruments was 2.440 bp a side. Those quotes are not from this strategy's stocks and large-move days, and do not cover the exact close, depth, auction fills or slippage.
- Cost check on $500 actually traded: two typical late stock half-spreads sum to about 2.15 bp ($0.11), and one late plus one opening-window median to 4.86 bp ($0.24). Two pooled late p90-sized crosses would cost 4.88 bp ($0.24); that is a stress illustration, not a round-trip percentile. The repo's 5 bp per side assumption costs 10 bp ($0.50). If 13.1 bp were a real gross trade return, it would be about $0.66 gross and $0.16 after that 10 bp allowance, before any omitted costs. Typical displayed spreads alone do not prove that 13.1 bp is impossible to trade, but the paper never shows a 13.1 bp trade. The pooled 3.5 bp shift is smaller than the 10 bp allowance and is not itself a return either.
- UNKNOWN: fills, paired net return, sample size of the scout's proposed +3% 3:30-to-3:59 continuation rule, and all corresponding quantities for its −3% late buy and next-morning exit. Neither rule, nor its estimated flow threshold of 1% of closing-auction volume, is tested by the paper. A long-only cash account cannot reproduce the direction of both sides of a relative reversal trade. Any use of a private membership list, same-day AUM available only later, or a close not available at entry adds another mismatch. The continuation trade also ends in under half an hour, outside the owner's stated holding range.
- Verdict: **drop the card and forward log**. Read the registered follow-up when reported; do not start a parallel paper-book or collect a new price feed.

### Rank 2: Kalshi earnings-call mentions and options-vs-Kalshi

- STATED, [Luiz Felipe Barbosa's personal research article](https://lfpmb.com/articles/cross-category-calibration-analysis): page byline says March 15, 2026; the corrected snapshot contains closes from January 30, 2025 through July 5, 2026 and no later revision date is shown. It has 20,979 resolved contracts with usable 2-hour VWAPs; 2,118 is the entire corporate earnings-call category, not the number of qualifying trades. Across categories, buying NO when YES VWAP was below 50% gives +12.4 cents on 10,158 contracts, 90.2% hit rate. The reported earnings benchmark is about +16 cents per contract after modeled taker fees, with eligible earnings count UNKNOWN.
- STATED, [Barbosa](https://lfpmb.com/articles/cross-category-calibration-analysis): that VWAP uses trades at or before the cutoff two hours before the **final recorded trade**, a retrospective clock, not two hours before the earnings call, scheduled close, or settlement. It is an in-sample accounting benchmark. It excludes spread, slippage, queue position, available size, event-level drawdowns, and inactive, delisted and canceled contracts. Multiple mentions can share one earnings call. The scout's proposed pre-call 10-to-49-cent YES midquote and resting NO order is a different, untested rule. No forced buyer is identified.
- STATED, [Cdude-d's GitHub project](https://github.com/Cdude-d/quant-prediction-markets): KXINXU sample spans 270 trading days, 1,455 events, 435,308 contracts and $94 million of reported volume. The options comparison using a delayed feed earned +4.7 cents gross per trade in the historical out-of-sample check, then lost 9 of 11 forward **paper** trades, −$293. That is not real-money evidence. The project attributes the failure to a 15-minute-delayed options feed and says its favorite-longshot edge did not clear costs.
- STATED, [Kalshi fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf): default taker formula before rounding is `0.07 * C * P * (1-P)` dollars with dollar price P and contract count C, subject to the relevant multiplier and rounding. The schedule shows $0.02 on one $0.50 contract and $1.75 on 100 at that price. Maker terms can differ; the taker benchmark does not validate passive fills.
- STATED, [Kalshi Developer Agreement, v1.1](https://assets.kalshi.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf): membership, acceptance and contact information are required; section 3.1 bars collecting, caching, aggregating or storing API data except to facilitate one's own Kalshi trading, and bars sharing without prior written authorization. No paper-only or general research exception is stated. This agrees with [doc 53](53_prediction_markets_checked.md) and Queue 22, 44 and 53. Public technical access to trades is not permission to retain books. An owner account alone would not authorize a research recorder. No books were pulled or recorded.
- Verdict: **WATCH** earnings only, with no action absent permission or a permitted own-trading purpose plus an executable pre-call test. **Drop** options-vs-Kalshi.

### Rank 3: Sunday night Blue Ocean overnight stocks

- STATED, [BlackRock's 2025 whitepaper](https://www.blackrock.com/gls-download/literature/whitepaper/blackrock-market-spotlight-24-hour-trading.pdf): nearly 10 times wider overnight spreads in its most actively traded iShares ETFs and examples of 30 times in less active ones. Figure 2, 20-day average through March 31, 2025, regular to overnight spreads in bp: TLT 1 to 5, IWM and IVV 1 to 4, SOXX 2 to 22, IEF 1 to 18. These are not measured costs for Sunday IBIT orders.
- STATED, [Sapinover LLC's April 2026 vendor report](https://sapinover.com/research/overnight-venue-patterns): 30 eligible dates from February 13 to April 20 across three ATSs. Blue Ocean was 83.7% of their reported notional, not of all US overnight trading. Per-symbol shares were 91.4% IBIT, 76.2% MSTR, 74.0% COIN and 86.2% MSTU. Average next-open minus overnight VWAP, divided by prior close, was +31, +56 and +48 bp respectively for IBIT, MSTR and COIN; not a Sunday-open fill-to-fill trade. SBIT halted there April 1; ETHU was halted throughout. The passive 20% reference band applied from March 1.
- STATED, [Alpaca 24/5 documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/245-trading): Blue Ocean runs the broker's overnight session, generally 8 p.m. to 4 a.m. ET, subject to holidays and symbol restrictions. Limit orders only, including supported fractional orders. Indicative real-time quotes are a paid feed; indicative trades are 15 minutes delayed and adjusted to fit the real-time bid and ask. No forced trade at the stale Friday price and no executable Sunday-lag edge established. Verdict: **drop**.

### Rank 4: IBIT and ETHA option-expiry pinning

- STATED, [Weiss et al., Finance Research Letters research article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612326008688), September 2026: Deribit Bitcoin options, January 2021 through December 2023; 1,058 observations, 8 a.m. UTC expiration. High open interest times negative gamma coefficient −0.1710 percentage points pre-expiry, t = −2.12; the three-way reversal interaction coefficient is −1.0524, t = −2.83. The paper says the 7-to-8 a.m. move reversed by 10 a.m. under those conditions. Not evidence for US ETF options at the US close.
- STATED, [Quant Data's archived vendor study](https://quantdata.uk/research/does-max-pain-pin): July 10 to October 2, 2026 snapshots; 1,146 observations, 43 tickers, 29 expirations, not independent by ticker and expiry. 465 (40.6%) moved closer to max pain; median distance went from 2.3% to 2.8%. The study covers MSTR and COIN but does not list IBIT or ETHA. The publisher calls the effect unestablished, not definitively refuted. Our desk has no verified signed dealer positioning or net directional return here. Verdict: **drop**.

### Rank 5: Defined-outcome and buffer ETF option rollovers

- STATED, [Zhaoyi Wang's March 2025 manuscript hosted by AFA](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=195748), not a confirmed journal publication: January 2018 through June 2023 implied-volatility study, 266 buffer ETFs in the wider sample, regression n = 23,591 and 23,781. A one-SD increase in rollover demand raised option IV by 0.008 SD in both specs (t = 4.60 and 5.11). The VIX result was insignificant (t from 0.103 to 1.18), not literally zero; no equity return was tested. Purchases generally span several days, mostly day one; exact times are not recorded. This is not a new post-2024 product or an executable equity rule. The desk cannot trade the option packages. Verdict: **drop**.

### Rank 7: Weekend stock tokens

- STATED, [Gaurav Agarwal for Bitquery Research](https://bitquery.io/investigations/tokenized-stocks-weekend-prices-monday-open), November 3, 2026: 17 weekends, July 10 to October 30, 121 stocks and six issuers. Using the token price just before Sunday 6 p.m. ET futures reopen, 286 of 533 qualifying Monday opens (54%) were called correctly versus 291 (55%) always guessing up; that subset moved at least 0.5%. Across 791 opens the median error was 1.2% versus 0.9% assuming no change, and 62% of forecasts got worse. A forecast comparison, not execution evidence.
- STATED, [Cha et al.'s NEST extended conference paper](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2610.21491): 218 common premarket windows within July 2025 to June 2026; MSTRx adds 0.014 to R² after equity premarket is included, total R² 0.923. Weekend-or-holiday versus weekday conditional increments are 0.112 and 0.122 (interaction p = 0.89). This particular result is not an early Sunday trade test. Neither paper establishes US customer access for each wrapper or a net trading rule. Verdict: **drop**.

### Rank 8: US retail crypto perpetual launches and expiry

- STATED, [The Defiant's reporting based on disclosures checked November 19, 2026](https://thedefiant.io/news/cefi/us-crypto-perps-coinbase-and-kraken-access-explained): Coinbase Financial Markets US perpetual-style access began July 21, 2025; a filing effective June 17, 2026 removed the scheduled five-year expiry on its four named nano BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP contracts, not every product. Kraken said eligible US clients could trade June 15, 2026 and describes the US Bitnomial-listed perpetuals as having no expiry. Both require separate derivatives eligibility. Exact launch count and volume share were not established. These product facts do not test any launch-week edge, and no scheduled roll remains on the named perpetuals. Verdict: **drop**.
