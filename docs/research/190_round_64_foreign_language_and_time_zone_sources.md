- **3. Foreign corporate filings / ADRs: DROP this blanket overnight trade.** CONFIRMED, STATED: [Hoang and Vo](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/03128962241286085?download=true) study 374 ADRs from 37 countries in 1991 to 2023; unadjusted opening-price efficiency 0.96 vs 0.89 (t=10.43) and overnight price-contribution 50% vs 21% (t=134.97). “50% of daily return” was WRONG as stated: contribution to price discovery is not a share of signed return or a minutes-level fill. [Research Square v1](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10584858/v1) confirms 1,506 gross announcements, including 399 across 38 ADR issuers, but the -0.0205 (t=-3.37) result is a post-release **processing-cost interaction** on 299 ADR observations, not a blanket decline for all foreign earnings. Placebo pre-period interaction is significant, segment confidence intervals overlap and returns are gross. WRONG to claim causal AI compression; UNKNOWN whether a lawful, timely subset is actionable. [Alpaca orders](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca) confirms 20:00 to 04:00 ET overnight on eligible symbols and extended-hours limit orders, but not uniform fills, commission-free status for this whole proposal or the scout's illustrative 50 to 150 bp half-spreads. [Alpaca margin rules](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/margin-and-short-selling) require at least $2,000 equity and borrow/locate constraints for shorts. NOT FOUND: actual source-first US-fillable announcement count, a modern live edge net of spread, and the alleged universal millisecond arbitrage. INFERENCE: an overnight reader can help triage filings, not infer stale actionable ADR quotes from home-market price discovery.

- **5. Asian-hours crypto gap: DROP the hours-only directional rule.** CONFIRMED, STATED: [Aldhahi](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/19/9/707), 87,672 Kraken XBT/USD hourly observations in 2016 to 2025, finds 50.6% of realized variance in fixed 13:00 to 21:59 UTC in 2022 to 2025 vs 38.4% in 2016 to 2018; holiday difference -13.9 pp (t=3.81, p<0.001) and a one-hour shift in the peak with US daylight saving. NOT FOUND: either study testing tradable Asia-to-US directional delay after costs, universal subsecond convergence of these venues, or a fixed round-trip cliff for this strategy. MEASURED: Replay 19 failed its different hours-only proxy. INFERENCE: a useful clock for risk is not a trading signal; new, specified event selection would be a different proposal.
