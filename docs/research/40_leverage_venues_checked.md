## Kalshi

- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Retail access and scope.** “US-based Kalshi users who have completed KYC verification are eligible to apply”; customers complete a suitability form and tutorial, and most retail users use Kalshi Prime ([application](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/15357656-applying-for-perpetuals-access.md)). The CFTC order approves the specific BTCPERP contract, and the venue page says “First use date: Nov 3, 2026”; that label is not proof every account got access then ([approval](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/documents/2026/orgdcmkexbtxperporder26601.pdf), [venue page](https://kalshi.com/perps)).
- **MEASURED · CONFIRMED: Contract lots and snapshot.** Minimum quantity is one whole contract, equal to 0.0001 BTC or 0.1 SOL; at $1,000 the two samples show BTC long 6.7919x and short 6.3207x, SOL long 3.5618x and short 3.0683x, not firm caps or quotes at the intended size ([BTC response](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/markets/KXBTCPERP), [SOL response](https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/markets/KXSOLPERP), [BTC spec](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/15357587-btc-perpetual-futures-contract-specifications.md)). The venue roster showed ETH, XRP and VVV, and the separate perps guide also surfaced LINK and HYPE ([venue roster](https://kalshi.com/perps), [perps guide](https://kalshi.com/perpetuals/learn)).
- **MEASURED · CONFIRMED: Exchange fee schedule is not a retail quote.** Entry crypto taker and maker rates are 0.12% and 0.02% for FCM members, and 0.04% and 0.02% for self-clearing members; the exchange charges the FCM, so use the account's own schedule, not the cheapest published tier ([fee filing](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules10152633992.pdf), [retail fee explanation](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/16071417-perps-fees-explained), [account fee API](https://docs.kalshi.com/margin-rest/fees/get-fee-tier-rates.md)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Funding and collateral.** Crypto funding changes hands at midnight, 8 a.m. and 4 p.m. Eastern; absolute rates below 0.01% become zero and each charge is capped at ±2% ([funding rules](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/15357613-how-funding-works.md)). The app uses isolated margin and the API portfolio margin; below maintenance the broker can liquidate, fill worse than the trigger, and require repayment of a resulting negative balance ([BTC spec](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/15357587-btc-perpetual-futures-contract-specifications.md), [risk guide](https://kalshi.com/perpetuals/learn)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Real order API and same product namespace for demo.** The perps order route is POST /trade-api/v2/margin/orders and the spec names separate production and demo servers; demo has mock money and separate credentials and may behave differently from live ([perps guide](https://docs.kalshi.com/margin), [spec](https://docs.kalshi.com/perps_openapi.yaml), [demo guide](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/demo_env)). The developer agreement limits API use to members' own trading and requires prior written approval for third-party data sharing ([agreement](https://assets.kalshi.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf)).
- **MEASURED · CONFIRMED: Funding sample and minute bars.** The BTC route returned minute bars in per-contract dollars; the funding responses showed 21 entries each for November 18 through 24 and three final zero entries each ([BTC bars](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/markets/KXBTCPERP/candlesticks?start_ts=1795564500&end_ts=1795564800&period_interval=1), [BTC funding](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/funding_rates/historical?ticker=KXBTCPERP&start_ts=1794960000&end_ts=1795564800), [SOL funding](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/funding_rates/historical?ticker=KXSOLPERP&start_ts=1794960000&end_ts=1795564800)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Flat-price example, not actual execution.** At assumed BTC $60,000 and SOL $120, 200 BTC or 100 SOL contracts make $1,200 each ([BTC units](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/markets/KXBTCPERP), [SOL units](https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/markets/KXSOLPERP)). Two 0.12% taker fills cost $2.88; two 0.02% maker fills cost $0.48 if they fill and the exchange prices pass through ([exchange tariff](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules10152633992.pdf)). The 21 rates sum to +0.0005138564 BTC and −0.0005159088 SOL, about +$0.62 and −$0.62 for a long at flat $1,200; this makes taker cost $2.88 for either in the final day, and $3.50 BTC or $2.26 SOL for the full sample ([BTC rates](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/funding_rates/historical?ticker=KXBTCPERP&start_ts=1794960000&end_ts=1795564800), [SOL rates](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin/funding_rates/historical?ticker=KXSOLPERP&start_ts=1794960000&end_ts=1795564800)).
- **UNKNOWN · NOT FOUND: Idaho-specific approval, deposit minimum, customer liquidation fee, demo market availability and delivered fee tier.** The public demo BTC lookup did not return a readable response in this check, and the fee API needs an authenticated margin-enabled account ([application](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/15357656-applying-for-perpetuals-access.md), [demo BTC lookup](https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2/margin/markets/KXBTCPERP), [fee API](https://docs.kalshi.com/margin-rest/fees/get-fee-tier-rates.md), [risk guide](https://kalshi.com/perpetuals/learn)).

## Coinbase Financial Markets

- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Eligible residents, not blanket approval.** Coinbase says the US-regulated offering is for residents of “the US, Canada, and most countries” in the EEA; US clients use CFM and must pass derivatives onboarding with a verified Coinbase account ([onboarding](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-derivatives-intro)). Separate Coinbase spot margin requires eligible US state residence and Eligible Contract Participant status, generally $10 million investable for an individual, so it is not ordinary US retail spot margin ([spot margin eligibility](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/margin/spot-margin-get-started)).
- **MEASURED · CONFIRMED: Whole lots and coins.** One BIP is 0.01 BTC, SLP 5 SOL, and ETP 0.10 ETH; the platform takes whole futures lots ([specs](https://help.coinbase.com/en/derivatives/perpetual-style-futures/contract-specifications), [onboarding](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-derivatives-intro)). Advertised maxima are 10x BTC and ETH and 5x SOL; further contracts include XRP, ADA, LINK, BCH, DOGE, AVAX, HBAR, LTC, DOT, SHIB, XLM, SUI, AAVE, ENA, NEAR, ONDO, PAXG, PEPE, ZEC, HYPE and BNB ([US roster](https://www.coinbase.com/derivatives-trading/us-derivatives), [contract specs](https://help.coinbase.com/en/derivatives/perpetual-style-futures/contract-specifications)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Carry, forced closure and deficit.** Posted leverage varies by contract and side; the API guide quotes 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. ET for its intraday feature, retail margin help describes a 6 p.m. to 4 p.m. window, and the perps overview says 6 p.m. to 5 p.m., so use current order and carry requirements, not a single schedule ([API guide](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/guides/futures), [retail margin](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-derivatives-leverage-margin), [perps overview](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-perpetual-futures-overview)). The retail page gives 100% as the liquidation threshold and CFM can liquidate without prior notice; US clients owe any deficit ([liquidation](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-derivatives-risk-management-and-liquidations), [agreement](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/futures-account-agreement)).
- **MEASURED · CONFIRMED: Published fees are not today's entry schedule.** API docs quote a “0.05%” introductory beta fee and the marketing floor is “0.005% maker” excluding “a $0.15 charge per contract”; neither establishes the current small-account taker rate ([API pricing](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/guides/futures), [price footnote](https://www.coinbase.com/derivatives-trading/us-derivatives)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Hourly funding and document mismatch.** Perpetual-style positions pay or receive funding on the hour with accumulated cash settled around midnight ET weekdays; dated contracts instead have expiries ([US perps overview](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-perpetual-futures-overview), [onboarding](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-derivatives-intro)). The product overview names the December 20, 2030 contracts, while the other specifications page says “no expiration date and no final settlement date”; use the actual symbol's terms, not the word “perp” to infer expiry ([US overview](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-perpetual-futures-overview), [specs](https://help.coinbase.com/en/derivatives/perpetual-style-futures/contract-specifications)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Live orders, not matching paper.** POST /api/v3/brokerage/orders takes authenticated orders and the CFM guide links order management for US products; sandbox replies are “static and pre-defined” ([REST](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/rest-api), [CFM guide](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/guides/futures), [sandbox](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/sandbox)). ## Tax and records

- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Qualifying US futures, not all perps.** IRS Form 6781 provides year-end mark-to-market and 60% long-term and 40% short-term treatment for qualifying Section 1256 contracts; that is a gain or loss split, not a tax rate, and the Kalshi contract approval is not a tax ruling ([IRS form](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f6781--2026.pdf), [Kalshi CFTC order](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/documents/2026/orgdcmkexbtxperporder26601.pdf)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Spot reporting.** IRS treats digital assets as property; brokers report gross proceeds for covered reportable sales and report basis on covered assets for transactions after 2025, not necessarily on transfers in ([IRS digital assets](https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets), [1099-DA instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Holding ETF shares is distinct from holding its futures.** The prospectus describes tax treatment of shareholder distributions and share sales separately from positions held inside the fund; avoid treating the ETF shareholder as the direct holder of its Section 1256 contracts ([fund prospectus](https://www.volatilityshares.com/uploads/fund/5-16-ea0295205-01Volatility-Shares-Trust485BPOSProspectusWeb-Ready59.pdf), [IRS form](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f6781--2026.pdf)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Coinbase tax form and Idaho deduction.** Coinbase says US futures traders get a Form 1099-B; the Idaho deduction applies to defined Idaho property, not a general capital gains discount for these products ([Coinbase reports](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/taxes/forms-reports/download-report), [Idaho Tax Commission](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/income-tax/individual-income/popular-credits-and-deductions/capital-gains/)).
- **STATED · CONFIRMED: Coinbase large-trader notice is not a tax form.** Coinbase says the CFTC may request a “Statement of Reporting Trader” from customers holding more than 25 futures contracts at market close; not everyone meeting the threshold gets a request, but failing to complete a requested Form 40 can lead to “Position Closing Only” ([CFM onboarding and Form 40](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-derivatives-intro)).
