## Part 2: Your Own Lawful Claims (Zero Capital, Done Personally)

- Search Idaho unclaimed property yourself for free at [YourMoney.Idaho.Gov](https://yourmoney.idaho.gov/): the Idaho State Treasurer states "If your name is on the list, the claim is free" and safeguards more than $291 million in unclaimed funds, so no paid finder is needed ([Idaho State Treasurer](https://sto.idaho.gov/About/Current-News/ArticleID/128/Ho-Ho-Ho-Idaho-State-Treasurer-Delivers-Gifts-Early)). Note also that under Idaho Code 14-5-1301, third-party finder agreements are void for 24 months after property is delivered to the state ([Idaho Code 14-5-1301](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title14/T14CH5/SECT14-5-1301/)).
- Prepare what Idaho asks for and expect up to 90 days after a complete claim (MEASURED): Idaho Code 14-5-903 states "A person claiming to be the owner of property held under this chapter... may file a claim for the property on a form prescribed by the administrator. The claimant must verify the claim as to its completeness and accuracy" ([Idaho Code 14-5-903](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title14/T14CH5/SECT14-5-903/)). Under Idaho Code 14-5-904, a claim is complete once "all the information and documentation requested by the administrator as necessary to establish legal ownership" is entered, and "The administrator shall allow or deny a claim no later than ninety (90) days after a claim is complete unless the administrator extends the review date for reasonable cause" ([Idaho Code 14-5-904](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title14/T14CH5/SECT14-5-904/)).
- Search every other state where you have lived using the state treasurers' association site [unclaimed.org](https://unclaimed.org/) (MEASURED): NAUPA states "Most states participate in MissingMoney.com, a free website, managed by NAUPA, from which you can search participating state's databases for unclaimed property," and provides direct links to every state's official government program ([NAUPA unclaimed.org](https://unclaimed.org/)).
- Check open federal refund cases on the official [FTC Active Refund Programs page](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/refunds) (MEASURED): "The chart below includes all active refund programs managed by the FTC," listing each case's official claims administrator; check any private class action only through the court-approved settlement administrator named in your official notice email or postcard, and heed the FTC's warning that "The FTC will never threaten you, say you must transfer money to 'get a refund,' or promise you a prize. That's a scam" ([FTC Refunds](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/refunds)).
