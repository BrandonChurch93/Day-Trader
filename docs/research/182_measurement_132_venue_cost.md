# Measurement 132: what a small USD coin order costs

- STATED: [Ledger 227](../../research_rig/TRIAL_LEDGER.md) fixes a forward-only cost measurement with no profit claim. Use the 20 [Plan 70 coins](../../research_rig/PREREG_70_coin_sweep.md), on Coinbase Exchange and Binance.US, USD pairs only. There are 720 wakes over 30 days, starting at the first future UTC hour plus 10 seconds and then at the same offset every hour. Do not replace a coin, venue, pair or wake.
- MEASURED: At each wake, read the Coinbase level-2 book and Binance.US 100-level book for each coin. Keep the best bid, best ask, midpoint and average buy and sell prices for $25 and $250 in the private log, not the raw levels. A valid level has a finite positive price and size. Bids must strictly descend, asks must strictly ascend, and the best bid must be below the best ask. Mark a failed check “bad book” with no price or cost. Walk the levels until the order uses or receives the whole dollar amount; mark a side that cannot fill “too thin” with no fill price or cost. Report costs in bp: divide the gap to the midpoint by that midpoint and multiply by 10,000, then add one taker fee per side.
- STATED: The `measure_132` settings use the lowest-volume tier from the [Binance.US fee schedule](https://www.binance.us/fees) (2 bp, or 1 bp for BNB/USD) and the [Coinbase Exchange fee schedule](https://help.coinbase.com/en/exchange/trading-and-funding/exchange-fees) (refer to the existing `replay_47_plan` taker fee). The [fee survey](180_round_57_cost_as_the_binding_limit.md) checked both on 2026-11-26. Coinbase Exchange is not Coinbase Advanced, and no fee discount is assumed.
- STATED: Check Binance.US exchangeInfo once at start. A pair marked missing or nontrading then is excluded throughout this run. This is a start-time roster, not proof the pair remains unavailable. If metadata fails without a venue refusal, the pair status is UNKNOWN and the logger still tries the exact USD book. Pace each host to at most 2.5 requests per second with no more than two retries on HTTP 429. Honor Retry-After up to an 8-second wait or stop retrying. A timeout fails that request. HTTP 401, 403 or 451 stops the logger and records the host and status. Do not switch routes around a refusal.
- STATED: Start collection no earlier than +10 seconds, with a 120-second window from the scheduled wake. Each HTTP attempt has a 5-second total limit or the time left in the window, whichever is shorter. No request starts after the window and no late response is used. A wake cut short by the window is counted as partial; a missed or interrupted wake is marked void when next checked, never fetched later.
- STATED: Start pins SHA-256 for this plan, the logger and the settings block, plus the resolved settings and first and last wake. The private folder must resolve outside all Git worktrees and have mode 0700; logs, locks and the tail anchor have mode 0600. The logger syncs new directory entries, then writes an empty anchor before the first row. It syncs each log append and atomically updates and syncs the anchor before requesting any data for a claimed wake. A log and anchor that disagree refuse without network calls. If only the final line is torn and the last complete row matches the anchor, a writing command replaces the torn bytes with a recovery row. A previously claimed wake is voided and never fetched again. A crash after a complete write but before the anchor update refuses for review.
- MEASURED: Alive and publish show counts and the pooled median and 90th percentile cost by venue, size and side, across all coins. They do not expose per-coin observations, quotes, or returns. The lead reads the detailed output once after the last wake's window; the time gate does not check identity or prevent a second read. The table shows costs by coin, venue, size and side for all hours, 00:00 UTC ([Plan 75](181_plan_75_heldback_result.md)'s order time), and BTC drop hours. Use the middle value or mean of the middle two for the median and the smallest observed value with at least 90% at or below it for p90.
