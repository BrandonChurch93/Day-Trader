# Measurement 132: what a small USD coin order costs

- STATED: [Ledger 227](../../research_rig/TRIAL_LEDGER.md) fixes a forward-only cost measurement with no profit claim. Use the 20 [Plan 70 coins](../../research_rig/PREREG_70_coin_sweep.md), on Coinbase Exchange and Binance.US, USD pairs only. There are 720 wakes over 30 days, starting at the first future UTC hour plus 10 seconds and then at the same offset every hour. Do not replace a coin, venue, pair or wake.
- MEASURED: At each wake, read the Coinbase level-2 book and Binance.US 100-level book for each coin. Keep the best bid, best ask, midpoint and average buy and sell prices for $25 and $250 in the private log, not the raw levels. Walk asks from low to high and bids from high to low until the order uses or receives the whole dollar amount. If a side cannot fill, mark it “too thin” with no fill price or cost. Report costs in bp: divide the gap to the midpoint by that midpoint and multiply by 10,000, then add one taker fee per side.
- STATED: The `measure_132` settings use the lowest-volume tier from the [Binance.US fee schedule](https://www.binance.us/fees) (2 bp, or 1 bp for BNB/USD) and the [Coinbase Exchange fee schedule](https://help.coinbase.com/en/exchange/trading-and-funding/exchange-fees) (refer to the existing `replay_47_plan` taker fee). The [fee survey](180_round_57_cost_as_the_binding_limit.md) checked both on 2026-11-26. Coinbase Exchange is not Coinbase Advanced, and no fee discount is assumed.
- STATED: Check Binance.US exchangeInfo once at start. A known missing or nontrading pair is counted and has no price. If metadata fails without a venue refusal, the pair status is UNKNOWN and the logger still tries the exact USD book. Pace each host to at most 2.5 requests per second with no more than two retries on HTTP 429. Honor Retry-After up to an 8-second wait or stop retrying. A timeout fails that request. HTTP 401, 403 or 451 stops the logger and records the host and status. Do not switch routes around a refusal.
- STATED: Start collection no earlier than +10 seconds, with a 120-second window from the scheduled wake. No request starts after the window and no late response is used. Missed or interrupted wakes are marked void when next checked, never fetched later. Start pins SHA-256 for this plan, the logger and the canonical settings block, along with the resolved settings and first and last wake. The private chain and locks use a 0700 directory and 0600 files.
- MEASURED: Alive and publish show counts and the pooled median and 90th percentile cost by venue, size and side, across all coins. They do not expose per-coin observations, quotes, or returns. Read unlocks after the last wake's window. It prints the cost table by coin, venue, size and side for all hours, 00:00 UTC ([Plan 75](181_plan_75_heldback_result.md)'s order time), and BTC drop hours. Use the middle value or mean of the middle two for the median and the smallest observed value with at least 90% at or below it for p90.
- STATED: Clarification, lead agent, before any measurement quote was recorded: a coin qualifies only with paired buy and sell $250 costs at 25 or more of the 30 midnight wakes and each side's own median at or under 25 bp; 15 or more qualifying coins meets the line; no Coinbase comparison gate. Calculate each side's median from its recorded costs in that group. The signal-hour split uses bitcoin's last closed hour, close to close against the adjacent prior candle, at E09's 70-102 log-drop threshold. If either candle is missing, count that hour as UNKNOWN for signal status, not as a drop or non-drop; it stays in the all-hours group and the midnight group if applicable.
- STATED: If the Binance.US line is not met, keep 75 bp per side as the main cost line. Meeting the line changes only the cost line for new plans. Do not rescore a finished test or open an account. No strategy return is computed.
- UNKNOWN: A snapshot once an hour is not a fill, and books are read in sequence, not at the exact same instant. This is taker only, with no maker fill evidence. Our host's network path is not the owner's. The owner starts the logger after audit.
