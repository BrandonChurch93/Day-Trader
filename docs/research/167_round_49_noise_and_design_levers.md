# Round 49: Reducing LLM Trader Run-to-Run Noise and Testing Design Levers

- Written: 2026-11-26 18:21 UTC.
- Status: CURRENT.
- Scope: Controlled published evidence (ablations, repeated seeds, paired designs) on (A) what reduces run-to-run noise of an LLM trader without changing its average behavior, and (B) which non-news design levers show a measured positive, null, or negative return effect under repeat runs, costs, no look-ahead, and post-cutoff dates.
- Baseline from our own records: MEASURED in `docs/research/163_tm09_power_count.md`, our six TM04 same-model stock repeat pairs imply a pooled weekly repeat-gap SD of `91.4 bp` a week (`64.4` to `113.5 bp` across models), giving about `22.6%` two-mark power at `+20 bp` a week over 20 weeks, whereas cutting primary SD in half lifts two-mark power to `77.1%`.
- Already covered in repo and not repeated: News-vs-no-news ablations (`docs/research/162_round_48_what_news_adds_to_an_ai_trader.md`), FinCon, FinAgent, FINSABER, FinPanel, Alpha Arena, AgentRouter (`docs/research/03_llm_trading_agents_evidence.md`), `rta-bench-1` seed gaps (`docs/research/18_ai_systems_with_live_records.md`), and general protocol rules (`docs/research/11_ai_trader_experiment_design.md`).
- Label key: MEASURED means our own repo measurements; STATED means a directly opened external source reports the claim (with exact link and quoted sentence); UNKNOWN means no controlled published measurement was found.

## Short Answer

- STATED: No single prompt tweak eliminates LLM trading noise, because even at `T=0` with a fixed seed, hosted and local LLMs still show `4.3%` SD over 13 weeks in [TradingAgents Reproducibility](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3800973.3801029) and `0.36` to `0.88` pairwise action agreement across models in [AlphaForgeBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18481).
- STATED: Averaging `k` parallel sessions of the same model does NOT reduce variance like `1/sqrt(k)`, because same-model samples are correlated at `rho = 0.85` (`N_eff` about `1.1` independent voters), whereas combining distinct model families lowers `rho` to `0.38` (`N_eff = 2.0`) in [Correlation limits the reliability of ensemble decision making with large language models](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44564-026-00040-z).
- STATED: The two noise-reduction levers with the clearest controlled evidence are (1) moving execution into deterministic code driven by structured model outputs or discrete grids ([AlphaForgeBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18481), [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3), [Macro-to-Tilt Ablation](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08283)), and (2) adding execution inertia or lower rebalance frequency ([Can Blindfolded LLMs Still Trade?](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17692), [ChatGPT in Systematic Investing](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26228)).
- STATED: Across post-cutoff, cost-aware, repeated-seed ablations (Part B), reflective memory is null or negative ([CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3), [On the Role of Tools](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4283/paper196.pdf)), pairwise debate hurts Sharpe and widens variance ([Market Regime Council](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.24490v2)), deterministic risk overlays cut drawdowns while trimming average return ([Representation Signatures](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28850), [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3)), and only technical-indicator tools plus slower rebalancing show repeatable positive post-cost Sharpe effects ([Toward Expert Investment Teams](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23330), [ChatGPT in Systematic Investing](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26228)).

## Part A: What Reduces Run-to-Run Noise Without Changing Average Behavior

### Temperature and Deterministic Decoding

- STATED: In [TradingAgents Reproducibility](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3800973.3801029) (GOOGL, May to July 2025, post-cutoff, 10 runs per setting), lowering `Qwen3:30B` temperature from `T=1` to `T=0` cut 13-week return SD from `9.0%` to `5.5%` (a `39%` SD reduction), and fixing `seed=42` at `T=0` cut SD further to `4.3%`, with zero extra API cost and no statistically significant change in mean return (`18.1%`, `21.3%`, and `16.8%`, `p > 0.26`): "Reducing temperature to 0 (C3) decreases the standard deviation to 5.5%, demonstrating that lower temperature constrains financial variability."
- STATED: In [TradingAgents Reproducibility](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3800973.3801029), full zero variance required locking all four local sampling controls (`T=0, seed=42, top_k=1, top_p=0`), which hides seed dependence rather than removing it: "Only the fully deterministic configuration (T = 0, seed = 42, top_k = 1, top_p = 0) achieves zero entropy and zero variance, but its 28.2% return reflects the specific choice of seed = 42 and constitutes a form of cherry-picking."
- STATED: In [AlphaForgeBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18481) (BTC daily, 2025, 5 runs per model, zero cost), direct per-bar trading remained highly unstable at `T=0` across six frontier models, with pairwise run agreement ranging from `0.36` to `0.48` (`gemini-3-pro-preview`) up to `88.2%` (`gpt-5.2`): "Even with temperature=0 (deterministic decoding), LLMs generate completely different trading action sequences across runs on identical market data, exhibiting no consistency in decision-making patterns."
- STATED: In [Evaluating "Thinking" LLMs for Stock Prediction](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08608) (NIFTY equities, walk-forward, net of brokerage, slippage, STT, and borrow costs), switching from a thinking/reasoning model (`gpt-5`, `B=512`) to a direct non-reasoning LLM (`gpt-4o-mini`, `B=0`) lowered date-by-date dispersion and improved calibrated net Sharpe from `-5.667` (raw calibrated TLLM) and `-0.426` (calibrated+blended TLLM) to `+1.471` (direct LLM): "TLLM outputs exhibit higher dispersion date–by–date than direct LLMs, increasing error bars and turnover."

### Majority Vote or Averaging Across k Parallel Sessions

- STATED: In [Correlation limits the reliability of ensemble decision making with large language models](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44564-026-00040-z) (562 MMLU questions, September 2026), averaging `N` exchangeable votes with pairwise error correlation `rho` gives variance proportional to `rho + (1 - rho) / N`, so the effective number of independent voters `N_eff = N / [1 + (N - 1) * rho]` never exceeds `1 / rho`: "The effective number of independent voters, a variance-based effective sample size, is capped by the reciprocal of the correlation: a hundred agents correlated at 0.3 have the variance of about three independent ones."
- STATED: In [Correlation limits the reliability of ensemble decision making with large language models](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44564-026-00040-z), five parallel temperature samples (`T=0.8`) or five persona prompts of the same model (`gpt-4.1-mini`) both measured `rho = 0.85` (`95% CI 0.81` to `0.89`), yielding `N_eff` of about `1.1` independent voters at `5x` token cost and zero accuracy gain over the best single member: "The two cosmetic ensembles are almost indistinguishable: temperature samples and personas of the base model are both correlated at 0.85 (95% interval 0.81 to 0.89), each ensemble is worth about 1.1 independent voters, and its plurality vote is no more accurate than its best member (0.843 against 0.843 for temperature, 0.838 against 0.847 for personas)."
- STATED: In the same study ([Correlation limits the reliability of ensemble decision making with large language models](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44564-026-00040-z)), five models from different developers (`gpt-4.1-mini`, `Mistral-Large-3`, `DeepSeek-V3.2`, `Kimi-K2.5`, `grok-4.3`) cut error correlation to `rho = 0.38` (`95% CI 0.30` to `0.45`), raising `N_eff` to `2.0` and plurality accuracy to `0.923` (`+4.5 pp` above average member, though still below the single best member `0.940`): "The family ensemble differs in kind. Its correlation is 0.38 (0.30 to 0.45), with pairwise values between 0.23 and 0.48; its effective size is 2.0; and its plurality vote reaches 0.923, 8.4 points above the base model and 4.5 above the average member."
- UNKNOWN: No controlled post-cutoff trading study directly measures `N_eff` of averaging `k` parallel equity-portfolio sessions of the same model as a function of `k` (`k = 1, 3, 5, 9`) with weekly return SD reported.

### Structured Output With Fixed Position Grids

- STATED: In [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3) (~72 S&P 100 equities, 26 bi-weekly rounds June 2025 to June 2026, post-cutoff, LOW cost tier, 5 seeds), structured workflow mode outperformed unconstrained autonomous mode on every reliable model tested (`deepseek-chat-v3.1`: Sharpe `2.99` vs `1.67`; `claude-haiku-4.5`: `3.21` vs `1.92`; `gpt-5-mini`: `2.62` vs `1.76`; `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`: `2.31` vs `1.75`), with zero constraint violations across all 13 structured ablation runs; per-mode SD is UNKNOWN because Table 5 prints means without per-mode SD.
- STATED: In [Macro-to-Tilt Ablation](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08283) (15 US commodity-related ETFs, 124 weekly rounds October 2023 to February 2026, `gpt-4o-mini` at `T=0`, API cost `$0.002` per weekly observation, `0` to `30 bp` one-way costs), constraining the LLM output contract to a five-level discrete tilt grid (`{-2, -1, 0, +1, +2}`) fed into a deterministic inverse-volatility allocator with `0.30` single-name cap and `0.50` weekly turnover cap kept annual return and Sharpe tightly clustered across Hawkish (`7.74%`, Sharpe `0.57`), Dovish (`7.61%`, Sharpe `0.56`), Debate (`7.69%`, Sharpe `0.57`), and deterministic Rule (`7.11%`, Sharpe `0.53`) agents: "all strategies share the same data pipeline and the same portfolio construction engine."
- UNKNOWN: Neither study reports a paired repeated-seed SD comparison of a discrete tilt grid (`{-2, -1, 0, +1, +2}`) against free-form dollar sizing on the same model and dates.

### Fewer Decisions Per Week (Rebalance Cadence)

- STATED: In [ChatGPT in Systematic Investing](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26228) (S&P 500 equities, post-cutoff test January 2024 to March 2025, `gpt-4o-mini` October 2023 cutoff, `2 bp` transaction costs), cutting decision frequency from weekly to monthly raised out-of-sample Sharpe from roughly `0.7` to `1.1` while cutting API calls by about `75%`: "Panel (a) shows that the LLM-enhanced strategy performs remarkably better at a monthly rebalancing frequency than at a weekly one, with Sharpe ratios of approximately 1.1 and 0.7, respectively. This suggests that the cost of higher turnover outweighs the benefits of more frequent signal updates."
- UNKNOWN: [ChatGPT in Systematic Investing](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26228) did not report repeated-seed return SD for weekly versus monthly cadence, so the exact run-to-run SD reduction from fewer decisions per week remains unmeasured in a multi-seed equity benchmark.

### Turnover Caps and Execution Inertia

- STATED: In [Can Blindfolded LLMs Still Trade?](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17692) (point-in-time anonymized S&P 500 stocks, 2025 OOS, `10 bp` per unit turnover, 20 seeds), replacing naive equal-weight top-20 rebalancing with cost-aware execution smoothing (`rebalance_eta = 0.08` to `0.10`) cut daily turnover from `139%/day` to `1.7%/day` and turned net Sharpe from `-1.17` to `+1.08 ± 0.31` (full system `1.40`): "What if we simply invest equal weight in the top 20 GNN scores? Turnover reaches 139%/day, and after cost deduction, Sharpe collapses to -1.17. The RL policy suppresses turnover to 1.7%/day while achieving Sharpe 1.08±0.31."
- STATED: In [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3) (5 seeds, 52 weeks), removing cost-modeling and systematic execution increased realized trading friction by `18%` while leaving Sharpe inside the `±0.42` noise band (`+0.19` mean, range `[+0.04, +0.44]`).
- UNKNOWN: No controlled multi-seed study directly isolates the change in same-model run-to-run weekly return SD from adding a hard turnover cap or weight-change deadband alone.

### Rule Layer That Executes the Model's Stated Thresholds or Code

- STATED: In [AlphaForgeBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18481) (7 assets: BTC, ETH, AAPL, GOOGL, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA; 270 structured queries; 5 generations per query at `T=0` and `T=0.7`), moving the LLM from per-bar trading decisions to emitting deterministic strategy code cut execution variance to zero, held intra-query Sharpe SD across five generations to `0.02` to `0.06` (an order of magnitude below inter-query SD of `0.26` to `0.28`), and kept mean Sharpe difference between `T=0` and `T=0.7` at `0.001` to `0.008` across all six models: "Under the code-generation paradigm, the backtest execution is fully deterministic: given the same generated code and the same market data, the output metrics are identical with zero variance."
- STATED: In [AlphaForgeBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18481), this rule-layer shift also replaced negative direct-trading Sharpe ratios with positive code-execution Sharpe ratios (`0.415` to `0.628` across the six models) while cutting inference from one LLM call per bar to one call per strategy, though the paper's 2021 to 2026 code-generation window is not post-cutoff clean and has conflicting friction notes (`10^-3` in main text vs `0` in Appendix E).

### Committee and Debate Designs

- STATED: In [Market Regime Council](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.24490v2) (13 crypto assets, 1,037 trading days March 2023 to December 2025, 5 seeds, `0 bp` main table with `5` and `10 bp` sensitivity), pairwise Socratic debate between specialist agents degraded Sharpe relative to the best individual specialist (`1.34`, `1.10`, and `0.98` vs `1.54`) AND preserved or amplified inter-seed variance (CR SD up to `±95.2%`, SR SD up to `±0.28` vs `±0.05` to `±0.22` for solo specialists): "Stage 2 pairwise coalitions consistently underperform the best Stage 1 individual portfolio on SR (1.34, 1.10, and 0.98 versus 1.54) while preserving or amplifying inter-seed variance, with A1 ⊕ A3 CR variance reaching ±95.2%, and offer no compensating improvement in MDD."
- STATED: In [Market Regime Council](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.24490v2), replacing free-form debate with numerical Shapley-weighted coalition aggregation cut 5-seed Sharpe SD to `±0.07`, cut max drawdown by `13.4 pp` (`34.1%` vs `47.5%`), and achieved the only statistically significant IR gain (`0.47 ± 0.20`, `p < 0.05`) at a cost of `0.03` Sharpe (`1.51` vs `1.54`): "The full MRC system records SR = 1.51 and MDD = 34.1%, reducing MDD by 13.4 percentage points relative to A1 alone at a cost of only 0.03 in SR, tightening seed-level SR variance to ±0.07, and producing the only statistically significant IR advantage in the table (p<0.05)."
- STATED: In [Multi-Agent Debate for Explainable Trading](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.29701) (3 quarterly equity scenarios 2022 to 2023, pre-cutoff, zero transaction costs, `N = 2` to `3` runs), 3-agent enriched debate lowered drawdown (`5.33%` vs `9.73%`) and raised Sharpe (`0.76 ± 0.48` vs `0.35 ± 0.17`) while WIDENING return SEM (`±1.74%` vs `±0.99%`), and 4-agent 5-round debate turned returns negative (`-0.27%`, Sharpe `-0.05`): "The 4-agent 5-round debate produces negative returns (-0.27%) despite maintaining stable per-round CRIT scores (ρ̄ ≈ 0.83–0.85, B.4), illustrating that extended debate can degrade performance through over-convergence."
- STATED: In [Macro-to-Tilt Ablation](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08283) (124 weekly rounds, `0` to `30 bp` costs), adding a Debate Agent over the average of Hawkish and Dovish single agents changed Sharpe by `+0.001` (`p = 0.769` vs Hawkish, `p = 0.163` vs Dovish), a clean null at extra token cost.

## Part B: Design Levers in Controlled Tests (Positives, Nulls, and Negatives)

### Memory and Reflection

- STATED (NULL / NEGATIVE): In [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3) (~72 US equities, post-cutoff 2025 to 2026, LOW costs, 5 seeds), removing memory raised Sharpe by `+0.52` (range `[+0.24, +0.72]`), removing reflection raised Sharpe by `+0.09` (`[-0.16, +0.39]`), and removing both (`no-learning`) raised Sharpe by `+0.53` (`[+0.12, +0.78]`, cumulative return `+29.6%`): "Every module-off ΔSharpe is positive (means +0.09…+0.66): removal never cleanly hurts, which is mechanistically implausible."
- STATED (NEGATIVE): In [On the Role of Tools in Agentic LLM-Based Trading Systems](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4283/paper196.pdf) (TSLA and BTC, chronological walk-forward August 2024 to January 2026, `ChatGPT-4.1-nano`, single run, costs unspecified), adding 5-day or 10-day rolling reflective memory degraded TSLA Sharpe from `-0.76` to `-1.14` (5d) and `-0.85` (10d) and left BTC Sharpe negative (`-0.27` and `-0.04` vs `-0.29`): "Contrary to our hypothesis, the introduction of reflective memory consistently deteriorates performance across both assets."
- STATED (TINY POSITIVE ON SYNTHETIC ONLY): In [Representation Signatures and Risk-Feedback Alignment](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28850) (3 synthetic assets, seeds 3, 7, 11, with slippage), a mechanical memory-aware exposure overlay (cutting exposure after recent drawdowns or rejected orders) slightly improved return (`0.427` vs `0.423`) and Sharpe (`7.878` vs `7.696`), but this is a synthetic risk-feedback throttle rather than free-text LLM reflection.

### Tool Access to Quantitative Indicators

- STATED (POSITIVE, POST-CUTOFF, COSTED, 50 TRIALS): In [Toward Expert Investment Teams](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23330) (TOPIX 100 Japanese equities, post-cutoff September 2023 to November 2025 with `GPT-4o` August 2023 cutoff, `10 bp` one-way cost, 50 independent trials, monthly rebalance), removing the fine-grained Technical indicator agent (RoC, MACD, RSI, KDJ, volatility) caused statistically significant Sharpe drops across portfolio sizes 20 to 50 (`-0.42`, `-0.40`, `-0.56`, `-0.66`, all `p < 0.0001`), whereas removing the Quantitative, Qualitative, News, or Macro agents IMPROVED Sharpe (`+0.12` to `+0.50`): "In the fine-grained setting (Table 2a), however, the 'w/o Technical' condition shows predominantly negative differences, especially for larger portfolio sizes. This implies that the Technical Agent provides particularly strong predictive signals."
- STATED (MIXED / NULL): In [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3) (post-cutoff, 5 seeds, LOW costs), removing `advanced-quant` tools changed Sharpe by `+0.16` (range `[-0.06, +0.32]`, inside the `±0.42` noise band). In [On the Role of Tools](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4283/paper196.pdf), replacing standard technical-indicator tools with a random-forest ML signal improved BTC Sharpe (`-0.29` to `+0.15`) and TSLA with naive news (`+0.31` SR) but hurt TSLA with enhanced news (`-0.24` SR).
- STATED (POSITIVE WHEN ANONYMIZED + COSTED + 20 SEEDS): In [Can Blindfolded LLMs Still Trade?](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17692) (point-in-time anonymized S&P 500, 2025 OOS, `10 bp` cost, 20 seeds), keeping LLM-interpreted technical+news features in a GNN+RL pipeline beat RAW technical indicators alone (`Sharpe 1.40` vs `1.14 ± 0.02`, `p < 10^-4`), though the graph structure contributed three times more (`-0.78` SR) than the LLM features (`-0.26` SR): "If we remove LLM features and use only RAW technical indicators (passed directly to the GNN without SBERT encoding), Sharpe drops from 1.40 to 1.14±0.02 (Δ = -0.26, p < 10^-4, 20 seeds)."

### Role Split (Scout and Boss / Multi-Agent vs Single-Agent)

- STATED (NULL / NEGATIVE): In [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3) (post-cutoff, 5 seeds, LOW costs), stripping the entire multi-module analyst and learning stack down to `bare-workflow` (mandate + cost-aware execution only) matched or beat the full multi-agent stack on returns (`+27.3%`, Sharpe `2.52` vs `2.26`, `ΔSharpe +0.26`, range `[-0.40, +0.66]`, IR vs SPY `0.00`): "On this benchmark and period, the agentic scaffolding contributes little to Sharpe, while the axes register what returns hide (Table 7)."
- STATED (NEGATIVE ON EXTRA ROLES): In [Toward Expert Investment Teams](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23330) (post-cutoff, `10 bp` cost, 50 trials), removing any non-technical specialist role (Quantitative, Qualitative, News, Macro) in the fine-grained multi-agent team raised Sharpe by `+0.12` to `+0.50`, showing that extra analyst roles injected noise or redundancy: "Overall, most ablation settings show positive differences relative to the full-agent configuration, indicating that most individual agents may introduce noise or redundant signals."
- UNKNOWN: No controlled post-cutoff, cost-included, multi-seed stock study shows that splitting a price scout from a boss improves net return over a single model seeing the same structured price table directly.

### Longer Horizon (Weekly vs Monthly vs Daily)

- STATED (POSITIVE, POST-CUTOFF, COSTED): In [ChatGPT in Systematic Investing](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26228) (S&P 500, post-cutoff 2024 to 2025, `2 bp` cost), monthly rebalancing beat weekly rebalancing by roughly `+0.4` Sharpe (`~1.1` vs `~0.7`) because lower turnover preserved net returns.
- UNKNOWN: No multi-seed post-cutoff study isolates holding-period length (1 week vs 4 weeks) on the same LLM stock desk with repeated-run variance reported.

### Risk Overlays (Position Caps, Drawdown Gates, Vol Scaling)

- STATED (CUTS DRAWDOWN, LOWERS AVERAGE RETURN): In [Representation Signatures and Risk-Feedback Alignment](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28850) (120 heterogeneous synthetic markets with slippage and fills), enabling the deterministic max-position risk gate lowered average return by `-0.045` (`95% CI [-0.059, -0.030]`, `p < 10^-9`) while cutting max drawdown (`+0.015`, `p < 10^-11`, `80%` win rate), raising fill rate (`+0.200`, `p < 10^-12`), and cutting rejected orders by `38.2` per market: "Compared with the no-risk ablation, the risk-aware agent has lower return on average (-0.045, p<10^-9) but materially better drawdown, much higher fill rate, and 38.2 fewer rejected orders per market."
- STATED (CUTS DRAWDOWN, SLIGHT SHARPE TRADE-OFF): In [ChatGPT in Systematic Investing](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26228) (S&P 500, post-cutoff 2024 to 2025, `2 bp` cost), enforcing a `15%` single-stock weight cap improved risk management at a small Sharpe cost: "Applying diversification constraints to portfolio weights reduces the Sharpe ratio slightly, but results in substantial improvements in terms of risk-management."
- STATED (INFERRED DRAWDOWN PROTECTION): In [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3) (post-cutoff, 5 seeds, LOW costs), hard position caps and the staged risk-constraint layer held max drawdown between `-3.2%` and `-4.7%` across all 13 module ablations (versus `-8.9%` for SPY), though the constraint layer was not ablated alone: "The attribution is by elimination, since the constraint layer is not ablated alone."

## Three Concrete, Cheap Changes to Our Desk Tests (Queued as Rows 122 to 124)

Each proposal targets the MEASURED `91.4 bp` weekly repeat-gap SD in `docs/research/163_tm09_power_count.md` using levers supported by the controlled evidence above, before spending judged weeks on TM09.

1. **Row 122: Deterministic code executor on a fixed five-level tilt grid (`{-2, -1, 0, +1, +2}`) at `T=0` with a `2 pp` turnover deadband.** STATED by [AlphaForgeBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18481), [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3), [Macro-to-Tilt Ablation](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08283), and [Can Blindfolded LLMs Still Trade?](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17692), replacing free-form order sizing with structured discrete tilts executed by deterministic portfolio code removes execution-side noise and suppresses excess turnover. What we would measure: same-model weekly repeat-gap SD (`P1` minus `P2`), weekly turnover, and mean weekly net return versus the `91.4 bp` baseline. Count needed: MEASURED in `docs/research/163_tm09_power_count.md`, **7 unused post-cutoff price-only stock weeks** (28 boss sessions across 2 bosses x 2 repeats) bound the repeat-gap SD within a factor of `2` at `90%` confidence (`14` weeks / `56` sessions for a factor of `1.5`), testing whether SD falls below `45 bp` a week.
2. **Row 123: Cross-family averaging (one `T=0` session per distinct non-reasoning model family) instead of `k` repeats of one model.** STATED by [Correlation limits the reliability of ensemble decision making with large language models](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44564-026-00040-z), [TradingAgents Reproducibility](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3800973.3801029), and [Evaluating "Thinking" LLMs for Stock Prediction](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08608), same-model repeats correlate near `rho = 0.85` (`N_eff` about `1.1`) and reasoning models have higher dispersion than direct models, whereas distinct model families cut error correlation to `rho = 0.38`. What we would measure: pairwise weekly return correlation `rho` within-model (`P1` vs `P2`) versus across model families (`Boss A` vs `Boss B`), implied `N_eff`, and the weekly SD of the cross-family average versus the same-model average. Count needed: **7 to 14 unused post-cutoff price-only stock weeks** (reuses the same 28 to 56 boss sessions as Row 122 if run on the same weeks with 2 to 4 direct model families).
3. **Row 124: Single weekly wake with pre-computed technical indicator tools and deterministic caps, keeping reflection and debate off.** STATED by [ChatGPT in Systematic Investing](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26228), [Toward Expert Investment Teams](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23330), [CLQT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29771v3), [On the Role of Tools](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4283/paper196.pdf), and [Market Regime Council](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.24490v2), fewer rebalances and technical-indicator inputs are the only levers with post-cutoff costed Sharpe gains, while reflection and debate are null or harmful. What we would measure: weekly repeat-gap SD (`P1` minus `P2`), weekly turnover, and mean weekly net return for a single-wake weekly schedule (with pre-computed local technical indicators and hard single-name caps, no intra-week wakes, no reflection, no debate) versus the multi-wake TM09 schedule. Count needed: **7 unused post-cutoff price-only stock weeks** (28 boss sessions, cutting API calls roughly `80%` relative to five daily wakes per week) to measure whether weekly repeat-gap SD drops by at least `25%` without degrading average net return.
