# Brokers, Market Data, and Legal/Regulatory/Tax Constraints for an Automated Retail Trading System (US stocks + crypto)

Research date: November 24, 2026. Scope: an individual in Idaho trading only their own money through AI agents. Evidence tags: MEASURED = official rule text, regulator page, or vendor's own pricing/limits page (a primary source for what the vendor charges or allows); STATED = vendor marketing or secondary-source claim not independently verified; UNKNOWN = could not verify. A vendor pricing page is tagged MEASURED for the price itself, but any vendor claim about quality, speed, or reliability is STATED.

## Headline: the Pattern Day Trader rule is gone (mostly)

- The SEC approved FINRA's amendments to Rule 4210 on April 14, 2026 (File SR-FINRA-2025-017, Release 34-105226), and FINRA set an effective date of June 4, 2026 ([FINRA Regulatory Notice 26-10](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/Regulatory-Notice-26-10.pdf); [Federal Register approval order](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/04/17/2026-07485.txt)). MEASURED.
- The new intraday margin standards "replace in their entirety" the day-trade-count test, the PDT designation, and the $25,000 PDT minimum equity requirement ([FINRA RN 26-10](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/Regulatory-Notice-26-10.pdf)). MEASURED.
- Brokers that need more time may phase in implementation until October 20, 2027, so some brokers may still apply legacy PDT logic today ([Investor.gov margin bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/margin)). MEASURED. Verify with each broker.
- How the new rule works: for each margin account the broker computes an "intraday margin deficit" (the largest gap between required maintenance margin and equity after any exposure-increasing trade); the deficit must be met "as promptly as possible," stays outstanding up to 15 business days, and a customer who makes a practice of not meeting deficits by the 5th business day gets a 90-day freeze on new short positions or debit balances; deficits under the lesser of 5% of equity or $1,000 are excluded from the "practice" test ([FINRA RN 26-10](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/Regulatory-Notice-26-10.pdf)). MEASURED. Real-time monitoring by the broker is permitted but not required (same source).
- Broker status: Alpaca says it has lifted PDT in production for Trading API users ([Alpaca blog](https://alpaca.markets/blog/finra-retires-the-pdt-rule-introducing-alpacas-new-intraday-margin-framework/)), allows unlimited day trades, and says margin accounts still typically need $2,000 equity for intraday debits or shorts, with intraday margin calls due in two business days ([Alpaca Intraday Margin Rule doc](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/the-intraday-margin-rule-1)). Schwab stopped counting day trades on June 8, 2026 ([Schwab](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/schwab-changes-rules-around-day-trading)). MEASURED (broker's own pages).
- The $2,000 margin minimum is a Reg T/FINRA baseline the Notice does not mention; cash accounts are not covered by the new deficit regime ([FINRA RN 26-10](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/Regulatory-Notice-26-10.pdf)). MEASURED.

Implication: a small account can now day trade without $25k, but in a cash account the binding constraint becomes settlement (below), and in a margin account it becomes intraday margin deficits and the 90-day freeze.

## 4. Trading mechanics and market rules

- Cash accounts and settlement: Reg T requires full payment; buying with unsettled proceeds and selling before they settle is a good-faith violation, and selling before paying can trigger a 90-day freeze ([Investor.gov cash accounts bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/updated-9); [SEC 90-day freeze bulletin](https://www.sec.gov/files/investor/alerts/cashaccounts.pdf)). MEASURED. US equities settle T+1, so a cash-account bot can only recycle each dollar roughly once per day.
- Short selling: Reg SHO requires a documented "locate" before a short sale ([Federal Register, Reg SHO](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/html/2012/12/18/2012-30427.html)); Rule 201 restricts short sales at or below the national best bid once a stock drops 10% from the prior close ([SEC Rule 201 FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/divisions/marketreg/rule201faq.htm)). MEASURED. The bot must handle "not shortable" and short-sale-restricted states.
- Halts: LULD pauses single stocks on sudden moves ([SEC investor bulletin](https://www.sec.gov/file/circuitbreakersbulletinhtm)); market-wide circuit breakers trigger at 7%, 13%, and 20% S&P 500 declines ([Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/stock-market-circuit-breakers)). MEASURED. Stops do not execute during a halt, and gaps on reopen can blow through them.

## 5. Legal questions

- Is it legal to run an AI bot on your own account? Yes. No rule found prohibits an individual from automating their own account; major brokers now ship official agent products for exactly this ([Robinhood](https://robinhood.com/newsroom/robinhood-is-now-open-to-agents/); [IBKR](https://portal.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/ai-integrations.php); [Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/mcp-server)). Conditions: you are fully responsible for every order ([IBKR disclosure](https://portal.interactivebrokers.com/Universal/servlet/Registration_v2.formSampleView?formdb=4930&preferredFormat=html&lang=en)), and the use must stay within each broker's API agreement, e.g. personal non-commercial use for Public ([Public API agreement](https://public.com/disclosures/individual-api-program)). MEASURED.
Manipulation a bot must not commit:

- Spoofing/layering: placing non-bona fide orders to move price and trading the other side. The SEC charged an individual day trader for this, with $1.8M in profits ([SEC v. Xie](https://www.sec.gov/enforce/33-10989-s)). In futures and other CFTC-registered markets, CEA 4c(a)(5)(C) defines spoofing as bidding or offering with intent to cancel before execution ([CFTC fact sheet](https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/idc/groups/public/@newsroom/documents/file/dtp_factsheet.pdf)). MEASURED. A bot that rapidly posts and cancels large orders it never intends to fill creates this risk even without bad intent being coded in. Cap the cancel-to-fill ratio and order size relative to displayed depth.
- Wash trades and self-trades: FINRA treats trades with no change in beneficial ownership as a problem, including trades from "a single algorithm or related algorithms" ([FINRA Rule 5210](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/5210); [FINRA RN 14-28](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/14-28)). MEASURED. If multiple agents trade the same symbol (or the same coin across accounts), add self-trade prevention so they never cross with each other.
- When registration is needed: anyone paid for securities advice who is not excluded must register as an investment adviser with the SEC or the state ([SEC investment advisers](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/investment-advisers)); for futures/commodity advice, the CTA exemption covers 15 or fewer persons in 12 months for someone not holding out to the public ([NFA CTA](https://www.nfa.futures.org/registration-membership/who-has-to-register/cta.html); [17 CFR 4.14](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-4.14)). MEASURED. Trading only your own money needs no registration. Running the bot for friends, family, or coworkers, selling signals, or pooling money changes that.
- Regulator statements on AI: FINRA RN 24-09 (June 2024) reminds member firms that existing rules apply to GenAI use ([FINRA RN 24-09](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/24-09)); the CFTC staff advisory of Dec 5, 2024 says the same for CFTC registrants ([CFTC Letter 24-17](https://www.cftc.gov/csl/24-17/download)). The SEC/NASAA/FINRA joint alert and 2026 enforcement target "AI trading" scams that promise guaranteed returns ([Investor.gov AI fraud alert](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/artificial-intelligence-fraud); [SEC press release 2026-95](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-95-sec-charges-multiple-entities-fraud-schemes-totaling-least-15-million-used-whatsapp-other-platforms)). MEASURED. No rule specific to individuals using AI on their own accounts was found.

## 6. Taxes (federal + Idaho)

- Short-term gains (held one year or less) are taxed as ordinary income; 2026 single brackets: 22% over $50,400, 24% over $105,700, 32% over $201,775, 35% over $256,225, 37% over $640,600 ([IRS 2026 inflation adjustments](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2026-including-amendments-from-the-one-big-beautiful-bill)). MEASURED. Day trading produces almost entirely short-term gains, so for a typical employee they are taxed at 24% to 35% federal plus state. Long-term rates (0/15/20%, with the 20% rate for single filers starting above $545,500 in 2026) rarely apply to day trading ([Kiplinger](https://www.kiplinger.com/taxes/irs-updates-capital-gains-tax-thresholds)).
- Wash sale (stocks): a loss is disallowed if you buy substantially identical stock within 30 days before or after, including purchases in an IRA or Roth IRA ([IRS Pub 550](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550)). MEASURED. A high-frequency bot that re-enters the same tickers will create many wash sales; the disallowed loss is deferred into the new lot's basis, but losses from the last trades of December can end up deferred into the next tax year. Wash sales also apply across accounts and brokers, which no single broker's 1099-B tracks.
- Wash sale (crypto): under current law, wash sale rules do not apply to most spot crypto. H.R. 10357 (Digital Asset Tax Certainty Act) passed Ways and Means 38-5 on Sept 16, 2026 and would apply wash sale rules to sales after Sept 14, 2026, retroactive to its introduction date, if enacted; it has not passed the full House or Senate ([Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/house-panel-advances-crypto-tax-bill-that-would-limit-certain-loss-write-offs/); [KoinX](https://www.koinx.com/us/tax-guides/crypto-wash-sale-rule-usa)). STATED (secondary); bill status after Nov 2026 is UNKNOWN. Design for the retroactive scenario: do not rely on crypto loss harvesting.
- Trader tax status (TTS) requires seeking profit from daily price movements, substantial activity, and continuity and regularity; expenses go on Schedule C ([IRS Topic 429](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc429)). MEASURED. Whether trades made by an autonomous bot count toward "time devoted" is untested. UNKNOWN; get a CPA opinion.
- Section 475(f) mark-to-market: gains and losses become ordinary, reported on Form 4797 Part II, with no wash sale rule and no $3,000 capital-loss limit; the election is due by the unextended due date of the prior year's return (for 2027, by April 15, 2027) ([IRS Topic 429](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc429)). MEASURED. 475(f) covers securities; whether it covers crypto is unsettled (UNKNOWN).
- Forms: brokers report stock sales on 1099-B. For crypto sold after 2025, brokers must report gross proceeds on Form 1099-DA, plus basis for covered digital assets ([IRS 1099-DA instructions 2026](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da)). MEASURED. You reconcile on Form 8949. Keep your own trade-level ledger (timestamp, venue, lot, fees), because brokers may use your acquisition data for lot selection but not for reporting basis ([IRS 1099-DA PDF](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i1099da--2026.pdf)).
- Idaho: flat individual rate of 5.3% effective Jan 1, 2025 ([Idaho State Tax Commission instructions](https://tax.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/forms/EIN00046/EIN00046_03-02-2026.pdf); [Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/location/idaho/page/2/)). MEASURED. Idaho gives no special capital-gains rate for this activity, so short-term gains are taxed at about 5.3% state on top of federal.

## 7. Operational safety

- Account protection: SIPC covers up to $500,000 including $250,000 cash at a failed member broker, and does not cover market losses ([Alpaca SIPC page](https://alpaca.markets/support/is-my-account-insured-what-is-sipc); [SIPC brochure](https://www.sipc.org/media/brochures/HowSIPCProtectsYou-English-Web.pdf)). SIPC does not protect non-security crypto or stablecoins ([SIPC brochure](https://www.sipc.org/media/brochures/HowSIPCProtectsYou-English-Web.pdf)); Alpaca Crypto is not a SIPC member ([Alpaca disclosures](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/additional-resources)). IBKR crypto is custodied at Paxos or zerohash, outside IBKR ([IBKR crypto](https://portal.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/products-cryptocurrencies.php)). MEASURED.
- Ring-fencing: Robinhood MCP accounts limit the agent to funds deposited in that account ([Robinhood onboarding](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/onboarding-an-external-agent/)); IBKR AI Partitions limit cash and products, but revoking access does not cancel pending orders ([IBKR AI integrations](https://portal.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/ai-integrations.php)); Coinbase keys can be restricted to one portfolio ([Coinbase API keys](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-business/authentication-authorization/api-key-authentication)). MEASURED.
- Key hygiene: never enable withdraw/transfer on bot keys. Coinbase separates View, Trade, and Transfer; Kraken warns that "Add Withdrawal Addresses" combined with "Withdraw Funds" lets an attacker drain the account ([Kraken withdrawal-address permission](https://support.kraken.com/articles/withdrawal-addresses-api-permission)); Gemini defaults keys to the Trader role ([Gemini roles](https://developer.gemini.com/roles.md)). Use IP allowlists where offered, and keep secrets in a vault (not in prompts, logs, or the LLM context). MEASURED for vendor features.
- Kill switches: Kraken's CancelAllOrdersAfter dead-man's switch cancels all orders if the heartbeat stops (recommended: call every 15 to 30 s with a 60 s timeout) ([Kraken docs](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/trading/cancel-all-orders-after-x)); Robinhood offers one-tap disconnect and per-trade notifications ([Robinhood external agents](https://robinhood.com/us/en/agents/external-agents/)). MEASURED. Build your own as well: max daily loss, max position, max orders/minute, flatten-all, and an independent watchdog that does not depend on the LLM.

## Recommended stacks

(a) Paper-trading MVP at about $0/month
- Alpaca paper (stocks + crypto in one API, same code path as live, official MCP) with the free IEX feed ([Alpaca market data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api)).
- Crypto market data from free exchange websockets (Coinbase/Kraken public feeds) plus CoinGecko Demo for reference prices ([CoinGecko pricing](https://www.coingecko.com/en/api/pricing)).
- News: Finnhub free (60 calls/min, 1 year of company news) ([Finnhub pricing](https://finnhub.io/pricing)) or the Benzinga free basic API ([AWS listing](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-xwgvhwowjmw3g)).
- Simulate realistic costs in paper: Alpaca paper fills against quotes and models neither market impact nor the 0.25% crypto taker fee realistically, so add slippage and fees to the ledger yourself.
- Optionally run tastytrade's sandbox, which has real simulated money and order flow, as a second implementation check ([tastytrade sandbox](https://developer.tastytrade.com/docs/sandbox/)).

(b) Small live account
- Stocks: Alpaca live with Algo Trader Plus ($99/mo SIP) once signals depend on volume or spread; otherwise stay on IEX. Alternative: IBKR Pro with an AI Partition. Its official MCP server lists Perplexity as a supported platform and enforces hard product and cash limits, but Autonomous mode is cash-only, has no shorting, accepts only day orders, and needs 10 human-approved trades first ([IBKR AI integrations](https://portal.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/ai-integrations.php)).
- Crypto: Kraken (dead-man's switch, granular keys, 0.40%/0.80% entry fees) or Alpaca Crypto (0.15%/0.25%, single API). Avoid Coinbase, Gemini, tastytrade, and Webull crypto at entry tiers for intraday trading (about 2% or more round trip).
- Use a cash account first: no margin-deficit freezes, and losses are capped at deposits. Accept the T+1 recycling limit.
- Isolate the bot's capital in a dedicated account or sub-account funded with an amount you can lose entirely.

## Implications for our build

1. Do not design around the $25k PDT rule; it was replaced on June 4, 2026. Still query the broker's account flags at runtime, because brokers have until Oct 20, 2027 to switch.
2. Default to cash accounts and model T+1 settled cash explicitly so the bot never causes good-faith violations.
3. Put hard risk limits in deterministic code outside the LLM: max loss per day, max orders per minute, max notional per symbol, self-trade prevention, and a cancel-to-fill ratio cap (guards against accidental spoofing patterns). Large-model decisions should only propose orders; a rules engine approves them.
4. Bot keys get trade-only scope, IP allowlisting, no withdraw permission, and separate keys per agent tier; secrets stay out of model context.
5. Use the dead-man's-switch pattern everywhere: Kraken natively, and for brokers without it, a watchdog that cancels all orders if the orchestrator heartbeat stops.
6. Budget costs honestly: at entry tiers, crypto round trips cost 0.4% to 2.4%, and stock "$0 commission" still costs spread. Short-term gains are taxed at about 30% to 40% combined federal and Idaho for a typical employee. A strategy needs net edge after both.
7. Keep a full trade ledger from day one (for 8949, wash-sale tracking across accounts, and a possible 475(f) election by April 15, 2027). Get a CPA opinion before electing.
8. Trade only your own money. Do not share signals or manage others' accounts without adviser/CTA registration analysis.
9. Check data licenses before sending paid feeds into hosted LLMs; prefer broker-provided data for the agent's context.
