## Exchange-by-exchange details and terms

### Binance.US
- **Access and limits:** Public keyless endpoint `GET https://api.binance.us/api/v3/klines` returns up to 1,000 bars per request (default 500), weight 1 ([Binance.US API docs](https://docs.binance.us/), `MEASURED`). No official bulk download portal was found (`UNKNOWN`).
- **History and critical USD gap:** `BTCUSD` starts 2019-09-17 and `ETHUSD` starts 2019-09-18 (`MEASURED`). Both USD pairs have a **585-day gap** from 2023-07-14 to 2025-02-19 (`MEASURED`), reflecting Binance.US's suspension and later restoration of USD trading. `BTCUSDT` and `ETHUSDT` start 2019-09-23 and have zero daily gaps through 2026-11-25 (`MEASURED`). None of Binance.US's pairs cover the 2018 bear market.
- **Timestamps:** Open time and close time are returned in milliseconds UTC (`open_time` = 00:00:00.000 UTC, `close_time` = 23:59:59.999 UTC for daily bars, `MEASURED`).
- **Licence terms:** [Binance.US Terms of Use](https://www.binance.us/terms-of-use) (last updated 2026-06-05, `MEASURED`) mentions BAM APIs and third-party applications relying on them, but contains no explicit clauses on storing historical market data, keeping data after closing an account, or sending market data to a hosted AI model (`UNKNOWN`).

### Binance.com bulk data (`data.binance.vision`) and US-person eligibility
- **What it offers:** Free bulk archives (klines, aggTrades, trades, bookTicker, metrics) hosted at `data.binance.vision` and keyless REST market data at `data-api.binance.vision` ([Market Data Only URLs](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/spot/faqs/market_data_only), `MEASURED`). Spot `BTCUSDT` history starts in August 2017 (`STATED`, [Coinranking](https://coinranking.com/blog/best-historical-crypto-data-apis/)).
- **Can a US person use `data.binance.vision` under its terms?** This is a legal gray area with a clear practical stop sign for live trading:
  - On 2026-08-26, Binance published **Binance Vision Dataset Terms v1.0** ([TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/binance/binance-public-data/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md), `MEASURED`).
  - Clause 2.3 grants access free of charge to "any individual or legal entity" and contains no country exclusion (`MEASURED`). Clause 1.2 incorporates the [Binance Terms of Use](https://www.binance.com/en/about-legal/terms), which historically prohibit US persons ([FinCEN Consent Order 2023-04](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/enforcement_action/2023-11-21/FinCEN_Consent_Order_2023-04_FINAL508.pdf), `MEASURED`), while Clause 1.5 says the Vision Dataset Terms prevail over the Binance Terms of Use in case of conflict (`MEASURED`). In GitHub issue [#524](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/issues/524) (2026-11-17, `STATED`), Binance's repo response stated that the same Vision terms apply regardless of country of residence for personal non-production backtesting without a Binance account.
  - **Even if a US person relies on Clause 2.3 and Clause 1.5 for offline research, Clause 4.1 and Clause 4.2 strictly limit use:** the datasets are licensed under `CC BY-NC-SA 4.0` (Clause 3.1), permitted only for "algorithmic historical backtesting for purely personal non-production research" (Clause 4.1), and **Clause 4.2 expressly prohibits using the datasets for "live proprietary trading execution"** (`MEASURED`). Clause 2.4 extends these restrictions to any "models, and derivative analytical indicators" derived from the datasets (`MEASURED`).
  - **Storage, keeping, and AI:** Clause 1.4 and 3.3 confirm that future amendments do not revoke `CC BY-NC-SA 4.0` rights to datasets already downloaded (`MEASURED`). The terms do not mention hosted AI models, though derived models remain governed by Clause 2.4 (`MEASURED`).
  - **Practical takeaway:** Do not build a US retail trading pipeline on `data.binance.vision`. Between the Binance.com US-person conflict and Clause 4.2's ban on live proprietary trading execution, using Coinbase, Bitstamp, or Kraken is cleaner and avoids offshore USDT venue risk.

### Kraken
- **REST OHLC limit vs. bulk downloads:** `GET /0/public/OHLC` is hard-capped at the 720 most recent candles per interval regardless of `since` ([Kraken Historical Data Guide](https://docs.kraken.com/exchange/guides/general/historical-data), 2026-10-29, `MEASURED`). For 1-minute data, that is only 12 hours; for daily data, ~2 years.
- **Two free ways to get full history:**
  1. **Downloadable OHLCVT and Time & Sales ZIPs:** [Kraken OHLCVT Support](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360047124832-downloadable-historical-ohlcvt-open-high-low-close-volume-trades-data) (updated 2026-11-02, `MEASURED`) provides direct multi-part ~2 GB ZIP downloads covering every pair from its first trade through 2026-09-30 at 1, 5, 15, 60, 240, 720, and 1440-minute intervals, plus separate full trade tick ZIPs ([Time & Sales Support](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360047543791-downloadable-historical-market-data-time-and-sales-), `MEASURED`). Note: Kraken's developer guide says "Kraken does not provide a bulk historical data dump" ([Historical Data Guide](https://docs.kraken.com/exchange/guides/general/historical-data), 2026-10-29, `MEASURED`), which conflicts with Kraken's newer support article (updated 2026-11-02, `MEASURED`) hosting live `assets.kraken.com` ZIP parts. The 2026-11-02 support article is newer and matches the downloadable archive.
  2. **REST Trades endpoint:** `GET /0/public/Trades?pair=XXBTZUSD&since=0` returns 1,000 trades per call starting `2013-10-06 21:34:16 UTC` for BTC/USD and `2015-08-07 14:03:25 UTC` for ETH/USD (`MEASURED`). You can stitch the quarterly ZIPs through 2026-09-30 with the REST OHLC endpoint for the last 720 bars to stay current.
- **Known flaws:** Empty intervals (no trades) are omitted rather than zero-filled ([OHLCVT Support](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360047124832-downloadable-historical-ohlcvt-open-high-low-close-volume-trades-data), `MEASURED`). CSVs have no header row and use UNIX epoch seconds (`MEASURED`).
- **Licence terms:** [Kraken Global Terms of Service](https://www.kraken.com/legal/global-terms) (updated 2026-11-18, `MEASURED`) has no separate data-retention or hosted-AI clause noted on the OHLCVT support page (`UNKNOWN` for specific AI/retention restrictions).

### Coinbase Exchange and Coinbase Advanced
- **Endpoints and limits:**
  - Legacy Exchange API: `GET https://api.exchange.coinbase.com/products/{product_id}/candles` caps at **300 candles per request** (returns HTTP 400 if exceeded, `MEASURED`), rate limit 10 req/s per IP (burst 15) ([Rate Limits](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/rest-api/rate-limits), `MEASURED`).
  - Retail Advanced Trade public API: `GET https://api.coinbase.com/api/v3/brokerage/market/products/{product_id}/candles` is keyless (`MEASURED`) and caps at **350 candles per request** ([Get Product Candles](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/products/get-product-candles), `MEASURED`).
- **History and survivorship:** `BTC-USD` daily starts `2015-07-20` and 1-minute starts `2015-01-14 00:01 UTC` (`MEASURED`). `ETH-USD` daily starts `2016-05-18` and 1-minute starts `2016-05-23 06:51 UTC` (`MEASURED`). Delisted pairs (`OMG-USD`, `REP-USD`, `WLUNA-USD`, `MCO2-USD`) still serve full historical candles (`MEASURED`).
- **Measured data-quality bug (2026-11-13):** Today's measurement caught a corrupted daily bar on Coinbase Exchange's legacy endpoint: for `2026-11-13`, `api.exchange.coinbase.com` returned `L=64113.26, C=64113.26` (6.0% below Binance.US, Kraken, and Gemini), leaking the next day's (`2026-11-14`) close into `2026-11-13` (`MEASURED`). Coinbase Advanced's public endpoint and Coinbase Exchange's own 1-hour bars both showed the correct `2026-11-13` close of `$68,236.54` (`MEASURED`). Use Coinbase Advanced's endpoint or cross-check daily bars against hourly sums.
- **Known flaws:** Intervals with zero trades are omitted ([Coinbase Candles docs](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-candles.md), `MEASURED`). Timestamps are bucket start times in UNIX seconds UTC (`MEASURED`).
- **Critical licence restriction (AI and automated systems):** Coinbase's Market Data Terms of Use, as enforced verbatim via Tardis.dev's pass-through clause ([Tardis Terms of Service](https://docs.tardis.dev/legal/terms-of-service), Clause 23.1, last modified 2026-10-28, `MEASURED`), prohibit using Coinbase Data or derived works "to develop, train, fine-tune, validate, benchmark or otherwise improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, algorithm, agent or other automated system, or for any other purpose related to the research, development or operation of artificial intelligence technologies." If you plan to feed historical crypto bars into a hosted AI model or AI coding/analysis agent, Coinbase data carries an explicit contractual restriction.

### Gemini
- **Endpoint and limits:** `GET https://api.gemini.com/v2/candles/{symbol}/{time_frame}` takes no `start` or `end` parameters and returns only a fixed trailing window: 363 to 364 daily bars (~1 year), 1,462 hourly bars (~61 days), and 1,149 1-minute bars (~19 hours) (`MEASURED`). Public trade history is capped at 500 records and 7 calendar days ([Gemini SDK Docs](https://developer.gemini.com/tools/typescript-sdk/reference/market-data/books-trades-candles), `MEASURED`).
- **Usability for backtesting:** Unusable alone for multi-year replay testing because you cannot pull data older than ~1 year from the public API.
- **Licence terms:** [Gemini User Agreement](https://www.gemini.com/legal/user-agreement) (dated 2026-02-19, `MEASURED`) does not include explicit market-data storage or AI clauses (`UNKNOWN` whether separate API terms apply).

### Alpaca Crypto Historical Bars
- **Access and limits:** `GET https://data.alpaca.markets/v1beta3/crypto/us/bars` is keyless (`MEASURED`) and returns up to 10,000 bars per page (default 1,000) with `next_page_token` pagination ([Alpaca Crypto Bars Docs](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.4.2/reference/cryptobars-1), `MEASURED`).
- **History:** Both `BTC/USD` and `ETH/USD` begin `2021-01-01` for daily and 1-minute bars (`MEASURED`). This misses the 2018 bear market completely.
- **Critical quality quirk:** Alpaca executes crypto on its own venue and **fills bars with quote mid-prices when no trades occur** (so bars can have `volume = 0` and non-zero price movement) ([Alpaca Market Data FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq), `MEASURED`). That makes Alpaca crypto bars convenient for matching Alpaca's own live crypto feed, but poor for long-term multi-cycle research.

### Bitstamp
- **Access and limits:** `GET https://www.bitstamp.net/api/v2/ohlc/{market_symbol}/` is keyless, returns up to 1,000 candles per call (`step` from `60` to `259200` seconds), with rate limits of 400 req/s and 10,000 req/10 min ([Bitstamp API Docs](https://www.bitstamp.net/api/), `MEASURED`).
- **History:** Longest continuous keyless API history for BTC/USD: daily starts `2011-08-18` and 1-minute starts `2011-08-18 12:37 UTC` (`MEASURED`). `ETH/USD` daily starts `2017-08-16` and 1-minute starts `2017-08-17 00:00 UTC` (`MEASURED`).
- **Survivorship and terms:** Delisted pairs (`omgusd`, `ustusd`, `fttusd`) return HTTP 404 (`MEASURED`), so survivorship is not preserved on the public API. Commercial use or redistribution requires a signed Data License Agreement ([Bitstamp API Docs](https://www.bitstamp.net/api/), `MEASURED`); personal research terms on Bitstamp USA's ToS could not be loaded (`UNKNOWN`).

## Known data-quality problems across spot crypto sources

- **Exchange outages and silent gaps:** Coinbase and Kraken omit candles when no trades occur (`MEASURED`). Binance.US `BTCUSD` and `ETHUSD` have a 585-day halt from 2023-07-14 to 2025-02-19 (`MEASURED`). Replay engines must check timestamp continuity rather than assuming contiguous rows.
- **Corrupted venue candles:** As measured today, Coinbase Exchange's legacy `granularity=86400` endpoint returned a corrupted `2026-11-13` BTC-USD daily close ($64,113.26 vs. ~$68,240 everywhere else and on Coinbase Advanced, `MEASURED`). Always cross-check daily bars against hourly rollups or a second venue.
- **Quote-midpoint synthetic bars:** Alpaca fills zero-trade crypto intervals with quote midpoints (`MEASURED`), creating bars with `volume = 0` that cannot be matched to real executions.
- **USD vs. USDT vs. USDC basis:** Stablecoin pairs drift from USD during banking stress (such as the March 2023 USDC de-peg or Tether premiums). Even in calm trading over the last 30 days on Binance.US, `BTCUSDT` closed an average of **+11.20 bps** above `BTCUSD` (max 18.68 bps, `MEASURED`).
- **Wash volume and thin-venue wicks:** Offshore exchanges have a documented history of inflated volume; among US spot venues, volume differs by 200x. Over the last 30 daily bars (`MEASURED`), Coinbase averaged 9,937 BTC/day and Kraken 3,361 BTC/day, compared with Gemini's 226 BTC/day and Binance.US's 50 BTC/day (`BTCUSD`). Thin books produce sharper single-venue flash wicks (measured daily low spreads hit 123 bps at p95 and 545 bps max for BTC, `MEASURED`).
- **Timestamps and open vs. close convention:** All four US venues tested (Binance.US, Kraken, Coinbase, Gemini) plus Bitstamp index candles by **bucket open time in UTC** (`MEASURED`). Older third-party CSVs (such as legacy CryptoDataDownload files) sometimes mixed NY EST labels with UTC timestamps; verify epoch seconds before merging with US equity data.

## Plain answers to the four core questions

### (a) Cheapest good-enough source for daily and hourly BTC and ETH back to at least 2017

**Winner: $0 using Bitstamp public REST API (`btcusd` and `ethusd`) paired with Kraken downloadable OHLCVT ZIPs (plus Kraken REST OHLC for the latest 720 bars), accessed directly or via MIT-licensed CCXT.**

- **Why this combination wins:**
  - **Bitstamp** is keyless, free, allows 1,000 bars per call, and covers BTC/USD back to `2011-08-18` and ETH/USD back to `2017-08-16` at daily, hourly, and 1-minute resolution (`MEASURED`). Both the 2018 and 2022 bear markets are fully inside the sample for BTC and ETH.
  - **Kraken downloadable OHLCVT ZIPs** ([Kraken Support](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360047124832-downloadable-historical-ohlcvt-open-high-low-close-volume-trades-data), `MEASURED`) are free, cover BTC/USD from `2013-10-06` and ETH/USD from `2015-08-07` through `2026-09-30` at 1m, 60m, and 1440m, and let you cross-check Bitstamp for single-venue bad prints or outages.
  - **Coinbase Advanced public API** (`api.coinbase.com/api/v3/brokerage/market/products/{id}/candles`) is also free and covers BTC-USD from `2015-07` and ETH-USD from `2016-05` (`MEASURED`), and is the deepest US spot venue today, **but** Coinbase's Market Data Terms ban AI/ML use of Coinbase data ([Tardis Terms](https://docs.tardis.dev/legal/terms-of-service), Clause 23.1, `MEASURED`) and its 350-bar request limit is slower to paginate.
- **Known flaws of this $0 setup:**
  - Bitstamp drops delisted coins from its API (HTTP 404, `MEASURED`), so it is good for BTC and ETH, not for survivorship-free altcoin baskets.
  - Kraken OHLCVT ZIPs omit zero-trade intervals and update quarterly (so you must append recent bars from Kraken REST OHLC).
  - Neither gives historical order book depth (only OHLCV, plus Kraken trade ticks).
  - For daily reference prices back to 2010 (BTC) / 2015 (ETH) as a third sanity check, **Coin Metrics Community API** (`PriceUSD` daily, $0 keyless, `MEASURED`) is free for non-commercial research.

#### How much BTC and ETH price movement since ETF launch happened while US stock markets were closed (`MEASURED`)

**Method:** Downloaded every 30-minute bar (`step=1800`) from Bitstamp's public OHLC endpoint (`https://www.bitstamp.net/api/v2/ohlc/{sym}/`) from exact ETF trading open to 2026-11-24 20:30 UTC: **50,316 bars** for `btcusd` (2024-01-11 14:30 UTC to 2026-11-24 20:30 UTC) and **41,006 bars** for `ethusd` (2024-07-23 13:30 UTC to 2026-11-24 20:30 UTC). Each 30-minute bar's close-to-close log return was classified using `America/New_York` local time (handling DST) as **US Open (RTH)** if Mon to Fri 09:30 to 16:00 ET (13:00 ET on NYSE early closes), excluding all 2024 to 2026 NYSE holidays (including the 2025-01-09 National Day of Mourning), and **US Closed** otherwise (split into weekday overnight vs. weekend starting Fri 16:00 ET through Sunday).

| Metric (`MEASURED`, Bitstamp 30m) | BTC (2024-01-11 to 2026-11-24) | ETH (2024-07-23 to 2026-11-24) |
|---|---|---|
| Clock time: US Open / US Closed | 18.6% / **81.4%** | 18.6% / **81.4%** |
| Total price change (start to end) | $48,664 to $59,710 (**+22.70%**) | $3,440.60 to $1,673.81 (**-51.35%**) |
| Share of total absolute movement (`sum(|r|)`) while **US Closed** | **71.1%** (46.9% overnight + 24.2% weekend) | **73.2%** (46.5% overnight + 26.7% weekend) |
| Share of return variance (`sum(r^2)`) while **US Closed** | **61.8%** (42.5% overnight + 19.2% weekend) | **66.5%** (41.2% overnight + 25.3% weekend) |
| Cumulative return during **US Open** hours | **-46.44%** (log -62.44%) | **-68.71%** (log -116.18%) |
| Cumulative return while **US Closed** | **+129.10%** (log +82.90%: +68.50% overnight, +14.40% weekend) | **+55.47%** (log +44.13%: +71.20% overnight, -27.07% weekend) |

**What this means for trading spot ETFs vs. spot crypto:** US stock market hours carry higher volatility per hour (~2.7x per hour for BTC), yet **62% to 67% of total return variance and 71% to 73% of absolute movement still happen while US stock exchanges are closed** (`MEASURED`). Holding an ETF overnight captures those closed-market returns, but you cannot enter, exit, rebalance, or trigger a stop-loss during 81.4% of the week, exposing any stop-based rule to multi-percent overnight and weekend gaps.
