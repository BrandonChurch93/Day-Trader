## Bottom line

- **Daily trend, 20 to 50 liquid ETFs:** Start with **Kibot EOD at $14 for one month**, the lowest published price I found for a durable daily archive that fits. MEASURED: Kibot explicitly advertises up to 64 years of EOD history across stocks and ETFs on that plan, and daily API access, raw/split-only/split-plus-dividend versions, and permanent rights to downloaded data; earliest day for each ETF and retrieval through an actual subscription remain UNKNOWN ([Kibot plans](https://www.kibot.com/subscribe.html), [delivery](https://www.kibot.com/delivery/data-updates.html), [licence](https://www.kibot.com/license.html)). Check the 19 sample symbols, corporate actions and date coverage against another source. As a backup, **EODHD EOD Historical is $19.99** with a clear one-call-per-symbol download, split/dividend-adjusted close and raw OHLC, plus explicit post-cancellation and own-only hosted ChatGPT/Claude permissions ([pricing](https://eodhd.com/pricing/), [daily data](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/api-for-historical-data-and-volumes), [storage](https://eodhd.com/help/storage), [AI tools](https://eodhd.com/help/ai-tools)). The free alternatives do not clear all the same tests: Alpaca starts in 2016; Tiingo Starter forbids durable data storage; Stooq's full history, consistent adjustment and permissions were not verified ([Alpaca plan table](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), [Tiingo terms, Nov 5, 2026](https://www.tiingo.com/tos/), [Stooq terms](https://stooq.com/terms.html)).
- **SPY/QQQ one-minute:** Start with Alpaca Basic's **historical SIP**, not IEX, if 2016 onward is enough. MEASURED: Free SIP requests need an end time at least 15 minutes old. The $99 upgrade does not buy an earlier first year ([Alpaca FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/market-data-faq), [Alpaca plan table](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api)). For 2008, EODHD lists raw minute data from 2004 at $29.99/mo; check both symbols and early sessions before trusting its fill test ([pricing](https://eodhd.com/pricing/), [intraday docs](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/intraday-historical-data-api)). FirstRate's SPY and QQQ files both explicitly start Jan 3, 2000, and Kibot's custom files start at $19, but a two-fund quote and specific quote history must be checked before choosing ([FirstRate SPY](https://firstratedata.com/i/etf/SPY), [FirstRate QQQ](https://firstratedata.com/i/etf/QQQ), [Kibot FAQ](https://www.kibot.com/faq.html)). Minute trade bars are enough for signal screening, not fills: for the fill test use bid and ask where available, use only bars finished before the order, and distinguish regular from extended hours. Do not fill every order at the signal bar's close.
- **Single-stock survivorship:** The clear desktop daily option with both delistings and historical index members is **Norgate Platinum, $346.50 for six months or $630/year**, to 1990. Diamond is $433.13 for six months or $787.50/year, to 1950. MEASURED: The vendor describes the delisted coverage as select security types. For full-history daily US stock and fund prices without needing an index-membership tape, direct Sharadar lists **$39/mo or $299/year**; its full bundle with historical S&P 500 membership lists **$69/mo or $499/year**. Do not assume those are Nasdaq Data Link checkout prices ([Norgate US packages](https://norgatedata.com/stockmarketpackages.php), [direct Sharadar plans](https://sharadar.com/llms.txt), [direct bundle tables](https://sharadar.com/bundle)). Delisted price rows alone do not make a survivorship-free universe: use dated eligibility, persistent identifiers, and a stated rule for delisting proceeds and missing bars.
