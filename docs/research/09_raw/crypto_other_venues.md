# Other crypto venues and the Idaho eligibility boundary

Research date: November 24, 2026. Scope: a US retail individual residing in Nampa, Idaho. This is a check of venue eligibility, licensing disclosures and product terms, not a legal opinion or a guarantee of account approval. MEASURED means directly read on a primary page today, not independently tested. UNKNOWN means not verified. No accounts were opened and no keys or orders were requested.

## What is unfinished

UNKNOWN: No account was opened to test funding holds, exact account-specific fee quotes, eligibility messages, minimum order sizes, first funding time or actual API permissions. Where not individually stated, institution/LLC access, full AI terms, operational caps beyond the published limits, complete 2025–2026 incident history, order-routing economics, insurance, regulatory pass-through, data/other fees and live fill quality were not verified. No SEC Rule 605/606 report was verified for any listed crypto spot or futures product; do not treat the lack of a report as evidence of poor execution. For stock ETPs the relevant broker disclosures and data plan are broker-specific.

## Idaho decision

- **Preferred additional spot candidate: Binance.US.** MEASURED: Idaho is listed as supported, and BAM Trading lists Idaho Money Transmitters License MTL-306. The standard Advanced Spot taker rate is 0.02%, with 0% maker across all pairs. This is Binance.US, not Binance.com. [Supported states](https://support.binance.us/en/articles/9842798-list-of-supported-and-unsupported-states-and-regions), [BAM licenses](https://support.binance.us/en/articles/10471695-licenses), [spot fees](https://www.binance.us/fees).
- **Other spot candidates: OKX US, Crypto.com Exchange, IBKR through zerohash.** MEASURED: OKX explicitly supports Idaho token-to-token and USD-to-token. Crypto.com has US retail Exchange funding documents and Foris DAX lists Idaho MTL-292; the Exchange spot restriction page does not list Idaho, though it is not an affirmative by-state Exchange matrix. IBKR sends eligible US individual accounts to zerohash, which publishes its Idaho MTL-0225. [OKX state licenses](https://www.okx.com/en-us/help/us-licenses), [Foris DAX US licenses](https://crypto.com/document/legal_us.pdf), [Crypto.com US retail Exchange ACH](https://help.crypto.com/en/articles/12291079-us-retail-users-usd-cash-deposit-and-withdrawal-via-ach-bank-transfer-exchange), [Exchange spot restrictions](https://help.crypto.com/en/articles/6320975-spot-trading-geo-restrictions), [IBKR provider FAQ](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/articles/508757108), [zerohash US licenses](https://docs.zerohash.com/page/us-licenses-and-disclosures).
- **Do not build a new US retail integration on Bitstamp.** MEASURED: Bitstamp USA lists an Idaho money transmitter license, but the retail migration makes it close-only beginning February 1, 2027. Institutional clients and non-US clients are not in this rollout. [Bitstamp USA licenses](https://www.bitstamp.net/legal/usa-inc/), [Bitstamp US retail migration FAQ](https://www.bitstamp.net/faq/moving-from-bitstamp-by-robinhood-to-robinhood/).
- **Futures are a separate decision.** MEASURED: Coinbase Financial Markets and Kraken Derivatives US offer US-regulated retail futures, subject to approval, and CME Micro Bitcoin and Micro Ether futures are available through a futures broker that supports the contracts. These are leveraged derivatives, not BTC or ETH that can be withdrawn to a wallet. [Coinbase US derivatives onboarding](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-derivatives-intro), [Kraken US derivatives eligibility](https://support.kraken.com/gb/articles/360023786632-kraken-derivatives-eligibility), [IBKR futures commissions and CME contracts](https://portal.interactivebrokers.com/en/pricing/commissions-futures.php).
