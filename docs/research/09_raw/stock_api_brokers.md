## FINRA: how much equity is required now?

| Case | Answer as of November 24, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Rule and phase-in | MEASURED: Notice 26-10, published April 20, 2026: “Effective Date: June 4, 2026; Phase-in Period: Ends October 20, 2027.” The amendments replace both the trade-count designation and the $25,000 PDT equity minimum with intraday margin requirements. FINRA also says, “Your firm might continue operating under the old day trading margin requirements during the transition.” [FINRA Notice 26-10](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10/) · [FINRA investor guidance, April 20, 2026](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/intraday-margin-requirements) |
| Margin account, broker migrated | MEASURED: No special FINRA dollar floor for unleveraged day trading; funding must cover the trade and firm restrictions can still apply. FINRA: “You can trade in a margin account with less than $2,000 in equity, but you cannot use leverage.” To borrow, “$2,000 is the minimum equity required to engage in leveraged trading.” Rule 4210(b)(4) says “equity of at least $2,000 except that cash need not be deposited in excess of the cost of any security purchased.” New paragraph (d)(2) governs intraday margin deficits; the broker may block orders or issue deficiency calls. [FINRA investor guidance](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/intraday-margin-requirements) · [Current Rule 4210](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/4210) |
| Margin account, broker has not migrated | MEASURED: Old PDT treatment can still apply during the phase-in, including $25,000 for customers designated as pattern day traders. The deleted text says, “The minimum equity required for the accounts of customers deemed to be pattern day traders shall be $25,000.” That is not a floor for every single same-day trade. [FINRA marked-up rule, Attachment A](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/Regulatory-Notice-26-10-Attachment-A.pdf) · [FINRA investor guidance](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/intraday-margin-requirements) |
| Cash account | MEASURED: No FINRA PDT or margin-leverage dollar minimum for a fully paid cash-account round trip. The constraint is the amount of settled cash needed to pay for the purchases, plus any broker account minimum. FINRA: “if you want to engage in intraday trading in a cash account, be sure that you fully pay for those securities at the time of purchase with settled funds.” Most equity trades settle T+1; selling stock bought with unsettled proceeds before those proceeds settle can be a good-faith violation. [FINRA frequent intraday trading guidance](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/frequent-intraday-trading?chain_id=Name1K9-3FXPhg.1l0fcs0) · [FINRA Interpretation 4210(b)(4)/024, valid from June 4, 2026](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/interps-4210-202606), which explicitly says the $2,000 minimum “does not apply to transactions in a cash account” · [Rule 4210](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/4210) |
