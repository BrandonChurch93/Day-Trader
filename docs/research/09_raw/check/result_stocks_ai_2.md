## Most Serious Problem

- **S23 (OVERSTATED) - IBKR Autonomous Trading eligibility and lifetime cap**: The claim leaves out three material restrictions on IBKR's own FAQ (`https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/connecting-ibkr-account-ai-platform-1437187133`). First, margin accounts are disqualified ("Individual account only (no margin accounts)"), along with Lite, IRA, joint, trust, and LLC/org accounts. A trader who opens an IBKR margin account cannot use Autonomous Trading at all. Second, the 10% / $1,000 minimum / $25,000 maximum transfer cap is a **lifetime total** across all AI Partitions, and trading losses do not reset it. Third, the 10 qualifying trades over two weeks must be placed specifically through Review & Approve mode on at least 3 distinct days.
