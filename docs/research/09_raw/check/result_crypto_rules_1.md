# Fresh check: crypto venues, taxes and rules (checker 1)

Checked 2026-11-24. Pages opened live with caching off unless noted. I did not read any other file in /brokers/.

## Top of file: unchecked items and most serious problem

Unchecked or only partly checked:
- C5 (Binance.US proof of reserves): the dedicated page https://www.binance.us/proof-of-reserves and the help article would not load (fetch failed twice). I could not confirm that no numerical report exists. What Binance.US does say about custody is quoted below.
- C11 (Coinbase Advanced entry tier): I could not open any primary Coinbase page that shows the Advanced entry-tier rates. The US VIP page (https://www.coinbase.com/advanced-trade/vip) loaded but its fee table is not in the served page, the help page says rates are only shown after sign-in, and the blog URL I tried had no fee text. The 0.50% / 0.90% figure is reported only by third parties (for example Investing.com) as a Coinbase announcement dated September 16, 2026.
- C3 and C5 both claim that something does not exist. I could only check that I found no sign of it. I did not prove it is absent.
- C24: the Idaho Tax Commission page I opened states the rate for 2025 only. For 2026, 5.3% comes from secondary sources (EY, citing the 2026 withholding tables). I found no primary page that states the 2026 rate.

Most serious problem (C11): the public 0.40% / 0.60% schedule is for Coinbase Exchange, which is a different product from Coinbase Advanced. Many sources say the US Advanced entry tier since September 16, 2026 is 0.50% maker / 0.90% taker. Coinbase's own help page says Advanced rates show only after sign-in, so I could not confirm this on a primary page. If a cost model uses 0.40% / 0.60% for Coinbase Advanced, it understates US taker cost by about a third (0.60% against 0.90%). Treat Coinbase Advanced entry cost as about 0.50% / 0.90% until it is confirmed in the signed-in fee page or the order preview.

Second most serious (C8): Kraken spot sub-accounts are only for "managed institutional clients". A retail Idaho account cannot rely on spot sub-accounts.
