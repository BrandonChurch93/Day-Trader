# Claims to check: crypto venues, taxes and rules

C1. Binance.US Advanced spot fees today: 0% maker and 0.02% taker on BTC/USD and ETH/USD for ordinary accounts, with no volume needed. State whether USDT pairs differ.
C2. Binance.US serves Idaho residents, including US dollar deposits and withdrawals by ACH, and ACH is free.
C3. Binance.US API keys can be set to read plus spot trading with withdrawals off, and limited to chosen IP addresses. Binance.US has no test or paper environment and no official Python SDK.
C4. The SEC's civil case against Binance entities including BAM Trading (Binance.US) was dismissed with prejudice on May 29, 2025.
C5. Binance.US publishes no numerical proof-of-reserves report (state what it does publish about custody).
C6. Kraken Pro spot fees: 0.40% maker / 0.80% taker at $0 volume; 0.30% / 0.60% from $2,500 of 30-day volume; 0.22% / 0.38% from $10,000 of volume or $20,000 of assets held on the platform. The asset-based route took effect July 9, 2026.
C7. Kraken serves Idaho (only Maine and New York are excluded among US states).
C8. Kraken API keys can be created without withdrawal permission and with an IP allowlist; Kraken supports sub-accounts.
C9. Kraken proof of reserves: latest snapshot September 30, 2026, BTC reserve ratio 103.2%, ETH 100.7%.
C10. Kraken ACH through Plaid: credited within about five minutes, tradable at once, withdrawable after seven days. Intermediate verification can take up to 45 minutes.
C11. Coinbase Advanced fee rates are shown only after sign-in. The public Coinbase Exchange schedule shows 0.40% maker / 0.60% taker for $0 to $10,000 of 30-day volume, 0.25% / 0.40% from $10,000, 0.15% / 0.25% from $50,000. Try hard to find any primary Coinbase page that shows the Advanced Trade entry-tier rates today, and report whether a September 2026 change to 0.50% / 0.90% is real.
C12. Coinbase API keys can be View and Trade only (no Transfer), limited to IP addresses, and tied to one portfolio. Coinbase's sandbox returns static mocked responses only.
C13. Coinbase One: the Preferred plan ($29.99 a month) gives a 25% rebate on Coinbase Advanced spot fees up to $100 a month.
C14. Alpaca crypto fees: 0.15% maker / 0.25% taker for $0 to $100,000 of 30-day volume. Minimum order for USD pairs is about $10 (another Alpaca page is said to say $1). Maximum $200,000 per order. Alpaca crypto is available in Idaho.
C15. Alpaca crypto wallet withdrawals: one Alpaca page says a 0.5% fee plus network fee, another says network fees only.
C16. Gemini ActiveTrader entry fees are 0.60% maker / 1.20% taker, effective September 1, 2026. Gemini has a full sandbox exchange.
C17. Robinhood Crypto Trading API: version 2 orders at the lowest tier are charged 0.95% per side today even for resting limit orders.
C18. Bitstamp USA: US retail accounts become close-only (no deposits, no buying) on February 1, 2027 because of the move to Robinhood.
C19. OKX US serves Idaho; standard spot fees are 0.20% maker / 0.35% taker. Crypto.com Exchange without CRO is 0.25% / 0.50%.
C20. Interactive Brokers spot crypto: 0.18% of trade value with a $1.75 minimum per order (capped at 1% of trade value).
C21. Spot crypto wash sales are not covered by the wash sale rule today. H.R. 10357 was ordered reported by House Ways and Means 38 to 5 on September 16, 2026 and has not become law.
C22. Form 1099-DA: brokers report gross proceeds for digital asset sales in 2026, and cost basis only for assets acquired after 2025 and held in the same custodial account.
C23. Section 475(f) mark-to-market election: must be made by the due date, without extensions, of the tax return for the year before the year it takes effect. For 2027 that is April 15, 2027.
C24. Idaho individual income tax rate is a flat 5.3%, and Idaho's capital gains deduction does not apply to stocks.
C25. A person who trades only their own money does not meet the federal definition of investment adviser (needs advising others for compensation) and FinCEN ruling FIN-2014-R002 says investing in virtual currency for one's own account is not money transmission.
C26. SEC Section 31 fee is $20.60 per million dollars of sales from April 4, 2026. FINRA's trading activity fee is $0 from October 1 to December 31, 2026.
C27. Coinbase's BTC and ETH perpetual-style futures for US customers have had no expiry since a June 17, 2026 CFTC filing. CME crypto futures have traded around the clock since May 29, 2026.
C28. BlackRock's IBIT and ETHA spot funds charge a 0.25% annual sponsor fee.
