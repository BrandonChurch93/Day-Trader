Date: 2026-11-26 21:45 UTC

Target band: positive before costs, thin enough to die at 50 to 75 bp per side (100 to 150 bp round trip) and plausibly survive at 5 to 25 bp per side (10 to 50 bp round trip), given Binance.US's STATED 0 bp maker and 2 bp taker USD schedule (`docs/research/180_round_57_cost_as_the_binding_limit.md`).

## Bottom Line

INFERENCE: Across both our own record and the published academic literature, genuine 1 to 30 day rules with thin 0 to 150 bp gross edges are rare and fragile. In our own record, only three 1 to 30 day tests printed before-cost gains inside 0 to 150 bp per round trip (Replay 25 already passed; Replay 39 and Replay 65 failed before costs on t or Sharpe), while all thin coin gains in our record (Replay 03 and Plans 70 and 71) had holds of 1 to 13.5 hours, not 1 to 30 days. Outside the repo, the two strongest dated academic candidates that fit a 1 to 30 day hold and a US long-only spot account on Binance.US are (1) weekly cross-sectional momentum on the top 20 to 30 coins (long-only leg, avoiding the losing short leg and Binance.US's no-shorting limit) and (2) 24-hour post-issuance drift after large on-chain stablecoin mints. Daily short-term reversal, post-listing day +2 to +3 shorts, and Monday Bitcoin timing either require shorting, fail independent post-2018 replications, or have already failed in our own tests.

## Part B: Six Ranked Leads from Published Academic Papers and Repo Record

### Rank 1: Weekly Cross-Sectional Momentum on Top 20 to 30 Coins (Long-Only Focus)

- Exact rule as published: STATED in Grobys, Sandretto, and Äijö (2026), Finance Research Letters 92, article 109602 ([Osuva open-access PDF](https://osuva.uwasa.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/0cfe3c29-392b-434d-9e20-e4df1a434985/content)): rank the top 30 coins by past 30-day return skipping the most recent daily quote, go long the top quintile and short the bottom quintile (equal-weighted), hold 1 week, rebalance weekly. Companion rule STATED in Han, Kang, and Ryu (2024) ([ACFR/AUT working paper PDF](https://acfr.aut.ac.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/918729/Time_Series_and_Cross_Sectional_Momentum_in_the_Cryptocurrency_Market_with_IA.pdf)): rank coins by past 14-day return into quintiles, buy Q5 (long-only) or Q5 minus Q1 (long-short), hold 5 days (`(14, 5)` portfolio, 1/k daily overlapping tranches).
- Sample and dates: STATED in Grobys et al. (2026): first week of January 2017 to fourth week of August 2024 (398 weekly observations), top 30 coins by year-end market cap (PCMP) plus 9 survivor coins (SCMP: BTC, ETH, XRP, DOGE, LTC, XLM, ETC, XMR, NEO). STATED in Han et al. (2024): CoinMarketCap active and inactive coins (market cap >= $1M, volume >= $1M, 96 stablecoins excluded), January 1, 2017 to August 28, 2023, plus Binance futures (239 coins, February 12, 2020 to November 17, 2023).
- Before-cost and after-cost figures (STATED): In Grobys et al. (2026) Table 5 ([Osuva PDF](https://osuva.uwasa.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/0cfe3c29-392b-434d-9e20-e4df1a434985/content)): untrimmed PCMP gross = +56 bp/week (t 0.65), net at 25 bp = +35 bp (t 0.41), at 50 bp = +14 bp (t 0.16), at 75 bp = -7 bp (t -0.08), break-even TC = 66.68 bp. At 5% trimming: gross = +93 bp/week (t 2.62), net at 25 bp = +72 bp/week (t 2.02, p < 0.05), net at 50 bp = +51 bp (t 1.42, insignificant), net at 75 bp = +30 bp (t 0.83), break-even TC = 111.01 bp. Verbatim quote: "the trimmed data (5 % level) on PCMP exhibits an average payoff corresponding to 0.93 % per week, which is statistically significant on a common 1 % level (t-statistic 2.62)." And: "A standard transaction-cost level of 50 bps, as considered in the literature (Fieberg et al., 2025), fully offsets the statistical significance of the trimmed PCMPs." In Han et al. (2024) ([ACFR PDF](https://acfr.aut.ac.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/918729/Time_Series_and_Cross_Sectional_Momentum_in_the_Cryptocurrency_Market_with_IA.pdf)), top 5% coins `(14, 5)` long-only earned 156.56%/yr gross (Sharpe 1.61) and 149.54%/yr after 15 bp/side (Sharpe 1.54), whereas the short-only leg lost -32.35%/yr gross and -41.61%/yr net.
- Out-of-sample testing by others: STATED in Grobys et al. (2026) and Ballis and Ioannidis (October 2026, [arXiv:2610.23283](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23283)): original Liu, Tsyvinski, and Wu (2022) momentum weakens sharply on survivor-only coins (SCMP = +36 bp/wk, t 0.75) and is highly sample-dependent post-2021. Pang and Wang (November 2026, [arXiv:2611.01951v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01951v1)) also show the related CTREND factor collapses to +5 bp/wk gross and -87 bp/wk net after correcting universe and construction rules.
- US retail tradability on Binance.US or Coinbase: INFERENCE: Long-only Q5 (top 4 to 6 coins of our 20-coin roster) is directly tradable on Binance.US USD spot at STATED 0 bp maker / 2 bp taker plus spread (no shorting required). Full long-short is NOT tradable on Binance.US or Coinbase spot because neither offers retail spot shorting; shorting would require Coinbase CFM or Kalshi perps (whole-lot and funding constraints).
- What we could test that we have not already looked at: MEASURED: Our Replay 13 Rule A (`docs/research/54_replay_13_year_or_method.md`) tested monthly/weekly top-coin selection at 15 bp/side and lost -62% to -73% in bear years (2022, 2025, 2026). We have NOT tested a 5-day or 7-day long-only `(14, 5)` or `(30-1, 7)` top-quintile rule restricted strictly to Binance.US-listed USD pairs with a cash-filter regime gate (such as Han et al.'s market-tercile filter) on fresh post-2024 forward data.
- Main reason it would fail: INFERENCE: Bear-market beta (long-only crypto still draws down 50% to 75% in crypto winters, just like Replay 13A), plus Grobys et al. (2026) show survivor-only momentum on the 9 longest-lived large coins was statistically indistinguishable from zero (+36 bp/wk, t 0.75) even before costs.

### Rank 2: Stablecoin Issuance 24-Hour Post-Event Drift (Long-Only, 1-Day Hold)

- Exact rule as published: STATED in Ante, Fiedler, and Strehle (June 2020), Blockchain Research Lab Working Paper No. 11 ([BRL open-access PDF](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/The_Influence_of_Stablecoin_Issuances_on_Cryptocurrency_Markets_BRL_Working_Paper_No_11.pdf), published in Finance Research Letters 41, 2021): observe an on-chain stablecoin issuance event of at least $1 million across major USD stablecoins, buy major cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, XRP, or LTC) at issuance time t = 0, and hold for 24 hours (0 to +24h).
- Sample and dates: STATED: 565 issuance events >= $1 million across 7 stablecoins on 4 blockchains, April 2019 through March 2020 (excluding one mistaken 5B USDT mint on TRON burned shortly after).
- Before-cost and after-cost figures (STATED): 0 to 24 hour cumulative abnormal returns (CAAR) in [BRL WP 11](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/The_Influence_of_Stablecoin_Issuances_on_Cryptocurrency_Markets_BRL_Working_Paper_No_11.pdf): BTC +57.0 bp (0.570%), ETH +66.8 bp (0.668%), XRP +47.4 bp (0.474%), LTC +69.4 bp (0.694%). Transaction costs are NOT modeled in the paper (UNKNOWN after-cost figure in paper; INFERENCE: +47 to +69 bp/RT dies at 50 to 75 bp/side = 100 to 150 bp RT, and could only survive at 5 to 25 bp/side = 10 to 50 bp RT). Verbatim quotes: "We analyze the influence of stablecoin issuances on the returns of major cryptocurrencies across 565 issuance events of $1 million or more for seven different stablecoins on four different blockchains between April 2019 and March 2020." And: "We identify highly significant positive CAARs of between 0.31% and 0.47% for -12 to -1 hours and between 0.25% and 0.34% for -6 to -1 hours before an issuance, but fail to find significant abnormal returns immediately after the issuances, from 0 to 2 hours."
- Out-of-sample testing by others: STATED in later literature summarized in [arXiv:2508.09429](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.09429) (citing Kristoufek 2021): issuance largely reacts to prior crypto price moves, with weak net spillovers once contemporaneous correlations are controlled. A 2021 to 2025 daily stablecoin-flow study ([arXiv:2611.26180](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.26180)) similarly finds no average five-day relationship after instrumenting flows.
- US retail tradability on Binance.US or Coinbase: INFERENCE: Fully tradable long-only on Binance.US USD spot (BTC, ETH, XRP, LTC are all liquid pairs; 0 bp maker / 2 bp taker + spread; no shorting needed). Public on-chain mint events (Ethereum/Tron USDC and USDT treasury mints) are observable in real time.
- What we could test that we have not already looked at: MEASURED: Our repo has NEVER tested on-chain stablecoin issuance or net mint/burn events (zero overlap with all 44 scored tests and Plans 70 and 71). We could run a clean post-2020 count-and-event study (2021 to 2026) or a forward read-only logger on verified USDC/USDT treasury mints >= $10M paired with Binance.US BTC/USD and ETH/USD quotes at t+0m, t+5m, and t+24h.
- Main reason it would fail: INFERENCE: Reverse causality and regime change. Even in Ante et al. (2020), hours 0 to +2 had no significant abnormal return while hours -12 to -1 had +31 to +47 bp, suggesting mints follow demand rather than cause future returns; post-2021 institutional treasury minting (authorized participants creating USDC/USDT on demand) may have erased any 24-hour post-mint drift.

### Rank 3: Daily Short-Term Reversal Conditional on Low Volume Shocks (1-Day Hold)

- Exact rule as published: STATED in Bianchi, Babiak, and Dickerson (May 2022), Sveriges Riksbank Working Paper No. 413 ([Riksbank open-access PDF](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/rapporter/working-papers/2022/no.-413-trading-volume-and-liquidity-provision-in-cryptocurreny-markets.pdf)): among the top 100 most liquid USD-quoted pairs each month (ranked by trailing Amihud illiquidity, >= 365 days history, excluding stablecoins and wrapped coins), sort daily at 00:00 UTC into 3x3 portfolios on previous-day return and 30-day standardized log-volume shock. Go long low-return/low-volume coins and short high-return/low-volume coins (or long-only low-return/low-volume), hold 1 day.
- Sample and dates: STATED: March 1, 2017 to March 1, 2022; >300 USD pairs aggregated across >80 exchanges (179,023 pair-day observations), restricted to top 100 liquid pairs per month for portfolio sorts.
- Before-cost and after-cost figures (STATED): Table 3 gross daily returns ([Riksbank WP 413](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/rapporter/working-papers/2022/no.-413-trading-volume-and-liquidity-provision-in-cryptocurreny-markets.pdf)): low-volume long-short = +122.6 bp/day equal-weighted (p < 0.01) and +62.5 bp/day value-weighted (p < 0.01); low-return/low-volume long-only leg = +72.2 bp/day EW (p < 0.01) and +29.8 bp/day VW (p < 0.10). Table 5 net daily returns at 20 bp long / 30 bp short (50 bp RT): EW low-volume reversal drops to +72.6 bp/day (p < 0.01), while VW drops to +12.5 bp/day (insignificant) and mid/high-volume VW fall to -57.0 and -68.7 bp/day. Verbatim quotes: "Specifically, a simple equal-weighted reversal strategy conditional on low trading volume generates a daily average return of 1.26% (p-value: 0.001) against an average of 0.54% (p-value: 0.002) when conditioned on high trading volume." And: "Similarly, the average returns from a value-weighted reversal strategy conditional on low volume is 0.65% (p-value: 0.002) against -0.19% (p-value: 0.251) obtained by focusing on high volume pairs."
- Out-of-sample testing by others: STATED in Han, Kang, and Ryu (2024) ([ACFR PDF](https://acfr.aut.ac.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/918729/Time_Series_and_Cross_Sectional_Momentum_in_the_Cryptocurrency_Market_with_IA.pdf)), testing 2018 to 2023 CoinMarketCap data at 15 bp/side: every long-short reversal portfolio with a holding period under 1 week yielded a negative mean return, and the largest 5% of coins showed short-horizon momentum rather than reversal. STATED in Mercik et al. (2025) ([UEWR PDF](https://www.wir.ue.wroc.pl/docstore/download/UEWR135bdc5e34ad49868fe8a5e77fa021c7/Mercik_Up_or_down_Short-term_reversal.pdf)): daily reversal concentrates in illiquid small coins while the largest tradable coins exhibit daily momentum.
- US retail tradability on Binance.US or Coinbase: INFERENCE: Long-only low-return/low-volume leg is tradable on Binance.US USD spot across our 20 coins (0 bp maker / 2 bp taker), but full long-short is NOT tradable on US spot. Crucially, the paper's EW return relies on ranks 21 to 100 (illiquid coins), where Binance.US books are thin or unlisted; VW (large-coin) gross return was only +29.8 bp/day long-only (+62.5 bp/day LS).
- What we could test that we have not already looked at: MEASURED: Our Plans 70 and 71 tested 1 to 2 hour drops on 20 coins; we have NOT tested a 24-hour close-to-close low-volume conditional dip-buy (`return_1d < threshold` AND `volume_shock < 0`, 1-day hold) on the 20-coin roster.
- Main reason it would fail: INFERENCE: Bid-ask bounce in illiquid coins and adverse selection on resting orders. Once restricted to the 20 largest US-tradable coins, both Han et al. (2024) and Mercik et al. (2025) find daily reversal disappears or flips to momentum, and VW net return in Bianchi et al. Table 5 was already insignificant (+12.5 bp/day) at 20/30 bp.

### Rank 4: Exchange Cross-Listing Post-Listing Unloading Short (Days +2 to +3, 2-Day Hold)

- Exact rule as published: STATED in Ante and Meyer (2021), Decisions in Economics and Finance 44(2): 957 to 980 ([EconStor open-access PDF](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/286840/1/s10203-021-00323-0.pdf)): following an exchange cross-listing event (day 0), short the newly cross-listed token across post-listing days +2 and +3 to capture post-listing unloading.
- Sample and dates: STATED: 250 exchange cross-listings of 135 ICO tokens across 22 cryptocurrency exchanges, mostly Q4 2017 to Q4 2018 (overall dataset 2013 to 2019).
- Before-cost and after-cost figures (STATED): Listing-day CAAR = +6.51% (market model) / +6.73% (constant mean); 7-day window (-3, +3) = +9.97%; post-listing days (+2, +3) CAAR = -140 bp (-1.40% market model) / -145 bp (-1.45% constant mean). Transaction costs are NOT modeled in the paper (UNKNOWN after-cost figure; INFERENCE: a 140 bp gross 2-day short dies at 75 bp/side = 150 bp RT and could only live at 5 to 25 bp/side plus borrow/funding). Verbatim quote: "For days two and three after the event (+ 2, + 3), we find a significant CAAR of − 1.45%/− 1.4%."
- Out-of-sample testing by others: MEASURED in our own repo: Replay 20 (`docs/research/71_replay_20_upbit_listing_short.md`) independently confirmed a post-listing short on Upbit listings (139 trades, +320.88 bp net at 15 bp/side + funding on offshore Binance perps), whereas Replay 46 Leg B (`docs/research/101_replay_46_cftc_altcoin_futures.md`) found only +127.9 bp net (paired t 0.572, FAILED) on US CFTC altcoin futures launches.
- US retail tradability on Binance.US or Coinbase: INFERENCE: NOT tradable on Binance.US or Coinbase spot because it requires shorting. Even on US perpetual futures (Coinbase CFM, Kalshi, Robinhood), newly cross-listed small tokens are rarely listed as US CFTC futures at launch.
- What we could test that we have not already looked at: MEASURED: Round 56 (`docs/research/179_idea_round_56_cousins_of_the_passes.md`) already audited non-Upbit listing/delisting cousins and dropped them on US short-venue unavailability. Only if a US-legal perp venue (such as Kalshi or Coinbase CFM) lists a coin before a major spot exchange cross-listing could a forward count be logged.
- Main reason it would fail: INFERENCE: Venue access (no US-legal retail shorting on newly listed small tokens), plus borrow/funding squeeze risk on thin post-listing shorts.

### Rank 5: Bitcoin Monday Day-of-Week Effect (1-Day Hold, Long-Only)

- Exact rule as published: STATED in Caporale and Plastun (2018/2019), Finance Research Letters 31 ([Brunel open-access PDF](https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/bitstream/2438/17208/3/FullText.pdf)): "open long positions on Monday and close them at the end of this day" on Bitcoin only (no effect found in Litecoin, Ripple, or Dash).
- Sample and dates: STATED: Bitcoin daily data 2013 to 2017 (trading report: 2013.01.01 to 2017.09.22, 245 Monday trades).
- Before-cost and after-cost figures (STATED): 60.00% profitable trades (147 wins, 98 losses), total net profit 16,990 on 10,000 initial deposit, expected payoff 69.35 (~69 bp per trade on fixed 10,000 sizing), t = 2.01 (critical 1.78). Paper does not separate before-cost and after-cost returns explicitly (report lists "Spread: 2", no explicit percentage exchange fee; INFERENCE: ~69 bp/trade dies completely at 50 to 75 bp/side = 100 to 150 bp RT and only survives near 5 to 25 bp/side). Verbatim quotes: "There is clear evidence of an anomaly only in the case of BitCoin." And: "On the whole, there is no conclusive evidence that the cryptocurrency market is inefficient."
- Out-of-sample testing by others: STATED in Baur, Cahill, Godfrey, and Liu (2017/2019, [SSRN 3088472](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3088472)): across >15 million observations on 7 global Bitcoin exchanges, time-of-day and day-of-week effects are time-varying with no consistent or persistent pattern. MEASURED in our own repo: Replay 19 (`docs/research/64_replay_19_monday_asia.md`) tested Monday Asia hours on BTC and lost -27.80 pp before fees (-53.88% net).
- US retail tradability on Binance.US or Coinbase: INFERENCE: Fully tradable long-only on Binance.US BTC/USD (0 bp maker / 2 bp taker + ~2 to 5 bp half-spread).
- What we could test that we have not already looked at: MEASURED: Replay 19 tested Monday Asia session hours, not full 00:00 to 23:59 UTC Monday close-to-close on 2018 to 2026 BTC/USD. However, given Baur et al. (2019) and Replay 19, this is low priority.
- Main reason it would fail: INFERENCE: 2013 to 2017 sample artifact (38 trades in 2017 alone accounted for 11,662 of the 16,990 total profit in Caporale and Plastun's table; 2014 was negative and 2013, 2016, 2017 all failed the individual-year t-test).

### Rank 6: Repo-Internal Overextension Dip-Buy (`70-102`, `71-013`, `71-035`) Extended to Binance.US Spot (Sub-Day Caveat)

- Before-cost and after-cost figures: MEASURED net at frozen CFM cost (~20 to 30 bp RT + 1 bp/hr funding): `70-102` +56.5 bp/RT (t 3.51), `71-035` +56.8 bp/RT (t 3.54), `71-013` +84.1 bp/RT (t 3.52). INFERENCE: Implies ~+80 to +115 bp/RT gross. MEASURED at 160 bp/RT spot-fee comparison: all three died sharply (`70-102` -112.55%/yr, `71-035` -69.39%/yr, `71-013` -36.97%/yr).
- Out-of-sample testing by others: MEASURED in `docs/research/157_plan_73_first_minute_diagnosis.md`: half the gain vanished in the first minute (+75.6 bp to +39.4 bp; worst-of-minute -28.1 bp). Currently tracked forward on live quotes via `E09` and `E10` (with Binance.US second book added in commit `4056276`). Vault (2024 to 2026) remains sealed because carry draws (18.11% to 20.75%) missed the 33% bar.
- US retail tradability on Binance.US or Coinbase: INFERENCE: Long-only spot on Binance.US covers most of the 20-coin roster at 0 bp maker / 2 bp taker, **but** live depth during crash hours is UNKNOWN and already being measured by `E10`.
- What we could test that we have not already looked at: INFERENCE: Already under forward quote audit in `E10`; do NOT re-search 2020 to 2023. The only untested variant matching the 1 to 30 day horizon would be whether a 24-hour (1-day) hold after the same BTC-led altcoin flush retains any net edge once minute-1 bounce is excluded.
- Main reason it would fail: MEASURED / INFERENCE: Minute-1 stale-print / spread-bounce artifact (`doc 157`), thin Binance.US altcoin books during sharp selloffs, and hold length (1 to 2 hours) shorter than the owner's 1 to 30 day target.
