## Row 11: Customer earnings before the supplier reports

- **MEASURED, what the free map covers:** The [EDGAR supply-chain snapshot](https://github.com/GerritPro/edgar-supply-chain) is CC BY 4.0 and points to original annual filings. At revision `144465a55a460d7683470949b9c526530040c4cd`, keep only listed customers in a supplier's 10-K reporting at least 10% of revenue; deduplicate supplier/customer. That gives 281 links and 108 distinct customers. The filings are 23 in 2025 and 258 in 2026, counted per link. Do not carry those links backward as known in 2016.
- **MEASURED, one dated example:** Cirrus Logic's May 2025 10-K named Apple at 89% of fiscal 2025 net sales; Apple reported on April 30, 2026 that diluted EPS was up 22% year on year; Cirrus reported on May 6, 2026 ([Cirrus 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/772406/000077240625000014/crus-20250329.htm); [Apple release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/04/apple-reports-second-quarter-results/); [Cirrus release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/772406/000077240626000012/pressrelease_fullyear.htm)). This proves a public link and news order, not a consensus surprise, safe exit order or return. For a real rule, use only the filing already available that day, a news rule known at that time, and either a time-stamped pre-announced supplier report date or a fixed exit that doesn't use a date discovered later.
- **STATED, first projection:** Multiply 281 links by 43 completed quarterly slots, by 45,319 divided by 130,075 (about 34.8%, the old study's retained pairs after the date-spacing cuts among pairs with returns), by an assumed top fifth for good news. This gives 842 synthetic candidates, before same-supplier deduplication or missing public scheduling. The old estimate is from a paid Compustat/CRSP sample, 1976 to 2009, Table 1 p. 41 of [Cheng and Eshleman's customer-news paper](https://english.ckgsb.edu.cn/sites/default/files/does_the_market_overweight_imprecise_information_evidence_from_customer_earnings_announcements.pdf); it is not a measured modern rate. The free snapshot is a seed for a historical filing census, not a dated 2016 to 2026 census. Actual count and point-in-time consensus coverage are UNKNOWN.
- **STATED, strongest nearby return, not the exact trade:** [Pinchuk, Customer Momentum](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.11394v1), Table 36 (unpaginated HTML), gives +142 bp for the equally weighted high-customer-SUE decile, t = 4.33, and +94 bp value weighted. Table 37 has +107 bp for the high customer earnings-window return decile. Main sample January 1978 to June 2018; the appendix tables do not label a narrower sample, a precise entry after public news, an exit before the supplier reports, or cost deductions. These are monthly long-side means, not abnormal gains at the required print. [Zhu, 1983 to 2011](https://efmaefm.org/0EFMAMEETINGS/EFMA%20ANNUAL%20MEETINGS/2013-Reading/papers/EFMA2013_0548_fullpaper.pdf) is closer on the news clock: 1,083 links, 10,207 firm-quarters; Table 3, pp. 27–29, relates customer surprise to supplier abnormal returns on days +2 to +61, but reports a regression coefficient, not a long-only buy and sell. An executable modern long-side maximum for the queued rule is UNKNOWN.
- **STATED, later check:** The [September 2026 replication](https://dmurav.com/replications/releases/v2026-09-06/reports/paper-8a5bb9c63c95.pdf), p. 2 §4, reports +9.2 bp/month (t = 0.34) for the long-short monthly customer-return sort over January 2005 to December 2024 (38,875 link-years and 100,822 stock-months). It reports no modern long-leg breakdown and uses prior customer returns, not a news timestamp. Treat this as evidence of attenuation in a related rule, not a direct replication.
- **MEASURED on synthetic data:** Give the full +142 bp monthly raw result to the 15-session paired lift, then subtract 10 bp. For noise use 14.05%, half of the 28.1% supplier CAR standard deviation over 60 sessions in [Zhu, Table 1 p. 24](https://efmaefm.org/0EFMAMEETINGS/EFMA%20ANNUAL%20MEETINGS/2013-Reading/papers/EFMA2013_0548_fullpaper.pdf), via square-root time. This is a horizon proxy, not current paired noise. Joint power is 23.59%; assuming only 5% same-quarter dependence raises it to 48.08%. That sensitivity is not a pass: it requires a verified point-in-time roster, low dependence and a comparable after-news, pre-supplier-report lift, none of which is established. The zero net lift case passes 2.61% in the central model.

Count the 482 complete US calls once each; do not add the 290 selected segments or count turns as calls. The [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/florencejiang/earnings25) gives call timestamps and speaker labels, not evidence that this transcript version existed at the next market open in 2025. If scoring the audio rather than the later transcript, the original provider's audio terms and contemporaneous public webcast still need verification ([Earnings25 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23813v1)).
- **MEASURED, free filing clock:** Confirmed complete remarks and executive answers for [Liquidity Services](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1235468/000162828018010362/finaltranscriptq3earningsc.htm), [Premier](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577916/000119312525020426/d905299dex992.htm), and [ClearSign](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1434524/000110465925114991/tm2531893d1_ex99-2.htm). - **STATED, time and rights limits:** The broad research corpus used by [Scanlon](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/5EB217BB68B5FB054FE38541BAAC4679/S0022109022001181a.pdf/div-class-title-pead-txt-post-earnings-announcement-drift-using-text-div.pdf) is licensed Capital IQ/WRDS. Neither establishes a lawful free dated full panel. Use the next regular open or close after the complete call text can actually be scored; do not enter at the press release before the answers.
- **STATED, strongest nearby long leg and later checks:** [Scanlon, Table 2 p. 2306](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/5EB217BB68B5FB054FE38541BAAC4679/S0022109022001181a.pdf/div-class-title-pead-txt-post-earnings-announcement-drift-using-text-div.pdf), 2010–2019, 85,160 observations and 4,701 firms, reports +66 bp for the text top quintile over days 1–32 and +131 bp over 1–63, starting at the first close after the call with no stated costs. It measures the whole call, not a separate 15-session answers leg. [Fast Numbers, Slow Language](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2606.29734), Appendix H Table 6, has 5,428 events (2022–2025), 492 Q&A trades in its test, and a +76.6 bp top-minus-bottom spread at its NextOpen clock. That is intraday, not long-only, and the clock does not guarantee the morning call was finished. [Point-in-Time Encoders](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14325v2), Tables V–VI and the portfolio section, tests 2013–2025 and has a one-day long-short exercise, not the 15-session long-only answers result. The exact modern long-side size is UNKNOWN.
- **MEASURED on synthetic data:** Assign the full +66 bp 32-session whole-call lift to 15 sessions and subtract 10 bp. Use 704 bp noise, the rounded 7.042% one-day M4 top-quintile SD in [Which Voices Move Markets?, Table X, §V-C](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.13260v1) for 3,286 events. This study's window starts at a close before the call, so the SD is only a proxy, and its +130.1 bp mean is not executable. One quarter fails year breadth mechanically and has no valid time-cluster t. Spread the 482 calls across the full calendar as a favorable hypothetical: 16.29% joint, or 7.00% at double the noise. The corresponding flat net null is 2.54%; no eligible time spread is supplied by the licensed panel.

## Row 13: New funded contracts large against trailing sales

- **STATED, free clocks:** Use SEC acceptance for a disclosed contract; the [SEC's Form 8-K, pp. 7–8](https://www.sec.gov/files/form8-k.pdf) limits Item 1.01 to qualifying definitive agreements outside ordinary course, so not every business win appears there. Use the [Defense Department's 5 p.m. Eastern release](https://www.war.gov/News/Contracts/) for covered federal awards; look up initial obligations with [USAspending award and transaction endpoints](https://api.usaspending.gov/docs/endpoints), not the ceiling or aggregate obligations years later. The [USAspending guide](https://www.usaspending.gov/data/about-the-data-download.pdf) says awards generally show within five days but DoD/USACE procurement records can be delayed 90 days. Action date is not the public clock. If timing is uncertain, use the first provable public date and the next market session. For the size screen, use only trailing sales already filed in the [SEC financial data](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces); exclude awards without a funded initial amount, cap-only vehicles, amendments, and duplicate notices for the same deal.
- **MEASURED, first count:** The [CC0 defense announcement mirror](https://github.com/pushbuild/us-defense-contract-awards) at revision `5fdebeb85dcd8b88f85c48180697194b8817f1dd` has 36,557 announcement paragraphs on 2,668 dates from January 4, 2016 to November 25, 2026. Exact prefix matching into ten example parents gives 1,070 distinct parent-days. Keep one procurement ID on the row, its first sighting in the entire archive back to 2014, the ID's fiscal-year marker within one of the release year, and first announced dollars of at least $25 million. That leaves 252 parent-days; at $100 million, 83. This is a reproducible federal candidate count, not a census or a cut on obligations divided by sales. A first-sighted ID can already be a modification and a first dollar figure can be a ceiling. For example, [AeroVironment's August 28, 2024 Item 7.01 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1368622/000110465924093912/tm2422929d1_8k.htm) is not Item 1.01 and is not proof of a funded initial amount. An eligible USAspending/issuer join count and an Item 1.01 count for the sales filter are UNKNOWN.
- **STATED, strongest nearby effect:** [TenderAlpha/HKU](https://www.tenderalpha.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/White-Paper-Defense-Contract-Announcements-TenderAlpha-HKU-1.pdf), table “3-day returns by contract size relative to market cap” (PDF page not verifiable in accessed copy), covers January 2010 to May 2025, 183 US-listed firms, 16,491 usable announcement rows overall. The Top 5% relative-size bucket has 1,006 observations in 620 entry cohorts. Its long side is +30.0 bp value weighted (t = 2.35) or +28.3 bp equal weighted (t = 2.18) market-adjusted and gross. Entry is the first applicable close after the public timestamp, with after-hours notices rolled forward. At five days the same bucket is +23.1/+22.5 bp and no longer reaches t = 2 ([TenderAlpha/HKU](https://www.tenderalpha.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/White-Paper-Defense-Contract-Announcements-TenderAlpha-HKU-1.pdf)). This is an executable stock clock but a different denominator and a sample with awards and modifications from a proprietary feed. No exact sales-filter, new-only long leg was located.
- **STATED, what other samples say:** [Budrys, Table 1](https://zymantasbudrys.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/ZymantasBudrys_JMP_LastConsumer.pdf) finds up to +12.61 bp on day +1 for competitive awards (2,088 events), but the return starts from before the 5 p.m. announcement the prior day; it is not next-open performance. [Pyun 2025](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/ecolet/v252y2025ics0165176525001727.html) reports positive long-horizon results in its abstract but does not expose the exact clock or size. A [2014–2024 USAspending panel](http://arc.hhs.se/download.aspx?MediumId=6756) with 118 firms and 14,104 firm-months finds no significant coefficient in its preferred obligations-to-sales exposure model. It is not a timed new-award test. The older [Lerman and Livnat filing study](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~jlivnat/f8k%20current.pdf), Table 3D p. 33, finds +1/+10/+22 bp from the day after filing over 30/60/90 calendar days for all Item 1.01, in 2005–2006 only. The [8-K trading gap result](https://hls.harvard.edu/bibliography/the-8-k-trading-gap/) buys before disclosure and is not available to this rule.
- **MEASURED on synthetic data:** Use +30 bp gross and 10 bp friction. Approximate a cohort SD of 320 bp by scaling the printed mean divided by t by the square root of 620; this is a cohort proxy, not a recovered trade-level SD, and the authors note overlapping windows ([TenderAlpha/HKU](https://www.tenderalpha.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/White-Paper-Defense-Contract-Announcements-TenderAlpha-HKU-1.pdf)). The 252-count case fails the 300 mark. Simulate even all 1,006 vendor observations on a generous modern calendar: 13.16% joint power, 0.89% with zero gross edge after costs and 2.50% with zero net edge. Close before prices on the measured narrow count and the weak high-count power, not a claim that the whole public universe has fewer than 300.

## Reproduction

STATED. One hypothetical funded long per event, paired to a same-clock benchmark, not a live short. Costs are a 10 bp deduction on the net difference, equivalent to 5 bp per side when the benchmark is a paper comparison. These are favorable planning costs, not quotes. Whole-share affordability and auction eligibility for a small account are UNKNOWN. The five marks are at least 300 events, positive mean after costs, clustered t at least 2, mean still positive after dropping the best ceiling of 1% of events, and at least 7 positive calendar years out of 11.

MEASURED. The script runs 30,000 synthetic normal worlds per case. Quarter shocks (20%, or 5% in the row 11 sensitivity) capture earnings calendars and overlapping holds; recurring issuer shocks add 3% variance. These correlations are assumptions, not reported estimates. The t uses quarter and issuer clusters, subtracts their intersection and does not let the variance fall below either one-way estimate. The effective count for the quick screen comes from that shock design. The simulation applies no edge to one common-rally case and adds the same +50 bp to both sides; the paired result is unchanged. The saved output records all mark rates, both nulls, and the count assumptions.
