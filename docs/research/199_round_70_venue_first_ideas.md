## Candidate 2: Deep Resting Bids Far Below Touch on Thin Pairs (KILL / SKIP)

- Plain-words rule: On thin Binance.US USD or USDT altcoin pairs, post a `LIMIT_MAKER` (STATED 0.00% fee) $250 buy order resting 3% to 5% below the current best bid, refreshed every hour, aiming to fill only on a thin-book retail market-sell wick, then exit with a limit sell near the pre-wick midpoint or a taker exit after 2 to 6 hours.
- Comparison with Measurement 121 on closed-end funds (`docs/research/170_m121_result.md`, ledgers 223 and 224): In Measurement 121, pretend $500 bids resting at the **best bid** for 30 minutes on 23 CEFs filled 7.56% of the time (227 of 3,003), and 5 minutes after fill the best bid sat **-26.6 bp below the fill price** (95% interval -30.3 to -23.0 bp). Deep bids 3% to 5% below touch on Binance.US differ in two ways: they do not try to earn a 12.6 bp half-spread against ordinary order flow (targeting 100 to 300+ bp wick rebounds instead), and Binance.US charges 0% maker fee. However, they suffer a **worse** form of the exact same adverse-selection trap for an hourly-waking agent: in crypto, most 3% to 5% hourly drops are correlated market-wide crashes (such as the Oct 10 crash simulation in Bieganowski and Ślepaczuk 2026 cited in `doc 196`), not isolated local fat-finger wicks. Because an hourly agent cannot cancel its bid during the 59 minutes between wakes, any sudden BTC drop leaves our stale bid sitting on the book to be hit by fast arbitrageurs right as prices fall further.
- Who loses the money and why they keep doing it: Ideally, a retail seller who dumps a large market order into a thin altcoin book. In practice, with a 60-minute wake interval, **we** are the stale liquidity provider who loses to faster traders whenever the whole crypto market drops mid-hour.
- Why a human cannot do it but an hourly agent can: An agent can maintain resting bids across 20 to 50 thin pairs 24/7, but an **hourly** wake is structurally too slow to cancel stale bids during mid-hour market-wide moves.
- Count stage that reads no price outcome: Would require 30+ days of forward order-book and `aggTrades` logging to count how many 3% to 5% wicks are Binance.US-only vs correlated BTC drops (not worth running while E12 already logs resting bids).
- Pass mark and cost if ever tested: 0% STATED maker entry plus 2 bp taker exit plus exit spread walk; strict trade-through fill only; positive 5-minute and 2-hour bid markout with 95% day-clustered CI strictly above zero (matching Measurement 121's bar).
- What in the ledger it would repeat: Overlaps Measurement 121 (ledgers 223 and 224, closed on adverse selection), E12's already-running 300-second strict-trade-through resting-bid line on Binance.US USD pairs (ledger 230, queue 135), Round 61 Question 3, and Round 68 Decision 2.
- Data terms status: `api.binance.us` (`NONE` endpoints; full Terms **UNKNOWN**).
- Verdict: **KILL / SKIP.** Unattended hourly deep bids are stale-quote prey during market-wide crashes; wait for E12's already-running resting-bid readout before any new maker study.
