# Queue 101: can a free archive reproduce earnings-date advancements?

MEASURED. The completed 200-firm pilot found 59 prospective earnings-release dates in clocked SEC filings, but **zero qualifying advancements**. Dated issuer press releases were easier to find in a separate 20-firm check, but we could not establish when those historical pages first became public. **Close queue 101 on the tested free sources.** The EDGAR projection is below the 400-event close line; neither channel supplies evidence for the 1,000-event go line. This is a source and count decision, not a test of the trading effect.

- **STATED | Paper and public proxy.** Johnson and So write: “To characterize the extent of a given calendar revision, we create a variable, REV, which equals the number of trading days the expected date shifts as a result of firms’ disclosure.” REV is the prior trading day's expected date minus the newly disclosed date; the paper labels `REV > +5` “Advance” and requires the revision 11–31 trading days before the new date ([paper, section 3](https://www.wallstreethorizon.com/upload/SoandJohnsonTimeWillTellUpdatedOct2016.pdf); [MIT publication record](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/563ff4e7-3c2d-4168-b656-e4cb5dd7ce4c)). The [pre-draw protocol](../../research_rig/COUNT_101_earnings_date_advancers.md) uses a previously published Item 2.02 result for the corresponding prior-year quarter, projecting the same ordinal weekday in the same month. This tests an observable proxy, not Wall Street Horizon's proprietary prior forecast. A call-only notice, publication of actual earnings, or report that merely repeats an already announced date does not create a fresh release-date signal.
- **MEASURED | Pilot frame.** The March 31, 2025 [SPTM holdings filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064642/000175272425125150/0001752724-25-125150-index.html) gave 1,396 corporate-equity positions and 1,388 normalized issuer names. The [SEC company list](https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers_exchange.json) gave 1,230 uniquely matched NYSE/Nasdaq CIKs, with 155 names unmatched and 3 ambiguous. The fixed hash order drew 200 without replacement. This is a size-range proxy from one fund snapshot joined to a later company list, not historical index membership or a security-level orderability roster.
- **MEASURED | SEC coverage.** All 200 [EDGAR submissions histories](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) and 18 companion histories opened. Screened all 2,858 original Item 7.01/8.01 filing candidates dated 2023–2025; 2,855 primary bodies opened. Of 5,911 ordinary archive document or index attempts including exhibits, 5,786 opened, 117 reached the document-size limit, one returned HTTP 404 and seven returned HTTP 504. Eight explicit exhibit mentions remained unresolved after checking normal filing indexes; one filing hit the eight-exhibit screen limit. The 2 GiB disk guard stopped at 160 retained screens; after space was freed the same draw resumed at that checkpoint. No full-text search, wire or Nasdaq press archives, or refusal workarounds were used ([SEC fair-access guidance](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data)).
- **MEASURED | Announcements and lead time.** Adjudicated 109 machine-flagged filing/date pairs. Found 59 distinct prospective results dates, including notices accompanied by preliminary results: 19 in 2023, 20 in 2024 and 20 in 2025. Twenty-two firms supplied at least one new date; 24 of 200 firms (12%) supplied a new or repeated future release date in an in-scope 8-K. That is the verified share found, not a census of all SEC items or formats. Lead to the scheduled date was a median 18 calendar days (range 1–78, middle half 14–23). Item 2.02 confirmed release on the scheduled date for 53 notices, and established two different dates ([Chemours result](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1627223/000095017024037412/cc-20240327.htm); [Atlantic Union result](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883948/000110465924109994/tm2426326d1_8k.htm)). Among these 55, the actual lead was a median 20 calendar days (range 1–78); the actual release date for the other four is unverified by this check.
- **MEASURED | Advancement count.** Of the 59 notices, 28 fell outside the 11–31-session window and six within the window disclosed preliminary earnings information. Of the remaining 25, two lacked a unique qualifying prior-year Item 2.02 anchor. All 23 classifiable shifts were between 10 sessions later and three sessions earlier: zero exceeded five sessions earlier. The realized-history stage screened 579 original same-day Item 2.02 candidates across 35 relevant firms, yielding 555 machine-flagged earnings-result disclosures; the 23 prior-date anchors used by the forecast were checked against the source passages for the relevant quarter. For an example of the clock, [Atlantic Union's October 1, 2025 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883948/000088394825000094/aub-20251001xex99d1.htm) scheduled October 23 and was accepted at 08:06:53 ET. The history proxy expected October 20, so the announcement was a delay of three sessions, not an advance.
- **MEASURED / UNKNOWN | Issuer sites.** In a separate direct-site check of the first 20 seed-ranked firms, 15 had an issuer-hosted page showing a date and explicitly scheduling results; one more scheduled results on an undated page, and four checked notices dated a call only. The 15 firms yielded 16 dated notices, including a [Simply Good Foods change](https://www.thesimplygoodfoodscompany.com/news-releases/news-release-details/simply-good-foods-reschedules-date-report-first-quarter-fiscal). Displayed leads were a median 21 calendar days (range 7–57). None of the 16 had a matching prospective release date in the in-scope 8-K screen. For example, [TransMedics](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-report-first-quarter-2025-financial-results-may-8/) displayed April 24 at 16:05 ET for May 8 results, and [RXO](https://investors.rxo.com/news/news-details/2025/RXO-Schedules-First-Quarter-Earnings-and-Investor-Conference-Call-for-May-7-2025/default.aspx) displayed April 7 for May 7 results. The displayed date, even if accurate, is not an independent historical observation that the page was already public that day. No issuer-only notice was promoted into the strict count. The successful press probes show an important gap in the EDGAR channel; they do not establish the press-only share for all 200 firms. **STATED:** Wall Street Horizon told the paper's authors that most notices in its earlier historical sample came from press releases or company webpages and were rarely sourced from SEC filings ([paper, section 3.1](https://www.wallstreethorizon.com/upload/SoandJohnsonTimeWillTellUpdatedOct2016.pdf)).
- **MEASURED / STATED | Ten-year projection.** The registered 1,230-firm and illustrative 1,500-firm projections multiply the pilot count by 20.5 and 25, respectively. Both point estimates are zero. The firm-resampled interval collapses to zero because no firm had an advance; a more informative one-sided 95% Poisson range is 0–61 for the matched frame and 0–75 on a 1,500-firm scale, conditional on complete detection and a stable rate. Those ranges are not upper bounds on missed events, excluded companies, other archives or historic changes in filing behavior. Even counting both unresolved histories as advances, the same 1,500-firm scaling would give 50, not 400. The total across all free sources and actual $500 orderability remain UNKNOWN.
- **STATED | Halved-effect power.** Half the paper's 138.4 bp gross anchor is 69.2 bp; subtracting the assumed 20 bp round trip leaves 49.2 bp against 800 bp paired noise ([paper](https://www.wallstreethorizon.com/upload/SoandJohnsonTimeWillTellUpdatedOct2016.pdf); [Round 32](../121_idea_round_32.md)). [Doc 117](117_power_screen_before_data.md) gives `0.00378225 × N_eff`: zero on the pilot and at its projected point count, even if every observation is independent. At the conditional 1,500-firm upper count of 75, the screen is at most 0.28, or 0.14/0.07 at 50%/25% effective independence. Even 1,000 events at full independence gives 3.78. The 4 and 8 screens require at least 1,058 and 2,116 independent observations. The inputs are planning assumptions, not estimated returns.
- **MEASURED | Disposition.** Close the tested free historical route, not the economic hypothesis. EDGAR supplies a defensible clock, but the detectable advancement count is below the line. Current issuer pages can name a date, but the pilot did not establish a ten-year series with the required public-time provenance. The off-repo count directory is private (`0700`) and retains metadata only. No whole submissions, market prices, replay, computed returns or score.
