# What actually works in crypto trading for a small U.S. account?

As of November 24, 2026, the most defensible experiments are low-turnover BTC/ETH spot trend and, as a separate later project, fully collateralized regulated futures basis. Neither is a demonstrated retail day-trading business: the directional evidence varies by period, and the historical basis is a gross spread with real financing and margin risks ([Liu and Tsyvinski](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/34/6/2689/5912024); [Hudson and Urquhart](https://link.springer.com/10.1007/s10479-019-03357-1); [BIS Crypto Carry](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1087.pdf)). Labels below distinguish MEASURED (empirical findings or official terms/rules), STATED (project, vendor or unaudited analyst claims), and UNKNOWN (not verified). MEASURED backtests are historical evidence, not verified live P&L.

## Retail base rates

| Evidence | Result and walkaway |
|---|---|
| Global exchange app adoption | MEASURED: Across 95 countries in 2015–22, BIS estimated 73–81% of retail users likely lost on their initial BTC investment, using app adoption as a purchase proxy, not audited trader statements; separate BIS analysis found a majority in nearly all economies lost on modeled BTC holdings and smaller users bought as larger users sold around Terra and FTX ([BIS Working Paper 1049](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1049-crypto-trading-and-bitcoin-prices-evidence-new-database-retail-adoption); [BIS Bulletin 69](https://www.bis.org/publications/bulletin-69-crypto-shocks-and-retail-losses)). |
| Actual transactions | MEASURED: About 200,000 eToro retail accounts in 2015–19 showed momentum-like buying in crypto, unlike contrarian behavior in stocks and gold; this is evidence of behavior, not a U.S. day-trader net-profit distribution ([Kogan et al., eToro transactions](https://conference.nber.org/conf_papers/f187152.pdf)). U.S. household payments to/from major exchanges offer a separate lens on adoption, not fills or complete strategy P&L ([Aiello et al., exchange-linked transactions](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w31856/w31856.pdf)). |
| Pump.fun and PumpSwap | STATED: CoinGecko’s Dune analysis reports positive monthly wallet P&L for 30.08% in June 2025 versus 73.28% in April 2026; across six months, a separate Dune analyst reported 43.77% profitable among wallets that both bought and sold ([CoinGecko's methodology and tables](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback); [Dune wallet analysis](https://dune.com/web3frank/pumpfun-6-months-trader-analysis)). These are neither person-level nor all-in results: never-sold inventory is missed, bots and wash activity contaminate the counts, and CoinGecko nets flows across tokens within wallets ([CoinGecko's methodology](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)). |
| Exact U.S. retail bot odds | UNKNOWN: I did not find a representative, longitudinal dataset linking U.S. retail intraday crypto accounts to full fees, holdings, closures, and benchmark-adjusted P&L; none of the above studies provides one ([BIS adoption study](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1049-crypto-trading-and-bitcoin-prices-evidence-new-database-retail-adoption); [eToro account study](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31317); [Pump.fun measurement limitations](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)). |

## What the strategies actually earned in studies

| Family | Reported result, implementation limits and signs of decay |
|---|---|
| Time-series trend (days to weeks) | MEASURED: The 2018 Liu–Tsyvinski draft found a one-standard-deviation increase in daily BTC return predicted a 0.33 percentage-point increase in the next day’s return in the early crypto sample; the published 2021 paper confirms strong time-series momentum for BTC, XRP and ETH, not an executable net return ([NBER draft, Table 14](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24877/w24877.pdf); [published RFS paper](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/34/6/2689/5912024)). Independent Hudson–Urquhart work tested 14,919 technical rules through 2017, found some historical BTC break-even estimates of 58–66 bps, but no positive BTC performance in the first half of 2018 holdout; other coins did better, and later research found select rules can improve BTC risk-adjusted returns out of sample under its cost assumptions ([Hudson and Urquhart](https://link.springer.com/10.1007/s10479-019-03357-1); [Ghent repository paper on BTC rules](https://backoffice.biblio.ugent.be/download/01HY3C3S169G1N6QNYR55NZMFB/01HY60XZGZYHNQ6188MSVJT0SG)). |
| Intraday momentum/reversal | MEASURED: A five-venue BTC/USD study through December 2020 reported a 16.69% annualized return and 1.72 Sharpe for a two-signal last-half-hour timing strategy versus 6.54% and 0.46 Sharpe for always going long in the same half-hour; unlevered break-even cost was only about 10 bps ([Bitcoin Intraday Time-Series Momentum, university manuscript](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/100181/3/21Sep2021Bitcoin%20Intraday%20Time-Series%20Momentum.R2.pdf)). Wen et al. found both continuation and reversal in March 2013–May 2020 data, conditional on jumps, liquidity, FOMC releases and COVID, corroborating conditional predictability rather than one universal rule; modern net U.S. retail results are UNKNOWN ([Wen et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm?abstractid=4080253)). |
| Cross-sectional factors (weekly) | MEASURED: Liu–Tsyvinski–Wu identify market, size and momentum; the 2019 draft reported roughly 3% gross weekly long-short momentum in 2014–18, not obtainable by simply holding a long-only basket ([Journal of Finance](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/jofi.13119); [Yale working version](https://economics.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/LiuTsyvinskiWu2019%20COMMON%20RISK%20FACTORS.pdf)). A follow-up using 28 signals across 2015–22 found 3.87% gross weekly and 1.94 annualized Sharpe for its composite, or 2.90% after modeled 30/40-bp long/short costs; turnover was 68.46% weekly ([Fieberg et al., JFQA](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/4C1509ACBA33D5DCAF0AC24379148178/S0022109024000747a.pdf/trend_factor_for_the_cross_section_of_cryptocurrency_returns.pdf)). A separate simpler 30-day momentum sort returned 1.74% weekly through July 2020 but negative, insignificant returns August 2020–December 2023; short availability and thin coins remain real constraints ([Cryptocurrency momentum has (not) its moments](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11408-025-00474-9)). |
| Expiry basis / perpetual funding | MEASURED: BIS found historical BTC/ETH basis averaging above 10% annualized, not net earnings; higher carry predicted more crash risk and margin/liquidation can impair a hedged book ([BIS Crypto Carry](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1087.pdf)). A 2020–March 2024 Binance perpetual backtest found deviations shrank over time and modeled 12.55% BTC / 18.18% ETH annualized excess returns after spread at its highest-cost tier (6.75/1.44 bps one-way spot/futures maker), still institutional pricing, not U.S. retail ([He et al., Table 8](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.06888)). Prefer fixed-expiry carry for the first paper test: expiry defines convergence, whereas funding can flip and cash is needed on both legs ([BIS cash-and-carry analysis](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1087.pdf); [Kraken funding mechanics](https://support.kraken.com/articles/us-perpetual-futures)). |
| Pairs/statistical and cross-venue arbitrage | MEASURED: Ten Binance perpetuals, 2021–23: a moderate market-neutral copula strategy reported about +15 bps gross per trade but −15 bps net after modeled execution and funding; its total net return was −15.4%, with roughly 50% winning trades ([2026 Quantitative Finance and Economics study](https://aimspress.com/aimspress-data/qfe/2026/2/PDF/QFE-10-02-016.pdf)). Makarov–Schoar documented historical U.S.–Korea BTC gaps exceeding 20% at times, much smaller within countries, consistent with capital and transfer frictions; a quote gap is not a fillable U.S. retail round trip ([Journal of Financial Economics manuscript](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/100409/1/Cryptocurrency_Markets_JFE_final_v4.pdf)). |
| Market making / order-book scalping | MEASURED in a preprint live experiment: February/August 2024 Binance BTC perpetual orders showed adverse selection; naïve maker simulations lost about 0.43 bp per round trip even with a 1-bp two-leg rebate, while a modeled reversal variant earned 0.71 bp including that rebate at minimum size; imbalance taking earned about +1 bp gross but −1.96 bp net ([The Market Maker’s Dilemma](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18625)). UNKNOWN: durable independent replication at small-account U.S. prices; a resting order is not a fill and fills can systematically occur just before an adverse move ([live fill experiment](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18625)). |
| On-chain and sentiment | MEASURED in historical studies: 2017–23 hourly flow regressions found ETH inflows negatively forecast ETH returns and USDT exchange inflows positively forecast BTC/ETH at some 1–6-hour horizons; the paper is not a verified live retail track record ([On-Chain Flows preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.06327)). A later attention study found Google search-volume effects for BTC/ETH in 2017–20 but not 2020–22, a warning against assuming stable predictive value ([2017–22 attention replication](https://www.iei1946.it/article/pdf/download/1733/cryptocurrencies-the-role-of-investor-attention)). |
| News and listings | MEASURED: 250 ICO-token cross-listings, mostly 2017–18, showed +6.51% listing-day abnormal return and +9.97% over a seven-day event window, with some venues near zero; BIS separately found large responses to regulatory headlines ([cross-listing event study](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10203-021-00323-0.pdf); [BIS regulatory news study](https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt1809f.htm)). UNKNOWN: a retail bot capturing those moves after public, timestamped receipt and costs; event-window returns include moves unavailable to a late buyer ([cross-listing event windows](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10203-021-00323-0.pdf)). |
| Weekend / intraday seasonality | MEASURED: In the main October 2020–May 2021 sample, BTC/ETH data from Coinbase Pro, Binance and Uniswap V2 showed lower Saturday volume and volatility and high volatility around the first UTC hour and after 16:00 UTC; model sizing and fills accordingly, not as proof of weekend directional alpha ([Hansen, Kim and Kimbrough](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.12142)). |

## Risks that change the build

- **Custody and cascading losses:** MEASURED: BIS documents exchange concentration and the FTX failure; STATED: Galaxy estimated over $19 billion notional liquidations in 24 hours on October 10, 2025, including automatic deleveraging of profitable shorts on Hyperliquid, and Binance acknowledged local USDe and wrapped-collateral depegs ([BIS G20 risk report](https://www.bis.org/publications/crypto-ecosystem-key-elements-and-risks.pdf); [Galaxy retrospective](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/10-10-anniversary-crypto-liquidation-19-billion-bitcoin-binance-hyperliquid-2026); [Binance incident notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/0989d6c7f32545bfb019e3249eaabc3f)). Avoid treating a stablecoin as equivalent to USD or relying on a stop to cap losses through a thin book; keep idle funds and exposure off execution venues where practicable ([BIS risk report](https://www.bis.org/publications/crypto-ecosystem-key-elements-and-risks.pdf); [Binance incident notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/0989d6c7f32545bfb019e3249eaabc3f)).
- **24/7 feeds and API credentials:** MEASURED: Continuous trading can still include venue maintenance, and Kraken’s domestic perps have scheduled maintenance windows ([Kraken perpetuals support](https://support.kraken.com/articles/us-perpetual-futures)). Use trade-only credentials with withdrawal/transfer disabled where supported, static-IP allowlists, separate portfolios, and no secrets in agent context; design a stale-feed halt, an independent exposure check and a manual cancel/flatten procedure ([Coinbase API key permissions and allowlisting](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/authentication-authorization/api-key-authentication); [Robinhood API security](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/crypto-api/)).
- **Misleading liquidity and on-chain ordering:** MEASURED: Cong et al. estimate wash trades averaged over 70% of reported volume on the unregulated venues in their 29-exchange sample, not across all venues today; Daian et al. document transaction ordering and priority-gas auctions used to front-run ordinary DEX trades ([NBER Crypto Wash Trading](https://www.nber.org/papers/w30783); [Flash Boys 2.0](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10159474)). Screen by verified depth and actual execution quality, not printed volume; exclude memecoin sniping, illiquid launches and DEX arbitrage from this build ([wash-trading study](https://www.nber.org/papers/w30783); [DEX ordering study](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10159474)).

## U.S. tax and records

- **Property and wash sales:** MEASURED: Federal IRS guidance treats virtual currency as property; sale or exchange of investment crypto is generally a recognition event, including crypto-for-crypto swaps, and short holding periods typically produce short-term capital results ([IRS Notice 2014-21](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-14-21.pdf); [IRS digital asset FAQs](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-digital-asset-transactions)). Current §1091 covers stock or securities, so ordinary directly held non-security coins such as BTC are generally outside it; tokenized securities can be subject to it, and proposals to extend it to digital assets are not enacted by the records checked ([26 U.S.C. §1091](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapO-partVII-sec1091.htm); [IRS 1099-DA wash-sales instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da); [H.R. 9172 text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9172ih/html/BILLS-119hr9172ih.htm); [H.R. 10357 status](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/10357)). Keep futures and funding in separate ledger categories and have a professional confirm contract-specific tax treatment, rather than assuming spot rules apply to every product ([IRS digital asset FAQs](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-digital-asset-transactions); [IRS 1099-DA instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da)).
- **1099-DA and wallet/account lots:** MEASURED: Covered custodial brokers report gross proceeds on reportable 2025 sales; basis reporting for 2025 was optional ([IRS 2025 Form 1099-DA instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i1099da--2025.pdf)). From 2026, proceeds reporting continues on reportable sales, and basis is mandatory for covered assets acquired after 2025 and held in the same custodial account until the broker effects the disposition; an older or transferred lot can be noncovered ([IRS 2026 Form 1099-DA instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da)). Basis is tracked per wallet/account; the IRS transition safe harbor allocates unused basis as of January 1, 2025, and adequate timely specific ID matters, otherwise default ordering applies ([IRS Rev. Proc. 2024-28](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-24-28.pdf); [IRS digital asset FAQs](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-digital-asset-transactions)). Congress disapproved a separate December 2024 rule concerning noncustodial intermediaries, not the underlying obligation to report gains or the custodial rules ([Public Law 119-5](https://www.congress.gov/119/plaws/publ5/PLAW-119publ5.htm); [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da)).
- **What to log:** Record UTC timestamp, asset and units, order/fill IDs, fees and fee currency, contemporaneous USD value, base/quote legs, venue/wallet, lot assignment, funding, and transfer transaction hash; reconcile fills to wallet movements and broker reports and retain raw exports ([IRS basis and substantiation FAQs](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-digital-asset-transactions); [IRS 1099-DA instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da)). Budget for Idaho reporting too; its special capital-gains deduction is limited to qualifying Idaho property, not a generic trading break ([Idaho State Tax Commission capital-gains guidance](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/income-tax/individual-income/popular-credits-and-deductions/capital-gains/)).

UNKNOWN: These projects provide code and features, but I found no audited prospective cohort measuring persistence of small-account profitability net of fees and slippage; stars, public examples or individual screenshots do not establish a base rate ([Freqtrade docs](https://docs.freqtrade.io/en/stable/); [Hummingbot docs](https://hummingbot.org/docs/); [Jesse repository](https://github.com/jesse-ai/jesse); [OctoBot repository](https://github.com/drakkar-software/octobot); [CCXT wiki](https://github.com/ccxt/ccxt/wiki/README)).

## Implications for our build

1. **First: BTC/ETH spot trend, long or flat.** Test a few preregistered rules on multi-hour/daily bars with low turnover and no leverage; compare against cash and risk-matched passive exposure, not just raw P&L, and expect possible failure given prior BTC holdout results ([Liu–Tsyvinski](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/34/6/2689/5912024); [Hudson and Urquhart](https://link.springer.com/10.1007/s10479-019-03357-1)).
2. **Second: regulated fixed-expiry carry, initially observation only.** Quote both legs with real fees, collateral opportunity cost and extra margin headroom; require positive net carry across stress cases, enough size precision and a viable unwind without an assumed instant transfer ([BIS Crypto Carry](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1087.pdf); [Kraken margin rules](https://support.kraken.com/articles/margin-on-us-futures)).
3. **Third: use seasonality, funding, public news and on-chain alerts for risk and execution, not autonomous directional bets.** An inexpensive monitor can summarize anomalies for a higher-level reviewer, but disconnect both models from credentials and keep sizing, limits, state reconciliation and stop logic deterministic ([volatility/volume periodicity](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.12142); [news event study](https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt1809f.htm); [Coinbase key permissions](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/authentication-authorization/api-key-authentication)).
4. **Do not graduate a backtest straight to live.** Freeze parameters, use point-in-time universe and news timestamps, walk forward across regimes, model spread, fees and missed fills, and paper/shadow test before considering any live order; decline market making, retail HFT, offshore perp and memecoin strategies for the first build ([BTC out-of-sample failure](https://link.springer.com/10.1007/s10479-019-03357-1); [execution experiment](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18625); [Hyperliquid terms](https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/terms); [memecoin measurement limitations](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)).
