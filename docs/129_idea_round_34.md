## Queued ideas (rows 106 and 107)

### Row 106: Institutional Threshold and Calendar 60/40 Stock-Bond Rebalancing Spread (`SPY` vs `IEF` or `TLT`)

- Source (opened): Harvey, Mazzoleni, and Melone (March 2025, revised January 2026), NBER Working Paper No. 33554, [The Unintended Consequences of Rebalancing](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w33554/w33554.pdf).
- Verbatim quotes (STATED): "When stocks are overweight, funds sell stocks and buy bonds, leading to a decrease in equity returns of 17 basis points over the next day." "We find a one-standard-deviation increase in the Threshold (Calendar) signal leads to a decrease in equity returns of approximately 16 basis points (bps) (17 bps) and an increase in bond returns of about 4 bps (2 bps) over the next trading day." "Economically, a one-standard-deviation decrease in the Threshold (Calendar) signal corresponds to an increase in equity returns of about 15.9 bps (16.9 bps) and a decrease in bond returns of about 4.1 bps (2.3 bps) over the next trading day." "The managed portfolio constructed using these rebalancing signals delivers significant positive alphas and achieves a Sharpe ratio exceeding 1 over the 1997 to 2023 sample period." "We have extended the sample through 2025 and find that the results become even stronger."
- Sample period and post-publication survival (STATED): Main daily futures sample is September 10, 1997 to March 17, 2023 (front-running Sharpe 1.11, volatility 9.17%, t-stat > 4; Sharpe 0.90 excluding September 2008 to March 2009 and March 2020). Post-sample extension through 2025 is STATED as stronger in the January 2026 revision, though exact post-2023 numbers are not printed (UNKNOWN exact magnitude until tested on our 2016 to 2026 SIP bars).
- Why this is not a repeat of Replay 25 or Replay 41: Replay 25 (passed) only held long `TLT` in the last 3 sessions of the month unconditionally. Replay 41 (failed, +1.48 bps, t 0.14) only switched long-only between `SPY` and `TLT` at month-end against always-`TLT`. Row 106 tests the state-dependent Threshold signal (60/40 equity-bond drift averaged across thresholds 0% to 2.5%, which fires mid-month whenever drift crosses the band) plus the Calendar final-week interaction on the next-day cross-asset spread (`SPY` minus `IEF`/`TLT`), where the equity leg carries 4x to 7x the bond leg's move.
- Cost per side: MEASURED 1 bp half-spread on `TLT` closing cross (`docs/research/70_measure_27_tlt_closing_cross.md` and Queue row 9f); modeled conservatively at 2 bps per side (4 bps RT single leg, 8 bps RT for a 2-leg long-short pair). On a $500 cash account without shorting, replacing short `SPY` with long `SH` (1x inverse S&P 500 ETF, about $40/share) adds roughly 2 to 3 bps per side plus 0.35 bps/day expense drag; both the hedged long-short pair (`SPY` vs `IEF`/`TLT`) and the cash-account (`SPY`/`IEF`/`SH`) variant should be pre-registered.
- Power screen (Doc 117, 10-year 2016 to 2026 SIP window): Discrete `|signal| >= 1 SD` non-overlapping events N = 400 (about 40 events/year), published 1-day cross-asset gross edge 20.0 bps (Threshold) / 19.2 bps (Calendar), net edge +12.0 bps after conservative 8 bps 2-leg RT (+16.0 bps at 4 bps RT), 1-day `SPY - IEF` spread noise 105 bps -> `400 x (12 / 105)^2 = 5.22` (and `9.29` at 4 bps RT). Equivalently, continuous daily front-running over T = 10 years at net Sharpe 0.70 to 0.90 (after 1.5% to 2.0%/yr retail cost drag from gross 0.90 to 1.11) -> `T x SR^2 = 4.90 to 8.10`. Clears 4 and reaches 8 under tight spreads.
- Data already held: `SPY`, `IEF`, and `TLT` daily SIP bars from 2016 onward are already staged off-repo; `SH` daily SIP bars are available via saved Alpaca credentials.

### Row 107: Predicted Regular Dividend-Month Spread (Hartzmark and Solomon 2013, Hedged vs Non-Dividend-Month Peers)

- Sources (opened): Open-Alpha-Lab Study 516 (as of May 31, 2026), [516-dividend-month-premium](https://github.com/Guillain-RDCDE/Open-Alpha-Lab/tree/main/studies/516-dividend-month-premium); Kreidl and Scholz (2021, Journal of Asset Management), [Exploiting the dividend month premium: evidence from Germany](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1057/s41260-021-00215-3.pdf).
- Verbatim quotes (STATED): From Study 516: "Predicted-dividend months earn +40.31 bps/month more than non-dividend months (Welch t = 2.56); the within-firm per-name premium is +52.28 bps/month at one-sample t = 3.63, random-calendar placebo p ≈ 0.001, robust to the prediction threshold (per-name t = 3.5–3.8)." "The past-only predicted flag (no look-ahead) is as strong as the contemporaneous actual ex-div-month flag (t = 3.63 vs 3.14)." "A long-only 'hold only in predicted-dividend months' overlay stays net-positive and beats buy & hold at every cost — but the surplus over simply staying invested is thin (~1.6 pp/yr at 5 bps, ~0.2 pp/yr at 10 bps) on a 30-name survivor basket." From Kreidl and Scholz (2021): "Our results are robust across various strategy specifications, and the exploitation of the dividend month premium is still profitable in the period after the publication of Hartzmark and Solomon (2013)."
- Sample period and post-publication survival (STATED): Original Hartzmark and Solomon (2013) covered 1927 to 2009. Kreidl and Scholz (2021) confirmed post-2013 profitability on German equities (2003 to 2017, beta-neutral monthly 1-factor alphas 0.13% to 0.47%, 45-day pre-ex CAR 1.79%, t = 2.92). Open-Alpha-Lab Study 516 confirmed US survival through May 31, 2026 (+40.31 bps/month pooled, +52.28 bps/month within-firm).
- Why this is not a repeat of Replay 60 or Plans 61 and 62: Replay 60 and Plans 61 and 62 test unscheduled special cash dividends. Row 107 tests regular quarterly dividends predicted strictly from prior-year calendar cadence (zero look-ahead) as a hedged cross-sectional spread (long predicted-dividend-month payers vs non-dividend-month peers or market hedge).
- Cost per side: STATED/modeled 2.5 bps per side on liquid US large-cap dividend payers (5 bps RT per leg, 10 bps RT for a 2-leg hedged spread).
- Power screen (Doc 117, 10-year 2016 to 2026 SIP window): N = 120 monthly rebalances, published US pooled gross spread +40.31 bps/month (+52.28 bps within-firm), net edge +30.3 bps/month after 10 bps 2-leg RT, diversified monthly spread noise 140 bps (central 130 bps) -> `120 x (30.3 / 140)^2 = 5.62` conservative (up to `8.70` at +35 bps net and 130 bps noise). Clears 4.
- Caveats and first check required: Study 516 used a 30-name survivor basket (mild upward tilt), and a $500 whole-share cash account cannot hold a 20+ stock hedged basket without fractional shares. First step is a price-blind corporate-action coverage check on Alpaca 2016 to 2026 regular ex-dividend dates (since Row 51 noted Alpaca omitted payable dates in 2016 to 2019; ex-date coverage is UNKNOWN until checked) plus a survivorship-free point-in-time payer roster.
- Data already held: Alpaca SIP daily bars and corporate actions from 2016 onward.
