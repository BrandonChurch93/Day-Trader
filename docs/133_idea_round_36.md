## Queued candidate (Row 108)

### Third Friday Price Spike (3FPS): `SPLG` overnight + `SH` intraday AM-settlement tent

- Forced actor and why they must trade on that date (STATED): S&P 500 index option market makers (and derivative participants settling standard monthly AM-settled SPX options). Standard SPX monthly options cease trading on the 3rd Thursday at 16:15 ET and settle at the 3rd Friday 09:30 ET Special Opening Quotation (SOQ). Market makers carry negative aggregate charm into expiry, so their inventory delta mechanically drifts downward overnight across the 17.25-hour window, requiring equity or futures purchases into the 09:30 ET open and subsequent unwind by midday/close.
- Sources (opened): Guido Baltussen, Julian Terstegge, and Paul Whelan (January 2024, [The Derivative Payoff Bias](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=98196)); revised version (August 31, 2026, Revise & Resubmit at Review of Financial Studies, [The Third Friday Price Spike](https://julianterstegge.com/assets/papers/third_friday_price_spike.pdf)); Quantitativo (June 28, 2025, [The Derivative Payoff Bias](https://www.quantitativo.com/p/the-derivative-payoff-bias)); Max Martone (July 2, 2026, [The Third Friday Price Spike in the S&P 500](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/third-friday-price-spike-sp-500-true-distortion-anomaly-max-martone-fczac)).
- Verbatim quotes (STATED): From Baltussen, Terstegge, and Whelan (Aug 2026): "the S&P 500 drifts upward from the close on the preceding Thursday to the 3rd Friday open, when the SOQ is fixed, then fully reverses by midday 3rd Friday." "The black line shows the combined strategy, which averages approximately 25 basis points per expiration and turns one dollar in November 1992 into $2.52 by December 2023." From Baltussen, Terstegge, and Whelan (Jan 2024): "A strategy that exploits the 3FPS-reversal pattern yields a positive return in 16 out of 19 years in our sample, and delivers a gross (net) Sharpe ratio of 1.3 (0.8)." From Martone (July 2026, 30-year SPY replication July 1996 to July 2026): "The intraday reversal replicates strikingly well: −15.16 bps here vs. −15.57 bps in the paper, both highly significant" "The overnight return on non-quarterly expiries is positive (+6.7 bps), directionally consistent with the paper. It is the quarterly, dividend-linked expiries that pull the full-sample overnight average negative (−28.3 bps average, heavily concentrated in March/June/September/December — see chart below)." From Quantitativo (June 2025, 20 post-publication events after Sept 20, 2023): "After publication, the mean return on the long leg is 0.7 bps, while the mean return on the short leg is -2.6 bps. Both minimal." "The result: t-stat = 0.51 and p-value = 0.61." "So, did this anomaly stop working after publication? Not quite. But it clearly weakened."
- Measured size after costs and post-publication status (STATED): In the Aug 2026 revision (Nov 1992 to Dec 2023, 371 expirations), S&P 500 close-to-SOQ averaged +9.80 bps (t = 3.04) and SOQ-to-close averaged -15.10 bps (t = -3.57); in the post-2011 subsample (Sept 2011 to Dec 2023), close-to-SOQ was +10.07 bps (t = 2.20) and SOQ-to-close was -15.51 bps (t = -2.34), for +25.58 bps gross combined. In the Jan 2024 Table V trading test (2003 to 2021), the combined strategy earned +27.48 bps gross (t = 3.94) and +24.19 bps net (t = 3.47, net Sharpe 0.80, positive in 16 of 19 years), and quarterly triple-witching expirations averaged +26.44 bps overnight (t = 4.36) plus -37.23 bps intraday (t = -3.54). Post-publication evidence (after Sept 2023) is mixed: Martone confirms the SPY intraday reversal (-15.16 bps) through July 2026, while Quantitativo finds ES weakened across 20 post-publication expirations (though NQ stayed +7.8 bps long / -15.1 bps short and the before-vs-after test p = 0.61 cannot reject unchanged returns).
- Event count 2016 to 2026 and Doc 117 power screen: 130 monthly 3rd-Friday expirations in 2016 to 2026 (44 quarterly triple-witching dates). Using post-2011 gross +25.6 bps and 98 bps combined per-event SD (implied by post-2011 t-stats): at 8 bps RT (4 sides at 2 bps/side on `SPLG` and `SH`), net edge is +17.6 bps -> `130 x (17.6 / 98)^2 = 4.19 >= 4`; at 4 bps RT (1 bp/side auction prints), net edge is +21.6 bps -> `130 x (21.6 / 98)^2 = 6.32 >= 4`. Quarterly triple-witching subset (`N = 44`, +55.7 bps net at 8 bps RT, 145 bps SD) -> `44 x (55.7 / 145)^2 = 6.49 >= 4`. Note that the intraday-only `SH` leg alone (`130 x (11.5 / 80.6)^2 = 2.65 < 4`) is underpowered; the full two-leg tent is required.
- $500 US brokerage execution and data needed: Whole-share $500 cash account buys `SPLG` (~$70/share, SPDR Portfolio S&P 500 ETF, avoids `SPY` >$500/share whole-share constraint) at 3rd Thursday close, sells at 3rd Friday open, buys inverse `SH` (~$10 to $40/share) at 3rd Friday open, and sells at 3rd Friday close (zero overnight inverse hold, so zero compounding drag). Data needed: Alpaca SIP daily and minute bars and corporate actions for `SPLG` (or `SPY` benchmark) and `SH` from 2016 onward (already held off-repo), plus the public Cboe 3rd-Friday holiday-adjusted calendar. Pre-registration must account for quarterly `SPLG` ex-dividend dates (which fall on 3rd Fridays of March, June, September, and December) and include a dedicated post-September-2023 post-publication split.
