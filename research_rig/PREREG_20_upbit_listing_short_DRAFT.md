## Judged rule

- Keep the first KRW notice per dated asset in the file, from 2020-01-01 through **2026-10-24 00:00 UTC inclusive**. The hourly archive sample ends at the UTC close of 2026-10-31 to leave the full red-day and planned hold runway. All later notices in the file still count for control cancellation. The API archive gives first-published times to the minute even for edited notices; it does not establish the exact original notice text or completeness.
- Use the independent dated USD-M roster and its notice-to-contract map to decide whether the contract was trading at publication. Do not use a Plan 16 filename flag. A roster-active event without its required notice candle or input archives is a pre-lock refusal, not an exclusion. Keep dead contracts. Count and name roster-ineligible coins separately.
- Compare the listing day's last hourly close with the prior UTC day's last hourly close. Use the first strict decline on that day or either of the next two days. If the prior close cannot exist because the contract had not launched, that day is not red; count it and keep waiting. A day with an unavailable close while the contract was active is an input fault. If no red day occurs in three days, there is no trade. Enter at the next UTC day's 01:00 open.
- Take the highest high from the notice candle through the last candle before entry. The notice candle can contain prints from before publication; that can help or hurt the replay. The cap is the lower of that high and 25% above entry. On the first positive-volume hourly high at or above the stop, cover at the worse of the stop and the hour's open. If the entry open is already above the pump high, cover immediately at that same open and charge both fees. The stop convention is pessimistic on fills, not necessarily on the choice of stop level. Hourly bars cannot recover the order of prints inside a candle.
- If not stopped, cover at the open 72 hours after entry. A zero-volume entry does not fill. A zero-volume hour cannot trigger a stop, and a zero-volume or absent timed-exit hour waits for the next positive-volume candle. Daily closes may come from zero-volume candles. After entry, an absent trade candle is an hour without prints, not a dropped trade; if the next candle gaps through the stop before the scheduled exit, charge the worse of the stop and its open. Missing required entry or signal candles refuse before the lock.
- If an official announcement gives a Futures delisting settlement time before the short can be covered, cover at that time at the worse of the capped stop and the last positive-volume hourly close, with funding through the end. Flag the exit. An explicitly announced halt on new positions blocks entry but does not close an existing trade. If a required window runs into a day-precision archive tail instead, refuse before the lock because the actual end is unknown. Do not substitute a zero-volume close or use an archive tail as evidence of removal. A gap can lose more than 25%. The unadjusted last positive-volume close is shown only.
- No leverage or compounding. Equal entry notional per trade, with overlaps allowed. Charge 15 bps on each leg's actual notional, with 30 bps shown only. A positive settled funding rate pays the short. Apply the rate to the hourly mark open times fixed quantity, normalized by entry notional. Include settlement on the exit boundary and exclude entry's boundary. Intrahour stop ordering is approximate. Missing required funding or mark data refuses before the lock with a fixed code and affected archive path. Do not impute funding or drop any opened short.
- Reconcile the first-notice cohort as traded, not trading at notice, no red day or no entry fill. Also report the subtotal initially traded on the roster and its trade disposition. An unresolved other case is a pre-lock fault, not a no-trade or a flat result.

- The judged control pool is **ordinary 25% jumps with no Upbit KRW notice for that asset from 7 days before the trigger through the entry time**. At an actual hourly trigger candle, the larger high of the previous two candles must be at least 25% above the close three candles back. Use the dated asset mapping for all notices, including renames and contract multipliers. Retain at most one trigger per asset per rolling seven days. Use the same event cutoff as the listing arm.
- For a supplement-only symbol, the pinned manifest fixes the intersection of its staged trade, mark and funding file spans before any archive is decoded. The prior midnight close, the three trigger lookback hours, the latest possible timed exit and eight hours after it must all fit inside one such span. Its staged months are listing-event months, so these controls sit near listing events by construction. Report the ordinary-trade counts separately for Plan 16 store symbols and supplement-only symbols.
- Start the red-day wait on the trigger's UTC day. Include the prior two pump candles in the high, and otherwise use the same 01:00 entry, stop, exit, zero-volume, contract-end, fee and funding rules. Cancel if a notice arrives through entry, not if it arrives after entry. Show a separate clean pool also excluding notices through exit. A control's required input fault is a pre-lock refusal just as for a listing.
- Draw 1,000 sets without replacement in each set, matching UTC notice year to UTC trigger year. Controls may reappear across sets. Use the fixed seed and linear interpolation for the 95th percentile. An insufficient annual pool cannot pass. Dependence across overlapping events means neither the draws nor the t-statistic is a probability claim.
- All five marks are required: at least 30 judged trades, mean net above zero, mean strictly above the 95th percentile of ordinary-jump set means, mean still above zero after removing the three best trades, and trade-mean t-statistic at least 2.0. Use the mean divided by the sample standard deviation over the square root of trade count. Undefined does not pass.
