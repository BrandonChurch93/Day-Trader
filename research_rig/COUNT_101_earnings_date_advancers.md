# Queue 101: earnings-date advancement source and count protocol

STATED. Ledger 202. This is source feasibility and a small metadata pilot, not a price pull or a scored trial. Lock this protocol and the ledger text before drawing the sample.

- **Paper rule.** Johnson and So's [October 2016 manuscript](https://www.wallstreethorizon.com/upload/SoandJohnsonTimeWillTellUpdatedOct2016.pdf), section 3, states: “To characterize the extent of a given calendar revision, we create a variable, REV, which equals the number of trading days the expected date shifts as a result of firms’ disclosure.” It defines REV as the prior trading day's expected date minus the new expected date and lists “1 (‘Advance’)” for “REV > +5”. The manuscript puts disclosure session r between t−31 and t−11 inclusive, where t is the new scheduled earnings date. The earlier expectation is the live vendor estimate (often unconfirmed and based on the firm's own history, sometimes an explicit prior company date). An earnings release that merely occurs early is not a scheduling signal. A press release mentioning only a call without saying when results will be released is not enough.
- **Public-history proxy.** Use the same-month, same ordinal weekday in the following calendar year, from the prior year's realized earnings release for the matching seasonal quarter, as in the paper's Oracle illustration; map an exchange holiday on the resulting date to the next XNYS session and treat nonexistent fifth weekdays as unclassifiable. Infer the matching prior-year release only if exactly one previously public original Item 2.02 result date falls within 45 calendar days of the one-calendar-year anniversary of the newly disclosed earnings date. Prior events must have their report date and acceptance date on the same New York calendar day and be clearly applicable to normal earnings rather than preliminary numbers, corrections or unrelated transactions; otherwise do not infer. Prefer an already observed explicit schedule for the same release within 45 days over the calendar proxy only when the later announcement explicitly revises it. Do not require the realized result to match the schedule as an ex-ante condition. This is an openly specified proxy, not Wall Street Horizon's reconstructed prior calendar.
- **Candidate sources, in order.** First look for exact date scheduling or revisions in original 8-K Items 7.01/8.01 and their issuer exhibits, using SEC acceptance plus dated release evidence when applicable; an accepted public filing is itself a timestamped disclosure, but not evidence that the issuer published earlier on another channel. Second, on an issuer-owned public IR archive only, require original dated publication and trustworthy same-day public provenance; dates on undated IR calendar entries or readily editable backfilled pages do not suffice. No PR Newswire, Business Wire, Nasdaq press pages, SEC full-text search endpoint, other syndications, or alternate access around refusals. Third, use actual Item 2.02 earnings dates and clocks from previously public original releases for the expectation, not as a substitute for a scheduling announcement. Retain refusal/missing coverage separately; an unread filing is not a verified absence.
- **Fixed frame and seed.** Take the March 31, 2025 [SPTM N-PORT filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064642/000175272425125150/0001752724-25-125150-index.html) as a one-snapshot S&P 1500-size proxy. Its XML supplies issuer name and holdings category but not ticker. Before drawing: retain corporate equity-common (`EC`, `CORP`) issuer identities; match names uniquely to CIK in the [SEC company list](https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers_exchange.json) for US NYSE or Nasdaq, with no fuzzy rescue. Normalize Unicode to ASCII, lowercase, discard punctuation, collapse words, remove leading “the”, remove trailing corporate/class/company suffixes and trailing SEC name jurisdiction delimited by slashes; replace “&” with “and”. Require one CIK, and take its alphabetically first SEC ticker. Do not parse or cache prices, quantities or holding values. Sort eligible CIKs by ascending SHA-256 of ASCII `row101:2026-11-26:v1:<10-digit CIK>`, then CIK ascending, and draw the first 200 without replacement. Disclose name-match losses and survivor and time-window differences; not a point-in-time S&P 1500 reconstruction. If non-price identity evidence for this frame cannot be opened normally, stop rather than drawing from a different frame.
- **Pilot.** Count original, clocked scheduling disclosures accepted in NY dates 2023–2025 and distinct issuing firms. Deduplicate same firm, release date and public disclosure episode; count at most one qualified advance per firm-quarter, earliest valid. Use a shift beyond five XNYS sessions, 11–31 XNYS sessions before the new date; report the actual number of calendar days to the scheduled release (and separately to a matched realized release if documented). If disclosure occurs at or after XNYS close or on a non-session, map the signal session to the next regular session when applying the window. Existing nonprice XNYS sessions are allowed. A current NYSE/Nasdaq common-listing proxy indicates potential retail access; historical broker orderability, fractional eligibility or ability to buy one whole share at $500 are UNKNOWN unless independently documented without prices. Blocked paths remain blocked. Persist only metadata under the private off-repo count directory and stop with less than 2 GiB available.
- **Thresholds and scaling.** Go forward only if a free source gives at least 1,000 advancements over ten years with public evidence no later than the same day for stock a $500 account can buy. Close below 400 or if the public-time proof cannot be made. Intermediate counts, opaque completeness or unverified broker access do not authorize market work. If the draw is complete and the source measured, scale first to the matched frame and then, as an assumption-only sensitivity, by `(1500/200)*(10/3)` for 1,500 firms. Report a descriptive 95% sampling range from 10,000 firm-level resamples with replacement, seeded 101202. The range does not bound archive gaps or survivorship; if all observations are zero, also show a one-sided Poisson upper limit conditional on complete coverage. An unavailable frame or inaccessible corpus means the population estimate is UNKNOWN, not zero.
- **Power sensitivity.** [Doc 117](../docs/research/117_power_screen_before_data.md) uses effective independent count times squared net-edge/noise ratio. Halve the published 138.4 bp gross to 69.2 bp, subtract the scouting assumption of 20 bp round-trip cost, and use 800 bp noise. Show the raw count upper bound and effective-count cases of 25%/50% of raw, plus distinct disclosure-date counts if available. That is planning, not a return measurement. No threshold, sample seed, or model changes after counting.
