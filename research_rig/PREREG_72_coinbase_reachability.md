## Lead commands and private custody

STATED: Use `nice -n 10 python -E -B -X pycache_prefix=/dev/null research_rig/plan_72.py` on a clean audited freeze. - **Hour collection:** `--collect-hourly` writes the authenticatable `hourly_manifest.json` and prints its hash and request count.

A failed run after claim spends this one use. All real modes serialize on the custody directory and refuse after the claim exists. Only the offline synthetic mode still runs.
