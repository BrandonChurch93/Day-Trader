## Files

- `calendar.csv`: ASCII, LF, header `session,close_et`. One ISO exchange session per row in ascending order, with its actual scheduled NYSE close in `HH:MM` Eastern time, including early closes. The first and last rows must equal `warmup_first` and `design_last`. Include `design_first`. Retain the exact calendar source pin.
- `identity.json`: UTF-8 JSON object with integer `schema: 1` and array `securities`. Each row has `id`, `kind`, `ticker_intervals`, `cusips`, `registration_date`, `delisted`, `delist_effective`, `eligibility_intervals`, `splits`, `quarter_first_202`, `share_counts` and `halts`. No price or current-list substitute.
- `id`: Permanent CIK plus class, not ticker. Use letters, digits, periods, underscores, colons and hyphens only. Do not merge reused tickers. Include IWM, QQQ and SPY as fund IDs, never as candidates. `kind` is `stock` or `fund`.
- `ticker_intervals`: Objects with `ticker`, `venue`, `first`, `last` and `asof`. Dates are ISO and intervals inclusive. `cusips` is a list of strings. `registration_date` is the 8-A12B date or null, with any approved alternate evidence recorded in the receipt.
- `delisted`: A true/false flag for documented delistings within this extract; `delist_effective` is the documented Form 25 effective ISO date if true, otherwise null. Missing bars alone are not a delist flag. Identify the last regular bar by its indexed session and reconcile it independently against the identity.
- `eligibility_intervals` and `halts`: Each entry has ISO `first`, `last` and reviewable `evidence`. Eligibility must be point-in-time, including dead listings, and subject to the registered exchange, security type, filing status and common-class checks. Halts are not inferred from missing bars. Leave genuinely inapplicable lists empty.
- `splits`: Objects with ISO `ex_date`, `announcement_date` and positive integer `new_shares`, `old_shares`. Do not use knowledge of a future split to restate any earlier bar. Do not infer an announcement date. Report announcements at a boundary or ambiguous as-of clock before activation.
- `quarter_first_202`: Earliest known qualifying Item 2.02 acceptance by issuer and quarter, with `quarter` as its fiscal-period-end ISO date, `accepted_et` as the exact Eastern acceptance timestamp with offset, and `accession` string. A missing timestamp must remain unknown, not midnight. Preserve provenance and earliest-filing evidence; do not substitute report dates or price moves.
- `share_counts`: Objects with ISO `filed_date` (not report-period end), exact `accepted_et` Eastern timestamp with its offset, ISO `report_date` (period of report), ISO `basis_date` (the share-count reference date), positive integer `shares` and `accession` string. Keep what was in the original filing and retain later filings as separate dated rows. Do not supply a current share count in place of a missing historical count.
- MEASURED, custodian's fixed 200-company probe: 143 had a cover-page ticker tag, 140 had a dated cover share count in all five design years, and 35 had none in any design year. Do not read these as population estimates. A name without a share count dated on or before t−1 gives no Card A signal; count the missing share name-days among candidates with the required price, turnover and history for the rank denominator.
- `bars_index.csv`: ASCII, LF, header `id,first_session_index,rows`. One row per contiguous segment of present bars, ordered by ID then zero-based calendar index. Length is a positive integer; segments cannot overlap or cross the design fence. A gap is a gap between segments, never a repeated bar or zero. Require at least one segment per registered ID in an extract. Reconcile wholly missing issuers separately; do not omit them silently.
- `bars.f64`: Opaque records in segment order, then ascending sessions within each segment. Headerless little-endian IEEE binary64: raw `open,high,low,close,volume`, 40 bytes per record. Use regular-session as-traded bars, with positive finite OHLC, internally valid high/low and finite nonnegative volume. Do not normalize bars or compute triggers, returns or marks in custody.
- `source_receipt.json`: UTF-8 JSON object with integer `schema: 1`, an independently assigned nonempty `review_id`, and nonempty object `source_pins` mapping source names to exact lowercase SHA-256 digests. Review the original source and identity hashes, calendar evidence, omitted IDs, session holes, delisted ends, splits, earnings clocks and filing dates. Include the ledger-mandated independent code audit in the handoff. Keep vendor receipts and evidence private.
- `manifest.json`: UTF-8 JSON object with integer `schema: 1`, the ISO `warmup_first`, `design_first`, `design_last` from config, integer `rows` and object `sha256` mapping exactly the five files above to hashes of their exact bytes. Disallow duplicate keys. Deliver the SHA-256 of the exact manifest bytes separately to the lead for pinning.
- `design_receipt.json`: UTF-8 JSON object with integer `schema: 1`, `manifest_sha256` and `go_id` equal to `p74d-` plus the same lowercase digest. The receipt is a binding to bytes, not itself an approval. The separate committed ledger approval must be exactly one `P74_DESIGN_GO=p74d-<manifest sha256>` token on row 226.
- `COUNT.lock`, `COUNT.receipt.json`, `RUN.lock`: Reserved. Do not create, delete, reset or edit them. The count and scoring locks are separate. The corresponding stage claims its own lock exclusively and syncs the file and directory before decoding anything. Count writes the durable receipt only after finishing, with the count go ID and integer-only output and branch lines. Design verifies both count files match its manifest, re-derives those lines and refuses if they differ. Second runs refuse. A post-lock error does not restore the one-time run.
