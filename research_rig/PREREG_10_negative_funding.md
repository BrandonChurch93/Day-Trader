# Replay 10: buy spot after negative perpetual funding

STATED. This is a fixed plan, not a result. Test the idea that shorts paying longs for a day can mark a crowded short trade and a later spot rebound. A bad day can instead be the start of a longer fall. Each rule starts with cash, buys only spot and pays no interest on cash. It never holds a perpetual, earns funding or places orders.

## Data and split

- Use settled funding from the [Binance USD-M public monthly archive](https://data.binance.vision/?prefix=data/futures/um/monthly/fundingRate/) and hourly US spot bars from [Alpaca](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/cryptobars-1). Match the instruments in `replay_10_funding.symbols`. Kraken's [public funding endpoint](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/historical-funding-rates/historical-funding-rates) is a coverage check only. Do not splice the two futures venues or substitute a gap.
- `calc_time` is the settlement time and `last_funding_rate` is the signed decimal rate. Negative means shorts pay longs. Round the timestamp to the nearest scheduled UTC settlement only within `replay_10_funding.timestamp_tolerance_ms`. Check each row is on the expected `settlement_hours` grid, declare duplicates, reject unusable data and do not impute a missing rate. Splits are not based on funding values or returns.
- The eligible UTC calendar runs from `replay_10_dates.start` through `end`, both inclusive. This is the overlap of full days in the local spot history and complete months of the common funding archive, with the partial first spot day omitted. The most recent `held_fraction` of those UTC days, rounded up to a whole day, is held back. Earlier days are design. Both slices start flat. Prior funding can warm up the trailing windows, but positions cannot cross the boundary.
- Phase 1 audits only timestamp and cadence metadata across the archives. It counts signals and scheduled entries from the design period only, using spot bar timestamps and volume for eligibility. No price return is calculated. Stop before looking at held-back rates, volumes, prices, signals or returns for this rule. The builder decides from those counts whether the single held-back run happens, and the plan is pushed first.

## Rules and fills

- Each symbol in `replay_10_funding.symbols` independently tests the three fixed bands in `replay_10_rules.entry_bps`, in listed order. List BTC first, then ETH, starting at `replay_10_statistics.first_trial_id`. Each pairing is a separate cash account. The account invests all available cash including entry costs.
- At each settlement, add the signed rates for the previous `replay_10_rules.lookback_hours`, including the rate just settled but excluding the rate exactly at the left edge. Convert the sum to basis points. Enter only when it crosses from strictly above the chosen band to at or below it, and both the current and preceding windows have all expected settlements. This counts a crossing once, not every settlement below the band. Missing windows cannot create a crossing. Ignore new entries while a position or order is open.
- A decision from a funding settlement at nominal hour T schedules its fill at T plus `replay_10_funding.fill_lag_hours`, even when the archived print is milliseconds late. Skip a buy if its scheduled Alpaca hourly bar is absent or has no trades, and do not retry it. A nominal buy and its nominal maximum-hold exit must both fit in the slice.
- Exit after the first settled funding rate strictly above `replay_10_rules.positive_exit_bps` after entry, with the same fill lag, or at entry hour plus `max_hold_hours`, whichever gives the earlier scheduled exit. If a funding print is missing, the timed exit still applies. If an exit bar is missing or has no trades, wait for the next traded hourly open inside the slice. Take no same-hour re-entry. If still open at the end, report it at the last known close. Carry the last known mark over a missing close; a quote-only close can mark but not fill. Take daily closing equity for every calendar day with starting cash included.
- Taker only. Use `practice_crypto_fee_bps_added` plus `tm_crypto_half_spread_bps` per side on Alpaca. Buy above the hourly open and sell below it by the half spread. Charge the fee on fill notional. As a fee-only sensitivity, repeat the arithmetic on the same fills with Kraken's [entry-tier spot taker fee](https://support.kraken.com/articles/cross-platform-fee-tier-changes), `replay_10_kraken_spot_taker_bps`, with the same assumed half spread. This is not a Kraken spot fill test. Model neither maker fills nor any funding payment on spot.

- Mark 2: across the two cash-reset slices, beat similarly reset BTC hold at zero cost and stay ahead up to at least `backtest_min_breakeven_cost_multiple` times Alpaca's modeled cost per side. Use the existing rig break-even grid. Scale fee and spread together and reprice the same fills for both rule and hold.
- Mark 3: use whole-sample daily returns and the standard deviation of finite daily Sharpes of this batch as the spread estimate. Require deflated Sharpe above `replay_min_deflated_sharpe`. Count `replay_10_statistics.previous_trials` (every counted fixed-rule trial at freeze, including R1 and R2, replay 07 and replay 09) plus every planned new symbol and threshold pairing in this file. Do not omit sparse variants.
- Mark 4: CSCV across all versions, using `replay_09_statistics.pbo_blocks` from `replay_10_dates.start` and each `pbo_later_starts` date through the held-back end. Drop earliest excess days, count ties against the rule and report every estimate. The largest must be below `backtest_max_pbo`.
- Mark 5: compound the two slices and beat strictly more than `replay_min_shuffle_percentile` of matched timing draws using `replay_09_statistics.draws` and `seed`. Within each slice shuffle the completed tickets, keeping the symbol, full account weight and realized duration. For each ticket in shuffled order, choose uniformly among unused entry times on the same scheduled settlement-plus-lag grid whose windows fit and have traded opens at both ends. Reject overlapping windows, including shared endpoints. A draw that cannot place every ticket is rejected and retried. Use at most `draws` placement attempts per accepted draw; exact matching failure makes the mark unknown and failed. Knowing execution availability is allowed only in the random control. If no slice has completed trades or a rule is still open at a slice end, the mark is also unknown and failed. Show the first matched draw as the random twin, with growth and sides per slice.

## How it can fool us

- Count eligible threshold crossings and entries separately. Flag any rule under `replay_10_rules.min_design_entries`; nearby events can still be the same selloff, and BTC and ETH can trigger together. Do not retune the bands using these counts. Keep all variants if the approved test runs.
- Funding rates can differ in sign across exchanges at the same time. Binance's contracts are USDT-margined; the spot execution model is Alpaca USD. The result would test that pairing, not a venue-independent funding effect. Archive timestamps do not prove when the print was visible live. Alpaca hourly opens and an assumed spread do not prove executable fills.
- These dates can overlap earlier research for other signals. Here the holdout is unused for this rule, not unseen market history. Even a pass would only justify a forward paper trial.
