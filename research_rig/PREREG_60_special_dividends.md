## Universe, clocks, execution

Keep the same docket, filing eligibility, strict acceptance-before-scheduled-NYSE-close rule, day +2 open and planned day +20 close, CBIO noncash skip, verified actions, settlement, independent whole-share $500 allocations, cost and all seven marked criteria including 300 placed stock/IWM pairs. Keep the original SPY, +1, higher-friction and five $100 settled sleeves lines shown only. Inherit the entire Plan 43 financial parameter block unchanged.

- **Pre-entry universe:** Use exactly the five frozen padding sessions immediately preceding the shown-only +1 session. Require a present, positive-volume stock raw bar on each and a median of raw close times raw volume of at least USD 100,000, with equality eligible. Thus a USD 500 allocation is at most 0.5% of the threshold daily volume. No after-entry liquidity test. Show unavailable-padding and low-dollar-volume exclusions overall and by acceptance year.
- **Entry:** Missing or zero-volume stock raw bar on day +2 means unfilled, not shifted. Share affordability uses the unchanged cost model. Treat an unavailable +1 open as omission of only that shown entry.
- **During the hold:** Keep the actual position unchanged on absent or zero-volume regular sessions; do not impute a mark or fill. Apply independently verified actions by their existing entitlement rules.
- **Exit:** If day +20 has a present positive-volume stock raw bar, sell at its close. Otherwise take the open of the first positive-volume stock raw session from +21 through +25 inclusive. Refuse the event if none exists. IWM and SPY use the same actual entry and exit sessions and clock sides as the stock; never substitute or impute a control fill. No feasible IWM trade means no judged pair; an unavailable SPY omits only the SPY comparison. Keep settlement lags relative to actual exits. In the sleeve line preserve same-open reservations and one active position per issuer among stock candidates that have supported fills. Delayed sales use the next open, not a later session's close.
- **Counting:** Count the execution categories separately by year, including no-trades, out-of-universe events, waits, missing interior sessions and refusals. There is no refusal, missing-session, delay or screening cap. The only count mark is the existing 300 judged pairs.

## Independent price-basis check

Use the original normalized price packet, native responses, acquisition receipts and independent offline rebuild from the spent Plan 43 acquisition by their exact hashes. There is no new price collection. Identity, timestamps, malformed bars, raw overlap inconsistency, cross-security aliases, native integrity and hash/approval faults remain packet HOLDs. Markets without a trade on a day are allowed under the rules above; missing bars are not zeros for prices.

Adopt a separate action-only vendor packet with native provenance. The vendor packet covers each event leg from the padded start to the padded end plus 90 calendar days, identifies the stable security and ticker, and identifies the underlying entity in both adjusted views. [Alpaca's V2 bars reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars) defines `asof` for symbol identity mapping, not an adjustment cutoff. A missing/unsupported vendor factor for an action that changes in-window adjusted prices refuses that event, rather than resetting a fit. A split's pre-ex factor is old shares divided by new shares. A cash dividend uses the vendor's explicit factor when supplied as such; otherwise use one minus vendor cash per share divided by the immediately preceding regular session's positive raw close from the sealed tape, which must exceed the cash. Sum same-day pure cash amounts with no explicit factors; an ambiguous mixed order refuses. A documented non-price action does not enter the factor. Actions effective on the first window date cannot change any earlier observed prices in the window and need no backward step. Do not substitute issuer-verified actions to make the vendor reconstruction fit.

Propagate those exact fractions backwards through the window and require one positive common tail multiplier for both adjusted views of the same mapped identity. At every positive raw/adjusted pair, each of the four adjusted OHLC fields must be within USD 0.00500001 of its raw field times the propagated factor and the common multiplier. Check exact feasibility by intersecting all intervals; never fit per bar or reset across a gap. Require at least three positive comparisons in each view, with no synthetic observation when a view is missing. Mark an individual leg as failed if it does not reproduce or if either view cannot be mapped to the same approved underlying identity. This tests in-window raw adjustment, not absolute out-of-window price scaling. Report separately how many event-legs have a vendor post-window price action and how many known post-window action products fall inside the implied common factor interval; missing post-window factors are not explanatory evidence. Compare vendor and independent action lists by in-window ex-date, price action type and exact terms, aggregate differences separately, and use only the independent list for actual cash credited and split lot sizes.

An unreproduced stock or IWM leg refuses the event. A SPY-only failure omits just that event's shown SPY comparison, is counted separately, and cannot change the judged result or other shown arms. The shown-only basis sensitivity includes otherwise feasible raw-funded stocks and IWM pairs from events otherwise excluded by a stock/IWM basis refusal. It does not bypass universe, entry, exit, board-lot, or packet-level integrity rules, and it does not affect marks. A reconstruction refusal does not turn a no-trade into a simulated fill.

## Sealed approvals and schema contract

The lead's sealed adoption and validation approvals will be separate single-parent sole-file additions on this draft's later audited freeze. No price or approval action is authorized by this document. The exact historical validation-approval file from commit `98fe76ca25af13867840a5a5eba2735da37979c6` supplies all inherited price packet/provenance hashes, not a historical validation result. Third-attempt freeze `f7f2fbf52f9993bbc8dbb9d8d45c0e09e12d8584` is the immutable term and code lineage reference. Use unchanged owner-only price custody and a separate owner-only `rig/data/replay_60/` run root; keep vendor actions in owner-only `rig/data/plan60_vendor_actions/`.

The scorer-facing `vendor_actions_windows.json` is separate from the custodian's identity-level `vendor_actions.json`. It pins the exact `vendor_action_index.json` and identifies the adopted bars, identity and docket hashes. It has one record per modeled accession, three legs, stable identity, ticker, both view identities, exact `start` and `through`, an explicit `complete` flag, and exact actions. Each item has a unique `id`, `type`, ISO `ex`, and `source_sha256` of its decoded native HTTP response body. Cash has exact `cash_usd` and a null `factor`; split preserves positive `new` and `old` as exact decimal strings, including fractional terms. Nonprice and unsupported items preserve `vendor_type`.

The pinned offline adapter independently parses the retained [Alpaca corporate-action responses](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1). The custodian's direct version-2 all-type transport index binds the exact outgoing symbol, inclusive process-date interval, parameters and page tokens, HTTP status, and the size and SHA-256 of both each retained file and the decoded HTTP body. It separately records retry attempts with their exact outgoing parameters. The first page starts without a token, continuations must match the prior native token, repeats are invalid, and the final native token must be null before the request contributes coverage. The adapter recomputes every projected row and coverage/identity flag and rejects a normalized snapshot that disagrees, including nonempty actions attributed to empty native bodies. Do not translate a legacy record that did not retain outgoing tokens.

Because the API filters on process date, HTTP success alone does not establish ex-date coverage. For each alias the pinned request streams must cover from 90 calendar days before padded start to padded end plus 90 calendar days. The adapter requires no holes in the process-date ranges, resolves documented symbol changes and CUSIP continuity conservatively, and marks incomplete or ambiguous windows instead of inventing actions or identity. It examines the supported response arrays, projects by ex/effective date, and retains explicit unsupported price actions. The scorer-facing projection ends at padded end plus 90 calendar days even if a common alias query covers a wider range. This is a vendor coverage contract, not a claim that the provider has no omissions or late-recorded actions. The endpoint does not supply an explicit adjustment multiplier or reference close; cash uses the in-window reference rule above.

Each file is one ASCII line and a final LF. The validation commit directly follows adoption; GO directly follows validation and pins the exact successful no-return receipt.
