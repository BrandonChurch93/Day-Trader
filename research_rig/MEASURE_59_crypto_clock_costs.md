# Measurement 59. Cost at the 21:00 and 23:00 UTC crypto clock

STATED. Ledger 197 is a measurement, not a trial. This spec fixes windows, quote eligibility and outputs before collection; it is not collection approval. It opens no bars, calculates no return and does not authorize a Plan 58 freeze or run. Raw price and size fields may be handled transiently to compute spread, but are neither persisted nor shown to the author. No file contains keys, raw quotes, trades, mids, per-event price changes or balance information.

## A. IBIT and ETHA historical SIP spreads

STATED. Custodian `/root/market_feed_builder` uses the licensed equities SIP feed, not IEX or an overnight ATS substitute. Attempt every Monday through Friday, `IBIT` from January 11, 2024 and `ETHA` from July 23, 2024, both through November 25, 2026 inclusive ([IBIT Nasdaq listing](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=ETP2024-04); [ETHA Nasdaq listing](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/content/newsalerts/2024/InfoCircular/ETHA_Circular_NQ.pdf)). Label closed and early-close weekdays separately with the official exchange calendars and special closure notices. Do not count those as missing full sessions; do not move clocks. Use `America/New_York`, not a fixed seasonal date heuristic. The two edges are 16:00 and 18:00 New York in standard time, 17:00 and 19:00 in daylight time. Fidelity's [standard after-hours session](https://www.fidelity.com/trading/faqs-placing-orders) is 16:00–20:00 New York and uses limit orders; that does not guarantee a fill or support weekends.

- **Wire request.** Use `GET https://data.alpaca.markets/v2/stocks/quotes` with a single `symbols=IBIT` or `symbols=ETHA`, `feed=sip`, `asof=-`, `currency=USD`, `sort=asc`, `limit=10000` and RFC 3339 UTC `start=edge−90s`, `end=edge+60s`. Traverse all `next_page_token`s. The provider end is inclusive; explicitly drop (t \geq edge+60s). Treat truncated fetches and pagination errors as unavailable, never as empty or covered ([Alpaca quote request contract](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockquotes-1)).
- **Fields in transient memory.** Inspect timestamp `t`, bid/ask levels `bp,ap`, posted sizes `bs,as`, venue codes `bx,ax`, conditions `c`, tape `z`. Accept finite positive bid/ask and positive sizes, with ask strictly greater than bid. Reject malformed, locked, crossed or explicitly non-firm/halted states; apply the feed's documented condition semantics, not guesses. Collapse exact duplicate same-time states; at conflicting same-time states with no authoritative sequence, mark ambiguous and do not choose a favorable quote. Do not retain raw levels, sizes or provider responses; only aggregate derived summaries leave custody.
- **Time-weighted spread.** For scored `[edge−60s, edge+60s)`, each quote state runs until the next state or 30 seconds from its stamp, whichever comes first. The prior 30-second buffer may seed the scored interval. Invalid/ambiguous updates supersede the old state; gaps are unquoted, not zero-spread. For each valid duration use the full relative spread in bp, `10000 * (ask − bid) / ((ask + bid) / 2)`.
- **Placement metric.** At each edge select the first eligible update explicitly stamped in `[edge, edge+60s)`; not a pre-edge carried quote. A dated event is paired only if both edges qualify. For each paired event take half the entry relative spread plus half the exit relative spread, without comparing levels across the two edges. Fills, queue position and routing remain UNKNOWN.
- **Released summaries.** By symbol, season and edge report candidate weekdays, full exchange sessions, holidays, early closes, complete fetches, empty and failed windows, zero-valid windows, first-eligible-within-60s counts, windows with at least 60 valid seconds, total valid seconds and median/p90 valid seconds. Report pooled valid-duration-weighted mean, median, p90 and p95 relative full spreads. Across paired full-session days report coverage counts and paired spread component mean, median, p90 and p95 across days; summarize failed/ambiguous states separately. Show full-sample and each seasonal group. No date-level levels, fills, trade prices, returns or price series in the handoff.

STATED. **IBIT route fails cost eligibility** if paired valid quotes cover less than 95% of scheduled full sessions overall or less than 90% in either seasonal regime, or if mean paired spread plus a fixed 0.30 bp sell-side fee allowance is at least 3.50 bp. A +7 bp gross literature reference must strictly exceed twice the modeled round-trip cost. Where the exact fee tariff cannot be verified, the route remains provisional; do not extrapolate from ETF sponsor fees, core-session spreads, or ETHA. The 0.30 bp allowance is the upper end of the [published BlackRock/Fidelity sell-fee illustration](https://www.ishares.com/us/strategies/ways-to-invest-in-bitcoin), not a fill cost measurement. The p90 spread is disclosed but does not replace the fixed mean gate. Passing costs and quotes alone does not settle synthetic power or permit a replay.

## B. BIP and ETP forward public top-of-book spreads

STATED. Custodian `/root/time_machine_builder` prepares the forward-only quote sampler. Coinbase's [public REST methods](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/rest-api) explicitly cover U.S. futures and do not require a JWT. The [public product book](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/public/get-public-product-book) exposes side, size and `pricebook.time`. UNKNOWN. The exact product-level endpoint has not been probed for live access here; a generic public schema is not proof that either contract is provisioned without an account.

- **Identity and request.** After separate approval, use the published catalog to validate the code and instrument type without displaying or retaining any level. Use `GET https://api.coinbase.com/api/v3/brokerage/market/product_book?product_id=BIP-20DEC30-CDE&limit=1` and the identical `ETP-20DEC30-CDE` query. Require the exact product in the response; no substitution with spot, INTX, an expiry future or offshore derivatives. Set `Cache-Control: no-cache`; do not authenticate or place orders.
- **Fixed clock.** Sample the two books on a 30-second UTC grid from 20:55:00 through 21:05:00 and 22:55:00 through 23:05:00 each UTC day, both end ticks included. Alternate/stagger the requests by at least one second, log nominal clock versus receipt and do not catch up missed ticks. Report Friday maintenance separately, 17:00–18:00 New York, which overlaps or touches the target window in both seasons ([Coinbase trading hours](https://www.coinbase.com/learn/futures/24-7-trading)).
- **Validation and retention.** Require exactly the requested book, time parseable and no more than 10 seconds old at receipt with no material future time, and one finite strictly uncrossed positive bid/ask pair with positive sizes. Compute `10000 * (ask − bid) / mid` in memory. Save only nominal clock, quote age, instrument, regime, availability/fixed refusal code and that spread in private custody. Ignore the API's last trade and do not save BBO levels, midpoint, size, order, funding rate or bars. Summaries give slot availability and median/p90/p95 spread by product, season and clock; a forward series is not a historical spread reconstruction. Published funding is calculated hourly; measuring bid/ask spread does not include funding, commissions or basis ([Coinbase funding](https://help.coinbase.com/en/derivatives/perpetual-style-futures/funding-rate)).
- **Catalog and maintenance detail.** Require FUTURE, FCM/CDE and the matching BTC or ETH root alongside the exact ID. The specific five-year perpetual-style contracts may be classified as EXPIRING; do not require a PERPETUAL enum. Mark and do not request slots inside scheduled maintenance. Intrahold closure and unavailable endpoints are not evidence of executable coverage.
- **Stop conditions.** An HTTP authentication or geographic block stops the public item without changing region, domain or network route. An account requirement means the item waits on the owner's futures account, not that authorization is implicit. Respect ahead-of-time maintenance, 429/rate-limit responses and venue access terms. Initial response test is covered only by explicit lead approval of this separate measurement and returns only a sanitized status and validity flag.

UNKNOWN. Actual bid/ask spreads, both-edge availability, retail futures commissions, and execution costs. This item reports cost components and availability only. Never calculate a cross-clock return or read any unapproved dataset.
