## Frozen basis rule

It accommodates ordinary nearest rounding to two, three or four decimals without selecting a grid per price or per event. Freeze a half-cent allowance plus exactly USD 0.00000001 slack. The slack protects the nominal rounding boundary; it is not an empirically fitted relative allowance. No adjustment or quantization changes raw fills, returns, costs, action amounts, or lot sizes.
