## Entry, tender, and exits

- **Entry clock:** Use the open of the first regular U.S. cash session that starts at least 30 minutes after initial EDGAR acceptance, inclusive at 30:00. Acceptance time is not always dissemination time, so the lag is only a proxy ([SEC webmaster FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions)). Use a frozen NYSE regular-session calendar with start times in UTC and holiday closures, not weekday arithmetic ([NYSE hours and calendars](https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars)). Regular sessions, including early closes, count as business days for the election and payment clocks.
- **Election cutoff:** No trade if entry is on or after the cutoff session: three business days before the then-known expiry for entries before 2024-05-28, and two business days for entries on or after that date. Use only amendments accepted at least 30 minutes before the entry open. No trade if the terminal result is already actionable at entry. This is a broker deadline proxy, not a guarantee of settlement or acceptance.
- **Entry price and sizing:** The unadjusted opening print must be at least 150 basis points below the floor in force at entry. Start with the original fixed price or Dutch minimum and apply the latest price amendment accepted at least 30 minutes before the entry open, inclusive. At equality with the 1.5% discount, enter. Buy the largest whole number costing at most $500 after entry friction, capped at 99; zero shares is a counted no-trade. For each buy or sell fill, friction per share is the larger of 10 basis points of the unadjusted fill price and $0.05.
- **Completed:** All qualifying shares receive the filed final price without odd-lot proration. Credit on the stated date only when the text identifies payment to holders for this tender; otherwise leave the date blank in the manifest and credit on the fifth regular business day after final expiry. Tender payment is not a market fill and incurs no market friction. For an exit rule triggered before completion, use the market exit instead.
- **Withdrawal or cut:** On a matched termination or withdrawal, or the first revised floor below the original economic entry price per share, sell at the open of the first regular session at least 30 minutes after that amendment's acceptance. After material basis changes, compare prices in the same share units; a change the filings cannot reconcile is a refusal. If withdrawal and a cut both imply the same open, there is only one sale. Ignore post-expiry cuts in a completed tender; do not read a cut into a below-expectation clearing price alone.
- **Unresolved fallback, shown only:** Keep a separate result-unmatched/unknown count. For a separately reported otherwise eligible offer whose terminal result cannot be matched by cutoff, sell at the first regular session strictly after the last expiry known by cutoff unless an earlier actionable price cut already caused a sale. None of these returns enters any judged mark.
- **Corporate actions:** Review splits, spin-offs, ordinary cash dividends and special cash distributions through the latest possible exit day, not just expiry. Check all possible market exits and the terminal cash-credit date, even if an earlier sale could occur. For dividends and distributions, start with issuer-filed SEC documents, including Schedule TO exhibits and amendments; consult official exchange or OCC notices if filings do not resolve a fact. An issuer's direct-address dividend press release may supply an ordinary dividend's record and payment dates only if SEC filings and applicable notices establish the amount but do not supply the record date. Pin the URL, fetch time and content SHA-256; flag `div_source_ir: 1`, and count events excluded as unresolved because no allowed first-party source states a required date. Do not use quote pages, calculators or dividend-history pages. A special distribution requires the documented exchange or OCC ex-date because due-bill timing can differ. If only an ordinary dividend's record date is published, use two regular exchange sessions before the record date before 2017-09-05, one session before from 2017-09-05 through 2024-05-27, and the record date itself from 2024-05-28. Set `ex_derived: 1` and report counts of derived distributions and affected offers. An unresolved spin-off, fractional disposition or loss of odd-lot status is a pre-lock refusal, not an event dropped after seeing prices. Never infer dividends from adjusted bars.
