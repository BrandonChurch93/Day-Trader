## Files and validation

- **Canonical location:** Use `/home/user/workspace/rig/data/plan_75_vault`, owned by the runner and inaccessible to group or others. Use only regular, single-link private files. This directory is separate from the repeat. Do not record source bars, snapshots or derived prices in Git.
- **Index:** `index.csv` uses ASCII and LF with exact header `date,coin,admission,open_present,close_present,sell_allowed`. Use all 1,035 dates, with all 20 coins alphabetically within each date, for 20,700 rows. Admission is `PROVED`, `INFERRED` or `NOT_ADMITTED`; presence and sale are single digits 0 or 1. Admitted implies sell allowed. The sale flag covers the UTC open and, on the last day, the closing liquidation clock. A nonadmitted coin may have sell allowed if a sell-only exit is proved. Admission counts cover the whole index grid, even when a price is missing or a channel is still warming.
- **Opaque prices:** `prices.f64` is 331,200 bytes, no header, with two IEEE little-endian binary64 values per index row, open then close. Present values are finite and positive. Missing values use a NaN and the respective flag 0. The custodian checks these numeric requirements during extraction; the scorer's validation only checks schema, clocks, row shape and counts, admission consistency, byte length and hashes.
- **Manifest:** `manifest.json` is one UTF-8 JSON object, no duplicate or extra keys: integer `schema` 1, string `start` 2024-01-01, string `stop_exclusive` 2026-11-01, sorted `names` from the pinned 20-coin inventory, integer `rows` 20700, and `admission_coin_days` containing exactly the three admission labels with integer counts. The custodian supplies the manifest file's SHA-256 for independent review.
- **Custody receipt:** `vault_receipt.json` is one object containing exactly integer `schema` 1, boolean `scorer_absent_during_extract` true, string `manifest_sha256` and string `go_id`. The go ID is `p75v-` followed by the full lowercase manifest SHA-256. This declaration attests to an off-process custody step; the scorer cannot prove the custodian's physical separation.
- **Order:** After extraction and review, the lead agent calls `score_75.py --validate-vault APPROVED_MANIFEST_SHA256` from the clean checked-out commit. ## Plan-facing calculation notes

- **Warm-up:** Contiguous completed closes inside this stretch feed unchanged indicators and roster logic. The primary needs 90 returns from 91 closes, with a decision at the last required close and a possible fill only at the next in-fence open. The printed 150-, 250- and 360-day channels need their full in-fence history and stay cash until warm. No first-day fill, carryover cash, positions or channel state.
- **Matched BTC:** Use BTC alone on the primary eligibility and warm-up clock with the frozen hold executor. Let \(g_t\) be its gross daily simple book return on the full scored calendar, including idle days; let \(s\) be sample SD of primary net simple returns divided by sample SD of \(g_t\). For each BTC gross fill at date \(t\), subtract the same per-side fee times \(s\) times absolute gross fill notional divided by prior gross BTC NAV, with initial NAV one. Include last-day liquidation and any other actual eligibility-driven fill. Compound \(1+s g_t-\text{scaled fees}_t\) over the same dates. This is an ex-post risk comparison, not a purchasable BTC strategy. Show unscaled terminal net BTC from the ordinary fee-paying BTC hold. Lead agent ruling: any missing BTC endpoint or undefined BTC volatility makes the BTC mark false and does not prevent printing the other three. If a scaled daily factor is nonpositive or not finite, the diagnostic is also unscorable: report it as such, not as a pass.
- **Marks and output:** Main fees alone judge positive net, strictly above matched BTC after its fees, daily-mark drawdown strictly below 40%, and net from the March 19, 2025 close through October 31, 2026 after terminal fees at least −10%. Use the same book without a reset, divided by the March 19 book mark. Stdout is aggregate admission, main, shown-only harsh, each shown-only book on its own line, the mark booleans, and one result line. Both cost lines show the individual primary legs, the three legs at the 200% cap, the nine-leg printed rule, BTC and the after-paper slice. Fill-level rows go only to private mode-0600 `fills.csv`; no coin rows go to stdout. A technical failure after claiming the lock may leave partial private output and never prompts a retry.
