## Files and validation

- **Canonical location:** Use `/home/user/workspace/rig/data/plan_75_vault`, owned by the runner and inaccessible to group or others. All four inputs must be regular, single-link files with exact mode 0600: `manifest.json`, `index.csv`, `prices.f64` and `vault_receipt.json`. This directory is separate from the repeat. Do not record source bars, snapshots or derived prices in Git.
- **Index:** `index.csv` uses ASCII and LF with exact header `date,coin,admission,open_present,close_present,sell_allowed`. Use all 1,035 dates, with all 20 coins alphabetically within each date, for 20,700 rows. Admission is `PROVED`, `INFERRED` or `NOT_ADMITTED`; presence and sale are single digits 0 or 1. Admitted implies sell allowed. The sale flag covers the UTC open and, on the last day, the closing liquidation clock. A nonadmitted coin may have sell allowed if a sell-only exit is proved. Admission counts cover the whole index grid, even when a price is missing or a channel is still warming.
- **Venue-wide outage:** A day with all 20 rows `NOT_ADMITTED` and `sell_allowed=0` is a one-day pause, not a monthly roster removal. Keep the raw opens and closes and raw presence flags in the extract. After authentication and lock, the held-back loader carries the prior day's eligibility, masks all opens for that date, and leaves closing marks and indicators unchanged. No purchase or sale can fill at that open. At the next open the frozen rules apply normally, including any pending sale and any still-valid buy signal. A single-coin 0/0 halt is not translated. The collector must not invent outage days, present endpoints, or prior-day price substitutes.
- **Opaque prices:** `prices.f64` is 331,200 bytes, no header, with two IEEE little-endian binary64 values per index row, open then close. Present values are finite and positive. Missing values use a NaN and the respective flag 0. The custodian checks these numeric requirements during extraction; the scorer's validation only checks schema, clocks, row shape and counts, admission consistency, byte length and hashes.
- **Manifest:** `manifest.json` is one UTF-8 JSON object, no duplicate or extra keys: integer `schema` 1, string `start` 2024-01-01, string `stop_exclusive` 2026-11-01, sorted `names` from the pinned 20-coin inventory, integer `rows` 20700, and `admission_coin_days` containing exactly the three admission labels with integer counts. Supply lowercase SHA-256 strings for `index_sha256`, `payload_sha256`, `source_receipt_sha256` and `eligibility_receipt_sha256`. The custodian supplies the manifest file's SHA-256 for independent review.
- **Custody receipt:** `vault_receipt.json` is one object containing exactly integer `schema` 1, boolean `scorer_absent_during_extract` true, string `manifest_sha256` and string `go_id`. The go ID is `p75v-` followed by the full lowercase manifest SHA-256. This declaration attests to an off-process custody step; the scorer cannot prove the custodian's physical separation.
- **Validation:** After extraction and review, the lead agent calls `score_75.py --validate-vault APPROVED_MANIFEST_SHA256`. After authenticating the index and opaque payload it prints only `VENUE_WIDE_DAYS <count>`, `MISSING_BTC_ENDPOINTS <count>`, and a fixed success or refusal code. Counts come from raw index flags, not prices. A venue-wide day on a month opening, on the first or last stretch date, or adjacent to another venue-wide day returns `REFUSE_VAULT_OUTAGE`. For each coin ever admitted, require a present open with `sell_allowed=1` strictly after its last admitted date or the final close present with `sell_allowed=1`; otherwise return `REFUSE_VAULT_NO_EXIT`. A nonzero missing-BTC-endpoint count is reported, not hidden by the outage mask; it makes the BTC mark false at scoring. Validation never decodes prices or creates a lock.
- **One-shot order and checkout:** Only after validation and independent audit, the lead agent may put exactly one literal `P75_VAULT_GO=p75v-<manifest-sha256>` in committed ledger row 225. The go key must not appear anywhere else in the ledger, and the repeat go is not a vault approval. Use a fresh worktree for the authorized go, with no untracked files, including ignored ones such as a leftover `__pycache__` from validation or self-tests. Use `python3 -I -B` for custody commands. The vault CLI prevents checkout-local imports and automatic bytecode writes before its checks; preexisting bytecode still causes `REFUSE_UNTRACKED`. The runner refuses dirty tracked files and any assume-unchanged or skip-worktree flag. One invocation of `score_75.py --go-vault /home/user/workspace/rig/data/plan_75_vault` verifies committed approval, the receipt and file modes, and reruns no-decode validation on the same directory handle. All of these refusals happen before it exclusively writes and syncs `RUN.lock` and its directory. It then decodes those exact authenticated bytes. There is no reset, retry or second run, including if the first run refuses after the lock.

## Plan-facing calculation notes

- **Warm-up:** Contiguous completed closes inside this stretch feed unchanged indicators and roster logic. The primary needs 90 returns from 91 closes, with a decision at the last required close and a possible fill only at the next in-fence open. The printed 150-, 250- and 360-day channels need their full in-fence history and stay cash until warm. No first-day fill, carryover cash, positions or channel state.
- **Matched BTC:** Use BTC alone on the primary eligibility and warm-up clock with the frozen hold executor. Let \(g_t\) be its gross daily simple book return on the full scored calendar, including idle days; let \(s\) be sample SD of primary net simple returns divided by sample SD of \(g_t\). For each BTC gross fill at date \(t\), subtract the same per-side fee times \(s\) times absolute gross fill notional divided by prior gross BTC NAV, with initial NAV one. Include last-day liquidation and any other actual eligibility-driven fill. Compound \(1+s g_t-\text{scaled fees}_t\) over the same dates. This is an ex-post risk comparison, not a purchasable BTC strategy. Show unscaled terminal net BTC from the ordinary fee-paying BTC hold. Any missing raw BTC endpoint or undefined BTC volatility makes the BTC mark false without hiding the other three. The intentional outage-open mask alone is not a missing raw endpoint. If a scaled daily factor is nonpositive or not finite, the diagnostic is unscorable.
- **Marks and output:** Main fees alone judge positive net, strictly above matched BTC after its fees, daily-mark drawdown strictly below 40%, and net from the March 19, 2025 close through October 31, 2026 after terminal fees at least −10%. Use the same book without a reset, divided by the March 19 book mark. Stdout is aggregate admission, main, shown-only harsh, each shown-only book on its own line, the mark booleans, and one result line. Both cost lines show the individual primary legs, the three legs at the 200% cap, the nine-leg printed rule, BTC and the after-paper slice. Fill-level rows go only to private mode-0600 `fills.csv`; no coin rows go to stdout. A technical failure after claiming the lock may leave partial private output and never prompts a retry. A pass earns a practice forward card only.
