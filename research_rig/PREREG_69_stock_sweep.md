## Search marks and output

- STATED: Return for the net mean is the return on the cash actually traded after both quote allowances. Simple annual net on $500 is the sum of dollar P&L divided by $500 and by six years, with idle days earning zero. It does not multiply a return by 252, assume simultaneous trades, grant fractional initial sizing or annualize only the active period. Require net mean above zero, annual net at least 12%, and positive raw net means in both halves. Remove the highest `ceil(0.01 × trade_count)` net trades and require positive raw net mean on the remainder.
- STATED: For each rule clock and instrument, compute an equally weighted no-cost mean of that instrument's gross price returns on all scheduled dates in the relevant block where both exact endpoints exist. Use the same fixed prints and same-day or next-exchange-session holding offset. Do not condition baseline dates on signal, ranking, affordability, settlement, or a weekday/month-end gate; these are purported sources of an edge, not part of the baseline. Use full/first/second block means separately; baseline information drives judging only, not the entry decision.
- STATED: On each entered trade subtract that matched gross mean from its net return. The standard error clusters these differences by entry day: sum demeaned differences within each day, square and sum the cluster totals, multiply by the day-count correction \(G/(G-1)\), take the square root, and divide by the trade count. Divide the mean difference by that error. A zero/undefined error does not pass. Require full t at least 3.5 and each half t at least 1.5. A rule needs at least 200 complete trades on 100 different entry days and 40 different entry days per half; otherwise it is too thin. With one entry per day, the 200-trade floor is stricter than the 100-day floor, but both are enforced.
- STATED: Rank eligible rules by highest full t, then rule identifier. From the ranked list, at most three become finalists. Compare Pearson correlation of raw net returns on entry days both rules traded, with no padded idle days; if the correlation is strictly above 0.7 to a higher-ranked finalist it is a duplicate. Undefined correlation does not establish duplication. Only per-rule JSON summary rows go to standard output in search mode. No bar or trade-level results are printed. No finalist means the vault stays closed.
- STATED: The vault mark remains exactly doc 150: positive net, day-cluster t at least 2.4 on net minus the vault's own same-clock baseline, positive after the best-1% trim, positive in at least 3 of the 5 calendar years, at least 8% simple annual net on $500, and at least 100 distinct days under Amendment 2. For the separate pre-opening power gate, estimate the chance of all vault marks with search clock frequencies, raw/excess day noise and half the search mean, using the unchanged rules and no vault observations; carry none below 33%. Neither that final projection nor vault scoring is run or served by this search-only script. Freeze and audit both separately before there is any vault access.

## Reproducing the checks and later search

- STATED: Run `python3 -B research_rig/sweep_69.py --synthetic` with Python and NumPy. It uses an invented NY-calendar-sized tape, 17 fake instruments covering each pool, correlated contemporaneous Gaussian shocks with no predictive edge plus stochastic volatility and volume, and fixed seeds 690100–690199. The full search marks and all registered rows are applied in each seed alongside a separate planted-signal test, the overnight-drift control, mutation of post-signal bars and sizing references, and a mocked vendor pagination/date fence. More than five noise seeds with a finalist stops the process. No cost, mark, seed or signal is retuned to pass. This calibration is not proof of false-pass control on a real feed with more cross-sectional dependence, stale bars, market microstructure, or tail events.
- MEASURED, made up: The planted S1-04 was named a finalist. The signal and sizing mutation checks agreed for all 64 long-side definitions, including the predeclared S12 thin rows. The drift control earned a raw mean of 1.733% per trade and 133.03% a year at its invented scale, but its matched t was -15.35 and it missed. All 100 noise seeds produced no finalist; at most 27 rules in a seed reached the trade/day floors, the highest full t among rules reaching those floors was 2.630, and six families (S1, S2, S6, S8, S9 and S10) had a row reach those floors in at least one seed. This does not validate outcomes on rare rules that were thin throughout the fixtures. Both collector and scorer refused a 2022 date before authorizations, files or endpoints were opened.
- MEASURED, made up: The power calculation uses 20,000 normal return draws at each count and noise level, equal half counts, one trade per entry day, a zero gross clock baseline, and 90% of the $500 deployed in each trade. It asks at what true net edge a single rule reaches the whole search bar with 50% or 80% probability, conditional on the stated trade counts and no competing finalist or duplicate. The simulator applies the count, t, half, trim, and annual lines; it does not claim that the real grid produces these counts or noise. Lower cash usage, positively dependent trade days, a nonzero gross baseline, or competition can alter power.
