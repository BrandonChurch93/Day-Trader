# Replay 09: new crypto rules on Alpaca

STATED. This is a frozen plan, not a result. The rules are cash-only and long-only. They place no orders. Each rule uses its own account and any cash pays no interest. Each method is counted, with IDs starting at `replay_09_statistics.first_trial_id`.

## Data, dates and costs

- Use public Alpaca US hourly bars for fills and marks, and its daily bars for daily signals. Pull history from `replay_09_dates.pull_start`, before the public history begins. Use the recorded minute tape for a data coverage check only. No Kraken substitution. Record missing and zero-volume bars, first and last timestamps, and input fingerprints. Quote-only bars can mark positions but cannot fill them. Gaps may also hide trades.
- Test the eight tape symbols named by `replay_09_basket` as the fixed candidate set. Daily ranking also screens by the median of the preceding `replay_09_liquidity.days` fully closed daily observations of close times venue volume, requiring all observations present and at least `replay_09_liquidity.min_median_usd`. This tests a shortlist of coins known to be available now and is not a historical listing-universe study.
- Development is 2024-01-01 through 2026-06-30, and held back is 2026-07-01 through 2026-11-24, inclusive UTC days (`replay_09_dates`). Each slice starts with cash; warm-up reads only bars dated before the decision, and neither slice carries positions to the other. A scheduled round trip must have both nominal entry and exit inside the slice. A missing or quote-only entry expires at once; while in a position no other entry is allowed. A missing or quote-only exit waits for the next positive-volume hourly open inside the slice. If there is none, report the still-open position at the last available close. At missing closes carry the last known mark, never a later price. Take daily closing equity for every calendar day and include the starting cash in metrics.
- Fill at the scheduled hourly open after the signal; daily signals use only the previous daily bar and older bars and give a full hour after midnight before a fill. Buy at open plus `tm_crypto_half_spread_bps`; sell at open less the same spread; pay `practice_crypto_fee_bps_added` on each side including benchmark entry, using the Alpaca entry taker rate from [Alpaca crypto fees](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-fees). Wallet spends no more than its assigned weight including fees. Holding bitcoin buys at the first eligible hourly open of each slice, with the same entry cost. All positions still open at a slice endpoint are marked, not fictitiously liquidated.
- No maker trial. An OHLC low or high, even on a bar with some trade volume, does not prove a trade through a resting price. Queue position, route and partial fills are also unknown.

## Frozen rules

All targets except VOL spend all cash if selected. Each cycle admits at most one entry. Fractional VOL sells the whole position before the next cycle. Ties between assets go in ascending symbol order. Do not fall back to a different coin if the selected coin cannot fill.

| Rule | Exact decision | Reason to test |
|---|---|---|
| ASIA | Hold BTC daily from `replay_09_calendar.asia_entry` for `asia_hours`, no signal. | Market participation changes across regional days. |
| WKND | Hold BTC each week from `replay_09_calendar.weekend_day` at `daily_entry` for `weekend_hours`, no signal. | Crypto trades through traditional-market closures, when the trader mix can change. |
| MOM | At Monday `replay_09_calendar.daily_entry`, buy the liquid coin with the greatest simple `momentum_days` return, based on the previous daily close and the close that many days earlier. Require all daily closes across that gap. Hold `weekly_hours`. | Leadership may persist across coins without a slow trend rule. |
| REV | At `replay_09_calendar.daily_entry`, buy the liquid coin with the lowest simple prior daily close-to-close return. Sell at `daily_exit`. | The weakest relative coin may rebound after temporary selling. |
| LEAD | At each UTC hour divisible by `replay_09_lead.every_hours`, use open-to-close returns of the hourly bar that starts `signal_lag_bars` before entry. All observed signal bars must have positive volume. If BTC exceeds `btc_min_return`, buy the eligible coin in `symbols` with the lowest return at or below `alt_max_return`; sell after `hold_hours`. | Bitcoin might reprice first while a smaller coin initially lags. |
| VOL | At Monday `replay_09_calendar.daily_entry`, buy BTC for the lesser of the full account and `replay_09_vol.target_daily_sd` divided by the sample standard deviation of the preceding `lookback_days` daily simple returns. Require all closes. Zero volatility means full weight. Hold `weekly_hours`. | Holding less after volatile weeks might cut large drops without a directional trend signal. |
| CAP | At `replay_09_calendar.daily_entry`, buy BTC if the previous daily close-to-close return is at most `replay_09_capit.max_daily_return` and that day's coin volume is at least `min_volume_multiple` times the median of the preceding `replay_09_liquidity.days` daily volumes, not including the selloff day. Require all volumes and a positive median. Sell at `daily_exit`. | Unusually heavy selling may briefly overshoot. |

## The five marks and the yardsticks

- Mark 1: held-back Sharpe must exceed BTC hold's and the largest daily closing drop must be no more than `replay_max_drop_ratio_vs_hold` times its drop. Report net growth too, not just the verdict.
- Mark 2: whole-sample compounded equity across the two independent slices must exceed similarly reset BTC hold at zero friction, then stay ahead up to at least `backtest_min_breakeven_cost_multiple` times modeled cost per side. Search with the existing rig's break-even grid; scale fee and spread together in the modeled ratio. Reprice the same fills; do not regenerate decisions.
- Mark 3: whole-sample daily returns, with the standard deviation of all finite per-day Sharpes among this batch as the available spread proxy. The count is 100 (`replay_09_statistics.trial_count`): 93 prior counted rule trials, including both reruns, plus 7 here. No replay 07 rows were in the ledger at freeze. The recorded older trials do not provide a single comparable spread for this venue and these data. Deflated Sharpe must exceed `replay_min_deflated_sharpe`.
- Mark 4: CSCV across all seven rule returns, from each one of these fixed starts through the held-back end: 2024-01-01, 2025-01-01, 2026-01-01 (`replay_09_dates.pbo_starts`). Use `replay_09_statistics.pbo_blocks`, drop unneeded earliest days, ties count against the rule, report all values, and gate on the largest estimate being below `backtest_max_pbo`.
- Mark 5: whole-sample terminal equity must beat more than `replay_min_shuffle_percentile` of `replay_09_statistics.draws` matched timing draws, using its seed. In each slice separately, shuffle the realized completed trade tickets (symbol, dollar weight as a fraction of equity, and hours held), put them into random distinct cycles, and pick a random viable window within each cycle; require real traded opening bars at both ends. ASIA controls use any `asia_control_entries`; WKND any `weekend_control_days`; the other rules use the same entry clock as their real rule. Viability can see execution availability because this is a hindsight null, not a deployable trader. Windows must end by the next cycle's first entry and stay inside the slice. A draw rejects and retries if placement is impossible without overlap; if the real rule has an open position at the end, has no completed trades across the two slices, or exact matching cannot be produced, report the mark unknown, which fails. Report the first matched draw as a random twin, with net growth and sides in both slices.

Use development and synthetic facts to verify timing, gaps, cash/fee arithmetic, controls and rule selection before opening the held-back data. Run the frozen rules on those dates once. Keep every failure. Emit figures from the output file, not by hand. A pass is a reason to try paper trading, not evidence of profit in a live account.
