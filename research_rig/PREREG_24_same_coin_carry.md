Status FROZEN.

Replay 18 lost money on its long-short book even though funding contributed 33.97 points. This plan keeps the funding exposure and tries to remove the directional bet by buying spot and shorting the perpetual on the same coin. Equal dollars at the daily fill do not hedge changes in spot-perpetual basis or all intraday moves. A backtest can fail even when the underlying spot and perpetual track each other closely.

## Portfolio and execution

- **Entry and exit:** Decide once per UTC day at 00:00; trade both legs at the next common 01:00 open, never later that day. The archived bar's positive volume confirms that an hour traded, as in the input replays; that is not proof of executable size at its open. Keep incumbents that remain eligible and whose signal is strictly above 5% annualized. Close on 5% or less, loss of rank or effective listing eligibility, or unavailable signal. Admit at 20% or more, highest lagged annualized rate first, then ascending canonical CMC ID and dated keys. Keep eligible incumbents before filling vacancies; a newcomer does not displace an incumbent above the exit level. No alternate name on a failed open. A failed new entry stays cash.
- **Capital:** Start with $500. Size a new sleeve only once: a fifth of the whole account NAV at that day's decision, half spot and half 1x short margin. At the fill, handle exits first, then fund new sleeves in their selected order. Cap each leg by free cash after fees: start from the lesser of the decision target and half free cash, reserve the fees quoted at that upper bound, then use the lesser of the bound and half the remaining cash. Charge actual order fees at the resulting size and leave any fee-reserve difference idle. Skip an entry if either leg would be under $1. Do not fill it with another name. Keep existing quantities and collateral unchanged except for market moves, funding, and maintenance. No daily resizing or budget search. Idle cash earns nothing, there is no external margin, and nonpositive equity cannot pass.
- **Margin:** After settling carried funding due at the open and marking all available carried lots to their opens, check carried margin every scored hour, including 01:00, before normal exits or entries. Handle a breach first; a forced close is not reopened at that fill. After the fill check surviving and new lots against that hour's spot low and worse available perpetual high, before marking any lot to its close. A fall below 10% of short notional triggers a proportional paired reduction: sell spot and buy back the same fraction of the short, using net spot proceeds to restore short margin. Apply the per-order fee on each leg. If the sleeve cannot restore margin, close it at the check prices; any deficit is a charge to the account. Record drawdown and test nonpositive equity at the common open, every forced close and top-up, normal exits and new fills, and hourly closes. At an execution checkpoint other sleeves have not yet taken their later hourly close marks. Simultaneous adverse extremes are a deliberate stress assumption, not a venue maintenance schedule or an exact intrahour execution claim.
- **Gaps:** On the first held hour with either leg absent, nontraded, or outside its dated segment, close both using the last common positive-volume hour: its spot low and worse perpetual high. If no bar completed use the executed entry. Do not skip the position or book funding stamped at or after gap detection. A valid earlier offset preceding the gap still belongs to the carried lot; if there is no common open, use the previous available perpetual mark for it. The surviving leg is priced at the same prior common bar to avoid a synthetic spread using different dates. A temporary data gap is a stop. Forced closes pay both leg fees.
- **Funding:** Positive settles as a short credit; negative is a debit. At an ordinary exact fill stamp, or a slightly earlier settlement archived into that hour, use the carried position; a slightly later settlement uses the new position. For a credit pick the lower available boundary mark, and for a debit the higher. When a scheduled event is missing inside a hold, debit at the larger of 2.00% and the dated contract's maximum valid recorded absolute rate in the loaded period strictly before the cutoff, using the worse available boundary mark. Assign ownership at the exact offset timestamp within the contract's half-open interval, including allowed off-grid observations. Assign every cash event to its exact UTC stamp, and take day/year snapshots after all events strictly inside the closing period. For example a payment stamped 23:59:59 goes in that day even if stored under the following midnight; an exact midnight timestamp goes in the new day. The maximum is a retrospective accounting stress, never a signal input. Terminal funding follows the separate cutoff rule.
