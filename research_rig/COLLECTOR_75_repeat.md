## Extraction and interface

- **UTC clocks:** Match each bar's UTC hour opening clock and its exact millisecond inclusive final tick; use the 00:00 hourly open and the 23:00 hourly bar's close for each UTC date. Check timestamps before interpreting the price fields. Reject wrong-month or out-of-fence timestamps, duplicate or unsorted stamps, identity or checksum mismatch, and invalid required prices. Leave absent endpoints missing rather than copying a stale value or a nearby print. Record missing hours from the pinned inventory; a missing interior hour is reported but does not invalidate otherwise proved day endpoints. Do not calculate strategy returns.
- **Canonical off-repo folder:** On the research machine the three fixed files are `manifest.json`, `index.csv` and `prices.f64` at `/home/user/workspace/rig/data/plan_75_repeat`. Use regular privately permissioned files, no filesystem links, and publish only after review. Keep both receipts private. Do not pass an unrestricted path or put source bars, snapshots or derived prices in the repo.
- **Index:** Exact ASCII header `date,coin,eligible,open_present,close_present,sell_allowed` with LF. Use one row per date from 2020-01-01 through 2023-12-31 and, within each date, each of the 20 symbols alphabetically (29,220 rows). Dates are ISO, flags are single digits 0/1. `eligible` means the product is admitted by that UTC day's known opening clock, and not if a later announcement on that date is the only evidence. `sell_allowed` means that USD product can be sold at the UTC open and, for the final day, at the close; it can be true for a documented sell-only window even with `eligible=0`. Eligibility implies sell availability. If a good-faith known liquidation clock or recovery cannot be represented, report the case before any scoring. Never set an eligibility flag merely because the Binance print exists.
- **Opaque prices:** Two little-endian IEEE binary64 fields per row in the exact index order, open then close in the proxy quote, 467,520 bytes with no header. For an absent endpoint put a NaN, not zero, and clear its presence flag. Present values must be finite and strictly positive. The custodian checks contents; the scorer's no-decode validation checks bytes, fixed record width, key order and flags without unpacking any price.
- **Manifest:** UTF-8 single JSON object without duplicate or extra keys: integer `schema: 1`, strings `start: "2020-01-01"` and `stop_exclusive: "2024-01-01"`, `names` in alphabetic order and integer `rows: 29220`. Provide lowercase hex SHA-256 strings in `index_sha256`, `payload_sha256`, `source_receipt_sha256` and `eligibility_receipt_sha256`. The custodian supplies the SHA-256 of the exact manifest bytes to the lead agent out of band after independent review. A self-asserted digest from an unreviewed manifest is not a pin.
