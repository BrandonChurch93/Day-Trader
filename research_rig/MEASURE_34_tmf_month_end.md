# Measurement 34. TMF on the Replay 25 Treasury month-end window

This is a measurement, not new evidence for the effect, because TMF's window return is close to three times TLT's by construction. That is the comparison to measure, not a guaranteed multiple: the fund targets three times its benchmark daily before fees and expenses, not three times TLT over several days, and resetting, financing, fees, tracking and fund market pricing can all affect the realized ratio ([Direxion TMF fund page](https://www.direxion.com/product/daily-20-year-treasury-bull-bear-3x-etfs)). UBT, which targets twice the daily benchmark return, is shown side-by-side on the same windows without marks ([ProShares UBT fund page](https://www.proshares.com/our-etfs/leveraged-and-inverse/ubt)). Ledger 169, queue 56. No price bar or quote was opened by the author for this draft.

## Frozen design

- **Windows**: Use `common_25.windows(days, 3)` and the byte-identical Replay 25 XNYS 4.13.3 calendar. Exactly 130 months, January 2016 to October 2026 inclusive. Buy at fourth-last session close and sell at last session close. Assign by exit year; January to October 2026 is one partial year. Cash otherwise, no interest, leverage on top of the ETF, or taxes. All 520 distinct held-window session closes are required for each fund. The reference is the immutable staged Replay 25 TLT bars and approved payout file, not previously reported results. No returned reference prices or vendor quotes belong in the repository.
- **Prices and payouts**: Raw, USD, historical Alpaca SIP daily closes with valid OHLC and positive volume ([historical bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars)). At each entry normalize to one share for window arithmetic; treat splits effective at open for an already-held position, and multiply shares by new/old. Credit issuer-declared cash per contemporary share for ex-dates after entry through exit, including the exit date and excluding the entry date. Never reinvest distributions within the window. Treat ex-date credits as cash equivalents even if payable later, as in Replay 25. Include all cash components (not stock payments), in documented contemporaneous share units after any split effective at that opening. No double adjustment. Direxion announced 1-for-10 TMF split trading on a split-adjusted basis starting December 4, 2023, outside that month's held window ([Direxion split notice](https://www.direxion.com/press-release/direxion-announces-reverse-splits-of-tmf-and-labu), [issuer distribution history](https://www.direxion.com/product/daily-20-year-treasury-bull-bear-3x-etfs)). Custodian must still certify complete actions and all split-era bases; the abbreviated product display alone is not proof of completeness.
- **Spread**: At each of the 260 distinct entry/exit sessions use the final consolidated SIP quote state timestamped in `[scheduled close minus 60 seconds, scheduled close)`; no look after the close, no earlier-minute backfill and no IEX substitution. Scheduled close is 4 p.m. New York, or 1 p.m. on the pinned early-close dates; the UTC offset follows New York time. Timestamp comparisons retain nanoseconds. Accept a positive finite bid and ask, positive displayed size on both sides, and ask at least bid (locked is zero); reject a missing or invalid final quote, including a crossed quote, rather than using a stale earlier valid one. At a tied latest timestamp accept only identical bid/ask/sizes. Half-spread in rate units is `(ask - bid)/(ask + bid)`. Charge the entry-day rate on the buy close and exit-day rate on the sell proceeds. This is a spread estimate from a snapshot, not a quote execution, auction price, guarantee about size, or actual slippage; financing and product expenses are already reflected in the instrument's observed performance ([Alpaca historical stock quotes](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockquotes-1), [SIP data description](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-stock-data-1)).

## Outputs and marks

- **Cost lines**: Fixed 2 bp each side first; measured quote half-spread each side second; fixed 5 and 10 bp each side sensitivities; show 2 bp + measured half-spread each side as the marked conservative line. One-side cost fractions apply to contemporaneous traded notional, not distributions. Same sample on every line for each fund. Show entry and exit spread count, mean, median, p95 and maximum in bps.
- **Window arithmetic**: For one share bought at the entry, net return is `(exit shares × exit raw close × (1 − exit cost) + earned cash)/(entry raw close × (1 + entry cost)) − 1`. Gross is the same at zero costs. Show arithmetic mean, one-sample t against zero using sample standard deviation (undefined if variance is zero), mean after dropping exactly the three best windows, worst dated window, worst year by compounded rule return plus the worst year by mean window return, and share of the 11 years with strictly positive mean window return. Show complete annual sample counts, means and compounded rule returns. Show aggregate tracking and same-cost net ratio for fixed-cost variants; for the quote-cost variants, use TLT at fixed 2 bp per side for the net ratio and label the unequal costs. Ratios of arithmetic means, not mean of unstable per-window ratios.
- **Rule equity drawdown**: Start at one before the first entry, fully invest the available value at each buy including entry cost (fractional shares), mark at each held-session raw close plus accrued cash, charge exit cost at the exit close, and hold flat while out of market. Maximum peak-to-trough loss is the largest `1 − equity/running peak` including the start, entry-cost marks and intermediate held closes, with peak/trough date; no intraday marks or buy-and-hold comparison. An initial peak is labelled `START`; ties retain the earliest dates. This is the rule's own equity line.
- **Mark 1**: Gross TMF mean window return divided by the gross TLT mean window return lies in [2.7, 3.1] inclusive. Undefined denominator is not a pass. Publish both means; also show the fixed-cost ratios separately.
- **Mark 2**: Arithmetic net TMF mean above zero at 2 bp plus the measured closing-minute half-spread on each side. All fixed-cost and half-spread-only rows are shown, not separately marked.
- **Shown UBT**: Same data, costs and outputs; 1.8 to 2.1 is an informational gross tracking band only. No mark or verdict. ProShares announced an August 18, 2020 2-for-1 forward split within data coverage; its November 19, 2026 reverse split falls beyond the window and must not be applied retrospectively ([2020 ProShares split notice](https://www.proshares.com/press-releases/proshares-announces-etf-share-splits-80420), [2026 split notice](https://www.proshares.com/press-releases/proshares-announces-share-splits)).
- **Worst window**: State its date and loss for every cost line; no mark. No trading permission or new trial inference follows from either mark.

## Input contract and gate

- **Draft freeze**: `FREEZE.json` is an exact SHA-256 map of this plan, scorer, common code, synthetic tests and calendar. Exact hashes are supplied independently; no runtime environment knob redirects paths or costs. The owner approves the draft and the private input manifest before a single run.
- **Private custody**: `/home/user/workspace/rig/data/measure_34/inputs` and its containing run directory are mode 0700, with regular owner-only JSON files. Exact files: `manifest.json`, `TMF.json`, `UBT.json`, `tmf_quotes.json`, `ubt_quotes.json`, `tmf_actions.json`, `ubt_actions.json`. Manifest keys: `schema:1, symbols:["TMF","UBT"], feed:"sip", adjustment:"raw", currency:"USD", bars_route:"/v2/stocks/{symbol}/bars", quotes_route:"/v2/stocks/{symbol}/quotes", quote_scope:"close_minus_60s_inclusive_to_close_exclusive", collected_utc, files`; `files` maps the other six names to their SHA-256. No silent source mixtures or fallback.
- **TMF.json and UBT.json**: Each is an ordered array of the 520 raw vendor daily bar rows (at least `t,o,h,l,c,v`), exactly the fourth-last through last sessions of each fixed month. Query explicit raw SIP; trim only non-required calendar dates, never prices or columns needed for validation. A continuity check cannot bridge an unobserved gap between monthly windows.
- **tmf_quotes.json and ubt_quotes.json**: Each is an ordered array of the 260 endpoint records `{session, request_start, request_end, complete, quotes}`. Times UTC, request start equals close minus 60 seconds, request end equals close, `complete:true` attests all pagination exhausted. Each inner array contains the raw ordered vendor rows in the half-open interval after explicitly dropping returned rows exactly at the end (the endpoint may include end); minimum fields `t,bp,ap,bs,as`. No selected-price summaries or assessed spreads. Preserve source route and independent custodian evidence for page chains off repo.
- **tmf_actions.json and ubt_actions.json**: Exact keys `schema:1, symbol, currency:"USD", coverage_start:"2016-01-04", coverage_end:"2026-10-30", share_basis:"contemporaneous_ex_date_post_split", reviewer, cash, splits`. Include a named independent reviewer of issuer/source coverage and payout/split bases. Cash rows: `{id, ex_date, amount, source}`; one row per distinct component or an issuer-row total, never both. Split rows: `{id, effective_date, new, old, source}`, with integer share units and effective first split-adjusted session. `source` is an official Direxion HTTPS URL for TMF or ProShares HTTPS URL for UBT. Include complete events over covered range, not just acted-on dates.
- **Validation and run**: `--validate-inputs --approved-freeze-sha256 PIN --approved-input-sha256 PIN` validates source, hash, schema, coverage, and clocks; prints only a fixed status with 130 windows and per-fund counts of 260 quote endpoints/520 bar rows. No returns, measured spreads, or lock changes. `--run` with the same pins is reserved to the lead. It repeats validation, exclusively creates `run_34.lock`, and synchronizes both lock and containing directory before any return or spread computation. It creates `results_34.json` exclusively and synchronizes both result and directory. Failure after claim consumes the attempt. No credentials, vendor API or network in either execution path. Do not run until independent audit and lead approval.
