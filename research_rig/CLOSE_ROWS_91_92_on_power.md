# Preferred shares and BDCs: January rebound closed on synthetic power

STATED. Queue rows 91 and 92, ledger rows 190 and 191. Close both before building point-in-time rosters or opening market data. The same Plan 48 marks, applied to ten December-to-January turns from December 2016 through January 2026, need a +4.00% gross preferred loser edge or a +5.25% gross BDC loser edge at central noise to reach 50% power without assuming a broader rally. These are simulated thresholds, not measured returns.

## Why the assumptions differ

- **Preferreds (row 91):** The prior [screen](../docs/research/106_row91_preferred_shares_tax_loss_rebound.md) counted 775 live issues, not 775 historical candidates. Schwab describes $25 as common par and notes variable liquidity, rate and issuer risk, and callability; S&P describes wider spread and impact costs than for common shares ([Schwab](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/preferred-securities-balancing-yield-with-risk), [S&P DJI](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/research/research-digging-deeper-into-the-us-preferred-market.pdf)). Use tighter common and residual shocks than for BDCs, but not bond-index noise alone: rate and credit shocks are shared, and issue-specific call and distress risk remains in the worst five. PSK is an illustrative exchange-listed preferred proxy, not a pure preferred-equity comparator; its definition includes preferred-like debt ([State Street](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-ice-preferred-securities-etf-psk)).
- **BDCs (row 92):** The prior [screen](../docs/research/107_row92_bdc_tax_loss_rebound.md) counted 47 live listed common BDCs, not 47 in each turn. The SEC notes leverage can amplify share volatility, underlying borrower distress, uncertain private valuations, and price discounts or premiums to NAV ([SEC investor bulletin](https://www.sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/investor-bulletin-publicly-traded-business-development-companies)). Use wider equity-like common and residual shocks; the small pool gives little reason to assume the worst five diversify those shocks away. BIZD is an illustrative sector-equity comparator ([VanEck](https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/blogs/income-investing/bizd-question-and-answer/)).

STATED. The sources justify the relative ordering, not the levels. Monthly Gaussian standard deviations (benchmark, roster minus benchmark, worst five minus roster) are 2/1/2%, 3/1.5/3%, 4/2.5/4% for preferreds and 3/1.5/3%, 4.5/2.5/4.5%, 6/4/6% for BDCs. Assumed all-in round trips (benchmark, equal-weight roster, losers) are 20/50/90 bp and 20/40/65 bp, respectively. Preferred loser cost corresponds to two 40 bp half-spreads plus 5 bp per side; at $25 par that is a hypothetical 20-cent full spread before the surcharge, not a quote. Stress the central case with Plan 48's 110 bp two-sided fallback on the loser sleeve. Ignore missing names, cash differentials, lot rounding, and extra price impact; full coverage makes this favorable on those dimensions.

Ten annual turns are the common information limit for [Plan 54](CLOSE_54_on_power.md), preferreds, and BDCs: the holdings within a turn do not supply more independent years. But with unchanged proportional breadth marks, **no number of turns at or above ten** reaches 50% power for any of the three at a +2% gross edge, central noise, and no shared premium. The model gives only 58.07%, 59.66%, and 57.83% chances of positive net in a single turn, respectively, against a mark requiring at least 80% positive turns. The exact binomial tail for 10 to 99 turns, and a Chernoff bound thereafter, put full power below 17% in every case. To gauge the value of longer history under a different rule, a paired-only t mark at 2 would first reach 50% at 20, 14, and 26 turns, respectively (noncentral t with net paired edge divided by paired SD and scaled by the square root of turns). These are planning numbers for a redesign, not release counts under the current marks.

Design lesson: the Plan 48 breadth marks, eight of ten years positive and seven of ten ahead of the benchmark, fit a quiet muni sleeve. Do not copy them into a noisier sleeve. Make the paired comparison the primary mark and fix it before the run, so a general January rally cannot stand in for performance by the losers. Even then, at exactly ten turns and a +2% gross loser edge, paired-only power is 31.87% for Plan 54, 40.84% for preferreds, and 25.88% for BDCs by the same central-noise noncentral-t calculation, all under 50%. The original Plan 54 simulation estimated 32.27% for its corresponding paired-only case.
