## Hypothesis and clock

STATED. [Rapp and Waibel, December 2025, Figure 1](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2026/program/paper/S2KkB95R) report that bank dealers reduce capital commitment ahead of reporting dates and rebuild balance-sheet positions “a few days into the new quarter.” That text does not specify an exact exit day. The [Federal Reserve staff note](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/dealer-balance-sheet-constraints-evidence-from-dealer-level-data-across-repo-market-segments-20240923.html) finds month- and quarter-end pressure in Treasury repo, not a corporate ETF return or timing estimate. The proposed ETF rebound is a test, not a finding of either paper.

- **Trade**: Buy LQD, HYG, JNK and VCIT with equal dollars at the last scheduled XNYS close of each quarter. Sell at the close after the number of new-quarter sessions fixed by `replay_52_plan.held_sessions`, choosing the third close before opening any data. T0 is the purchase close and T+1 is the first XNYS session of the new quarter. Use the scheduled close on shortened sessions. There is no second holding window.
- **Sample**: All 44 quarter-ends from Q1 2015 through Q4 2025 are judged. Show Q1 through Q3 2026 apart, with no marks and no influence on the judged statistics. Primary comparator: the 88 other month-ends in the judged years, bought on the last month session and sold after the same number of sessions. Compare the two unweighted event means; no return matching or filtering.
- **Position**: One $500 long-only account, four equal-dollar fractional sleeves, no leverage, no short legs, no between-window compounding, cash otherwise earns zero. It is four basket round trips a year on $500, not four independent discoveries. Dollar illustrations multiply the net basket return by the fixed budget; they are not annual forecasts or guaranteed fills.
- **Listing**: [State Street lists](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/institutional/etfs/state-street-spdr-bloomberg-high-yield-bond-etf-jnk) JNK on NYSE Arca from November 28, 2007. [Vanguard's original SEC-filed prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1021882/000093247109001821/scottsdale485b.htm) confirms VCIT began operations November 19, 2009 and its ETF shares were listed on Nasdaq. Both predate first entry; actual SIP coverage is UNKNOWN.
- **Proxy**: LQD and VCIT use IEF; HYG and JNK use IEI. For each sleeve subtract one unit of the proxy's simultaneous gross total return from the long fund's net return. This borrows the Replay 31 duration buckets, not its regression, stock factor, or weights. A proxy is an untraded yardstick, not a hedge fill, and cannot establish that dealer flows caused a net effect.

## Marks

One trial. All marks must pass. Use `replay_52_plan.marks`; one-sample t is mean divided by the sample standard error with entry-date clusters obtained by first averaging all sleeves. A zero or undefined standard error fails the associated mark; the t statistics do not correct for serial dependence or idea screening.

- **Net**: Raw net event mean strictly positive.
- **Net t**: Raw net t at least the configured minimum.
- **Proxy-net**: Event mean after proxy subtraction strictly positive.
- **Proxy-net t**: Proxy-net t at least the same minimum.
- **Control**: Raw net mean strictly greater than the same basket's non-quarter month-end net mean.
- **Trim**: Raw net mean strictly positive after dropping the highest configured number of raw net quarters.
- **Non-December**: Raw net mean strictly positive on the 33 March, June and September quarter-ends.
- **Breadth**: The configured minimum fraction of raw net quarters strictly positive; flat is not positive.
