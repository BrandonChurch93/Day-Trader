## Hypothesis and clock

STATED. [Rapp and Waibel, December 2025, Figure 1](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2026/program/paper/S2KkB95R) report that bank dealers reduce capital commitment ahead of reporting dates and rebuild balance-sheet positions “a few days into the new quarter.” That text does not specify an exact exit day. The [Federal Reserve staff note](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/dealer-balance-sheet-constraints-evidence-from-dealer-level-data-across-repo-market-segments-20240923.html) finds month- and quarter-end pressure in Treasury repo, not a corporate ETF return or timing estimate. The proposed ETF rebound is a test, not a finding of either paper.

- **Trade**: Buy LQD, HYG, JNK and VCIT with equal dollars at the last scheduled XNYS close of each quarter. Sell at the close after the number of new-quarter sessions fixed by `replay_52_plan.held_sessions`, choosing the third close before opening any data. T0 is the purchase close and T+1 is the first XNYS session of the new quarter. Use the scheduled close on shortened sessions. There is no second holding window.
- **Sample**: All 40 quarter-ends from Q1 2016 (first entry March 31) through Q4 2025 are judged. The draft originally included 2015; the lead cut 2015 solely because the licensed raw SIP source begins January 1, 2016, before any Plan 52 prices were read. No substitute or mixed-vendor source and no purchase. Show Q1 through Q3 2026 apart, with no marks or influence on the judged statistics. Compare the unweighted mean to the 80 other month-ends in the judged years, with the same entry and hold. No return matching or filtering.
- **Position**: One $500 long-only account, four equal-dollar fractional sleeves, no leverage, no short legs, no between-window compounding, cash otherwise earns zero. It is four basket round trips a year on $500, not four independent discoveries. Dollar illustrations multiply the net basket return by the fixed budget; they are not annual forecasts or guaranteed fills.
- **Listing**: [State Street lists](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/institutional/etfs/state-street-spdr-bloomberg-high-yield-bond-etf-jnk) JNK on NYSE Arca from November 28, 2007. Both predate first entry; actual SIP coverage is UNKNOWN.

This borrows the Replay 31 duration buckets, not its regression, stock factor, or weights. A proxy is an untraded yardstick, not a hedge fill, and cannot establish that dealer flows caused a net effect.

- **Non-December**: Raw net mean strictly positive on the 30 March, June and September quarter-ends.
