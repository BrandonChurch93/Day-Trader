## Public clock and eligibility

- STATED. The [CFTC product listing procedures](https://www.cftc.gov/IndustryOversight/ContractsProducts/ListingProcedures/index.htm) distinguish self-certification and approval paths. The [CFTC Part 40 final rule](https://www.cftc.gov/LawRegulation/FederalRegister/finalrules/2024-24388.html) calls for posting a notice and a copy on the DCM's website concurrent with submission in the 40.2 process. The archive calendar day is the study index. Its detail displays are not evidence of midnight publishing, and the representation is not a historical intraday capture record.
- UNKNOWN. No actual historical publication hour for the CFTC archive or website, no actual earliest publicly accessible copy, and no actual first trade were established. “On or after” is the publicly stated intended input; it is not proof of a print on that date. “After close-of-business on” is the stated initial offering date for Kalshi; the B entry still follows the later-clock rule. An official trade date supersedes an earlier effective session when both are given. The study does not assume the date is an executable timestamp.
