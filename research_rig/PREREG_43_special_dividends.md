## Clock, position and receipt

- **Clock:** The lead prospectively restored the filing-only rule before any price access, replacing draft fixed-16:00 language. For New York acceptance on a regular session date strictly before its scheduled close, early closes included, day 0 is that session. At the close, after it, or on a non-session date, day 0 is the next regular session. Buy at the raw SIP day `entry` open and sell at its day `exit` close, meaning the second open and twentieth close after day 0. Pre-open acceptance does not advance this entry date. This is the same as or later than a calendar-declaration day-0 convention where filings are not earlier than declaration. Earlier day `shown_entry` open is shown only. Daily aggregates proxy fills; they do not prove auction execution. Use exact scheduled closes from the shared XNYS calendar; its regular-session dates must match the companion calendar wherever they overlap.
- **Judged capital:** For each eligible filing allocate its own hypothetical `capital` dollars, independent of other events. Per the lead's preregistration decision before prices, size at the raw entry open including friction, rather than using a pre-open limit estimate. Buy as many whole shares as that cash permits. An unaffordable opening share is a counted no-trade. It never disappears from eligibility and no alternate time or symbol is selected. No cap/quote/liquidity screen is added after docketing. Repeated qualifying filings remain distinct windows. These are not concurrently fundable account-level returns.
- **Cost:** Per side per share pay the greater of price times `cost.bps` in basis points and `cost.floor` dollars. Subtract entry cost from available cash and exit cost from proceeds. Model no other fees or taxes. Net economic return divides gains or losses including distributions and costs by the entire `capital` allocation including idle cash. Return on deployed dollars uses all-in entry outlay and is shown alongside it.
- **Actions:** Raw prices are not adjusted in the outcome. Credit verified ordinary and special cash distributions for ex-dates strictly after entry and on or before exit. No entry-day entitlement; retain entitlement even when cash pays after exit. Reconcile each docketed special to its separately checked identity, disclosure accession and dates, amount and ex-date even outside the hold, or to a verified cancellation. Cash payment date is required. Update share count from verified exact integer new-for-old split ratios with the same ex-date rule; refuse ambiguous split/cash ordering, fractional positions at any modeled holding stage or unmodeled actions. Taxes and cash-in-lieu are not guessed.
- **Control:** IWM is the single judged comparator regardless of size. Buy its whole shares on the identical event window with the identical budget, raw convention, verified fund distributions and friction; a placed stock must have a placed IWM match or validation is blocked. This intentionally avoids current or missing point-in-time size stratification; it is a common small-cap proxy, not an exact matched portfolio. SPY is shown only; if one share is unaffordable in that shown arm its matched control is idle cash and the number of cases is displayed.

## Marks and calibration

All seven `marks` must pass. The fitted sample and paired sample are the same placed-stock windows; affordability counts and an idle-zero-inclusive strategy mean are shown only. Report no-trades by acceptance year and the count relative to all eligible rows. Money, shares, means, and tests are exact rational arithmetic. The scorer compares squared t against the squared threshold only for a positive mean and positive variance; t and results convert to decimal display after the comparisons. An exact zero cannot pass a strict positive mark.

- **Count:** At least `marks.placed` placed stock and IWM pairs.
- **Positive mean:** The equal-event net allocation-return mean must exceed zero.
- **Clustered t:** Intercept-only t for net must meet `marks.t`, clustered by New York entry calendar month, with cluster-count correction. With \(G\) clusters, residual cluster sums \(U_g\), and \(n\) observations, the mean's standard error is \(\sqrt{\frac{G}{G-1}\sum_g U_g^2}/n\). Undefined t fails.
- **Trim:** The net mean still exceeds zero after dropping the best ceiling of `marks.trim` times the placed count, ordering ties by accession.
- **Breadth:** Strictly positive placed-event means in at least `marks.years` of the full acceptance-year span, including partial 2026. No observations in a year cannot count as positive.
- **Recent:** Strictly positive pooled net mean for acceptance calendar years from `marks.recent`.
- **Paired:** The equal-event net difference versus IWM is above zero and its identically clustered paired t meets `marks.t`.

Calibration uses the filing-only entry-month and acceptance-year schedule. At each fixed seed, draw a shared market month shock, a shared independent residual month shock, a market event shock, a stock event residual with occasional symmetric widened tails, and an independent synthetic placement filter. Control equals the two market shocks; net stock equals control plus the two residual shocks and the planted edge. The same synthetic draws are reused across the configured edge grid. Run the exact combined marks and report all-mark passing share for every edge; the zero-edge share cannot exceed `calibration.null_ceiling`. This is a synthetic test of the marks under this stylized null, not a claim about real power or implementation costs.

An attempted opening order reserves its sleeve and issuer for that open even if it buys nothing. No substitution, retry, or backfill. Proceeds become spendable at the opening of the historical settlement session ([SEC T+2 rule](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2017/03/securities-transaction-settlement-cycle); [SEC T+1 FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/exams/educationhelpguidesfaqs/t1-faq)). Distributions are not spendable until the first regular open strictly after the independently checked cash-availability date, which cannot precede either payment or ex-date. A large special may have a payable date before ex-date under [FINRA Rule 11140](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/11140) and its due-bill timing must be checked. Exits take place after that day's open. Leave gains above the sleeve cap in cash. Report skip reasons and net economic gains on original capital, including earned but not-yet-paid distributions. This is shown only because capital constraints create skips distinct from the declaration signal.

## Custody, schema and one-shot procedure

- **Before market access:** The independent auditor reviews the uncommitted draft and synthetic proof, and the lead alone decides whether to freeze and permit acquisition. Use the point-in-time EDGAR class identity, not today's ticker. No vendor data or secrets in the repo. The custodian may stage inputs only after a distinct lead authorization. Normalize [Alpaca 1Day SIP bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars) with explicit entry-date and filing-date `asof` checks. The cash-action checker independently checks the entire expected scope against primary and separate sources before the lead approves input hashes.
- **External files:** Owner-only directory `/home/user/workspace/rig/data/plan43_inputs`, mode 0700, with three regular mode-0600 JSON objects. Each has version 1 and a `records` map keyed by all eligible filing accessions, each with `stock`, `IWM` and `SPY` legs. No extra events or legs. The scorer never fetches.
- **Identities:** `identities.json` adds a top-level `custodian`. Each leg has `identity` (stable class-level ID), `ticker`, ten-digit `cik`, `share_class`, `class_key`, and `source` (HTTPS documentary URL). Stock CIK and class label must match the docket. The canonical key removes ticker and wording aliases: use an explicit class/series, voting common if named, otherwise common. The [original Schneider 8-K cover](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692063/000169206320000164/sndr-20201026.htm) resolves its both-class dividend wording to the NYSE-listed Class B for pricing. A stable ID and its leg/CIK/canonical class are one-to-one, with different time-local tickers permitted for the same stable class. Each control uses its fixed ticker, one stable identity, and `class_key: etf`.
- **Tape:** `bars.json` declares `custodian`, `endpoint`, `feed`, `timeframe`, and `adjustments`. Each leg has `ticker`, `identity`, `entry_asof` (judged entry), `filing_asof` (local filing date), `start`, `end`, and arrays `raw`, `all`, `filing_all`. Requested span pads regular sessions before early entry and after exit as fixed by `stale_run`. Rows are native `t,o,h,l,c,v` only, at midnight New York, and all three views must have positive-volume rows on every padded session. Adjusted views are solely integrity controls. Use Plan 21's adjusted OHLCV fingerprint and unchanged-OHLC stale run, plus constant cross-day adjusted/as-of basis, raw-vs-adjusted intraday shape, and action-backed raw-to-adjusted basis transitions. A split changes basis by old shares over new shares; cash uses one minus cash per share over the prior raw close; other days leave basis unchanged. Each day is compared to the first padded session's basis advanced only by cumulative verified factors, so tolerances cannot compound. The OHLC and factor relative tolerance is one basis point, consistent with Plan 21's shape tolerance. Distinct IDs may not share exact positive-volume native OHLCV fingerprints on three or more distinct sessions across any retained view or overlapping request; raw data for repeated same-ID scope must agree. Missing/zero-volume pads, missing pre-IPO/delisted sessions, unproved basis shifts and stale fill dates refuse all inputs, never create a smaller sample.
- **Actions:** `actions.json` adds a top-level `reviewer`. Each leg has `identity`, padded `start` and `end`, `status: complete`, two separate-host HTTPS `coverage_sources`, `target` (stock's declared-special ID or null), `cancellation_sources` (empty unless documented target cancellation), and `items`. Stock also supplies `target_disclosure` with `accession`, issuer `filing` URL including that accession, `declared`, `announced`, and `record` (record may be omitted for a verified cancellation); comparator disclosure must be null. Bind disclosed declaration, record, and payment dates when known in the docket. Each item has stable `id`, `ex`, `kind` (ordinary, special, or split) and two separate-host HTTPS `sources`. Cash items have `value` (USD/share), `payment` and `available`; splits instead have positive integer `new_shares` and `old_shares` and null `payment` and `available`. Identical action facts must agree across windows sharing a security. A positive event-specific target may be outside the padded window; two filings may not target the same identity/action pair. The reviewer verifies unmodeled actions are absent and checks dates, source independence and scope; empty action lists are meaningful only with verified complete coverage.
- **Authorization and acquisition:** Lead authorship is an out-of-band custody requirement, not something mutable Git author names authenticate. Each approval is a clean-checkout, sole-file-add commit containing the exact one-line grammar specified by the scorer. The lead's `research_rig/ACQUISITION_43.md` binds the frozen source manifest and revised docket. The custodian must first use `python3 -I -B research_rig/score_43.py --claim-acquisition --approval-commit COMMIT` to create its exclusive external acquisition receipt. The scorer does not fetch data. The acquisition opportunity is once per approval; a transport failure with no bar kept requires a documented transport-only refreeze and new distinct approval before another acquisition. No acquisition is inferred from the freeze itself.
- **Validation:** The separate lead `research_rig/APPROVAL_43.md` binds the acquisition commit and receipt plus the three input hashes. `python3 -I -B research_rig/score_43.py --validate-inputs --approval-commit COMMIT` can be repeated on that validation checkout without limit. Its exclusive successful-validation receipt is idempotent and hashable. It prints status only. All real entry points require isolated mode, no bytecode writes, the pinned time-zone bytes, the configured process limits and minimum disk and memory headroom.
