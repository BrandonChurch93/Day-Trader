# Plan 43 successor amendment: per-event tape coverage

Status DRAFT.

Queue 74, successor ledger 188. Ledger 178 remains on HOLD without a scored run. This amendment supersedes only the tape and custody portions of the original Plan 43.

I recommend a separately approved successor because the all-or-nothing gate confounds unusable holding windows with unavailable diagnostic padding. This amendment was written after the custodian reported two incomplete 30-session windows and 44 events with one or more zero-volume padded bars, but before the author saw prices, returns, or counts classified under the rules below. The two reported categories may overlap. Use this proposed rule and its cap without checking how many events would clear them first.

## What the padded window does

Every request spans 30 regular sessions: five before shown-only day +1, day +1 through day +20 inclusive, and five after the exit. The judged allocation uses the raw day +2 open and day +20 close for fills. The cash, splits, and settlement rules use verified actions and the exchange calendar; no padded price or volume is a return or sizing input. Day +1 is outside the judged holding span but its raw open is used by a separate shown-only arm. It is not padding.

Padding is not irrelevant. It supports stale-price runs and neighboring-session, action-adjusted basis checks. The adjustment arithmetic uses the preceding raw close to verify a cash-related basis transition, including a transition into a fill session when an adjacent usable observation exists. The existing scorer also checks as-of identity, duplicate tape fingerprints, and raw consistency across overlapping requests. Removing the requirement for all padding to have positive volume must not turn those checks off.

## Prospective coverage and integrity rule

- **Judged coverage:** For every filing-eligible accession not already skipped under the frozen noncash rule, require a present, strictly positive-volume native bar in each of raw, entry-as-of adjusted, and filing-as-of adjusted on every regular session from day +2 through day +20 inclusive. Apply this to stock, IWM and SPY. If any required bar is missing or has zero volume, refuse that event as an entire unit from the judged score and all shown arms. Do not fill, shift, backfill, or substitute anything. An omitted accession, leg or required view wrapper, failed or partial request or pagination, malformed or duplicate row, wrong date, or negative volume is a packet defect, not a coverage refusal. A verified successful and fully paginated native response may contain no bars; an unrequested or unreturned page is not an empty response.
- **Reason codes:** Assign exactly one primary reason per refused event. In order, test entry, exit, then strictly interior sessions. Within the first failing location, missing takes precedence over zero volume. Use `missing_entry`, `zero_volume_entry`, `missing_exit`, `zero_volume_exit`, `missing_hold`, or `zero_volume_hold`; retain all failing legs, views and dates privately for audit. A code means a required observation was unavailable or reported zero in at least one view, not that the security definitively did not trade.
- **Padding and independent evidence:** Missing or zero-volume padding does not alone refuse an event; count its flags overall and by acceptance year over all 442 action-cleared events, with separate event counts for pre-entry padding and post-exit padding, excluding shown day +1. Use every present raw row for exact raw overlap equality and every positive native row in each view for cross-security fingerprints, even when the other views or the event are refused. For the adjusted identity check, compare each date with both adjusted views positive. Preserve per-day OHLC shape and the constant adjusted/as-of basis anchor across gaps, and retain `same_tape`'s volume-ratio rule: at least three positive paired dates, with at least 98% of consecutive pair ratios changing by less than 1%. Fewer than three pairs means insufficient evidence, not success; this can occur only for a coverage-refused event, on which available per-day shape and basis comparisons still run. Stale-run screening uses the positive entry-adjusted series independently; missing or zero-volume entries in that series alone break its run. No absent bar in one view may hide integrity evidence in another.
- **Raw/adjusted basis through gaps:** Use dates where the raw and entry-adjusted views are positive. Advance the original anchor from the preceding paired date through the intervening calendar sessions by the cumulative, independently verified factors. Bridge no-action and split-only gaps exactly. Bridge a cash date only if the preceding regular session's raw close is present, positive-volume and valid for the frozen cash factor; other missing views that date do not disqualify the raw close. If that cash input is unavailable, record `unproved_cash_bridge` and anchor again at the next paired observation; do not fabricate a close. Still verify any provable no-action or split-only transitions on either side where there are paired observations. All judged holding sessions require all three views, so such an undocumented bridge cannot be used inside a kept judged span. Use otherwise available diagnostics even on coverage-refused events. The two adjusted views retain a single cross-gap anchor.
- **Shown-only earlier entry:** Among kept events, require day +1 to be present and positive in all three views on all three legs. A missing observation takes precedence over a zero and is counted as `missing_day1` or `zero_volume_day1` over the coverage-kept denominator, overall and by acceptance year. This omits only the earlier-entry arm, not the judged event. A stale earlier fill detected on an available day +1, or any other detected integrity mismatch, remains a packet HOLD, not an omission. Use the frozen day +2 open for the other existing arms.
- **Packet integrity:** Authorization, hashes, identities, independently verified action coverage, cross-security aliases, cross-window raw conflicts, unexplained scale changes, adjusted/as-of mismatches, and other non-coverage integrity failures remain packet-wide holds. Stale detected at an available judged or earlier fill remains a hold. Preserve exact fraction and feasible whole-share lot checks for all arms actually used, and require an affordable matched IWM lot whenever a judged stock is placed, as before. Check available data for conflicts and fingerprints even for events refused on coverage, so refusal cannot conceal positive evidence of a packet integrity failure. Check coverage and the cap during no-return validation, before any score claim. Bind the sanitized native-response manifest and rebuilt normalized packet digest to the lead's validation approval alongside the packet hashes. Bind the checked provenance and private hashed coverage summary into the successful validation receipt, and require the separate GO approval to pin that exact receipt. An independent offline rebuild must match the normalized packet byte for byte before the receipt is accepted.

## Coverage cap and companion bound

I propose a hard maximum of **5% of the docket remaining after the already-frozen noncash skip**, rounded down once for the full run. That is at most **22 coverage refusals among 442**. One in twenty is the maximum coverage loss I would accept for a broadly framed event study; it is not a claim that selection at that level is harmless, or that the resulting sample represents all filings. More than 22 is a validation HOLD and no scored launch. Do not revise the cap after seeing the breakdown. The existing minimum of 300 placed IWM pairs remains a separate judged mark, not a substitute for the cap.

Show filing-eligible, noncash-skipped, coverage-refused, coverage-kept, judged-placed and unaffordable counts overall and by acceptance year, including zeroes. Break down coverage refusals by primary reason and year, with separate counts for padding flags and day +1 omissions. Coverage refusals are not affordability no-trades, and the existing idle-zero mean uses only coverage-kept events. Positive-year breadth still uses the original eleven-year span. No market-cap, liquidity, date, ticker, or price threshold is added to select survivors.

Place a downside bound beside the result, on the full 442-event denominator, not in the seven judged marks. Sum actual net allocation returns for placed, kept stocks and zero for kept stocks that cannot afford one share. For each coverage refusal use the conservative allocation-return floor \(-1 - 0.01 N_i / 500\), where \(N_i\) is 50,000 times the product of its independently verified stock split ratios in \((\mathrm{entry},\mathrm{exit}]\), or 50,000 if none. Divide the sum by 442. With the frozen $500 allocation, $0.01 per-share fee floor and 5 bp judged rate, initial shares are at most 50,000 and initial deployment cannot exceed $500. Cash entitlements cannot lower the return. For nonnegative exit price \(p\) and a rate \(b\) between 0 and 10,000 basis points inclusive, \(p-\max(pb/10000,0.01)\ge -0.01\), so the frozen exit charge cannot lower proceeds beyond one cent per exit share. This is a deliberately conservative model bound on absolute net return, not an estimate of the refused events, a matched-IWM bound, or a reconstructed trade. An unconditional finite paired lower bound cannot be inferred from absent comparator prices under these rules.

Required result limitation: “This result speaks only for events with complete positive-volume SIP views on every judged session that passed the tape checks and, for the judged marks, supported a placed stock and IWM pair. It does not establish a return for every eligible filing.”
