## What the published work supports

- **Closest direct short window:** STATED. In [Abarbanell, Bushee and Raedy (2003), Table 4](https://repository.upenn.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/e586dc6a-8482-4408-bb4f-59f22a1a2d74/content), 169 historical U.S. transactions from 1980–1996 showed subsidiary size-adjusted returns of −4.50% over days 1–35 and +2.11% over days 36–60. The latter is not statistically significant, not net of costs and not measured against IWM. Week eight was positive and significant on its own, which is not evidence of a preselected profitable holding window. The authors found little general support that institutional style restrictions explain the price move.
- **The queue's day-14 claim:** STATED. The [Copenhagen Business School thesis](https://research.cbs.dk/files/98732383/1643122_Spin_off_performance_An_unrelenting_anomaly.pdf) reports about −1.7% cumulative average abnormal return through day 14 and +12.9% after 12 months, in a multinational sample with zero modeled trading costs. The endpoints do not establish a day-15-to-day-60 recovery, net profit, or a forced-sale causal mechanism.
- **Broader check:** STATED. The [S&P study](https://www.spglobal.com/content/dam/spglobal/mi/en/documents/general/Capital-Market-Implications-Of-Spinoffs.pdf) covers 516 U.S. spin-offs in 1989–2015, with −1.37% at five days, −2.28% at 30 days and an insignificant +1.18% at three months. Its inception-to-horizon numbers are not an isolated later trade. UNKNOWN: an independently established recent U.S. day-15-to-20 entry and day-40-to-60 exit with positive net returns. There is short-window research, but the durable positive claims cited here are longer-horizon.

## Proposed rule and power, not a freeze

STATED, unbuilt. Day one is the first regular-way session. Use the day-35 close through the day-60 close, chosen before prices to align with the closest short-window study. Buy up to $500 in whole shares, with remainder in cash; compare net dollars per $500 to the same-window long IWM alternative, not a short. Attribute actual cash distributions, splits and terminal consideration; a missing held exit is unresolved, not an entry refusal. Any future freeze would specify fees, the SIP half-spread calculation, quote validity, ceilings and fallback. Only the custodian would collect licensed inputs after freeze and approval, validate execution quotes and fix the resulting cost inputs before scoring. Reason-code unavailable or ineligible entries, including an unaffordable whole share; refuse them individually without replacement, and fail the sample only above 10% aggregate refusals. No actual spread schedule was measured or frozen.
