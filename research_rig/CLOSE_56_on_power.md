# Plan 56 closed on synthetic power

MEASURED. The filing screen found 2,391 dated officer/director purchase-cluster candidates across 1,479 issuer CIKs in 2016–2025. Every candidate got a second-resolution EDGAR acceptance timestamp. In 5,000 synthetic worlds, the 20-session hold detected the literature-based +53 bp paired net effect in 460 worlds (9.20%), below the 50% release floor. The 10-session hold reached 230 of 5,000 (4.60%) at its separate, smaller modeled effect. No freeze, market-data acquisition, scorer, lock, or real return.

## Published drift and cost assumptions

- **Closest older U.S. cluster result.** STATED. Post-SOX through 2016, days 2–21 after the second filing returned 1.53% gross abnormal for clusters versus 1.01% for other purchases, a 52 bp difference. The 3.80% versus 1.95% figures instead start from the insider trade date. Neither series charges execution costs or uses IWM ([Cluster Trading of Corporate Insiders, Table 7](https://apjfs.org/file/download/6531?view=1)).
- **10-session gap in the evidence.** UNKNOWN. No clean current U.S. result was found for an unselected cluster entered at the next open and held for ten sessions. The German disclosed-purchase study reports gross abnormal returns of 1.13%, 1.46% and 1.99% through sessions 5, 10 and 20, respectively, including the disclosure day. In its 20-session quoted-spread hedge it reports returns of 0.08% in the lowest-risk quintile and 0.53% in the highest-risk quintile, before brokerage and additional shorting costs ([Market Efficiency Reloaded](https://www.fa.mgt.tum.de/fileadmin/w00chf/cefs/Working_Paper/200804_cefs_wp.pdf)).
- **Recent U.S. evidence.** STATED. A guest study of 2022 through June 2026 C-suite purchases enters at the open of the next session after the filing date, using SPY. It reports +1.009% gross five-session abnormal return, then +0.980% cumulative and +0.814% buy-and-hold abnormal at 21 sessions, with both 21-session confidence intervals crossing zero before costs. It has no 10-session result and does not use the actual acceptance clock ([QuantInsti event study](https://blog.quantinsti.com/sec-form-4-insider-trading-python-event-study/)). The 2018–2024 microcap preprint reports +3.2% gross at 20 event days with a 17.3% dispersion, but the first event day includes the overnight gap after after-close filings. Its +2.33% over days 2–10 is from the selected lowest 52-week-high-distance quintile, not a plain cluster. Its March 2025 through June 2026 liquid cluster extension misses the matched support gate, with only 55.9% covered and the estimated excess almost gone at 50 bp assumed friction ([Insider Purchase Signals in Microcap Equities](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06198v2)). The comparisons suggest less dependable drift after the initial reaction and costs, but are not an apples-to-apples estimate of shrinkage.
- **Recent cluster comparison.** STATED. The next-session-open guest study also reports its 21-session cluster-minus-solo difference at +0.175 percentage points cumulative (95% CI −1.184 to +1.535 points) and +0.406 points buy-and-hold (95% CI −1.022 to +1.833 points), both before costs and both compatible with zero. Its cluster only requires one other insider and a 10-calendar-day transaction window, unlike the proposed primary ([QuantInsti cluster comparison](https://blog.quantinsti.com/sec-form-4-insider-trading-python-event-study/)).
- **Plant and cost.** STATED. Use 153 bp gross for 20 sessions as the older U.S. cluster anchor, not the 52 bp comparison with solo buy filings. Ten-session gross is a modeling transfer only, 153 bp times 146/199, or 112.25 bp, because a like-for-like published number is missing. Take 100 bp stock round trip off both as an assumption, not a quote measurement; it is already below the preprint's 133 bp median high-low estimated spread for microcaps. This gives +53 and +12.25 bp net, respectively. Show 50 and 133 bp friction sensitivities. The alternative recent +320 bp gross proxy is shown only because it includes returns the next-open buyer cannot capture and is not a cluster result ([microcap trading-cost study](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06198v2)).

## What drove power

MEASURED from synthetic outcomes, not returns. Generate 5,000 worlds for each case on the 2,391 actual filing labels. The judge is a one-sided month-clustered t of at least 2 on equal-event stock-minus-IWM net excess, then positive mean, positive mean without the strongest calendar month by aggregate excess, and no more than 10% event refusals. Independently simulated attrition is 5% central, zero in low-noise cases, and 8% in stress-noise cases. Central paired per-event noise is 12% for 10 sessions and 17.3% for 20; only the latter is anchored to the preprint's gross-stock abnormal-return dispersion, not a fit of the proposed paired return ([microcap sample](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06198v2)). Low noise is 8% and 11.31%, and stress noise is 16% and 23.07%. Event, issuer and month innovations are independent draws before applying their variance shares.

| Hold and plant | t alone | + positive mean | + remove strongest month | + refusal cap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 sessions, +12.25 bp, central | 230 / 5,000 | 230 / 5,000 | 230 / 5,000 | 230 / 5,000 |
| 20 sessions, +53 bp, central | 460 / 5,000 | 460 / 5,000 | 460 / 5,000 | 460 / 5,000 |
| Zero net, central, either hold | 165 / 5,000 | 165 / 5,000 | 165 / 5,000 | 165 / 5,000 |

STATED. Issuer and calendar-month variance shares are assumptions, not sourced estimates or calibrated stock results. Central shares are 15% issuer and 20% month; the rest is event-level noise. The ranges tested are issuer 0%, 5%, 15%, 25% and month 0%, 5%, 20%, 30%. The 120 nominal calendar months are not 120 equally weighted month shocks: monthly event counts range from 1 to 209, so event weighting corresponds to about 54.75 equally weighted months for the month variance term. The count-only IID SE at 8% and no attrition is 16.36 bp; with the central dependence it is 51.13 bp even at that same low noise. Month dependence, not the best-month test, drives the loss.

| Dependence case under central noise | 10-session +12.25 bp power | 20-session +53 bp power | 20-session theoretical SE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| IID | 6.72% | 29.94% | 36.30 bp |
| Issuer 15% only | 9.14% | 32.10% | 40.12 bp |
| Month 20% only | 4.46% | 8.82% | 109.55 bp |
| Issuer 5%, month 5% | 5.90% | 15.68% | 63.92 bp |
| Issuer 15%, month 20% | 4.60% | 9.20% | 110.87 bp |
| Issuer 25%, month 30% | 4.44% | 8.04% | 133.52 bp |

MEASURED. The issuer-only row can appear more powerful than IID because the month t does not separately adjust for same-issuer dependence across months. This is not a benefit of issuer concentration. Central 20-session power is 15.08% at low noise and zero attrition, 19.48% at 50 bp costs and central noise, 4.78% at 133 bp costs, and 57.60% only at the non-comparable +220 bp net microcap sensitivity. The IID low-noise 20-session case reaches 61.40%, but assuming independence and less noise does not fit the decision baseline. Central zero-edge false passes are 3.30%, below 5%.

## Second attempt and what would have changed

MEASURED. [Plan 36](PREREG_36_form4_cluster_buys.md) was the first attempt. It [ended at validation](CLOSE_36_on_coverage.md) with 120 of 2,100 confirmed attempts unresolved (5.71% versus a 5% frozen maximum), no scoring lock and no real return. Plan 56 is a separate second attempt, not a repair or replay of Plan 36. Its proposed execution design below was never frozen.

| Aspect | Plan 36 | Proposed Plan 56 and reason |
|---|---|---|
| Signal and sample | Two insiders within two trade sessions with a five-session staleness gate, daily disclosure batch, filing years 2016–Sept. 2026 | Three distinct insiders inside 30 calendar days of filings, each at least $25,000 per original filing/class; filing years 2016–2025 complete. Third acceptance sets the signal clock. No news, price condition, or AI |
| Venue and plans | Affirmative market evidence; historical plan unknown allowed with positive venue evidence | Exclude evidence of non-market or plan purchases; keep silence to avoid selecting for writing style. Show strict variants without judging them |
| Entry, exit and overlap | Next session's close; 20 intervals and 21-session class cooldown | First regular open strictly after public acceptance; exit at the regular open 20 sessions later. Fixed 30-calendar-day class trigger suppression; 10 sessions considered synthetically only, never on real data. Refuse if a near-open acceptance has no evidence of pre-open dissemination, rather than silently shifting entry |
| Comparator and execution | $500 whole shares, cap-based IWM/IJH/IVV, close-spread model, prior $5 and $1m liquidity minimums, pre-history and cutoff gates | $500 unlevered whole-share tickets; same-window IWM for every placement, no market-cap lookup. Official open proxy plus precommitted half-spread on each side from valid SIP quotes and a fixed fee schedule, with quote age, size and timing validated before scoring. For comparison, IWM is hypothetical and not a short. No bid/ask guarantee at the auction |
| Refusal and outcomes | Coverage gate of at most 5% overall and 10% per year for confirmed attempts, along with stock, tail, recency and breadth marks | Reason-coded refusals for unavailable or ineligible entry evidence and required benchmark inputs; count them, do not replace them. Above 10% of the full primary docket fails the cap. An unresolved held exit is not a skipped event; value documented terminal consideration or fail rather than deleting the loss. Paired month t and the strongest-month check replace Plan 36's breadth marks |

STATED. If any later project were approved, an independent custodian would verify historical security identity and fund exclusions, clock-to-session joins, open/bid-ask observability and point-in-time cash, splits and terminal actions; no author would open SIP input. The 100 bp in this note is for feasibility only. Any eventual cost and refusal definitions would have to be fixed, signed and frozen before reading returns. Silent full validation, fixed-code refusals and the immediate pre-score lock would remain separate from approval.

UNKNOWN. Whether the historical rule would make or lose money after observed costs. This decision says the sample is poorly powered for a defensible paper-based net effect, not that the effect is zero.
