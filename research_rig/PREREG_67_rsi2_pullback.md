## What the sources actually show

- STATED, Connors and Alvarez. The [original book, chapter 9](https://studylib.net/doc/27785371/short-term-trading-strategies-that-work-larry-connors--ce...), pp. 55–56, reports stock returns over fixed forward horizons for RSI(2) below 10 in 1995–2007, with stocks above their 200-day moving averages as the baseline. The explicit recipe on pp. 61–62 buys SPY at the close after an S&P 500 index signal below **5**, then exits when the index closes above its five-period average. It is not this draft's per-ETF below-10 recipe. Page 54 gives the RSI ratio but not its initialization, warm-up or smoothing procedure. Page 14 cautions that the reported statistics exclude costs and slippage. [StockCharts' later formulation](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/trading-strategies-and-models/trading-strategies/rsi-2) describes the below-10 adaptation and presents pre-close execution or the next open as choices, not a measurement of next-open excess.
- STATED, Timon Krüger. The [index CFD study](https://kruegeralgorithms.com/en/research/rsi2-dip-buying-index-cfds-holdout) uses Yahoo Finance daily SPY, QQQ and DIA proxies for three CFDs. Its 2019–October 2026 holdout reports 206 trades, +48.3 bp net with modeled 2 bp round-trip spread/slippage plus overnight financing and dividend-credit assumptions, and +33.6 bp excess over a +14.7 bp every-day baseline for similar holding time. This is a post-publication result for pooled trades with both fills at the official close and a 20-session limit, not our day-weighted next-open result. The [full PDF](https://kruegeralgorithms.com/research/rsi2-dip-buying-index-cfds-holdout/Rsi2_Dip_Buying_Index_Cfds_Holdout.pdf) does not specify the RSI seed, warm-up or smoother, exact duration matching, whether the baseline was charged costs, or how the reported t was computed. A descriptive CFD check minutes after the close is not a next-open test.
- STATED, false-discovery claim. The [programme-wide scan and its October 8 addendum](https://kruegeralgorithms.com/en/research/fresh-scan-150-pattern-families) describe global Benjamini–Hochberg and include the index CFD rule at q = 0.005 across 59 holdout tests. Neither the scan nor the [rule report](https://kruegeralgorithms.com/en/research/rsi2-dip-buying-index-cfds-holdout) specifies the particular raw p-value, test statistic or null used for that q. It cannot be attached to the matched-hold excess as a verified adjusted p-value. The report expressly excludes preselection among three related rules from that correction. No such correction validates Plan 67.
- STATED, RIP Backtest. The [site keeps its operators unnamed](https://ripbacktest.com/about/). Its [SPY study](https://ripbacktest.com/posts/rsi-2-strategy-spy/) uses Yahoo daily prices and dividends with 4 bp round-trip modeled cost. It reports +47.6 bp net from 2008 onward and +53.2 bp net on 94 trades from 2016 to October 2026, but says the latter holdout was already used and QQQ passed only three of six gates. The primary fills are both same-close; the full-period next-open sensitivity changes both fills and is +54.3 bp **total net** per trade, not excess or post-publication-only. The quoted +31.6 bp random-uptrend-dip median is a full-sample primary-rule control with the same exit condition, not a published next-open matched-hold control. The [written specification](https://ripbacktest.com/posts/rsi-2-strategy-spy/rsy_spec.md) documents Wilder smoothing with two-change seed, 250 closes of warm-up and no time limit for the primary rule.
- STATED, Empirica Lab. The [research note](https://empiricalab.substack.com/p/connors-rsi2-spy) is bylined Empirica Lab, with no individual named on the page. It uses Yahoo dividend-adjusted SPY daily bars through September 2026, charges 2 bp per side and reports +51 bp total net on 149 post-publication trades from January 2009. It names Wilder RSI but does not show its seeding or an explicit warm-up beyond the rolling-average code. Its primary model buys and sells at the finalized close, although the suggested practice is to estimate before the close. Its random-entry result moves holds within full-sample halves; it does not establish a post-publication, prior-only or next-open excess.
- STATED, interpretation. The three public writeups use overlapping Yahoo SPY observations and adjacent or overlapping markets and dates. Separate writeups, some with distinct implementations, do not create independent samples or three replications of +33.6 bp. UNKNOWN: any comparable estimate of next-open excess for this panel. Krüger's [separate stock-CFD study](https://kruegeralgorithms.com/en/research/rsi2-dip-buying-sp500-stocks-confirmed-locally-not-globally) reports −6.95 bp net for next-open entry in its individual-stock holdout. That is a timing warning, not an index-panel replication or a matched-excess estimate.

## Judged draft design

- STATED, timing. Judge B: evaluate the close only once final; enter at the next regular-session open. Once held, test the first final close strictly above its five-session average, including the close of the entry session; sell at the following regular-session open. If no signal would yield an earlier exit, sell at the 20th open after entry. C is shown only: the textbook same-final-close fills are infeasible as an exact signal and fill. A, a 15-minute pre-close proxy, is not built: it would be a different signal with different timestamp and auction-order requirements.
- STATED, universe. Treat ONEK, SPLG and SPYM as a continuous fund, with [SEC issuer material](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1222333/000119312518248451/0001193125-18-248451.txt) dating the ONEK-to-SPLG ticker change to October 16, 2017 and the [ticker notice](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064642/000119312525245696/d57221d497k.htm) dating SPLG-to-SPYM to October 31, 2025. The [prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064642/000119312526034617/d53728d497k.htm) dates Russell 1000 to SSGA Large Cap on November 16, 2017 and SSGA Large Cap to S&P 500 on January 24, 2020. The fund did not track the S&P 500 before 2020. The other funds are QQQ, IWM and DIA; do not substitute SPY for any segment. An active alias and complete native series would have to be independently attested before a run.
- STATED, signal. Enter if the action-neutral signal close is strictly above its simple 200-session average and Wilder RSI(2) is strictly below 10. Both the 200- and 5-session averages include the current completed close. Seed mean gain and loss with the arithmetic mean of the first two positive and negative changes respectively, clipping the other sign to zero rather than skipping it. Thereafter each mean is half its prior value plus half today's clipped change. RSI is `100 - 100/(1 + mean_gain/mean_loss)`, with both zero giving 50, only loss zero giving 100, and only gain zero giving zero. The third close seeds RSI. Require 250 completed closes per fund including the signal close. Do not treat a missing close or open as a holiday or silently fill one.
- STATED, actions. Start the forward signal level at its first raw close. At each later session multiply the previous signal level by `(s*C + D)/previous_raw_close`: `s` is new shares per pre-action share, `C` the current raw close, and `D` confirmed ex-date cash per pre-action share, zero if none. Use only confirmed effective actions known by that close. Split first and express any same-date cash at the specified pre-action basis. For realized returns use raw opens; adjust units for a split while held and apply cash only if already held into the ex-date. An ex-date-open exit earns that cash; an ex-date-open purchase does not. Cash accrues at face value, not reinvested. Use the same mechanics for comparison holds. Unresolved actions, joins or gaps are input failures, not invented fills.
- STATED, overlap and costs. No second signal or position in a fund while entry is pending or a position is held. After selling at the morning open, that evening is eligible for a new signal. Measure returns, not $500 share sizing. Charge 2 bp at each trade fill: buy one raw unit at entry open times 1.0002, sell resulting split-adjusted units at exit open times 0.9998, add earned cash, divide by entry outlay and subtract one. Comparison holds have zero modeled cost, the conservative asymmetry fixed by the lead.
- STATED, expanding comparison. For a completed trade of length H exchange sessions from entry open to exit open, use the arithmetic mean return of all admissible H-session open-to-open holds of the same fund from its earlier history, with both endpoints strictly before the actual entry session. At least 60 such completed holds are required. Holding length does not allow any comparison date at or after the actual entry, and the control has no trend or RSI filter. Subtract that gross mean from each net trade return. Collapse simultaneous actual entry dates, using equal weight among completed eligible fund trades within each date.
- STATED, fixed marks. All six required: at least 150 entry dates; mean date-level excess strictly positive; intercept-only CR1 t at least 2 with weeks beginning Monday and clustered by actual entry week; mean date-level excess strictly positive after dropping the best ceil(1% of entry dates); mean date-level excess positive in at least six of the ten entry years 2017–2026, with an empty year not positive; and the plain mean date-level return after costs strictly positive. The t standard error is `sqrt((G/(G-1)) * sum(week_score^2)) / N`, where G is the number of entry weeks, N the number of entry dates, and a week score is the sum of its date-level deviations from the full-sample mean. Fewer than two weeks or an undefined t cannot pass.
- STATED, contamination. The project's daily-equity histories have been used in other replays, for example [Replay 14](../docs/research/52_replay_14_large_stock_momentum.md) and the direct IWM overlap in [Replay 39](../docs/research/84_replay_39_iwm_pre_holiday.md). The four funds are not a new bar sample. No RSI-conditioned return on the panel is known to have been computed in the project; this is not an untouched data sample. No real bars were opened for this draft or power check.

## Price-blind power and close

- STATED assumptions. Central scenario: 180 entry dates across ten years, not a measured count. Draw distinct dates uniformly from 250 artificial sessions per year, stratified by year, and group by artificial five-session week. Run 50,000 normal-noise worlds per scenario with shared within-week Gaussian shocks (central correlation 0.25) and independent weekly shocks. The central fixed synthetic calendar has 158 traded weeks. The six marks are applied to every world. Plain net returns equal excess plus 14.7 bp of assumed drift, not an estimated next-open control.
- STATED noise proxy. The full-period S&P 500 annualized realized volatility of 16.72% in [S&P DJI Exhibit 6](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/research/research-sp-500-low-volatility-index-five-decades-of-history.pdf) is scaled by the square root of 3.4/252 to 194.21 bp per assumed hold. The 3.4-session horizon is the [published same-close holdout mean](https://kruegeralgorithms.com/en/research/rsi2-dip-buying-index-cfds-holdout), not a measured next-open duration. Use 15.54% and 17.48% published subperiod volatilities for sensitivity. These are unconditional broad-market proxies, not measured conditional ETF-date noise. The Gaussian model does not reproduce gap risk, fat tails, cross-week exposure or an empirical entry process.
- STATED edge. The judged gate uses +16.8 bp, half the published +33.6 bp same-close excess because no comparable next-open excess has been found. +8.4 bp halves that assumption again. +33.6 bp is shown as a reference, not authority for the judged run.
