# Plan 58 draft. The 21:00 to 23:00 UTC Bitcoin window

STATED. Queue 98, ledger 196. This is a draft, not a freeze. The lead agent has not authorized a historical price pull, real preflight, scoring or lock. Both judged cost figures are **fixed from Measurement 59 before freeze**. Measurement 59 has its own custody and approval; its quote data are not Plan 58 return inputs.

## Why this clock, and what is new

STATED. [Quantpedia's original Gemini study](https://quantpedia.com/are-there-seasonal-intraday-or-overnight-anomalies-in-bitcoin/) bought BTC at 21:00 UTC and sold at 23:00 UTC, from October 9, 2015 through February 3, 2022. Its reported annualized return of 33% converts to about 7.8 bp per calendar day geometrically; this is not a printed arithmetic gross mean. The [same-start extension](https://quantpedia.com/the-seasonality-of-bitcoin/) through June 30, 2023 gives 40.64% annualized, about 9.4 bp per calendar day geometrically. Backing out their shared period by subtracting annualized log growth times elapsed years implies roughly 16.3 bp/day from February 3, 2022 through June 30, 2023, conditional on comparable CAGR calculation and a daily opportunity. It is not a standalone reported subperiod result. The extension describes drawdowns in 2022–23; the printed results do not establish that the effect faded by then.

STATED. [Janorium's 2020–September 2026 Binance study](https://janorium.com/articles/bitcoin-weekend-time-of-day-returns) gives compounded totals of +173.3% for 21:00–22:00 and +108.4% for 22:00–23:00, implying approximately +7.1 bp geometric per two-hour trade across about 2,465 days. [Swolecharts' 2019–November 2026 Binance averages](https://swolecharts.com/seasonality/btc) give +4.2 bp and +3.2 bp for the two start-labelled hours; summing is an approximation, not an exact two-hour compound. Neither gives a clean isolated numerical post-2022 mean for the exact window. UNKNOWN. True out-of-sample edge and magnitude of fade. Fix July 1, 2023 as the later-period boundary, the first UTC date after the exact-rule extension. This is subsequent to both motivating Gemini windows, but not out of sample to the newer pooled evidence or an untouched trading holdout. Report Jan 1, 2022 onward descriptively if desired; it does not decide a mark.

STATED. [Replay 03](../docs/research/15_replay_03_intraday_crypto.md) already checked conditional intraday patterns, including a conditional last-hour rule; it did not check this fixed unconditional two-hour window. [Replay 19](../docs/research/64_replay_19_monday_asia.md) uses a different Monday Asia rule, and [Replay 35](../docs/research/81_replay_35_cme_bitcoin_expiry_ibit.md) a monthly expiry clock. [Round 13](../docs/research/79_round_13_known_clock_premia.md) dropped a broad crypto-time/weekend switch on cost grounds. None is counted again here. The gross plant is fixed now at +7 bp, with +3.5 bp as a fade stress, not a second window.

## One clock, two judged cost versions

- **Spot input.** Coinbase Exchange BTC-USD one-hour candles, with bucket starts at 21:00 and 23:00 UTC; use the opens of those buckets, never their closes. The 23:00 open is an exit proxy, not a signal available at 21:00. The schedule itself is the signal. Tentatively Jan 1, 2016 through Nov 25, 2026, because the [Coinbase launch](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/coinbase-launches-first-regulated-bitcoin-exchange-in-the-u-s) was in 2015. UNKNOWN. Earliest quality-controlled full archive year; no endpoint or bars checked. If the tentative year cannot be supported, the lead agent may amend the dates based on timestamps and coverage alone and rerun synthetic power before freeze; do not move the bound after seeing returns. The [Coinbase Exchange candle schema](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-candles) defines timestamp as bucket start and a 300-candle limit. Use the documented lawful archive with explicit ranges, deduplication and no filling missing buckets. Raw data, snapshots and hashes of private custody manifests stay off-repo.
- **Futures-cost version.** Include every UTC date. Each valid spot gross observation is `10000 * (open23/open21 − 1)`; subtract a single frozen all-in BIP round-trip charge in bp on every valid date. Measure BIP top-of-book, not ETP as a substitute. All dates means all dates even before contract launch or during [Friday maintenance](https://help.coinbase.com/en/derivatives/perpetual-style-futures/contract-specifications); it models a Bitcoin clock effect at estimated cost, not historical contract eligibility, futures basis, tradeability every day or a fill record. If there is no public usable book, this cost version waits; if funding and commissions cannot be bounded credibly from approved evidence, it remains unfrozen rather than using spread alone.
- **IBIT-cost version.** Include Monday through Friday dates, including historical holidays, dates before IBIT existed and early-close weekdays. Apply the same BTC-USD return and a single frozen IBIT round-trip charge on each valid date. Under [Measurement 59](MEASURE_59_crypto_clock_costs.md), IBIT must first meet paired quotes coverage of 95% overall and 90% per New York seasonal regime on full sessions, and cost strictly below 3.50 bp. A weekday spot calendar with a cost estimate is not a record of which weekdays had executable IBIT orders.
- **Cash and validation.** Compare each fixed clock trade to uninvested cash at zero return for that window; do not mark outside-window holdings or select other hours. Use one equal-notional unlevered observation per eligible date. Missing/malformed bars at either edge are fixed-code cash zeros with planned-date denominator; never interpolate. Require at least 99% paired spot coverage overall and separately since July 1, 2023 for each judged version, with manifest validation complete before lock. No information from entry or exit prices sets eligibility other than fixed validity rules. No weekend filter for the futures-cost version, no momentum or volatility overlays, no clock search.
- **Shown-only ETF check.** On the 716 calendar full IBIT sessions since listing, compare approved IBIT same-window returns to matched spot if the lead agent later approves a separate sealed price input. Report counts, means, paired difference and correlation only where both are valid, by New York season and pooled. Neither ETF values nor this subsample judge the clock effect. ETHA and ETP are cost-only comparisons and cannot replace either judged version.

## Cost decision and pass marks

STATED. The literature +7 bp reference must strictly exceed twice all-in cost, so 3.50 bp is a break-even gate value and not a pass at equality. Use one cost constant per version and publish it before freeze. Take IBIT mean paired half spreads plus the stated 0.30 bp sell fee allowance under the assumed zero online brokerage commission; quotes do not establish fills ([Fidelity pricing](https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/pricing-fees/); [BlackRock fee illustration](https://www.ishares.com/us/strategies/ways-to-invest-in-bitcoin)). For BIP, build an explicit spread, per-contract fee, commission and funding allowance from the cost work before freeze and with no Plan 58 price input. The [venue fee page](https://www.coinbase.com/derivatives-trading/us-derivatives) includes a fixed $0.15 per contract per side plus a volume-based commission; published “as low as” is not the ordinary taker quote. The BIP [contract](https://help.coinbase.com/en/derivatives/perpetual-style-futures/contract-specifications) is 0.01 BTC with $0.05 tick value per contract and [hourly funding](https://help.coinbase.com/en/derivatives/perpetual-style-futures/funding-rate). For scale only, at a hypothetical $100,000 BTC, fixed clearing plus one spread tick is 3.50 bp before variable commission/funding; at hypothetical $3,000 ETH the corresponding 0.10 ETH figure is 11.67 bp. These are not measured spreads. [Kraken Pro](https://www.kraken.com/pro/fees) entry spot pricing is 40 bp maker and 80 bp taker per side at the standard entry tier, before spread or applicable [margin fees](https://support.kraken.com/articles/us-margin-fees); it cannot clear on standard spot fees and is not a replacement route.

STATED. After the cost and input gates, require both positive full-sample net versus cash with UTC Monday-week clustered t at least 2.0, and strictly positive net from July 1, 2023 onward. Use the finite-cluster factor `G/(G−1)` with intercept-only date means. Month clusters are a shown sensitivity, not a route rescue. Show raw and net means, standard errors, clustered t, by-year and pre/post-extension returns, coverage and denominators separately for both versions. At freeze, confirm two-arm family-wise net-null rate in the synthetic calibration or use the conservative sum of marginal null rates for the nominated weekly test. If costs miss eligibility, keep that cost scenario in the synthetic display only. A spot proxy plus measured cost is not a fill record, an endorsement of execution, or a tradable P&L claim.

## Synthetic feasibility, no prices

MEASURED in synthetic worlds only. Jan 1, 2016 through Nov 25, 2026 contains 3,982 calendar dates (1,244 later) and 2,844 weekdays (888 later), 570 week or 131 month clusters. The shown-only ETF schedule has 716 full sessions, 493 daylight and 223 standard. The simulator applies a constant net plant equal to gross less cost across both periods, 10,000 worlds per case, Student-t with five degrees of freedom plus a normal shared cluster shock, and perfect candle coverage. Central two-hour SD is an assumed 80 bp, with 60 and 100 bp shown; the motivating study's reported 20.93% annualized volatility is of the order of 110 bp/day under square-root-of-365 scaling ([original Quantpedia study](https://quantpedia.com/are-there-seasonal-intraday-or-overnight-anomalies-in-bitcoin/)). Actual dispersion and dependence are UNKNOWN. Both numeric marks must clear. The screen on independently measured route costs is outside this conditional power simulation; in particular 3.50 and 5 bp cannot pass the strict two-times gate at a +7 bp reference.

STATED. Two-arm weekly family-wise null rate is bounded by the sum of marginal simulation rates, 4.42% in these worlds; not a measured historical false-positive rate. At 2 bp, +7 bp has useful power in the central cases, while half the effect does not. At costs near the gate, or with stronger monthly dependence, even the full published effect can be hard to distinguish from noise. Do not turn a non-pass into proof of no effect.
