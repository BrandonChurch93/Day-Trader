## Basis and roster

STATED. [Gibson, Safieddine and Titman (2000), publisher abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/abs/taxmotivated-trading-and-price-pressurean-analysis-of-mutual-fund-holdings/3D000AB295A4406FCC2C9F8C4A65ECA7) describes accelerated October selling and a strong rebound for prior losers with high mutual-fund holdings in the first fully effective year, then weaker price pressure as managers spread sales. The full paper and numerical effect estimates were not verified. [A 2005 follow-up](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/BF02761559.pdf) identifies 1990 as the one supported year in that paper and reports evidence only in November's first week under its own method. [IRC §4982](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=(title:26+section:4982+edition:prelim)) gives a default October 31 capital-gain measurement date for the regulated investment company excise tax, with an election for eligible November/December year-ends. It is not a universal fund tax year-end.

MEASURED, public filing metadata only. iShares Trust CIK 0001100663, series S000004313, IJR, is present in the [2017 N-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1100663/000119312517271743/0001193125-17-271743-index.html) for June 30, filed August 29, and the [2024 NPORT-P](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1100663/0001752724-24-191760-index.htm) for June 30, filed August 26. IJR tracks broad US small caps, where the price-pressure hypothesis is more relevant than for large caps; its roster is neither the entire equity market nor a proxy for how much stock active mutual funds collectively own ([SEC IJR investment strategy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1100663/000119312525162603/R707.htm)). This confirms examples in both form regimes, not annual filing or identity coverage.

## Proposed rule and synthetic gate

STATED, unbuilt. On the penultimate October session, rank true losers on split-neutral price change since the first January close. Buy the worst ten at the last October close, with equal fractional sleeves on $500 and unfilled money in cash. Sell at the last November close, with the first November close shown only. Compare with IJR and the equal-weight sampled roster over identical dates. Include verified dividends, splits and terminal proceeds; an unresolved terminal value blocks judgment rather than removing the name. Candidate friction per side was the larger of the 10 bp floor and the duration-weighted median quoted NBBO half-spread, plus 5 bp; use the larger entry or exit spread at exit, and a 50 bp half-spread fallback when quotes are unusable. The fallback is not an observed spread.

STATED. The Plan 48-style gate requires positive mean net, at least eight net-positive years, positive mean excess over IJR, at least seven years ahead of IJR, positive net without the best year, and positive mean excess over the equal-weight roster. The strict gate also requires paired t of at least 2 against the roster. A paired-t-only result is shown for diagnosis, not as an alternate pass rule. Inputs are under `plan_54_closure` in `config/thresholds.yaml`.

MEASURED, synthetic only. Each cell uses 30,000 simulated worlds of ten independent years with Gaussian shocks. Benchmark noise has 6% standard deviation, roster-minus-benchmark noise 1.5%, and loser-minus-roster noise varies below. Shared exposure is one-to-one. Assume round-trip costs of 50 bp for losers and 30 bp for each comparison, so the gross planted loser edge is reduced by 20 bp relative to the roster. The shared-rally null adds 2% to all legs with no planted loser edge. These are assumptions, not historical estimates; there are no missing names or differential cash payouts in the simulation. In particular, the +1% effect is a plausible sizing assumption, not a published or measured modern estimate.

## Closed-end fund sensitivity, not a new plan

STATED. No established October seller clock for retail-held CEF shares. The October tax rule can govern a qualifying fund's realized gains inside its portfolio, not the retail owner's decision to sell the CEF share. Listed CEF shares generally trade in the secondary market and are not redeemable on demand ([SEC closed-end fund overview](https://www.sec.gov/answers/mfclose.htm)). Institutional ownership or internal tax trades could in theory move market price or NAV, but neither the two CEF rosters nor the IJR roster identifies that exposure. A December retail tax-loss result does not establish an October effect. Also, the prior CEF snapshots cannot simply be reused if public only after October formation; retime and recheck first if there is ever an independent reason to test this.

MEASURED, synthetic only. Holding the same last-October entry and strict marks, with MUB-like low-noise and PCEF-like higher-noise scenarios (assumed, no quotes or bars), +50 bp gives 2.99% and 1.74% power; +100 bp gives 14.30% and 5.64%; +200 bp gives 57.18% and 23.89%. False pass at zero edge is 0.18% and 0.38%; with a common +100 bp rally it is 0.97% and 0.90%. The two scenarios assume, respectively, 1.5%/3% benchmark noise, 1%/1.5% roster tracking noise and 1%/2% paired loser noise, with equal 50 bp round trips on losers and roster and 30 bp on the benchmark. See `python3 research_rig/calibrate_54.py --cef`. Do not advance: only an assumed +200 bp gross loser effect clears 50% even in the quieter scenario, with no established October mechanism to justify that size.
