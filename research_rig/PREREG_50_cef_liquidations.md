# Plan 50: Listed closed-end fund exits after public notice

## Question and scope

Buy a listed US closed-end fund after its first qualifying public liquidation or open-ending announcement, or before its already scheduled term date, then sell at its last regular trading close or the fixed earlier exit below. The question is whether this plain rule earns positive net returns and beats a matching plain fund. The [published Bradley, Brav, Goldstein and Jiang study](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/4118/activist_arb.pdf) supports a rationale for studying how discounts react to open-ending attempts, including anticipatory repricing; it does not establish this post-announcement, costed trading rule or its net outcome. No independently verified free dated entry NAV source was identified; neither the entry discount nor a proxy for it selects cases. This is not a test of targeting discounts, replicating the study, or earning the entire difference to NAV.

Only exchange-listed CEF common shares while still CEFs. Cash liquidation, scheduled term maturity and conversion to an open-end mutual fund or ETF qualify. Ordinary CEF-to-CEF mergers, tender offers, nontraded interval funds and OTC are out. For liquidation and open-ending use the first dated public board-backed proposal; retain its failures/withdrawals on the same footing as completions. A shareholder or charter proposal the board opposes or declines to recommend does not enter whether it passes or fails; show the count excluded for sponsorship. PCF's December vote is watch only, not a judged candidate. Accepted events must be reconciled with the discovery docket and a search for contemporaneous qualifying proposals regardless of outcome, including withdrawals, extensions, defeated votes and no-trigger cases. Keep each proposed transaction episode once; do not restart for proxy amendments, meeting postponement or improved paperwork. One campaign can contain different securities; maintain each separately and use shared month clusters. A retrospective success filing is corroboration, not entry evidence.

## Clocks and executable outcome

- **Announcement trades:** First public issuer release or SEC filing that communicates the board-backed terms. Use earliest credible publication; for EDGAR use filing acceptance, not upload label or meeting date. Normalize timestamps to UTC and cluster by New York public announcement month. Buy next regular SIP open at least 15 minutes after a timestamped signal (including same day if eligible). If public date only, use the next regular session; unknown public date is a listed pre-price exclusion, not assumed.
- **Term trades:** Buy at the open 20 regular sessions strictly before the legally operative term date known at that entry. Four trading weeks gives one round, practical lead time and neither optimizes the timing nor treats launch terms as new information. Use the original operative prospectus and dated intervening changes. If altered before the candidate entry, use the new operative schedule only when its own entry remains in the future; never reconstruct a missed entry with later filings. A board decision to accelerate an unentered term into an early liquidation is eligible as an announcement trade if independently qualifying; never enter both for one episode. Cluster on the term entry's New York month. Shared months across all types are a single cluster.
- **Completed trades:** Sell at the SIP close of the last regular-way exchange session of the CEF only if publicly scheduled before that close via issuer release, exchange notice or accepted filing. A same-day notice must have a verifiable pre-close release time; a date-only notice must precede the session date. A retrospective Form 25 may corroborate the actual final session, never establish prior awareness. Do not substitute delisting effective date, payout date, successor listing or a chart. When the lack of timely notice can be documented, use the 180-session close, never a last available print backdated from later knowledge. If that trade is no longer available it counts as missing exit coverage. If notice availability cannot be told from the records, exclude before prices and enumerate the uncertainty. The terminal conversion is not an intervening merger. Sell pre-conversion CEF at the scheduled close even if the shares later convert into a mutual fund or ETF; no NAV or successor execution in the judged line. Leaving any residual payout discount on the table is conservative to that extent; it need not underrate a cash hold if NAV falls.
- **Failed, extended, withdrawn or cancelled after entry:** Exit both legs at the first regular open at least 15 minutes after first public disclosure of failure, cancellation, withdrawal, or term extension. Date-only is next session; meeting date is not result time. Do not reenter that episode. Pre-entry failure/cancellation does not trigger a fill.
- **Time stop and scope:** Entry between January 1, 2016 and March 10, 2026 inclusive. Bars end November 24, 2026, 180 regular sessions after the last entry date. If a documented completed close or actionable cancellation exit does not precede or fall on session 180 after entry, sell at its close. A cancellation at the open of that session precedes the stop. If closing/cancellation evidence is materially incomplete and a time stop cannot be established, record an unresolved terminal clock (not a clean time stop). The pinned calendar reuses Plan 36 through Oct 30 and Plan 25's November session dates through Nov 24, with regular 7:30 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Mountain Time hours for that extension.

## Dollars, costs and corporate actions

Use independently allocated $500 event sleeves and $500 per matched ETF; buy only whole shares at cost-inclusive open, keep residual cash idle, no reinvestment, interest, margin or portfolio compounding. Zero separate commission and no modeled taxes; the 5 bp buffer includes minor charges, not itemized fees. Calculate dollar trading P&L plus eligible cash over the original $500, with equal event weights. Raw SIP regular-session open/close fields must have a bona fide print. This is an execution proxy, not evidence of an account fill at the opening or closing exchange auction. The benchmark is a long-only comparison, not a hedge.

Use Plan 36's duration-weighted median relative spread, as cited by Plan 48. Times here use Mountain Time (America/Boise); script timestamps are UTC. For opens, measure 7:24 a.m. up to 7:29 a.m. with warmup from 7:23 a.m. For closes, measure 1:54 p.m. up to 1:59 p.m. with warmup from 1:53 p.m., or the same minutes relative to the actual early close. These are the 09:24–09:29 and 15:54–15:59 exchange-local windows. Every quote update, including locked, crossed and zero states, ends the previous quote; locked, crossed and zero states count no valid time. Each valid quote lasts at most 60 seconds; require at least 180 seconds of valid coverage. Preserve independently evidenced vendor sequence for tied timestamps (last in sequence owns subsequent time). A nonpositive displayed size makes that side zero. If coverage is short use a 50 bp half-spread proxy and count the side. Pay 5 bp plus the entry half spread to buy, and 5 bp plus the larger of entry and exit half spreads to sell, measured or proxied. Compute each leg separately.

Credit documented ordinary and interim cash distributions with ex-date strictly after entry through exit date inclusive on both legs, from dated issuer records, including cash earned before an open exit on that ex-date. Do not credit an undocumented interim amount; report these zeros by leg. This lowers raw CEF P&L for missing CEF cash and can lower its paired excess. Missing benchmark cash pushes paired excess in the opposite direction; require independent oversight on both legs. Unknown, unreported payments can escape a count, so a diligence note must record the issuer coverage checked. An ordinary distribution known to exist requires an amount and date or the packet is incomplete. For shown-only cash liquidation/term holds, use the documented sum of entitled ordinary, interim and final cash, without a sale spread, only if the final amount is documented. Do not zero-credit a final payment, count it as a judged sale, or impute a value for a mutual-fund/ETF successor. The cash line is a potentially selected subset and is not paired with a benchmark on a mismatched exit date.

Refuse missing entry bars and report missing required exit bars, unknown terminal clock, inability to buy a whole share, and intervening splits/mergers distinctly. Refuse an intervening reverse/forward split or merger of either leg because no adjustment is modeled; the qualifying terminal conversion is not such a merger. A date-or-symbol ambiguity, unverified stable identity or incomplete packet is not a license to substitute a quote, trade, or proxy fund. Missing entry, missing exit and unresolved terminal clock together cannot exceed 5% of placed; otherwise the score is INCONCLUSIVE even if arithmetic marks pass. Eligibility exclusions and unaffordability do not count as feed omissions; still report them separately. Missing bars are explicit reviewed placeholders; a missing expected key is a malformed packet. No dropping rows after seeing prices.
