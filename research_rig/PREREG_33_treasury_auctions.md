# Plan 33: Intraday Treasury auction reversal

Status FROZEN.

Ledger 168, queue 52. This tests only a fixed long trade after nominal 10- and 30-year Treasury auctions, using liquid Treasury ETFs and displayed SIP quotes. It omits the pre-auction leg entirely. No ETF direction, spread, or return was measured to make these rules. Book coverage is not a measured result.

## Hypothesis and sample

[New York Fed Staff Report 1188, pp. 14–19 and table 2](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1188.pdf) measures the pre-auction window ending one minute before competitive bid close and the 180-minute post window beginning one minute after the actual results announcement. Its public result timestamp is based on Bloomberg stories; 1:10 p.m. is not proof that results have posted. A regular U.S. equity session closes at 4 p.m. The ETF replay is a clock proxy, not a replication of the paper's yield trades, release timing, or after-hours prices. After-sample means strictly after July 31, 2024, the paper's stated last sample date. There are 54 registered after-sample auctions, 27 per lane; April–October 2026 is descriptive only. The [NY Fed publication record](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr1188.html) gives a March 2026 publication and an October 2026 revision by month, not a timestamp adequate to verify a post-revision subset.

Take the 260 nominal 10- and 30-year events from January 2016 through October 2026 (130 each), including reopenings, on full XNYS sessions with a fixed 1:00 p.m. ET competitive close. Use the announced offering and auction date, not issue date or an ex-post CUSIP relabel. The [official Fiscal Data auction endpoint](https://api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov/services/api/fiscal_service/v1/accounting/od/auctions_query) projection is `auction_calendar_33.csv`; dates and announcements are fixed before any scoring. There are 12 per maturity in each 2016–2025 calendar year, 10 per maturity in the partial 2026 year, and 4–8 calendar days of announcement notice for every eligible event.

- **2019-11-05:** Treasury [announced a 3-year](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/instit/annceresult/press/preanre/2019/BPD_SPL_20191030_1.pdf) and only at the [result](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/instit/annceresult/press/preanre/2019/BPD_SPL_20191105_1.pdf) identified the offering as an unscheduled reopening of a 10-year CUSIP. Not eligible.
- **2019-06-21:** Genuine tenor-family reopening but an [11:00 a.m. contingency exercise](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/instit/annceresult/press/preanre/2019/BPD_SPL_20190620_4.pdf), not a regular 1:00 p.m. auction. Not eligible. Neither excluded date may be a control.

## Execution, coverage and capital

- **Fixed clocks:** On each auction or paired control, IEF for 10-year and TLT for 30-year. Buy at the first eligible ask timestamped within the inclusive window 13:10:00 through 13:10:15 ET. Sell the same entire quantity at the first eligible bid 15:59:00 through 15:59:15 ET. Do not use a prior quote, a later window, IEX, or a minute bar. Use America/New_York daylight-saving conversion and compare UTC nanosecond timestamps without truncation. Ties follow native API page order then row order: this stable convention is not exchange sequence priority.
- **Regular quotes:** Require a finite positive two-sided quote with `bid <= ask`, positive sizes and the exact sole condition `R`; a crossed quote is ineligible and a valid locked quote is eligible and counted. Convert historic SIP size to shares (100 times round lots before Nov. 3, 2025; share units on and after that date). Use only the relevant executable side's size for the order, not a midpoint or subsequent quote improvement. See [Alpaca historical quotes](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockquotes-1), [Alpaca quote-size change](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/changelog/marketdata-bid-and-ask-size-display-change), [CTA regular-quote specification](https://beta.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/data/TAQ_Pillar_Products_Client_Specification_v2.3f.pdf), and the [UTP NBBO specification](https://nasdaqtrader.com/content/technicalsupport/specifications/utp/uqdfspecification.pdf).
- **Sizing:** Two independently settled $250 sleeves in a $500 cash account. At each entry-quote candidate take the largest whole-share quantity whose ask plus buy fee fits its sleeve; if the entire quantity does not fit displayed ask size, wait for another eligible quote, not a partial fill. At exit, only the displayed bid size for the full fixed holding qualifies. Position is fully paid, no margin or shorting; no reuse of unsettled sales. Among dates assigned to a single sleeve, the minimum spacing is 17 XNYS sessions for each auction lane; controls are a separate replay whose IEF/TLT minima are eight and five. The pinned `sessions_33.csv` is the 2016–October 2026 [XNYS schedule projected with exchange-calendars 4.13.3](https://pypi.org/project/exchange-calendars/4.13.3/). Spacing clears historical regular settlement even under T+3, before the [switch to T+2](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2017-163) and current [T+1](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024-62). This is a fixed-capital trade demonstration with sleeve dollar P&L and idle capital shown, not a compounded $500 account return.
- **Not placed:** A complete, readable window with no eligible endpoint quote means the event remains in the denominator, is listed by date and missing endpoint, and contributes no return. If entry is missing, the exit can be flagged as missing when no otherwise regular quote exists; full-size exit eligibility cannot be assessed without a holding. If only the exit quote is missing, “not placed” describes retrospective replay completeness; it is not information knowable at entry or an estimate of a live safety exit. Exact start-minute [SIP trade bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars) are diagnostic only. An explicitly empty bar result does not prevent a trade. Missing/changed packet, broken schema, out-of-window timestamps, unresolved paging, or failed retrieval are refusals, not legitimate endpoint absences.

## Fees and controls

Model today's [Robinhood retail equity and ETF fee convention](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-fees-on-robinhood/) on all dates, not historical broker receipts: zero commission, SEC fee waived up to $500 sale principal (otherwise ceil to penny), FINRA TAF waived for sales up to 50 shares (otherwise nearest penny with the historical cap), and listed-equity CAT at $0.000003 per share per side, rounded to zero below $0.01 and otherwise to nearest penny. Report each separately. The pinned `fees_33.csv` supplies dated official Section 31 and TAF notices: SEC rate per $1 million principal, TAF per share. Apply dates to trades following the [FINRA trade-date charge convention](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/Information-Notice-20260317.pdf). Show a line restoring both full historical regulator fees at their exact rates and statutory caps without customer exemptions or penny rounding, with unchanged CAT. This line is not judged.

Net trade return is cash received after sell costs divided by cash paid including buy costs, minus one, equally weighted across placed trades. The spread is already paid at the ask and bid; report each half spread relative to its endpoint midpoint, and their sum in bps, without charging it again. Extra adverse 1 and 2 bp per side are shown on the same fills and fixed quantities as P&L stresses, not re-execution or claim conditions.

`control_pairs_33.csv` fixes the assigned ETF and clocks on one full-session control per event. Exclude all Treasury Note and Bond auction dates from controls, but allow bill dates. The 962-date `nonbill_auction_days_33.txt` is from [Treasury's Note/Bond auction extract](https://api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov/services/api/fiscal_service/v1/accounting/od/auctions_query?fields=auction_date,security_type&filter=auction_date:gte:2016-01-01,auction_date:lte:2026-10-31,security_type:in:(Note,Bond)&sort=auction_date&page%5Bsize%5D=5000). Reserve primary matches in auction-date order: same weekday, year, quarter, and paper-sample regime, nearest by calendar days, earlier on ties, no control reuse. Then relax quarter only for an unmatched event, at most 42 calendar days. The sole fallback is July 11, 2024 to May 30, 2024 (42 days); no price can change a match. Exclude incomplete pairs from paired statistics but not the pair-completeness denominator. An additional shown-only comparison removes a pair if either side falls on a scheduled FOMC policy statement date or on an unscheduled federal-funds target-rate decision date in the frozen `fomc_dates_33.txt` from the [current official FOMC calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm), [2016](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomchistorical2016.htm), [2017](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomchistorical2017.htm), [2018](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomchistorical2018.htm), [2019](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomchistorical2019.htm), and [2020](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomchistorical2020.htm). No rematching.

## All marks required

| Mark | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Placed count | At least 200 placed auctions overall and at least 100 each in IEF and TLT. |
| Placed share | At least 95% of all registered auctions (247 of 260). |
| Net mean | Strictly positive pooled mean, equal weight per placed trade. |
| Net t | One-sample t at least 2.0 with sample standard deviation; undefined/zero standard error fails. |
| Trim | Positive net auction mean after removing the three largest returns. |
| After-paper sample | At least 40 registered auctions strictly after July 31, 2024 and positive placed-trade mean in that subset. |
| Calendar-year breadth | At least 7 of 11 years with positive net mean, counting January–October 2026 as a year. |
| Paired control comparison | Strictly positive mean paired net auction-minus-control difference and paired t at least 2.0 among complete pairs. |
| Paired completeness | At least 95% of fixed pairs complete (247 of 260), no rematching. |
| Maturity breadth | Positive mean in each maturity lane. |
| Leave one year out | Positive mean after omitting each individual calendar year. |

Show cycle coverage and missing dates for both books, mean/median/sample dispersion/t, fees, half spreads, dollar results, paired differences by year, maturity and their intersection, both late subsamples, and all leave-one-year-out figures. Show equal-ISO-week means and descriptive ISO-week cluster standard errors for event and paired mean (pairs assigned to auction ISO week), not independent causal inference. A quote snapshot and displayed size do not establish actual routing, odd-lot protection, quote lifetime, or guaranteed fills.
