## Dates, fills and costs

- Use New York exchange sessions. `replay_06_data.first_bar` to the session before `design_start` is warm-up only. Design is `design_start` through the session before `held_start`. Holdout is `held_start` through `last_bar`, inclusive. A rule and its yardsticks reset to cash at the start of each slice. Use only past closes to form its first target: the last eligible month-end close for monthly rules or prior session close for the daily rule. No position crosses the boundary. Reserve the holdout for one builder-authorized replay.
- Signal from the last eligible close and fill at the next exchange session's Alpaca open. Report a next-session close sensitivity, not a second pass verdict. Use the exchange calendar for actual month-ends and for early closes. Do not treat the truncated last month as a complete month or look ahead to decide a month has ended. Fail the input check on a missing session, nonpositive price or required traded bar without volume. Do not fill on invented data, and do not sell fictitiously at the end.
- Judge at the upper end of `cost_model_bps_per_side_liquid_etf`. Charge it on each executed dollar buy or sell, including cash proxy trades and yardsticks. Sell before buying; scale purchases so cash covers costs. Model fractional units, long only, no leverage or tax. Report cost-free and doubled-cost comparisons without changing signals or choosing a cost after the fact.

That file does not establish the open auction spread or a cost for EFA, IEF, VNQ, DBC or BIL. The judging cost is a flat assumption for spread and slippage, not a measured fill.
- Park idle rule sleeves in `replay_06_data.cash_fund` (BIL); do not combine this with additional bill interest. Use its frictionless daily close return as the common risk-free proxy for Sharpe and the return above bills in G3. Residual settlement cash gets no interest. ## Rules and yardsticks

The six scheduled trials are fixed in this plan. Use `replay_06_rules` for the fund set and parameters. When a price or return exactly equals a threshold, it is not above it. Divide independent G1 and G2 sleeves equally at the first fill, then let the sleeves drift separately. Cash balances inside one sleeve cannot fund another. Compare with the corresponding same-fund yardstick in each test slice. Report SPY hold for every rule as an additional common yardstick regardless of which one is judged.

| Id | Rule | Main yardstick |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | For each of `g1_g2_funds`, hold the fund when its last month-end adjusted close exceeds the simple mean of its last `trend_months` month-end closes, including the signal close. Otherwise hold BIL for that sleeve. Review only at month-end. | Equal parts held in the same funds, rebalanced on the first session open of each year. |
| G2 | For each of the same funds, at each close find the fraction of `ensemble_sessions` simple averages the adjusted close exceeds, including the signal close in each average. That fraction is the fund target within its sleeve. Trade back to target only when its difference from the sleeve's actual weight at the decision close exceeds `practice_rebalance_band`, including moves to and from zero. | Same as G1. |
| G3 | At each month-end compare SPY's `momentum_months` close-to-close return to BIL's over the same window. Hold SPY if it is higher, otherwise hold IEF. | SPY hold; also report a fixed equal-weight SPY/IEF mix, rebalanced yearly. |
| G4 | Apply G1's month-end trend rule to SPY alone, else BIL. | SPY hold. |
| G5 | Each month-end screen all configured funds except BIL by G1's trend rule. Pick up to `leader_slots` of the survivors with highest `momentum_months` adjusted returns. Sort exact ties by symbol. Reset equal slot weights monthly, with unused slots in BIL. | Hold equal parts in the same eligible funds, rebalanced yearly. |
| G6 | Set SPY to `g6_spy_weight`, the remainder to IEF, on entry and the first session open of each year. No timing choice. This allocation is a context row, counted, not a candidate timing edge. | SPY hold. |

Yardsticks follow the same next-open convention at entry, reset at the same boundaries, and pay the same modeled side cost. At the close of each slice, report net growth, annualized Sharpe above BIL, largest drop, trade sides, turnover and average weights. Report exposure-matched comparisons against the primary yardstick plus BIL, labeled hindsight because the average exposure is known only after measurement.

## Pass marks

- **Mark 1**: On the held-back slice, daily excess-return Sharpe beats the main yardstick's Sharpe and maximum daily closing drawdown is at most `replay_max_drop_ratio_vs_hold` times its drawdown.
- **Mark 2**: Whole-sample growth compounded across the two reset slices beats the main yardstick at zero friction and stays ahead up to `backtest_min_breakeven_cost_multiple` times the judging cost. Use the existing rig's first-crossing grid. Reprice the same execution instructions, and charge the test cost to both rule and yardstick.
- **Mark 3**: Whole-sample excess daily returns. Deflated Sharpe must exceed `replay_min_deflated_sharpe`. Use the sample standard deviation of finite per-period Sharpes across the whole frozen batch as the comparable spread proxy. Count at freeze: `replay_06_statistics.prior_count_at_draft` plus `new_trials`, which is the 116 in the Counts section of `TRIAL_LEDGER.md` at freeze (reruns R1 and R2 included; unrun replay 10 and the AI runs not included) plus these six. Count G6, not the yardsticks or mechanical cost and fill sensitivities.
- **Mark 4**: CSCV across all frozen trials on aligned daily excess returns. Use `replay_09_statistics.pbo_blocks` equal contiguous blocks, dropping excess days at the start. Compute it from `replay_06_data.design_start` and the fixed `replay_06_statistics.pbo_later_starts`, each through the held end. Judge the largest estimate against `backtest_max_pbo`; count a median tie as overfit and report every estimate. An undefined mark fails.
- **Mark 5**: Beat strictly more than `replay_min_shuffle_percentile` of the whole-sample timing controls, with draws and seed from `replay_09_statistics`. Within each slice rotate the full target sequence as a vector, keeping the cross-fund targets together and the original decision clock (daily slots for G2, month-end slots for G1, G3, G4 and G5). Require at least `shuffle_min_daily_shift` or `shuffle_min_monthly_shift` slots away from zero in both directions. Reapply the same cash, band and fee rules. Report actual turnover and average weight against those in the draws because unchanged target weights do not guarantee unchanged realized turnover. A constant schedule, unavailable rotation or undefined mark cannot prove timing skill. G6 has no timing control and cannot pass as a timing rule.

Run synthetic tests for session boundaries, calendar gaps, no double dividend, next-open fills, cash sizing, drift, annual rebalance, rotation and the reset boundary. Use `fixtures2.py` and an independent audit before the one held-back run. A pass would only justify a forward paper test.

## Power and ways to fool ourselves

- **MEASURED, sample size**: Use the count of complete holdout months in `fund_coverage_06.json`, not the count of daily rows or the row count times the number of funds. Monthly decisions persist, the funds move together, and the rules overlap. The holdout is a few market regimes, not independent trials for every row. No signal frequencies or rule returns have been measured.
- **STATED, power sketch**: Assume independent monthly rule-minus-hold returns, annualized active volatility `replay_06_statistics.power_annual_active_sd`, and desired power `power_goal`. With N complete months, the annual standard error is assumed active volatility times the square root of months per year divided by N. For a one-sided screen at `replay_min_shuffle_percentile`, multiply the standard error by the sum of the normal critical value and the normal quantile for the desired power. The worked example is in `fund_adjustments_06.json`. The 46 complete held-back months counted in `fund_coverage_06.json` cannot confirm a small edge. Even a pass here is weak evidence. The practice desk already trades a rule in this family, T1 on VTI, and this is the cheapest check that the family is not broken on recent years.
- **STATED, bias risks**: Known funds are a selected surviving list. Index history and earlier replays informed what to test, and the period does not span the crisis the older draft assumed. Dividends are represented by revised backward factors, not a point-in-time event ledger; the levels rescale history, and vendor corrections and rounding can still affect close ties. Opening trades, capacity, taxes and actual reinvestment remain unknown. A missed or unexplained distribution and thin power are reasons to postpone, not reasons to tweak the rule list.
