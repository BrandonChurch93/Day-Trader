## Judged book and clock

- **Question.** Does the funding spread between perpetuals pay for a three-day rotation after stated costs? Use the cross-sectional version from queue 27. Do not also score the short-perpetual-plus-spot version, which asks a different question and would need its own row.
- **Period.** From 2022-01-01 00:00 UTC through the existing Plans 16 and 17 funding cutoff, 2026-10-26 00:00 UTC. Rebalance every 72 hours on the same clock from the first date, without resetting at a year boundary. Do not open a book in the final stub shorter than 72 hours. Count 2026 up to the cutoff, not as a full year.
- **Point-in-time roster.** At each rebalance use coins ranked 1 through 30 by market capitalization in the latest saved monthly snapshot whose publication time was at or before the rebalance. No backfilling from ranks below 30. If none is known, the latest is over 45 days old, or its identity or release order is ambiguous, refuse rather than guess. Use one live perpetual per coin, pinned to its dated identity with then-known listing evidence, not by ticker text. A full hour must be inside the dated fence; no unverified alias or symbol reuse. Use only then-known facts to choose the book, not future delistings or future coverage.
- **Funding rank.** Sum the nine completed Binance settlements on the 8-hour UTC clock in the preceding 72 hours, including the left end and excluding the rebalance timestamp. Require coverage at every hour of that window, exactly the nine expected events with finite rates, the same dated contract throughout, and a completed trading bar with positive volume immediately before rebalance. Contracts with absent or off-clock past payments are ineligible. This intentionally selects contracts on the 8-hour grid for ranking, not all listed contracts. Sort ascending by sum, coin ID, then contract key. Long the first three and short the last three, without overlap. If fewer than six qualify, stay in cash for that slot.
- **Orders.** Rank before reading the current open. Use exact executable perpetual opens for fills; if any selected opening fill is missing, stay in cash for all 72 hours with no replacement. Split pre-entry equity equally into six notional lots, three on each side, so gross starts at 1× equity and net starts at zero. Hold fixed quantities until the next rebalance; both sides close in full and the next book reopens, even if names persist. Charge fees on actual entry and exit notional. Cash yields zero.
- **Funding and risk.** Positive funding is paid by longs and received by shorts; use signed quantity times the preceding completed mark close times the recorded rate. An event at the rollover belongs to the outgoing book, not the new one. While a book is open, apply observed on- or off-grid events only where funding-event coverage is established. A verified covered hour without an event is not a zero-rate event. Refuse the proxy rather than invent a missing exit, mark, funding coverage, or price outside a dated fence. A nonpositive proxy equity also refuses marks. This is a fully collateralized research proxy, not a model of any venue's margin, liquidation, multipliers, minimum orders, or taxes.

## Marks and shown figures

Judge only the point-in-time proxy at 12 bps on each side of each fill. Show the same book at 1 and 24 bps a side. Check all three marks together. No other roster or cost can rescue the judged result.

- **Sharpe at least 1.0.** Use daily UTC close-to-close net equity returns, sample standard deviation, zero cash rate, and square-root 365 scaling. Use the old book's completed mark and settlement at a UTC boundary, and its exit if due, before any new book's entry. Include days spent in cash. An undefined Sharpe does not pass.
- **Largest fall at most 25%.** Measure peak-to-trough loss from hourly mark and funding events plus equity after fills and fees, not from 72-hour ends alone. A wholly inactive book does not pass.
- **Positive in at least three of five calendar years.** Compound daily returns onto the calendar day that ends at the next UTC midnight. Report all five and call the truncated 2026 period out.

Run 200 random books at the judged cost on the same eligible universe and dates. Use seed 18027 plus the zero-based draw number. Each rebalance draws six distinct contracts uniformly, in order, from the eligible pool sorted by coin ID and contract key; the first three go long and the last three short. Apply the same fill, coverage, and accounting rules, including cash for missing selected entry opens. Show median, 5th and 95th percentiles (linear interpolation on the sorted draws), and the fraction at or above ranked-book Sharpe, over draws with defined Sharpe. Also show undefined and incomplete counts and comparable return and drawdown quantiles. This is descriptive, not a fourth mark or an independent test. Do not shop for seeds.

Report price P&L, funding P&L, fees, traded/cash/refused slots and the funnel from monthly rank to dated eligible contract before comparing books. The draft computes the marks, core accounting and yardstick draws; a future audited adapter and report writer must publish these diagnostics and preserve refusals. UNKNOWN. Coverage near top ranks, duplicate mappings, off-grid events, missing opens and exits, and how much of each US roster can actually join to the archive are unmeasured. No score if the input provenance or scope does not support the stated point-in-time rules.
