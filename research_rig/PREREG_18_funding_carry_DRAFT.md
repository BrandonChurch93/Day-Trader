## Book, decision clock and executions

Use the cross-sectional strategy. Decide every 72 hours from 2022-01-01 00:00 UTC. At the next hourly open, long the three lowest-funding eligible contracts and short the three highest in equal dollars. No same-bar fills. Stop at the 2026-10-26 00:00 UTC archive cutoff. Do not open a book it would be impossible to hold for a full rotation before the cutoff. Cash yields zero, including the initial gap before the first fill and the final stub. This is not the different short-perp against spot question.

- **Rank clock.** The stored Binance bulk files contain settled values and event times, not publications or a revisions log. Treat a finalized rate as usable only strictly after its event time plus eight hours. On the standard 00/08/16 UTC grid the nine rate observations for a decision at hour (t) run from (t-80) through (t-16), inclusive. The (t-8) settlement is deliberately excluded. The dated FTT funding change below keeps the same window and lag but changes which hours are scheduled. A prior trade bar is taken from (t-2) so that its close is strictly before (t); it must have positive volume. This is a fixed lagged proxy, not a claim that the archived version was known then. No predicted or unsettled rate is used. Require observed coverage throughout the window, with a settlement in each scheduled hour and none outside the schedule. Use the configured tolerance only to assign archive events to hour buckets. Preserve exact source-clock offsets, in integer microseconds, for lag and realized cash accounting. Report off-grid, absent and uncovered exclusions.
- **Decisions and turnover.** Sort ascending by total input funding, coin ID, then dated contract key. Use disjoint tails. Fewer than six qualified contracts means cash. Any missing selected next open also means cash for that rotation with no substitute. Keep old positions until the next open. Settlements before the open use the outgoing quantity; a settlement exactly at the open uses the outgoing quantity before fills by convention. Settlements strictly after use the incoming filled quantity, including a resized survivor. Use the latest completed hourly mark at the settlement instant, not a price from a later close. Target equal-dollar fixed quantities from pre-trade equity across six legs, at 1× gross before fees. Charge only executed notional `abs(new_quantity - old_quantity) × open_price` per name. The comparison that exits in full, pays the exit charges, and opens from the resulting equity is shown separately at the judged cost. No costs are charged on idle cash or hourly marking. Recorded hourly opens do not prove real basket fillability. No margin, liquidation, spread beyond the scenario cost, partial-fill, minimum-order, or tax model is implied.
- **During a holding.** Apply observed on- and off-grid realized funding. Only off-grid hours bracketed by actual settlements from the same identity no more than one scheduled interval apart can count as covered non-events. A missing scheduled event, empty or truncated tail, or unbracketed hour is unknown, not zero. An unknown occupied hour takes the data-gap exit. If a mark is missing while trade continues, use the last eligible completed traded close. Positive funding debits longs and credits shorts. At an occupied trade or funding gap, fence, or missing exit open, close that leg at the last executable hourly trade close and charge the judged 12 bps on that notional at every cost. If no post-entry bar completed, use the executed entry price. A dated closure can zero a long only at the effective discontinuity, with observed post-last-trade rows and no evidence or unresolved gap suggesting later same-identity trading. An earlier gap or missing follow-up uses the last good trade close. No extra gain for a short and no funding after forced exit. Record each ranked and shown case; random books retain counts by seed. Nonpositive model equity still refuses marks.
