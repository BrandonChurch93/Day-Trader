## Book, decision clock and executions

Use the cross-sectional strategy. Decide every 72 hours from 2022-01-01 00:00 UTC. At the next hourly open, long the three lowest-funding eligible contracts and short the three highest in equal dollars. No same-bar fills. Stop at the 2026-10-26 00:00 UTC archive cutoff. Do not open a book it would be impossible to hold for a full rotation before the cutoff. Cash yields zero, including the initial gap before the first fill and the final stub. This is not the different short-perp against spot question.

- **Rank clock.** The stored Binance bulk files contain settled values and event times, not publications or a revisions log. Treat a finalized rate as usable only strictly after its event time plus one full funding interval. On the registered 00/08/16 UTC grid the nine rate observations for a decision at hour (t) run from (t-80) through (t-16), inclusive. The (t-8) settlement is deliberately excluded. A prior trade bar is taken from (t-2) so that its close is strictly before (t); it must have positive volume. These are conservative reconstructions, not proof the later archived rate version was published then. No predicted or unsettled rate is used. The interval filter requires coverage throughout the ranking window and one event on each expected 8-hour boundary, with no off-grid event. Normalize only bounded timestamp jitter using the configured tolerance and keep the original offset for the lag check. Report off-grid, absent and uncovered exclusions separately.
- **Decisions and turnover.** Sort ascending by total input funding, coin ID, then dated contract key. Use disjoint tails. Fewer than six qualified contracts means cash. Any missing selected next open also means cash for that rotation with no substitute. Keep old positions until the next open. Record old-book funding due at that open before orders, using the preceding completed mark, then mark old positions to the applicable executable opens. Target equal-dollar fixed quantities from pre-trade equity across six legs, at 1× gross before fees. Charge only executed notional `abs(new_quantity - old_quantity) × open_price` per name. Same-side survivors are resized, not closed and reopened. The comparison that exits in full, pays the exit charges, and opens from the resulting equity is shown separately at the judged cost. No costs are charged on idle cash or hourly marking. Recorded hourly opens do not prove real basket fillability. No margin, liquidation, spread beyond the scenario cost, partial-fill, minimum-order, or tax model is implied.
- **During a holding.** Apply observed on- and off-grid realized funding where archive coverage is established. A covered hour without an event is not a positive or negative payment; an uncovered settlement hour is a data-gap exit, not a zero payment. If a mark is missing while trade continues, use the traded close and count the fallback. Positive funding debits signed long quantity and credits shorts. Once an occupied contract hits a trade or funding gap, an accounting fence, or a missing rollover or final exit open, close that leg at the last executable hourly trade close and charge the judged 12 bps on that last-close notional at every scenario cost. If no post-entry bar completed, use its own executed entry price. An evidenced delisting with no subsequent same-identity trade through the available archive cutoff marks a long to zero. It never gives a short an additional gain. Without that evidence, use the last good trade price for either side. No funding is assumed for a leg after the forced exit. Record each instance, including side, date, last traded close, valuation, fee and reason; the other legs and the book continue. The ex-post terminal label is used for loss accounting only and never to pick names. Nonpositive model equity still refuses marks.
