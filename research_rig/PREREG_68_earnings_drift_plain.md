# Plan 68. Earnings drift without AI

This is the price-blind screen for queue 17 and ledger 213. It closes before prices: the count-weighted simulation reached 7.15% at the generous earlier-clock proxy after modeled costs, below the required 33%. This is not a score or permission to pull bars. The original announcement clock and the later structured filing clock are not interchangeable.

## Rule to count

- STATED. Work by issuer CIK and fiscal quarter, not current ticker. Use S&P 500 and S&P MidCap 400 membership known at the start of the acceptance quarter. Use the most recent published full roster snapshot available before local New York midnight on the first calendar day of the quarter, hold it fixed within the quarter, and keep names that later disappear. If both lists contain the same issuer and share class cannot be resolved, refuse it rather than guess. An archived roster is a known-as-of proxy, not proof that index changes were entered on their effective dates.
- STATED. On [SEC Financial Statement Data Sets](https://www.sec.gov/data/financial-statement-data-sets), join `SUB` to `NUM` by accession, and keep the original 10-Q or 10-K for the fiscal period with its SEC acceptance time, not the report date or the day EDGAR labels it filed. Require an unambiguous one-quarter, consolidated, standard US GAAP diluted EPS fact using USD per share. If any of the required quarters lack that EPS, use consolidated net income divided by positive weighted diluted shares for every quarter in the window, not a mixture of earnings measures. No annual EPS, annual-minus-YTD EPS, adjustments, amendments, forward revisions, price-derived inputs or analyst forecasts. A quarterly fourth-quarter fact is allowed; a full fiscal year is not. A known or unresolved split-basis mismatch would need separate SEC evidence before any trade. See [SEC field definitions](https://www.sec.gov/files/financial-statement-data-sets.pdf).
- STATED. For quarter `t`, take `D[t] = EPS[t] - EPS[t-4]`. Divide by the sample standard deviation of `D[t-8]` through `D[t-1]`. This requires 13 consecutive fiscal quarters, known by acceptance, with no zero denominator. Use the original as-filed historical figures; no fact accepted later than the signal can help compute it. Report ambiguous units, missing facts, transition periods and competing issuer identities as exclusions, not zero surprises.
- STATED. Top tenth cannot mean the best releases when the current quarter is over. Use the last completed acceptance quarter's pooled large and mid-cap score distribution. Sort descending by score, then ascending CIK; use the row at the ceiling of one tenth of the reference count as the cutoff. Buy when the current pair of score and tie-break is at least as high in that ordering, and skip if no prior cutoff exists. Counts show one tenth of usable releases as an expectation, not the number this cutoff would actually select.
- STATED. Buy at the first regular US equity open strictly after acceptance, then exit at the regular open 60 exchange sessions later. The matched comparison is SPY for the large-cap group and [IJH](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239763/ishares-core-sp-midcap-etf) for the mid-cap group, over exactly the same entry and exit sessions. A score, if ever authorized, would use the difference in total returns net of permitted fills, not the gross stock return and not the best-minus-worst spread.

## Published long side, not the hedge return

- STATED. [Liu, Mao, Tang and Zhou, Table 9, p. 49](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2024/program/paper/5GGEki7i) report +88 bp gross for the highest analyst surprise quintile on announcement days +2 through +60, S&P 500 issuers, 2010 to 2021. This is a CAPM abnormal return on an options-eligible sample, not return minus a matched ETF, and the equity entry print and costs are not reported. Their +294 bp top leg is conditional on ranking by options-implied earnings risk; that is not this rule. The paper's after-cost option result is not a stock result.
- STATED. [Scanlon, Table 3, p. 49](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/-/media/frbp/assets/working-papers/2021/wp21-07.pdf) reports +16 bp gross for the top conventional analyst-SUE quintile over days 1 through 63 in 2010 to 2019 against a size/book-to-market portfolio. The stock strategy's entry is the first close after the call, the largest positive comparable result found with a specified tradable close. No trade costs are deducted and no ETF benchmark is shown. The larger text-driven and short-leg numbers are not this rule.
- STATED. [Trading Studio's US study](https://blog.tradingstudio.finance/earnings-surprise-drift-us-stocks/) reports −4 bp gross for the best analyst surprises, 63 sessions from the next day's close relative to dividend-adjusted SPY, over 2000 to 2025. It does not isolate 2010 onwards or the large and mid-cap tiers. Its top-minus-bottom +142 bp is not a long-side estimate. The +88 bp above is the strongest close-duration unconditional comparable size found, not an exact tradable estimate; this −4 bp is the cleanest explicitly tradable stock-minus-market print found.
- STATED. [Gupta's 2023 to 2026 study](https://guptagaurav.info/articles/earnings-drift) uses next-day closes and a signal based on the firm's own past EPS. Its reported +200 bp large-cap and +140 bp mid-cap 60-day figures are top-minus-bottom. Its after-cost calendar book also buys and shorts. Neither can supply the long leg or be divided by two and called a measurement.
- STATED. The [Agentic Sciences write-up](https://agentic-sciences.github.io/assets/papers/pead_earnings_surprise.pdf) reports +376 bp long-side gross relative to an equal-weighted market in 2015 to 2024, but starts on announcement day 0 and gives no executable entry clock. This can count the announcement jump. Not an admissible next-print estimate.
- STATED. [QuantGPT's filing-date rule](https://quantgpt.co/library/post-earnings-drift) uses its own prior-year EPS and rebalances monthly to 20 stocks, so filing information may already be weeks old. It reports 10.2% annual growth from 2015 versus 14.2% for the S&P 500 total-return benchmark in its base comparison. Over its entire sample the separately reported 25 bp per side case compounded at 8.4% versus the benchmark's 9.4%. This is a later-clock long-only comparison, but neither number is a next-open 60-session paired mean and CAGR cannot be divided into an event return. [Li, Nekrasov and Teoh](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11142-019-09519-7) study drift after 10-Q filings through the next earnings release using a size/book-to-market comparison, not an after-cost 60-session top-decile return versus a fund.
- UNKNOWN. A published after-cost long-only estimate for the exact acceptance-to-next-open, 60-session stock-minus-fund rule was not found. For the screen, give the idea the earlier clock's +88 bp gross as a generous proxy, not a mathematical upper bound. Later filing likely gives up some announcement drift, but how much is unknown and a change of window cannot guarantee a smaller realized return. Apply a 10 bp minimum stock round trip against a gross matched leg for a +78 bp modeled net scenario; also plant the entire +88 bp as net to avoid closing solely on that friction assumption. Neither is a published net effect. The common plan cost would be 5 bp or a cent per share, whichever is more per side; no quote or notional was used to estimate the one-cent floor.
- STATED. [The Tilburg study, Table 1, p. 37](http://arno.uvt.nl/show.cgi?fid=192469) reports a 1,130.572 bp standard deviation for market-model CAR over days +2 through +60 among 42,893 observations on S&P 500 issuers, 2003 to 2025. It winsorizes at the first and 99th percentiles and uses an estimated market beta. This is a published marginal event-noise proxy, not the dispersion of top-decile return minus the matched fund or the dispersion of quarter means. The [Stockholm thesis, Table 1, pp. 16–18](http://arc.hhs.se/download.aspx?MediumId=6317) gives 19.042% for the full sample CAR on the same horizon; its pronounced 2021 to 2024 result is a long-short contrast, not the long leg. UNKNOWN. Actual within- and across-quarter dependence of the proposed matched difference.

## Synthetic decision

- STATED. Apply the [Doc 117 screen](../docs/research/117_power_screen_before_data.md) to all four fixed marks: mean stock-minus-fund above zero; quarter-clustered intercept-only CR1 t at least two; mean still positive after dropping the best ceiling of 1% of entries; and positive mean in at least seven of eleven calendar years. Use the actual entry quarter and entry year. For `N` entries and `G` quarters, each quarter score is the sum of its entries' deviations from the full-sample mean. The SE is the square root of the product of `G/(G-1)` and the sum of squared quarter scores, divided by `N`. Compute the t on all entries, not on the trimmed series.
- STATED. The [synthetic script](power_68.py) uses 43 quarters: four per year through 2025 and three in 2026. Model 50 events per quarter in the central case, 0.50 residual within-quarter correlation, independent quarters, and Gaussian event noise at the published lower dispersion proxy above. Also model 20 events with the same correlation, 90 events and lower within-quarter correlation, and shared residual shocks with 0.25 correlation between adjacent quarters. The common part of a rally with equal stock and matched-fund exposure cancels; any unequally shared move remains in the residual. Adjacent-quarter overlap and repeat issuers are not solved by CR1. No estimated dependence or normality claim is made.
- MEASURED on invented returns only. The [saved output](POWER_68.json) counts 30,000 worlds per case. With central heavy overlap, the +78 bp modeled after-cost case clears all marks in 9.34% of worlds, and giving the long side all +88 bp as net clears in 10.58%. With zero planted edge the rate is 2.61%. The nearest tradable explicit SPY comparison, −4 bp gross or −14 bp at the same modeled cost, clears in 1.98% at −14 bp. The first central tested edge to reach 33% is +200 bp net.
- MEASURED on invented returns only. With 90 entries and 0.25 residual correlation the +78 bp and +88 bp cases reach 13.84% and 16.45%; with 0.10 they reach 26.56% and 32.39%. They do clear if residual correlation is just 0.05, at 43.70% and 52.90%. That is a weak-dependence stress, not an estimate of dependence or authority to proceed. Under the requested heavy-overlap screen, even the earlier-clock proxy is below the line. Recommend closing before prices; the decision is about available evidence and power, not measured strategy profitability.
- MEASURED on invented returns only. Rounding each measured quarter's expected tenth to the nearest whole entry gives 1,150 artificial trades across the 43 quarters. With that uneven history and 0.50 residual correlation, the +78 bp and +88 bp cases cleared in 7.15% and 8.11% of worlds. At 0.25 they cleared in 10.06% and 11.69%; even at 0.05 they cleared in 25.50% and 31.07%. The first tested edge to reach 33% in the count-weighted heavy-overlap case was +250 bp net. The favorable weak-correlation crossover above needs both much higher event counts and weak dependence, neither established for this rule.
