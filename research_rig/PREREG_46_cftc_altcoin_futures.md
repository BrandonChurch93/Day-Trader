## Two rules

- **A | Long before the stated launch.** Enter spot at the first trade in the archive-plus-two UTC bucket. Require at least five calendar days from the start of that UTC day to the 00:00 UTC launch-date boundary. Exit at the last trade in the UTC daily bucket before the launch-date boundary. Match the same side in BTC-USD on exactly the same buckets and fields.
- **B | Short after launch.** Enter using the spot-open proxy at the later of the first 00:00 UTC after the stated first trade date and 00:00 UTC on archive date plus two. Exit on the first trade in the bucket starting seven calendar days after entry. Same-day and short-notice certifications are not skipped. For a launch dated before publication the max rule enters at archive date plus two. Match a short BTC-USD position on identical buckets and fields.
- STATED. Fixed study boundary 2026-11-26 UTC. No window whose end boundary is later than that day. Only necessary complete buckets go in the private packet, even if a bucket is used only for its open. Within each leg, sort by entry then archive date, venue code and detail ID; keep the earliest same-coin overlapping window, with touching windows allowed. ## Marks and power

Each valid event has equal weight. Net P&L is signed adverse-fill exit minus entry, less a fee on each fill and any funding debit, divided by unadjusted entry reference notional (funding is then debited at its stated fraction). `paired` is that net coin return minus the net same-side BTC return. No capital stacking or reinvestment is implied. The standard error is for the mean, clustered by CFTC archive date, with the finite-cluster factor G/(G-1); zero/undefined variation gives no t-statistic and fails the t mark.

Pass each leg only if it clears all six marks: at least 30 placed events; at least 12 distinct archive dates for A or 25 for B; positive mean raw net; positive mean paired net; paired date-clustered t at least 2.0; positive mean paired net after dropping all observations on the best three archive dates ranked by within-date mean paired net (earlier date breaks a tie). Show earliest-in-this-docket per coin, and B without negative-notice events, without new marks. Do not call the earliest roster record first-ever: wider historical docket coverage is UNKNOWN.

MEASURED (public dates only). The pre-candle ceilings are 43 events on 15 dates for A and 113 events on 33 dates for B. At perfect within-date dependence, unequal weights reduce the equivalent number of dates to 7.87 and 14.11, respectively. STATED synthetic calibration in `calibrate_46.py`: with 35 independent equally weighted Normal posting-date means and t at least 2.0, the mean paired edge needs about 0.478 date standard deviations for 80% power (about 2.39 percentage points at an illustrative 5 pp date SD). This is for the t mark alone; real variance, cross-date correlation, the missing-bucket rate, and chance of all marks clearing are UNKNOWN. There is no familywise adjustment behind the t cutoff. The [SF Fed Bitcoin futures letter](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/economic-letter/2018/05/how-futures-trading-changed-bitcoin-prices/) motivates the pre/post directions, but it does not establish these clocks or an altcoin effect.
