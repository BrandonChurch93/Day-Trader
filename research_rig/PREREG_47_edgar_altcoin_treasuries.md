## Filing docket first

The [price-blind docket](DOCKET_47_preprice.md) accounts for all 50 scout rows and the independent discovery pass. The final audited count was 29 eligible rows (26 Coinbase, 3 Kraken), below the floor of 30 and not the scout's 40. No prices were requested and the docket is retained as a forward list. For reuse: a 30-row roster would leave no slack, so one refused primary event would fail the count mark and leave other return figures descriptive only.

Use [EDGAR full indexes](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/full-index/), [EDGAR full-text search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) and the original 8-K or 6-K plus incorporated exhibits. The discovery date range is January 2025 through November 25, 2026 Eastern. Scan for treasury-language variants without anchoring to the scout's selected names; test the scout's list separately. A keyword search can miss euphemisms, image exhibits, and announcements filed under other forms, so report the search and admission funnel, not a census of all real-world plans. Use a declared contact and follow the [SEC's access policy](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/accessing-edgar-data.htm). Exact acceptance is the SEC submission header, converted from New York time to UTC; no guessed filing-day clock. Earliest-event date (cover/header, if the form identifies one), dated press release and timed release (if present) are separate fields; record the gap and its precision. A Form 6-K period date is not automatically an earliest-event date. Do not open prices, vendor candles, chart or quote pages, or separate news before the freeze; flag any incidental price exposure and keep the reviewed docket price-blind.

An initial plan is the first public SEC disclosure wherein the issuer or the actual resulting entity commits to a named token treasury (for example, adopts the plan, launches it with board approval or announces a signed funding transaction for it). Conditional completion is allowed when the named financing or combination is real; generic exploration without such a commitment, standing asset management, customer inventory, indirect ETP exposure, mining/staking only, advisers announcing another company's plan, purchases under an existing plan and recap/closing updates are out. Do not count BTC, XBT or ETH. Treat a symbol or an ambiguous asset name as unresolved until the filing identifies the token. Check previously filed reports, aliases, issuer/partner and predecessor documents where relevant. Deduplicate issuer/coin across its CIK history and the same public transaction filed across CIKs; when a joint event is truly the same resulting entity and strategy, take the earliest eligible EDGAR acceptance that discloses it. A multi-coin announcement yields separate rows only for expressly adopted eligible token plans, with acceptance-day clustering reflecting the shared date.

The lead's stale-news rule: if the first earlier public release has a stated time and time zone, require EDGAR acceptance no later than the configured hour cutoff after it, inclusive. If there is only a calendar date for the public release, or otherwise the 8-K's earliest event date, use it only if it matches the New York acceptance date. An expressly contemporaneous public release can control over a dated private signing that precedes it. Keep demonstrably filing-only disclosures, flagged; no separately timed earlier release may be inferred from an undated attachment. If the first public timing is uncertain, exclude. Record later release timing separately when the filing was first. Preserve the earlier accession, dates or quoted narrative that supports exclusion.

Only a Coinbase USD spot market documented at acceptance makes Coinbase eligible. Use Kraken USD spot only with dated Kraken evidence for that exact spot product and dated official evidence for earlier Coinbase nonlisting. Under the lead's 06:37 UTC ruling, a later exact USD spot opening counts as such evidence only when all three hold: Coinbase's dated announcement says the exact pair will add support, open or start trading after the event minute (or specifies an opening no earlier than the event time); it contains no wording about a relisting, migration or reactivation; and a recorded search of Coinbase's own announcements, including aliases and old listing terms, finds no earlier exact listing. This is affirmative first-opening evidence plus a search check, not proof from silence alone or a complete listing-history archive. The doctrine is applied identically to Applied DNA BNB and both TON rows. Only their pinned CoinbaseMarkets post URLs are allowed for fallback, bound to exact issuer CIK, accession, token and UTC acceptance in the validator. The docket retains the search and corroboration links. Do not infer coverage from current pair metadata, USD stablecoin pairs, trading in another country, or candles. No venue switch triggered by missing data. Keep unresolved identity, timing, precedence, or venue in the audit, not the scored manifest.

## Price protocol and score

Buy the underlying coin long at the raw opening print of the first one-minute bucket on or after acceptance plus the configured lag. An exact minute boundary qualifies. Exit at the exact opening print of the bucket one primary hold later. Use Bitcoin/USD opens at the same times on the same venue and report the equal-weight mean of the paired coin-net minus Bitcoin-net returns. This is not a hedged account: Bitcoin is a matched long benchmark, net of the same cost on both legs, and is not traded or shorted in the rule. The shorter and longer holds are shown only and cannot rescue a failed mark.

Source after written approval only: [Coinbase Exchange historical candles](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/reference/exchangerestapi_getproductcandles) and [Kraken historical minute OHLCVT archives](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360047124832-downloadable-historical-ohlcvt-open-high-low-close-volume-trades-data). Kraken's [live OHLC endpoint](https://docs.kraken.com/api/docs/rest-api/get-ohlc-data/) cannot provide archived minutes beyond its recent buffer and is not a substitute. Retain each requested key as observed with positive volume or explicitly documented missing from a proven completed archive/request. A documented missing entry on either leg refuses all holds; a missing exit refuses only its own hold, including shown-only holds. Count each refusal and do not advance the clock. An incomplete source, unexplained gaps, archive period not published yet, or unavailable authenticated access blocks the entire bundle before score, including for shown-only keys.

Cost equals the published retail entry-tier taker fee per side where available, plus the fixed half-spread per side. [Coinbase Advanced](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/trading-and-funding/advanced-trade/advanced-trade-fees) directs customers to a signed-in fee schedule; actual owner rate is UNKNOWN, so use the [public Coinbase Exchange lowest-volume taker schedule](https://help.coinbase.com/en/exchange/trading-and-funding/exchange-fees) as the judged 60 bp proxy and show 120 bp on Coinbase only. [Kraken Pro ordinary spot](https://www.kraken.com/pro/fees) publishes the base ordinary USD spot retail tier (80 bp); confirm schedule and pair exceptions at freeze. The assumed 15 bp half-spread is not measured. Actual opening spread, queue position, slippage, price impact, order minimums, and the historical account-specific tariff are UNKNOWN. Net the exit after taker fees and the half-spread against the entry after the same costs, compounded, for both the coin and BTC. This is a per-event return screen with separate hypothetical allocations, not a single deployable capital curve.

Require all six: configured minimum placed primary events; strictly positive raw coin net mean; strictly positive paired excess net mean and an acceptance-date clustered t at or above the configured threshold; both means strictly positive without the best three paired-excess events; both means positive with SOL removed; both means positive with each coin capped at its earliest three placed events. Use chronological UTC acceptance, accession, then coin for ties in ranking or caps. For the paired t, use an intercept-only event-weighted mean with the one-way UTC filing acceptance-day CR1 adjustment (G/(G−1) times the sum of squared day residual sums divided by n²). Entry can fall on the next UTC day without moving the cluster. Fewer than two days or zero variance is undefined and fails. This does not remove dependence across neighboring date clusters, campaigns, venues, or repeated coins. A sensitivity result is not a mark.

## Independent custody contract

Do not collect until the last requested minute, including the shown-only 72-hour exit, has closed and the official source actually covers it. A recent filing can postpone all custody; a Kraken publication lag does not make any archive minute absent.

After approval only, the custodian preserves exact HTTP body bytes under `inputs/native/<body_sha256>` and request/coverage receipts under `inputs/native/<receipt_sha256>.json`. These receipts name each key covered, request URL and parameters, venue and product, HTTP status, retrieval UTC, any redirect and published archive period/member identity, and each native timestamp located or proven absent. For Coinbase use a successful complete 60-second-granularity response requested for the exact UTC minute and next-minute boundary; do not use out-of-window rows. An empty list alone does not prove absence: require repeat successful exact coverage and a confirmed liquid-side neighboring bracket for that product with no outage or transport ambiguity, otherwise refuse custody. For Kraken retain the original archive with the official release and period, validated archive integrity and exact case-sensitive USD pair member and 1-minute interval; require the expected period and neighboring observations within that covered member before interpreting absence as no published candle. Record archive member and byte-level extraction procedure. For either venue duplicate/malformed bars, zero-volume published bars and conflicting retrievals refuse custody; do not convert them to missing. Reuse identical body objects by hash, but record all request/coverage receipts for each byte-identical body in its sealed per-object metadata.

A separate independent no-return source reviewer checks all four keys per hold for every event against the frozen scope, traces each to a successful complete native request or archive member, independently parses exact open and volume and asserts decimal equality with the bundle, or independently proves the absence from closed coverage. The lead obtains that reviewer's `custody.json` SHA-256 separately from the collection bundle and supplies it as `--custody-sha256`; never take both pins from the collector alone. The review's exact schema is `schema: 1`, `status: INDEPENDENT_NATIVE_NO_RETURN_REVIEW_COMPLETE`, hashes `approval_commit`, `manifest_sha256`, `bundle_sha256`, native `objects` (keyed by body hash with `urls`, an exact product-to-source-endpoint map without Coinbase query parameters, and `receipt_sha256`), and sorted `keys` rows of venue, product, UTC minute, reviewed state and body hash. The runner checks exact binding and rehashes retained bytes. It cannot certify the source's real-world authenticity, the contents or sufficiency of the receipts, or infer a missing candle from bytes by itself; the independent review is mandatory, not a row label supplied by the collector. This reviewer is distinct from the price-blind preregistration auditor. No return, price comparison across minutes, or airing of sample prices during custody review.

## Power and authorization

Forty events is thin, particularly if 16 are SOL as in the unverified scout inventory and several can cluster on the same day. The price-blind calibration used 2,000 synthetic runs per sample size, about three distinct days per four events, 40% SOL, 5% event noise and 2% shared UTC-day noise in excess, 3% BTC day noise, and an 8% chance of an independent signed 10 percentage-point shock. BTC has the modeled Coinbase fee/spread drag and mean-zero gross returns. All synthetic events are placed; a shorter real sample, worse clustering or higher volatility is not measured here.

In the planted model, 40 events caught a very large +4 to +6 percentage-point mean excess in about 87% to 99% of all-six trials, but caught a modest +1 to +2 pp excess in only about 7% to 29%. Even +3 pp missed all-six acceptance about four times in ten. With 30 synthetic events the corresponding all-six rates for +1/+2/+3 pp were about 7%/24%/53%. These simulations are sensitivity checks, not historical power estimates; a failed screen would not rule out a modest edge. Do not collect prices below the configured count floor. Any rerun using the actual pre-price concentration structure would be shown-only and cannot move a mark.
