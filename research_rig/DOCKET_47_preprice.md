# Plan 47 price-blind docket: scout and independent discovery

This is a **price-blind docket, not an approved event manifest**. The 50 scout accessions below have exact UTC acceptance from their original SEC submission headers. The SEC header date is the reported period; for a 6-K it is not treated as the event date. Dates in the notes are issuer-stated public dates where evidenced, distinct from private signing. A date-only clock does not have an exact timestamp or exact gap. No prices were fetched. An independent reviewer signed the count after checking the disputed issuer rows and the three fallback venue rows against the lead's 06:37 UTC rule.

The original [EDGAR full-text search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) discovery run queried five treasury phrases by month (January 2025 through November 25, 2026) and found 2,223 unique document hits covering 1,687 distinct accessions before issuer, token and narrative review. A separate named-token phrase pass yielded 378 document hits, including false matches from generic legal, asset-manager and naming language. Per-issuer backsearch checked named strategy phrases in 8-K, 6-K, 425 and selected periodic and registration forms back to 2022; issuer-specific synonyms and historical predecessor gaps remain. Neither hit count is a count of events.

“Candidate” denotes the initial scan, not admission; “Replacement” means reject the submitted accession and adjudicate an earlier lead independently. The count reconciliation supersedes initial scans; 40 cannot be used as a real sample size. The independent reviewer locked a stratified blind SEC sample before viewing this table; the acceptance clocks matched. One search-result session later surfaced third-party price-related headlines to the auditor during venue metadata work; those pages were not opened, their values were not retained and none of the SEC determinations used them.
