# Plan 53: taxable closed-end fund year-end tax-loss rebound

Status DRAFT. Queue 90, ledger 187. This draft does not authorize a price pull, validation, or a scored run. The point-in-time taxable roster and independent review are pending.

STATED: [Brauer and Chang (1990)](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/3/2/255/1595700) find a January seasonal in closed-end fund shares but not their net asset values, and a negative relation between turn-of-year share returns and prior returns. Plan 53 takes that direction and applies Replay 48's rule to taxable rather than municipal closed-end funds. It is not a replication of their regressions.

## Window, bars and orders

- **Window:** For each turn from December 2016 through January 2026, use the pinned NYSE calendar. Rank by raw price return from the first January session close of year Y to the second-to-last December session close of Y. Enter at the last December close of Y. Judge the last January session close of Y+1. The fifth January session close and a zero-cost replay are shown only. Zero cost uses the judged whole-share sizes. All shared numeric controls live in `replay_48_plan`; the taxable scope and benchmark controls live in `replay_53_plan`.
- **Bars:** Use vendor raw, unadjusted SIP daily OHLCV bars; timestamps are UTC Z corresponding to midnight New York on the session, not an execution timestamp. Use the daily close, not an intraday bar, midpoint, NAV, vendor-adjusted close, or the next available close. Volume is a nonnegative integer. Keep zero-volume records in transport but treat them as missing trades and unusable closes. A missing or zero-volume session between anchor and entry inclusive disqualifies a fund for that turn. PCEF must have every usable session through the judged exit.
- **Ranking and sizing:** Among roster members with complete usable bars through entry and no split after anchor through entry, rank the lowest raw return first, tied alphabetically by ticker. Skip funds for which one share is unaffordable after entry cost; buy the worst five that fit, each in its own $100 sleeve. Use the largest whole-share count that fits, with idle leftover cash and zero commission. Refuse if too few qualify in any turn. Net dollars divided by $500 give the yearly portfolio return. A held fund lacking a usable close on an exit is carried at the last usable close on or before it, with the larger of entry half-spread and proxy plus the side fee, and counted. Never drop such a position from the replay.
- **Quote inputs:** Use SIP bid and ask updates from 15:53:00 New York time up to but not including 15:59:00, in timestamp order with vendor order preserved for ties. Measure the duration-weighted median of the relative spread (ask minus bid divided by midpoint) from 15:54:00 to 15:59:00 New York time, requiring at least 180 valid seconds. An update is valid from its timestamp until the next update, its 60-second expiry, or the window end, whichever comes first. Locked, crossed, or zero bid/ask updates end the prior state at their timestamp and contribute no valid duration; only the last quote at a tied timestamp can contribute positive duration, and a window with zero updates falls back to the proxy. The side charge is half the spread plus 5 bp. Use a 50 bp half-spread proxy if coverage is short; the exit charge uses the larger of the entry and exit half-spreads plus 5 bp. Quote prices determine costs, not fill prices.

## Point-in-time roster

- **Discovery:** Start with the [SEC closed-end fund snapshots](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/closed-end-fund-information). For year Y, use the year Y snapshot and restore omissions only on proof of inclusion in an SEC snapshot at or before Y, or dated registration of the common share listing (Form 8-A12B) by December 1 of Y, as in plan 48. Later snapshots can identify candidates for lookup, not establish eligibility at an earlier date. Require dated proof of listing as of December 1 on a US national securities exchange. Identify the common share ticker from SEC or exchange records, not the present-day master. Exclude names with effective delisting by the cutoff from Form 25-NSE or equivalent dated issuer/exchange evidence; keep dead and merged funds in all their earlier eligible years. A fund that stops trading after entry stays in the replay.
- **Taxable classification:** Require a traditional, exchange-listed closed-end management investment company's common shares and affirmative taxable investment mandate. Use the latest controlling SEC prospectus or N-2 and amendments public by December 1 of Y; use a dated SEC shareholder report quoting the operative objective or its dated issuer equivalent if necessary. Keep an older still-operative mandate if there is no superseding change. Taxable means the mandate is not to invest chiefly in municipal debt for tax-exempt income and is not to deliver federal tax-exempt income. Taxable municipal debt by mandate is not automatically municipal tax exempt; no fund-year in Replay 48 is admitted. Test both CIK/year and historical ticker/year against the reviewed Replay 48 roster. Fund name, present status and the tax character of a distribution do not establish the mandate.
- **Exclusions:** Exclude interval funds from a Rule 23c-3 or equivalent prospectus/report disclosure of periodic repurchase, and exclude open-end funds/ETFs, BDCs, OTC or unlisted shares, and preferred shares based on the registration, mandate, share class and listing evidence. Exclude a term trust whose binding termination date on the record public by December 1 is on or before the entry plus 12 calendar months. Use a dated prospectus, charter or binding amendment; an unexercised extension option is not an extension. If listing, mandate, fund type, or applicable term cannot be documented, exclude the fund-year with a reason and the checked record. Do not use prices or present-day survival to make these decisions.
- **Files:** `roster.csv` has `turn_year,ticker,cik,fund_name,listing_exchange,listing_source_url,mandate_asof,mandate_source_url,classification,term_end,term_source_url,sec_roster_url`; `classification` is `taxable` for every admitted row. Dates use ISO format and CIKs have leading zeroes. For a perpetual fund, leave `term_end` empty and provide `term_source_url` for the operative full prospectus or charter that establishes no binding termination when read with its amendments. Silence in an annual report is not enough. `roster_exclusions.csv` has `turn_year,cik,ticker,reason,evidence_asof,source_url`; blanks for unestablished facts are allowed and explained in the reason. Sort included and excluded rows by turn year and stable security identity. The reviewer checks evidence and the muni anti-joins before the lead pins both files by SHA-256 and authorizes any price acquisition.
- **Cash workload:** UNKNOWN until the checked roster is delivered. The live scout count is not the historical roster size. Report the file's exact number of fund-years, by year and total, plus one separate benchmark check per turn, before collecting distributions. Use permitted SEC EDGAR filings, issuer machine-readable histories, dated issuer PDFs and issuer-authored releases on a wire used by the issuer, or exchange records if machine-readable access is permitted. Stop on blocked paths; no aggregators, vendor distributions, blocked-site bypasses or inferred ex-dates. An exception for a mirrored original requires a separate lead ruling, not a generic syndication rule.

## Cash, splits and yardsticks

- **Cash:** Add documented cash per share held, ordinary and special, with ex-date strictly after entry and on or before the exit or last usable held close. No reinvestment or tax adjustment. Record exact ex-date, amount and source from issuer, issuer wire, SEC or exchange evidence. December declarations with January ex-dates count; January pay dates alone do not. Check continuous records for extras, not just the regular declaration. A confirmed no-ex-date window is not a cash gap. A fund-turn with no documented cash and no confirmed absence has empty cash, a checked source, zero credit and a gap count; include it in the off-repo coverage file. Unlike Replay 48's empty-list wording for missing cash, Plan 53 explicitly keeps verified events if another payment is suspected but cannot be documented, and counts that partial fund-turn as a gap. No guessing. Missing cash could move the roster comparison either way; the price-only loser mark is still required.
- **Splits:** Document forward/reverse splits with ex-date after the first January close date through the judged exit, from a dated issuer, SEC or exchange record. The raw pre-entry ranking excludes a fund split in that span before or on entry. After entry, multiply held shares by the new-per-old ratio from the split ex-date, including cash on that date. Keep native vendor split responses and a raw versus split-adjusted close check with only row counts and factor steps. A large consecutive usable close change with no recorded split refuses for review; see `split_check_ratio` in `replay_48_plan`. Do not use split-adjusted prices to rank or score.
- **Roster yardstick:** Equal mean of the eligible $100 sleeves, same clock, cost and cash rules. This is the direct taxable mix comparison.
- **Tradable yardstick:** PCEF in one $500 whole-share sleeve with the same clocks and costs. [The 2014 PowerShares prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1378872/000119312514072819/d656290d485bpos.htm), [the revised 2019 Invesco filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1378872/000119312519198906/d754378d497.htm) and [the December 2025 Invesco prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1378872/000119312525325627/d54028d497k.htm) identify PCEF as an NYSE Arca-listed fund tracking the S-Network Composite Closed-End Fund Index. [The SEC's March 2016 structured filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1378872/000119312516499448/R260.htm) also states the same index and its taxable CEF categories. These holdings better represent underlying closed-end fund discounts and leverage than a stock and bond ETF blend; its index categories and weighting do not match the replay roster exactly. Refuse without sourced PCEF cash or a documented no-ex-date window for each turn. Refuse any benchmark split, missing session, or unaffordable share.

## Marks and interpretation

- **Turns:** All ten turns score.
- **Net mean:** Mean yearly portfolio return is above zero.
- **Positive years:** At least eight years above zero.
- **Against PCEF, mean:** Mean paired portfolio minus PCEF is above zero.
- **Against PCEF, years:** At least seven years ahead of PCEF.
- **Without best year:** Mean after removing the single best year is above zero.
- **Loser effect:** Mean paired portfolio minus roster is above zero with documented cash and above zero with all cash zeroed on both sides. Report both.
- **Shown only:** Fifth-session exit, zero cost, per-turn roster, eligible, held, missing exits, quote-coverage proxy sides, and distribution gaps. `held_quote_coverage_proxy_sides` counts only sides whose quote windows did not meet the coverage rule. The missing-exit count is separate: a missing exit bar forces the missing-exit cost even if its quote window had adequate coverage. Pair the ten judged taxable and municipal portfolio returns by turn year and report Pearson correlation, year pairs and sample count. Report UNKNOWN for correlation if either series is constant. No correlation threshold or extra mark. The lead decides whether results count as one family; overlap in years and mechanism means a second pass is not independent proof.
- **Limits:** These are only ten turns. A broad closed-end fund rally can look like a tax-loss rebound. No tax lots or seller motives are observed. The close is a fill proxy. Delisting valuation and missing payout records are explicit limitations. Synthetic calibration is a check of the marks under stated assumptions, not measured market power. It uses the same noise, seed, marks and planted edges as Replay 48 so the benchmark wrapper cannot change the synthetic pass rule.

MEASURED: `research_rig/CALIBRATION_53.json` records the synthetic pass rates. The null is below the configured ceiling; a market-wide premium without loser excess still passes some worlds, and small planted loser excess often fails. No real bars or payout inputs enter this calibration.
