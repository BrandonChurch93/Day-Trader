## Calendar and trade

- **Expiry sample:** All 34 actual monthly CME bitcoin expiries after IBIT's listing through October 2026, 33 Fridays plus Thursday 2024-03-28. Use last Friday and that single holiday shift. Keep the 2024-11-29 and 2025-11-28 scheduled early-close expiries because the exit clock is before the early close and those are actual expiry events ([CME rulebook chapter 350](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/350.pdf), [Nasdaq IBIT listing](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=ETP2024-04), [holiday and early-close calendar](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/nyse-group-announces-2024-2025-and-2026-holiday-and-early-closings-calendar-2023-11)).
- **Friday controls:** Every other full regular-session Nasdaq Friday in exactly the same span, not matched within month. There are 108 scheduled controls, split 39, 38 and 31 by calendar year. Show actual placed/eligible control counts and excluded-date reasons. Early closes and closures are excluded from controls even if the window itself would fit ([Nasdaq 2026 schedule](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/trader.aspx?id=Calendar), [2024–2026 holiday and early-close calendar](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/nyse-group-announces-2024-2025-and-2026-holiday-and-early-closings-calendar-2023-11)).
- **Clocks:** Long IBIT from 11:00 to 16:00 Europe/London; convert each boundary on that local date to UTC before fetching. This is 06:00 to 11:00 ET when US/UK clocks are aligned and 07:00 to 12:00 ET during DST mismatches. Select the first eligible quote at or after each boundary in the half-open first minute `[boundary, boundary + 60 seconds)`. The minute is the opening minute of the paper's hour, chosen from its time scale rather than the collected spread data. It is a fixed execution tolerance, not a claim that the paper specifies minute fills. No look-back, stale fill, other feed or later fallback. The shown-only shorter window uses 14:00 with the same tolerance and exit ([paper, §3 and Table 4](https://zaguan.unizar.es/record/125822/files/texto_completo.pdf), [Webull session times](https://www.webull.com/help/faq/10960-What-are-the-trading-hours-for-stock-and-ETF-orders)).
- **Eligibility and fill:** Require a native SIP quote with finite positive bid/ask, noncrossed (`ask >= bid`, locked quotes allowed), and positive bid and ask sizes. At a repeated timestamp use source order. Submit a hypothetical marketable buy limit at the ask; assume fill at that ask, including pre-market. Sell at the bid of the first eligible exit quote. Routing, queue, accessible displayed size, order handling and actual fills are not verified. Equal initial cash of $500 per event, no compounding, leverage or fractional shares. Buy the most whole shares the entry ask permits; unused cash earns zero. No snapshot or no affordable share means no scored round trip. If a placeable entry has no exit quote, report `no_exit` and exclude it as an unpriced trade, not as a cash/no-fill trade or a successful close. No late exit or substitute data in any arm ([Alpaca historical quote endpoint](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockquotes-1), [Webull session order types](https://www.webull.com/help/faq/10960-What-are-the-trading-hours-for-stock-and-ETF-orders)).
- **Costs and unit:** Zero commissions and published historical SEC Section 31 and FINRA TAF on sales, keyed by trade date as a charge-date approximation. Round each positive modeled regulatory charge up to cents per sale; zero stays zero. Do not charge the quoted spread again. This is a stated sell-fees-only model, not claimed exact Webull billed fees; broker pass-throughs such as CAT, taxes and uncaptured slippage are outside the judged line. P&L is sale proceeds minus sell fees minus purchase cost, divided by the starting $500, including idle cash. In the shown friction lines subtract the fixed extra bps once per round trip on deployed buy notional, not each leg ([Webull pricing](https://www.webull.com/pricing), [SEC 2024 advisory](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024-47), [SEC 2025 advisory](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/fee-rate-advisories/2025-2), [SEC 2026 advisory](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/fee-rate-advisories/2026-2), [FINRA historical TAF](https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2024-27764), [FINRA 2026 pause](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-09/SR-FINRA-2026-021-91-FR-60435.pdf)).

## Judgment and shown lines

All six marks must pass on the same primary net-return series. Use equal weighting across placed round trips; include the shifted Thursday in expiry marks except the explicitly Friday-only comparison. No resampling or best-of. Numbers are machine-readable in `config_35.json`.

- **Placement:** At least 30 placed expiry round trips.
- **Net mean:** Strictly greater than zero.
- **Student t:** One-sample t at least 2.0 using sample variance.
- **Trim:** Drop the best two expiry outcomes; the remaining mean must be strictly greater than zero.
- **Friday control:** Placed expiry-Friday mean minus placed non-expiry Friday mean strictly above zero, with unpaired Welch t at least 2.0. Compute on the same IBIT execution/cost rules. List eligible and placed control counts; a missing or unpriced round trip is not a zero. These groups are not randomized or equal in size; report a Welch comparison, not a matched or causal result.
- **Breadth:** Net mean strictly above zero in at least two of the three calendar years; a year with no placed events is not positive. Undefined means, t statistics and zero standard errors cannot pass their marks.

Shown only: 14:00–16:00 London, +2 and +5 bps added round-trip friction, and the same clock on Bitstamp BTC/USD hourly, expressed as an open-to-open descriptive spot cross-check without fees, not a placeable fill proxy or substitute. No shown line rescues or changes any mark. Give event and control counts for each line. No variant entries, exit rules, data venues or cuts beyond those listed here.
