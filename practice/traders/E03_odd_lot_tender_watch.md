## Check filings each business day

1. Run `E03_SEC_USER_AGENT='Your organization actual-contact@example.org' python3 practice/forward_e03.py scan` before 9:30 a.m. New York time and check again after the open before recording a new entry. Replace the example contact with a real monitored address. The command combines the [documented EDGAR full index](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/accessing-edgar-data.htm) with the [Latest Filings Atom feed](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions) for filings not yet in the index. It reads serially, with a minimum 0.4 seconds between starts; do not run concurrent copies or exceed the SEC's aggregate limit of 10 requests per second. A failed scan is not a zero-filings day. Use `--since YYYY-MM-DD` to cover missed days, including quarter boundaries. The default four-day lookback intentionally repeats unreviewed lines; each accession is printed at most once per scan.
2. Each `CANDIDATE` is a Schedule TO-I to screen, not an assertion that it pays cash. Each `AMENDMENT` is a Schedule TO-I/A to match to the original accession, not merely by issuer. Read the primary filing and the actual Offer to Purchase to distinguish fixed-price or Dutch-auction all-cash offers for listed common equity from noncash, preferred, NAV-formula, BDC, nontraded fund and plan-only offers. For a confirmed candidate, quote the controlling affirmative sentence covering beneficial holders, and save its SEC exhibit URL. Keep purely record-owner-sounding transmittal wording only if it does not prohibit beneficial participation and flag it. Discretionary priority and silence do not qualify. A required fixed ownership date that a new buyer cannot satisfy excludes; the holder's own signature date plus holding through expiry does not. Check and flag that wording. Do not treat mentions of odd lots in risk factors as priority.
3. Copy the SEC filing-detail acceptance time in UTC, not the filing date, press-release time or index time. Save one `confirm` per original and one `amend` per linked amendment, then run `plan ORIGINAL` to print the entry clock without a price. Recheck EDGAR before recording the entry. Read and retain needed market inputs privately as specified below, even for a possible cut or withdrawal that occurs before final tabulation. At final results, use `outcome`; no result means pending, not zero and not a pretend sale just because the daily check found nothing.

## Entry and outcome

- **Entry:** The first regular NYSE open at least 30 minutes after acceptance, inclusive, using the filed floor and expiry known at that open, with the same 30-minute lag for amendments. The schedule uses the [official NYSE closures](https://ir.theice.com/press/news/details/2024/NYSE-Group-Announces-2025-2026-and-2027-Holiday-and-Early-Closings-Calendar/default.aspx); early-closing days still count. There is no entry on or after the election cutoff session, which is two sessions before expiry under the current date rule. There is no entry where terminal results were already actionable. The recorded entry uses the first opening price, not the clock time the operator gets to the record.
- **Sizing:** Pretend entry only if the verified unadjusted SIP daily open is at least 1.5% below the then-effective nominal floor. Use at most $500 after per-share cost of the larger of 10 bps or $0.05, with whole shares capped at 99. Equality counts. If the opening print is not yet available from the approved feed, wait; do not substitute a quote, an indicative print, a close or the next day's open. An eligible same-session entry prints an `E03 ALERT` line for the lead with issuer, accession, date, discount and pretend share count. The script does not send messages. After the entry day, a record is marked late and cannot produce a forward alert; likewise, known terminal filings prevent a same-day alert on retrospective entry. Do not backfill older entries as forward evidence.
- **Tape review:** As in Plan 21 use point-in-time security identity, raw SIP for any fill, and adjusted data only for identity and staleness QA. Document the filing-date and entry-date symbol mappings, three-session positive-volume identity check, and the five-session unchanged-OHLC and zero-volume checks. The operator performs these checks; the recorder enforces the bar date, source, raw feed labeling and positive volume, but does not pull Alpaca or mechanically verify the corroborating tapes. Use only data available at record time and do not read market data for the filings-only open list. A required print that cannot be verified is a refusal, not a no-trade attributed to the discount.
- **Exit:** A matched withdrawal or first qualifying price cut leads to the first raw SIP open at least 30 minutes later, with the same market friction. Otherwise use filed tender consideration without proration, crediting cash on the explicitly stated holder-payment date or the fifth session after final expiry if absent or ambiguous. A later same-issuer SEC filing can provide explicit final per-share consideration for this offer; pin its exhibit or filing URL, never infer from totals. Verify corporate-action coverage and source dates through the modeled window; follow the [plan's distribution hierarchy and ex-date convention](../../research_rig/PREREG_21_odd_lot_tenders.md). Withhold the numerical outcome if evidence is incomplete. The $0 fee is unchanged for the recorded pretend return, not an assurance of any broker fee or successful election.

## Record packets

Run `python3 practice/forward_e03.py MODE ORIGINAL /private/packet.json`; for `amend`, add the amendment accession between the original and packet. Use a private file outside all repos (mode 0600), UTC times as `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`, and calendar dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`. Numeric cash amounts and opens may be decimal strings; never put them into the public log by hand.

| Mode | JSON fields |
|---|---|
| `confirm` | `cik` as a digit string, `issuer`, `symbol`, `filing_date`, `acceptance_utc`, `offer_type` (`fixed` or `dutch_auction`), `floor` (fixed price or Dutch minimum), `initial_expiry`, verbatim `quote`, `url` (the same accession's Offer to Purchase), `review_url` (the reviewed transmittal or corroborating filing), `review` (`eligible`, `eligible_form_wording`, `eligible_signature_date`, or `eligible_both_flags`). These values attest to manual all-cash, listing, eligibility and priority checks. |
| `amend` | `acceptance_utc`, same-accession SEC exhibit or filing `url`; optional `new_expiry`, `new_floor`, `terminal_notice` (`completed` or `withdrawn` when already filed) and private `note`. Record material terms as filed. Use `outcome` separately when ready to assess the terminal filing. |
| `entry` | `filings_checked_through` (UTC, at or after the modeled open), private `tape_review` (or clock-based reason no bar is needed), `bar` unless election cutoff or a terminal result rules it out. A bar has `symbol`, `feed` (`sip`), `adjustment` (`raw`), `session`, `open`, `volume` and credential-free `source` on Alpaca's data host. |
| `outcome` | Terminal filing `accession`, `acceptance_utc`, matched `url`, `status` (`completed` or `withdrawn`), `final_expiry`. If completed, give `price`, optional `payment_date` if proven and `price_url` if a later filing supplies the price. For a pretend trade also give `coverage_through`, `coverage_note`, `corporate_actions` and `exit_bars` (bar schema above, empty if no possible market exit was used). Actions have `kind`, `date`, `url`, plus `ratio` for a split or `payment_date` and `cash_per_share` for a `cash_distribution`. The distribution's `date` is its ex-date. Incomplete, ambiguous or unsupported facts belong in the operator's private notes until resolved. |

Each stage is written once. The full private packets are exclusively created in `/home/user/workspace/rig/data/forward_e03`; `practice/forward/E03_log.json` contains only filing evidence, dates, stage summaries, private payload hashes and a previous-record hash. It is created only on the first record; add its INDEX row then. If the history or a private receipt is missing or mismatched, stop and reconcile against the committed history. Never amend a previous observation or delete a receipt to force the next write. Calendar coverage ends in December 2027; extend it from an official calendar before using a later event.
